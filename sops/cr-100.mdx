## Key points

- CRFs must capture all primary and secondary outcome measures defined in the protocol, plus safety data, medical history, and concomitant medications as required. **Only data defined in the protocol** will be entered in the CRF.
- Each CRF must include site and participant identification, visit number and date. Standard CRF sections may include: Randomization/Registration, Demographic, Eligibility, Baseline/Screening, Treatment, Vital Signs, ECG, Physical Exam, Laboratory Evaluation, Imaging, Medical History, Prior/Concomitant Medications, Adverse Events, <Tooltip tip="An adverse event meeting seriousness criteria: death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability, or congenital anomaly." headline="Serious Adverse Event (SAE)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#sae">SAE</Tooltip> Reporting, Follow-up, End of Study, and Investigator Sign-off.
- Each CRF field must map to a source document or source data entry point. Fields that cannot be sourced must be justified.
- CRF design must be reviewed against the protocol and the Data Management Plan ([SOP-CR-103](/sops/cr-103)) before database setup begins ([SOP-CR-104](/sops/cr-104)).
- Version control applies to CRFs: every change to a CRF after study start must be documented, version-stamped, and the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>/<Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip> updated.

## 1 · Purpose {#purpose}

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the processes involved in the
design, development, approval and distribution of Case Report Form(s) (CRFs).
CRFs are designed to collect the information required by the clinical research or study
protocol for each study subject.

---

## 2 · Scope {#scope}

This SOP applies to investigator-initiated clinical research, whereby Case Report Form
design through distribution is the responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator.

---

## 3 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

The **Sponsor-Investigator** is responsible for ensuring that CRF design meets all applicable regulatory, ICH-<Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, and local requirements. Responsibilities may be delegated to appropriately trained team members.

## 5 · Procedure {#procedure}
### 5.1 General principles

**5.1.1** The CRF is a printed or electronic document designed to ensure that all data required by the protocol are captured and reported to the Sponsor-Investigator on each trial subject. Only data defined in the protocol will be entered in the CRF.

**5.1.2** CRF design must be completed and reviewed against the study protocol and the Data Management Plan ([SOP-CR-103](/sops/cr-103)) before database setup begins → [SOP-CR-104](/sops/cr-104).

**5.1.3** Each CRF field must map to a source document or source data entry point. Fields that cannot be sourced must be justified in the Data Management Plan.

### 5.2 CRF sections

Depending on the data required by the study protocol, a standard CRF may include, but is not limited to, the following sections:

| CRF Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Site and participant identification | Links data to a specific site and participant |
| Visit number and date | Identifies the visit and date of data collection |
| Randomization/Registration form | Documents randomization assignment |
| Patient Demographic form | Captures basic demographic information |
| Patient Characteristics form | Study-specific baseline characteristics |
| Eligibility form | Documents inclusion/exclusion criteria assessment |
| Baseline/Screening form | Pre-treatment assessments |
| Treatment form | Treatment, doses, administration routes, frequency, escalations/reductions |
| Vital Signs form | Blood pressure, heart rate, temperature, weight, height |
| ECG form | Electrocardiogram results (if applicable) |
| Physical Exam form | Physical examination findings |
| Laboratory Evaluation (tests) form | Laboratory test results |
| Imaging form | Radiology/imaging results (if applicable) |
| Medical History (including relapse/recurrence form) | Medical history and relevant events |
| Prior and Concomitant Medication log | All medications taken before and during the study |
| <Tooltip tip="Any untoward medical occurrence in a participant, whether or not related to the investigational product." headline="Adverse Event (AE)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ae">Adverse Event</Tooltip> form | All adverse events observed during the study |
| SAE Reporting form | <Tooltip tip="An AE meeting seriousness criteria: death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability, or congenital anomaly." headline="Serious Adverse Event (SAE)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#sae">Serious adverse event</Tooltip> documentation |
| Other Important Safety Issues Reporting form | Any other safety concerns |
| Follow-up forms | Post-treatment follow-up assessments |
| Patient Completion form | Documents date, reason, and circumstances for cessation of visits/data collection (withdrawal, death, progression, or other) |
| End of Study (EOS) form | Final study visit documentation |
| End of Treatment form | End of treatment documentation |
| Investigator date sign-off form | <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI sign-off on completed CRFs |

### 5.3 Version control

**5.3.1** Apply version control to all CRFs. Each version must have a version number and date clearly indicated on each page.

**5.3.2** Any change to a CRF after study start must be documented, version-stamped, and filed in the <Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip>/<Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>. Communicate CRF version changes to all affected data entry personnel.

**5.3.3** Superseded CRF versions must be retained in the TMF for audit purposes.

### 5.4 Electronic CRFs (eCRFs)

**5.4.1** Electronic CRFs must comply with 21 CFR Part 11 or equivalent electronic records requirements → [SOP-CR-014](/sops/cr-014) and [SOP-CR-104](/sops/cr-104).

**5.4.2** The eCRF system must maintain a complete audit trail of all entries, edits, and deletions with user identifier, date, and time.
