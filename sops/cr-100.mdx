### 1.0 Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the processes involved in the design, development, approval and distribution of Case Report Form(s) (CRFs). CRFs are designed to collect the information required by the clinical research or study protocol for each study subject.

<a id="scope"></a>

### 2.0 Scope

This SOP applies to investigator-initiated clinical research, whereby Case Report Form design through distribution is the responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator.

<a id="responsibilities"></a>

### 3.0 Responsibilities

The Sponsor-Investigator or Qualified Investigator (<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>) is responsible for ensuring that the processes involved in case report form(s) design through distribution are followed and meet the applicable regulatory, International Conference on Harmonisation (ICH) Good Clinical Practice (GCP), and local requirements.

Any or all parts of this procedure may be delegated to appropriately trained study team members, but remain the ultimate responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator or Qualified Investigator (QI).

<a id="definitions"></a>

### 4.0 Definitions

See [Glossary of Terms](/kb/foundations/glossary).

<a id="procedure"></a>

### 5.0 Procedure

<a id="5-1"></a>

#### 5.1 CRF design, development, and review

<a id="5-1-1"></a>**5.1.1** Create a draft of the <Tooltip tip="Document designed to record all protocol-required information to be reported to the sponsor for each participant." headline="Case Report Form (CRF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#crf">CRF</Tooltip> using the appropriate software, taking into account the following:

- The study protocol
- Consistency within the project/therapeutic indication
- Applicable regulatory requirements
- Data collection
- Data entry
- Monitoring requirements
- Avoidance of the collection of data not required by the protocol
- Consistency with protocol statistical plan

<a id="5-1-2"></a>**5.1.2** Assign a version control number and/or date to the CRF.

<a id="5-1-3"></a>**5.1.3** Provide the draft CRF, for review by the clinical research team, as required. This may include the following: Sponsor-Investigator or QI, Study Coordinator(s), Data Management personnel, Biostatisticians, Drug Safety representative, and Quality Assurance personnel.

<a id="5-1-4"></a>**5.1.4** Revise CRF, based on comments received, and obtain formal sign-off, as/if required.

<a id="5-2"></a>

#### 5.2 Research Ethics Board (REB)/independent ethics committee (IEC) approval of CRFs

<a id="5-2-1"></a>**5.2.1** Obtain <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip>/IEC approval prior to study activation, if required by local procedures.

<a id="5-2-2"></a>**5.2.2** Revise CRF, if required by REB/IEC.

<a id="5-3"></a>

#### 5.3 Final CRF distribution

<a id="5-3-1"></a>**5.3.1** Generate the final CRF, and distribute to the Clinical Research Team and participating sites, as required.

<a id="5-3-2"></a>**5.3.2** Retain a copy of the final CRF in the Investigator Study File or Trial Management File.

<a id="5-4"></a>

#### 5.4 CRF revisions

<a id="5-4-1"></a>**5.4.1** Revise the CRF, as a result of protocol amendments, or the need to clarify or expand data collection to better reflect the protocol.

<a id="5-4-2"></a>**5.4.2** Follow the same process as described above for original CRFs.

<a id="5-4-3"></a>**5.4.3** Assign a new version control number and/or date to the revised CRF.

<a id="5-4-4"></a>**5.4.4** Retain a copy of all revised CRFs in the Investigator Study File or Trial Management File.

### 1.0 Specific RI-MUHC policies and procedures

<a id="1-1"></a>

#### 1.1 Case report forms (CRF)

<a id="1-1-1"></a>**1.1.1 General purpose**

The CRF is a printed or electronic document to be designed to ensure that all data required by the protocol are captured and reported to the Sponsor on each trial subject. Only data defined in the protocol will be entered in the CRF.

<a id="1-1-2"></a>**1.1.2 CRF sections**

Depending on the data required by the study protocol, a standard CRF document might include, but is not limited to, the following sections:

- Site and patient identification number, visit number and date
- Randomisation/registration form
- Patient Demographic form
- Patient Characteristics form
- Eligibility form
- Baseline/Screening form
- Treatment form (treatment, doses, administration routes, frequency, escalations or reductions)
- Vital Signs form
- ECG form
- Patient Completion (documenting the date, reason and circumstances for the cessation of visits or data collection due to withdrawal, death, progression or other)
- Medical History (including relapse/recurrence form)
- Physical Exam form
- Laboratory Evaluation (tests) form
- Imaging form
- Prior and Concomitant Medication log
- Adverse Event
- Serious Adverse Event Reporting Form
- Other Important Safety Issues Reporting Form
- Follow-up forms
- End of study (EOS) form
- End of treatment form
- Investigator date sign-off form

<a id="1-2"></a>

#### 1.2 Electronic CRF

<a id="1-2-1"></a>**1.2.1** You can create an electronic Case Report form (eCRF) via an Electronic Data Capture System (EDC). Ensure that the EDC system has been validated, it is password protected, it has an integrated audit trail and it has a backup to be compliant with ICH-GCP E6 R2

Note: The use of Excel as an EDC does not conform to ICH-GCP, R2 and should not be used as an electronic CRF as it is difficult to maintain an audit trail.

<a id="1-2-2"></a>**1.2.2** The RI MUHC supports the [REDCap](/apps/redcap) system (http://project-redcap.org/) for studies of low or moderate complexity, ideally, containing no more than 1000 variables. For a more complex study, requiring higher numbers of variables, it is recommended to program a few variables per form.

Note: For Sub-Is from other institutions who are not eligible for Investigator Access on the RI-portal and who wish to be trained on REDCap, contact the CIM Data Manager directly.

<a id="1-3"></a>

#### 1.3 CRF/eCRF review

<a id="1-3-1"></a>**1.3.1** Once the CRF/eCRF is reviewed/quality controlled, based on comments received, it should obtain formal Sponsor sign-off, as/if required.

<a id="1-3-2"></a>**1.3.2** As per RI-MUHC and MUHC CAE SOPs, the REB does not review and approve CRFs/eCRFs.

<a id="1-4"></a>

#### 1.4 Data management assistance

<a id="1-4-1"></a>**1.4.1** For assistance in creating your project, set-up of CRF and patient surveys in REDCap contact biometry@muhc.mcgill.ca.
