## Key points

- **Notify RI-MUHC QA (QAclinicalresearch@muhc.mcgill.ca) within 24 hours** of receiving a Health Canada inspection notice. This ensures sufficient time for QA to help the site prepare, review documentation, and implement any corrective actions before the inspection.
- For **routine inspections**, Health Canada provides approximately 4 weeks' notice. For **for-cause inspections**, inspectors may arrive with no advance notice. FDA inspections in Canada require FDA Form #482 Notice of Inspection (no notice for suspected fraud). Have all essential documents accessible and audit-ready at all times.
- **Obstruction of inspectors is not tolerated.** Per Health Canada POL-0030, knowingly providing false or misleading information is a violation subject to fines from $500 to $5,000,000 and/or imprisonment of 3 months to 2 years.
- Ensure all study documents are complete and up-to-date before any audit/inspection: <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>, CRFs, source data, signed ICFs, training records, CVs, monitoring logs, <Tooltip tip="An adverse event meeting seriousness criteria: death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability, or congenital anomaly." headline="Serious Adverse Event (SAE)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#sae">SAE</Tooltip>/<Tooltip tip="Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reaction — unexpected in nature, severity, or frequency; must be reported to Health Canada and the REB." headline="SUSAR" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#susar">SUSAR</Tooltip> reports, <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">IP</Tooltip> accountability records, and Protocol Deviation Log.
- No participant personal data (original or copy) may leave the institution. Provide de-identified certified copies to inspectors. Maintain a tracking log of all certified copies provided.
- Health Canada inspectors do not sign confidentiality agreements and will not have their own OACIS access — have a team member ready to access electronic records for them.
- Inspection findings are classified as **Critical (risk 1)**, **Major (risk 2)**, or **Minor (risk 3)**, with an overall rating of "C" (Compliant) or "NC" (Non-Compliant). An NC rating may result in suspension of the clinical trial authorization. Respond to all observations within the timeframe specified and implement all <Tooltip tip="Actions taken to resolve a compliance issue and prevent recurrence: identify, correct, prevent." headline="Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#capa">CAPA</Tooltip> as promised.
- An optional TalentLMS course "Hosting a Health Canada Inspection" is highly recommended for the entire study team before hosting an inspection.
