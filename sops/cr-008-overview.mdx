# Informed Consent Process

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-008_08</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 08</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 May 2025</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Training levels I–IV</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes how to obtain and document informed consent for human research studies at the RI-MUHC — covering written, electronic, and remote consent, special populations (minors, incapacitated adults, non-English/French speakers), and ongoing re-consent requirements. The **Informed Consent Process Checklist (Appendix 3)** is the central compliance document.

## Key points

- Only team members on the **<Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip>** who are trained on [SOP-CR-006](/sops/cr-006), SOP-CR-008, and the study protocol may obtain consent. Where possible, the consent obtainer should not be the participant's treating physician.
- All required signatures must be obtained **before** any study-related screening procedure or enrolment begins — no exceptions. The <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#icf">ICF</Tooltip> must be the most recently <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip>-approved version; verify the version number before each use.
- For **minors and incapacitated adults**, a legally authorized representative must sign. When a participant reaches age 18 or recovers capacity during a study, obtain consent from them directly as soon as practicable.
- For **<Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">investigational product</Tooltip> studies** (drug, device, NHP), file MUHC Form FMU-9997 and a copy of the signed ICF in the Alert section of the participant's OACIS medical record, and provide the participant with an REB-approved wallet card.
- Participants affected by an **amended ICF** must be re-consented at their earliest visit after written REB approval is obtained. Document re-consent using Appendix 3 and provide participants with the new ICF version.
