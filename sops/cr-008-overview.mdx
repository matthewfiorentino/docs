# Informed Consent Process

<Note>
This SOP describes how to obtain and document informed consent for human research studies at the RI-MUHC — covering written, electronic, and remote consent, special populations (minors, incapacitated adults, non-English/French speakers), and ongoing re-consent requirements. ## Key points

- Only team members on the **Task Delegation Log** who are trained on [SOP-CR-006](/sops/cr-006), SOP-CR-008, and the study protocol may obtain consent. Where possible, the consent obtainer should not be the participant's treating physician.
- All required signatures must be obtained **before** any study-related screening procedure or enrolment begins — no exceptions. The ICF must be the most recently REB-approved version; verify the version number before each use.
- For **minors and incapacitated adults**, a legally authorized representative must sign. When a participant reaches age 18 or recovers capacity during a study, obtain consent from them directly as soon as practicable.
- For **investigational product studies** (drug, device, NHP), file MUHC Form FMU-9997 and a copy of the signed ICF in the Alert section of the participant's OACIS medical record, and provide the participant with an REB-approved wallet card.
- Participants affected by an **amended ICF** must be re-consented at their earliest visit after written REB approval is obtained. Document re-consent using Appendix 3 and provide participants with the new ICF version.
