<a id="purpose"></a>

### 1.0 Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for developing study protocols specifically for human subject research.

<a id="scope"></a>

### 2.0 Scope

This SOP is applicable to investigator-initiated human subject research whereby protocol development is the responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator

<a id="responsibilities"></a>

### 3.0 Responsibilities

The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator is responsible for ensuring the study protocol is written in compliance with all applicable regulatory, International Conference on Harmonization (ICH) Good Clinical Practice (GCP), sponsor, and local requirements.

Any or all parts of this procedure may be delegated to appropriately trained study team members, but remains the ultimate responsibility of the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator.

<a id="definitions"></a>

### 4.0 Definitions

See [Glossary of Terms](/kb/foundations/glossary).

<a id="procedure"></a>

### 5.0 Procedure

<a id="5-1"></a>

#### 5.1 Protocol preparation

<a id="5-1-1"></a>**5.1.1** Optional: Prepare a draft protocol synopsis outlining the rationale for the research study, study hypothesis, objectives, design, eligibility criteria and procedures to be performed. Consult with methodologists, statisticians, etc., as necessary. Ensure that the contents are in accordance with applicable regulatory, ICH GCP, and local requirements.

<a id="5-1-2"></a>**5.1.2** Circulate the draft protocol synopsis to all interested parties, such as select Steering Committee members, research study manager, methodologists, statisticians and study sponsors, etc., as required, for review and discussion/comments. Revise draft, as required.

<a id="5-1-3"></a>**5.1.3** Optional: Form a protocol development committee to expand the synopsis into a full research study protocol.

<a id="5-1-4"></a>**5.1.4** Develop the protocol, including the following sections:

- Introduction
- Rationale
- Hypothesis
- Design
- Eligibility Criteria
- Primary and Secondary Study Outcomes (as applicable)
- Study Procedures
- Statistical Considerations
- Investigational Product (if applicable)
- Handling of Adverse Events, including Serious Adverse Events (as applicable)
- Quality Management
- Investigators Responsibilities, according to regulatory guidelines (as applicable)
- Description of Study Committees (Steering Committee, Data Monitoring Committee, Events Adjudication Committee) (as applicable)

<a id="5-1-5"></a>**5.1.5** Obtain agreement on the final study protocol from all reviewers and concerned parties, as described above.

<a id="5-1-6"></a>**5.1.6** Generate final study protocol, including a version control number and/or date.

<a id="5-1-7"></a>**5.1.7** Obtain approval signatures, as required. This usually includes the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, and all site <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/investigators.

<a id="5-1-8"></a>**5.1.8** Retain a copy of the approved/signed protocol in the study file.

<a id="5-2"></a>

#### 5.2 Protocol amendments

<a id="5-2-1"></a>**5.2.1** Determine need for amended protocol, e.g., study procedure change, changes requested by Research Ethics Board (<Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip>)/Independent Ethics Committee (IEC) and/or regulatory authorities, etc.

<a id="5-2-2"></a>**5.2.2** Prepare, review, and finalize protocol amendments, as described above (with or without synopsis step).

<a id="5-2-3"></a>**5.2.3** Finalize protocol amendment, and assign new version control number and/or date.

<a id="5-2-4"></a>**5.2.4** Obtain approval signatures, as required. This usually includes the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, and all site QI/investigators.

<a id="5-2-5"></a>**5.2.5** Retain a copy of all approved/signed protocol amendments in the study file.

<a id="5-3"></a>

#### 5.3 Informed consent form (ICF) preparation

<a id="5-3-1"></a>**5.3.1** Prepare a draft <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#icf">ICF</Tooltip>. Ensure that the contents are in accordance with the protocol, and applicable regulatory, ICH GCP, and REB requirements.

<a id="5-3-2"></a>**5.3.2** Review ICF, and revise, as required. Finalize ICF, and assign version control number and/or date. Recommended: Document ICF verification in study file.

<a id="5-3-3"></a>**5.3.3** Translate ICF, as required.

<a id="5-3-4"></a>**5.3.4** Revise ICF if the study protocol is modified, if changes are requested by regulatory authorities, or REB/IEC, or if any new information becomes available that may affect the subject’s willingness to participate in the clinical study.

<a id="5-3-5"></a>**5.3.5** Obtain approval signatures, as required. This may include the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, and all site QI/investigators.

<a id="5-3-6"></a>**5.3.6** Retain a copy of all approved/signed ICF and ICF amendments in the study file.

<a id="5-3-7"></a>**5.3.7** Submit the ICF to regulatory authorities and/or REB/IEC, as applicable.

<a id="5-4"></a>

#### 5.4 Submission to regulatory authorities and REB/IEC

<a id="5-4-1"></a>**5.4.1** Submit the study protocol/ protocol amendment and/or ICF/ICF amendments to Health Canada, or other regulatory authority, as required.

<a id="5-4-2"></a>**5.4.2** Obtain the No Objection Letter (<Tooltip tip="Letter from Health Canada confirming a CTA is satisfactory and authorizing the sponsor to proceed." headline="No Objection Letter (NOL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#nol">NOL</Tooltip>) or equivalent issued by the regulatory authority/ies. Submit this document to the REB/IEC.

<a id="5-4-3"></a>**5.4.3** Submit the study protocol/ protocol amendment, ICF/ICF amendment to the REB/IEC, as required by local procedures.

<a id="5-4-4"></a>**5.4.4** Research studies not subject to regulatory authority oversight: the protocol and ICF are considered final following approval by protocol development committee, Steering Committee, Sponsor, and/or other team members, as applicable. Submit final protocol to REB/IEC.

### 1.0 Specific RI-MUHC policies and procedures

<a id="1-1"></a>

#### 1.1 Confidentiality agreement

<a id="1-1-1"></a>**1.1.1** In the case of clinical studies initiated by a Sponsor, a Confidentiality Disclosure Agreement (CDA) between the Sponsor, the Institution and the Investigator must be signed and dated prior to obtaining confidential study documents.

<a id="1-1-2"></a>**1.1.2** The Institution requires review and approval of the CDA by the RI-MUHC Office of Contracts (for clinical research studies) before signing. Once approved the Investigator must submit the signed and dated CDA in the [Nagano](/apps/nagano/overview) system (Refer to [SOP-CR-007](/sops/cr-007)).

<a id="1-1-3"></a>**1.1.3** A copy of the signed and dated CDA should be kept with the essential study documents.

<a id="1-1-4"></a>**1.1.4** The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator is responsible for having each participating center sign and date the CDA prior to the start of any study. A copy of the signed and dated CDA should be kept with the investigator study files at each center.

<a id="1-2"></a>

#### 1.2 Additional comments on Sponsor/ Sponsor-Investigator responsibilities

<a id="1-2-1"></a>**1.2.1** The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator is responsible for writing the study protocol or protocol amendment and the Investigator’s Brochure according to guidelines such as provided in ICH E6 R2: Sections 6 and 7. This task may be delegated to another team member and should be agreed and documented in the final Services Agreement.

<a id="1-2-2"></a>**1.2.2** If the study protocol/protocol amendment and the Investigator’s Brochure are written by another member of the research team or by an external person, the QI should review the content of these documents according to guidelines as per ICH E6 R2: Sections 6 and 7. This task may be delegated to another team member and should be documented in the final Services Agreement.

<a id="1-2-3"></a>**1.2.3** To facilitate document verification and ensure important elements are not missing, you may use Appendices 1 and 3: Reference for Verification and Appendices 2 and 4: Confirmation of Verification which provides a checklist of all ICH-GCP elements which should be included in a protocol and Investigator’s Brochure respectively. It is recommended to keep the documentation with the essential study documents..

<a id="1-2-4"></a>**1.2.4** The date and version should be clearly indicated on each page of the protocol and of the Investigator’s Brochure.

<a id="1-2-5"></a>**1.2.5** Any new version of <Tooltip tip="Compilation of clinical and nonclinical data on the investigational product relevant to the study." headline="Investigator's Brochure (IB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#ib">IB</Tooltip> or amendment to protocol must be submitted and receive REB approval.
