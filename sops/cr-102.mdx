## Key points

- In the case of clinical studies initiated by a Sponsor, a **Confidentiality Disclosure Agreement (CDA)** between the Sponsor, the Institution, and the Investigator must be signed and dated prior to obtaining confidential study documents. The RI-MUHC Office of Contracts must review and approve the CDA before signing. File the signed CDA in <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip> → [SOP-CR-007](/sops/cr-007).
- The protocol must contain all **ICH E6(R2) and applicable Health Canada required elements** before submission for <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> review and Health Canada authorization (<Tooltip tip="Application to Health Canada for authorization to conduct a drug trial under Division 5 of the Food and Drug Regulations." headline="Clinical Trial Application (CTA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#cta">CTA</Tooltip>/<Tooltip tip="Health Canada authorization to conduct a clinical investigation of a medical device. The device equivalent of a CTA." headline="Investigational Testing Authorization (ITA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ita">ITA</Tooltip>).
- The protocol must be reviewed by a qualified biostatistician, the RI-MUHC Research Hub, and (where applicable) a pharmacist before finalization.
- All protocol amendments must go through the same verification process as the original protocol before submission to the REB and Health Canada.
- The **Reference for Verification of Protocol/Amendment** (Appendix 1) and **Confirmation of Verification of Protocol/Amendment** (Appendix 2) checklists must be completed for the protocol and every subsequent amendment.
- If the study uses an Investigator's Brochure (IB), complete the **Reference for Verification of IB/Amendment** (Appendix 3) and **Confirmation of Verification of IB/Amendment** (Appendix 4).
- The date and version must be clearly indicated on each page of the protocol and Investigator's Brochure.
- A copy of the signed and dated CDA must be kept with the essential study documents in the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>/<Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip>.

## 1 · Purpose {#purpose}

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for developing study
protocols specifically for human subject research.

---

## 2 · Scope {#scope}

This SOP is applicable to investigator-initiated human subject research whereby
protocol development is the responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator

---

## 3 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

The **Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator** is responsible for ensuring the study protocol is written in compliance with all applicable regulatory, ICH-<Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, Sponsor, and local requirements.

Any or all parts of this procedure may be delegated to appropriately trained study team members, but remain the ultimate responsibility of the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator.

## 5 · Procedure {#procedure}
### 5.1 Confidentiality Disclosure Agreement (CDA)

**5.1.1** In the case of clinical studies initiated by a Sponsor, a Confidentiality Disclosure Agreement (CDA) between the Sponsor, the Institution, and the Investigator must be signed and dated prior to obtaining confidential study documents.

**5.1.2** The Institution requires review and approval of the CDA by the RI-MUHC Office of Contracts (for clinical research studies) before signing. Once approved, the Investigator must submit the signed and dated CDA in the <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip> system → [SOP-CR-007](/sops/cr-007).

**5.1.3** A copy of the signed and dated CDA must be kept with the essential study documents in the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>/<Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip>.

**5.1.4** The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator is responsible for having each participating center sign and date the CDA prior to the start of any study. A copy of the signed and dated CDA must be kept with the investigator study files at each center.

### 5.2 Protocol development and review

**5.2.1** The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator is responsible for writing the study protocol or protocol amendment and the <Tooltip tip="Compilation of clinical and nonclinical data on the investigational product relevant to the study." headline="Investigator's Brochure (IB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ib">Investigator's Brochure</Tooltip> (IB) according to ICH E6(R2): Sections 6 (Protocol and Protocol Amendments) and 7 (Investigator's Brochure). This task may be delegated to another team member and must be agreed and documented in the final Services Agreement.

**5.2.2** If the study protocol/protocol amendment and the IB are written by another member of the research team or by an external person, the <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip> must review the content according to ICH E6(R2): Sections 6 and 7. This task may be delegated and must be documented in the final Services Agreement.

**5.2.3** To facilitate document verification and ensure important elements are not missing, complete the applicable verification appendices:
- **Appendix 1** — Reference for Verification of Protocol or Protocol Amendment (ICH-GCP Chapter 6 checklist)
- **Appendix 2** — Confirmation of Verification of Protocol or Protocol Amendment
- **Appendix 3** — Reference for Verification of Investigator's Brochure (IB) or IB Amendment (ICH-GCP Chapter 7 checklist)
- **Appendix 4** — Confirmation of Verification of Investigator's Brochure or IB Amendment

Retain the completed verification documentation with the essential study documents in the ISF/TMF.

**5.2.4** The date and version must be clearly indicated on each page of the protocol and of the Investigator's Brochure.

### 5.3 Protocol content requirements

The protocol must contain all ICH E6(R2) Chapter 6 required elements, including:

| Category | Required elements |
|---|---|
| General information | Protocol title, identifying number, development phase, date, sponsor contact, qualified physician, clinical trial site details, laboratory information |
| Background | Name and description of <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">investigational product</Tooltip>, summary of known and potential risks, description and justification of route of administration |
| Trial objectives | Primary and secondary endpoints, outcome measures |
| Design | Type, design, control, blinding, treatment groups, assignment method |
| Subject selection | Inclusion criteria, exclusion criteria, estimated sample size with statistical justification |
| Treatment | Product description, doses, administration schedule, duration, rescue medication |
| Assessments | Efficacy parameters, safety parameters, assessment schedule |
| Statistics | Statistical methods, power calculation, significance level |
| Direct access | Agreement to allow sponsor/CRO/regulatory authorities access to source data |
| Quality control | Monitoring procedures, data handling and record-keeping |
| Ethics | Ethics committee compliance, informed consent |
| Data handling and record-keeping | Retention period, access provisions |
| Financing and insurance | Funding source, insurance coverage |
| Publication policy | Authorship, publication rights |

### 5.4 Investigator's Brochure (IB)

**5.4.1** The IB must contain all ICH E6(R2) Chapter 7 required elements, including:
- Title page (Sponsor name, IP identity, release date, edition number)
- Confidentiality statement
- Signature page
- Summary of physical, chemical, pharmaceutical, and toxicological properties
- Summary of effects in humans (clinical pharmacology, efficacy, safety)
- Summary of data and guidelines for the investigator

**5.4.2** Update the IB at least annually or more frequently if new information becomes available that is relevant to the safety of participants.

### 5.5 Protocol amendments

**5.5.1** All protocol amendments must go through the same verification process as the original protocol (Appendices 1 and 2) before submission to the <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> and Health Canada.

**5.5.2** Submit amendments to the REB for approval before implementation, except for emergency changes to protect participant safety → [SOP-CR-026](/sops/cr-026).

**5.5.3** Submit <Tooltip tip="Application to Health Canada for authorization to conduct a drug trial under Division 5 of the Food and Drug Regulations." headline="Clinical Trial Application (CTA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#cta">CTA</Tooltip> amendments (CTA-A) to Health Canada as required → [SOP-CR-018](/sops/cr-018), [SOP-CR-023](/sops/cr-023), [SOP-CR-024](/sops/cr-024).
