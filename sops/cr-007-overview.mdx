# Initial REB Review, COI and Clinical Trial Registration

<Note>
This SOP describes the required documentation and communication for initial study submission between the Sponsor-Investigator or QI/PI and the MUHC REB/Scientific Review, along with the institutional feasibility review process to obtain MUHC Authorization. It also covers annual continuing REB approval, conflict of interest (COI) reporting, and clinical trial registration requirements.

## Key points

- **No study activity may begin** before written Health Canada approval (if applicable), written REB approval, MUHC Authorization, and the Sponsor's Go Letter/confirmation (if applicable) are all obtained.
- All initial and annual submissions must be made through **Nagano**. Paper submissions are not accepted.
- The **MUHC Authorization Letter** is issued only after REB approval AND all site-specific feasibility reviews are completed (QA privileges/training check, Research Agreements, DPS review, nursing, laboratory, pharmacy). Do not start any study activities — including recruitment — before receiving this letter.
- For multi-centrique studies (multiple Quebec sites within the Québec public healthcare network), one evaluating REB reviews on behalf of all sites. This is distinct from a multicenter study with sites in multiple provinces, where each site submits to its own REB.
- **Annual renewal must be submitted at least one month before the current REB approval expires.** A lapse in REB approval is a major regulatory finding — enrollment must stop during any lapse, and renewal is never retroactive.
- All **conflicts of interest** (real, potential, or perceived) must be disclosed to the REB at submission and updated if circumstances change.
- Clinical trials meeting the TCPS2/ICMJE definition must be **registered in a recognized web-accessible registry** (e.g. ClinicalTrials.gov) before the first participant is enrolled. The registry name, URL, and registration number must appear in the ICF.
- Investigators are not to sign agreements on behalf of the RI-MUHC — all research agreements must be reviewed and processed by the **Research Agreements Office** via My Contracts on the RI-MUHC portal.
