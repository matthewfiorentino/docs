## Key points

- **No study activity may begin** before written Health Canada approval (if applicable), written REB approval, MUHC Authorization, and the Sponsor's Go Letter/confirmation (if applicable) are all obtained.
- All initial and annual submissions must be made through **<Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip>**. Paper submissions are not accepted.
- The **MUHC Authorization Letter** is issued only after REB approval AND all site-specific feasibility reviews are completed (<Tooltip tip="All planned and systematic actions to ensure trial data are generated, documented, and reported in compliance with GCP and SOPs." headline="Quality Assurance (QA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qa">QA</Tooltip> privileges/training check, Research Agreements, DPS review, nursing, laboratory, pharmacy). Do not start any study activities — including recruitment — before receiving this letter.
- For multi-centrique studies (multiple Quebec sites within the Québec public healthcare network), one evaluating REB reviews on behalf of all sites. This is distinct from a multicenter study with sites in multiple provinces, where each site submits to its own REB.
- **Annual renewal must be submitted at least one month before the current REB approval expires.** A lapse in REB approval is a major regulatory finding — enrollment must stop during any lapse, and renewal is never retroactive.
- All **conflicts of interest** (real, potential, or perceived) must be disclosed to the REB at submission and updated if circumstances change.
- Clinical trials meeting the TCPS2/ICMJE definition must be **registered in a recognized web-accessible registry** (e.g. ClinicalTrials.gov) before the first participant is enrolled. The registry name, URL, and registration number must appear in the <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#icf">ICF</Tooltip>.
- Investigators are not to sign agreements on behalf of the RI-MUHC — all research agreements must be reviewed and processed by the **Research Agreements Office** via My Contracts on the RI-MUHC portal.
