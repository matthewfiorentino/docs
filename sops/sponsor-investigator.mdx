When you conduct a clinical trial without an external sponsor — designing it yourself, holding the Health Canada authorization, and taking full regulatory accountability — you are acting as both Sponsor and Investigator. This dual role is called the Sponsor-Investigator role, and it carries obligations that go well beyond those of a site investigator in an industry-sponsored trial. The SOP-CR-100 series covers the Sponsor-Investigator's responsibilities for protocol development, data management, database operations, and study reporting.

<Note>
The Sponsor-Investigator SOPs are required **in addition to** your applicable Level I, II, or III SOPs — not instead of them. If you are conducting a Sponsor-Investigator drug trial, you must read both the Level I SOP set and the full SOP-CR-100 through 107 series.
</Note>

## When the Sponsor-Investigator role applies

You are acting as a Sponsor-Investigator when **all** of the following are true:

- You are the Principal Investigator at the site.
- There is no external commercial or institutional sponsor — you designed the study and hold the IND/CTA/ITA yourself, or you are sponsoring a study at your own site without a separate sponsor organization.
- You are responsible for initiating, organizing, and financing the study.
- You hold the Health Canada authorization (CTA, ITA, or NHP CTA) in your own name or in the name of your institution, not a pharmaceutical company or CRO.

<Warning>
The Sponsor-Investigator role creates direct regulatory accountability to Health Canada. Adverse event and SUSAR reporting, CTA amendments, protocol amendments, and annual progress reports to Health Canada all become your personal responsibility. See [Safety Reporting SOPs](/sops/safety-reporting) for the applicable reporting timelines.
</Warning>

## SOPs in this series

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-100 — CRF Design · v01-Sep-2018 · Sponsor-Investigator">
    This SOP defines the standards for designing Case Report Forms (CRFs) for Sponsor-Investigator studies. CRFs are the primary data collection instruments for the study database and must be designed before the study begins to ensure all protocol-required data points are captured accurately.

    **Key points:**
    - CRFs must capture all primary and secondary outcome measures defined in the protocol, plus safety data, medical history, and concomitant medications as required.
    - Each CRF field must map to a source document or source data entry point. Fields that cannot be sourced must be justified.
    - CRF design must be reviewed against the protocol and the data management plan (SOP-CR-103) before database setup begins (SOP-CR-104).
    - Version control applies to CRFs: every change to a CRF after study start must be documented, version-stamped, and the ISF updated.

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-101 — Study Analysis and Reporting · v01-Sep-2018 · Sponsor-Investigator">
    This SOP defines the standards for conducting statistical analysis of study data and preparing the Clinical Study Report (CSR) for a Sponsor-Investigator study. It covers the statistical analysis plan (SAP), interim analyses, final analysis, and the format and content requirements for the final study report.

    **Key points:**
    - The Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) must be finalized and dated before the database is locked (SOP-CR-107) and before any unblinding occurs.
    - Protocol-specified interim analyses must follow a pre-specified stopping rule or statistical adjustment to protect the overall study alpha.
    - The Clinical Study Report must follow ICH E3 format and include all required sections: synopsis, introduction, study objectives, design, results, safety data, and conclusions.
    - The final report must be submitted to the REB as the study completion report and made available to Health Canada upon request.

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-102 — Protocol Development · v01-Sep-2018 · Sponsor-Investigator">
    This SOP defines the requirements for developing, reviewing, and finalizing a clinical trial protocol for a Sponsor-Investigator study at the RI-MUHC. It covers the content requirements for the protocol, the internal review and approval process, and how to manage protocol amendments after REB approval.

    **Key points:**
    - The protocol must contain all ICH E6 and applicable Health Canada required elements before submission for REB review and Health Canada authorization.
    - The protocol must be reviewed by a qualified biostatistician, the RI-MUHC Research Hub, and (where applicable) a pharmacist before finalization.
    - All protocol amendments must go through the same verification process as the original protocol before being submitted to the REB and Health Canada.
    - The **Reference for Verification of Protocol/Amendment** (Appendix 1) and **Confirmation of Verification of Protocol/Amendment** (Appendix 2) checklists must be completed for the protocol and every subsequent amendment.
    - If the study uses an Investigator's Brochure (IB), use **Reference for Verification of IB/Amendment** (Appendix 3) and **Confirmation of Verification of IB/Amendment** (Appendix 4) to document IB review.

    **Downloadable forms:**
    - [Appendix 1 — Reference for Verification of Protocol/Amendment](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=105488990073765593)

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-103 — Data Management Plan · v01-Sep-2018 · Sponsor-Investigator">
    This SOP defines the requirements for creating a Data Management Plan (DMP) for a Sponsor-Investigator study. The DMP documents how data will be collected, transferred, validated, corrected, and stored throughout the study, and must be in place before the database is built.

    **Key points:**
    - The DMP must specify: the data collection method (paper CRF, EDC, or both), the source data verification approach, the data validation rules, the query management process, and the data transfer procedures if applicable.
    - The DMP must align with the protocol (SOP-CR-102) and the database setup plan (SOP-CR-104). Changes to one document typically require updates to the others.
    - The DMP must define roles and responsibilities for data management activities, including who may enter, edit, and query data.
    - The completed DMP must be version-controlled and filed in the TMF.

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-104 — Database Set-up · v01-Sep-2018 · Sponsor-Investigator">
    This SOP defines the requirements for building and validating the study database for a Sponsor-Investigator study. It covers database design, testing and validation, user access control, and audit trail configuration before the database is opened for data entry.

    **Key points:**
    - The study database must be built according to the CRF designs (SOP-CR-100) and the Data Management Plan (SOP-CR-103).
    - User access must be role-based: data entry users, data managers, statisticians, and auditors must each have access only to the functions they need.
    - The database must have a complete audit trail that captures all entries, edits, and deletions with user identifier, date, and time — this is a regulatory requirement and cannot be disabled.
    - Validation testing (User Acceptance Testing) must be completed and documented before the database is opened for participant data entry.

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-105 — Database Maintenance and Management · v01-Sep-2018 · Sponsor-Investigator">
    This SOP defines the ongoing maintenance and management requirements for the study database throughout the active phase of a Sponsor-Investigator study. It covers routine backups, system access reviews, query management, data validation checks, and handling of database issues.

    **Key points:**
    - The database must be backed up at a frequency sufficient to prevent data loss, using a method that ensures backups are recoverable.
    - User access must be reviewed at regular intervals and updated promptly when team members join, change roles, or leave the study.
    - Data validation checks must be run routinely to identify missing data, out-of-range values, and cross-field inconsistencies. Findings must be queried and resolved.
    - Any system issues that could affect data integrity must be documented, assessed for impact, and corrected. Affected data entries must be reviewed and verified.

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-106 — File Transfer · v01-Sep-2018 · Sponsor-Investigator">
    This SOP defines the requirements for transferring study data files between systems or to external parties (e.g., statisticians, data safety monitoring boards, or regulatory authorities). It covers transfer methods, validation, documentation, and security requirements.

    **Key points:**
    - All data transfers must use secure, encrypted methods. Unencrypted transfer of identifiable or study data by email or unencrypted file sharing is prohibited.
    - Every transfer must be documented: what was transferred, to whom, by what method, on what date, and who received and confirmed the transfer.
    - Recipient confirmation of receipt and data integrity must be obtained and filed in the TMF.
    - For transfers to regulatory authorities (Health Canada), use only the submission formats and channels specified by the regulator.

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-107 — Database Lock and Archiving · v01-Sep-2018 · Sponsor-Investigator">
    This SOP defines the procedures for locking the study database at the end of data collection and archiving the database and associated metadata for the required regulatory retention period. Database lock is a prerequisite for final statistical analysis and study reporting.

    **Key points:**
    - Database lock requires confirmation that all data have been entered, all queries resolved, all protocol deviations documented, and all required data validation checks completed.
    - The database lock must be documented with a formal lock memo signed by the data manager and the QI/PI, and the Statistical Analysis Plan must be finalized before lock.
    - After lock, the database must be made read-only. Any post-lock data corrections require a formal unlock procedure with full documentation of the reason, correction, and re-lock.
    - The locked database, audit trail, validation documentation, data dictionary, and DMP must all be archived together for the regulatory retention period (25 years under Canadian GCP regulations).

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

## Sponsor-Investigator workflow overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Develop the protocol">
    Draft the study protocol and Investigator's Brochure (if applicable) using SOP-CR-102 verification checklists. Have the protocol reviewed before REB submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Obtain Health Canada authorization">
    Submit a CTA (SOP-CR-018), ITA (SOP-CR-024), or NHP CTA (SOP-CR-023) as required. Wait for authorization before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design CRFs and write the Data Management Plan">
    Design CRFs (SOP-CR-100) and document the data management approach in the DMP (SOP-CR-103) before database setup begins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build and validate the database">
    Set up the study database (SOP-CR-104), complete validation testing, and open for data entry only after all access controls and audit trail settings are confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Conduct the study">
    Manage data entry, queries, and database maintenance throughout the active study (SOP-CR-105). Transfer files securely as needed (SOP-CR-106).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lock the database and analyze">
    Complete database lock procedures (SOP-CR-107), finalize the SAP, then conduct the analysis and prepare the Clinical Study Report (SOP-CR-101).
  </Step>
</Steps>
