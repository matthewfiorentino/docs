# Research Team Roles and Responsibilities

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-002_09</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 09</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 19 Jun 2023</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All training levels</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP defines who does what in a clinical research study at the RI-MUHC — from the Sponsor down to third-party vendors. It governs how research teams are structured, what each role is responsible for, and how task delegation must be documented. The **<Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip>** is the central compliance document this SOP governs.

## Key points

- The **<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI** holds ultimate responsibility for all study activities at the site and must hold RI-MUHC Institutional Privileges or Researcher Status before any study begins.
- No person may perform any study task before they appear on the **Task Delegation Log**, their training is documented, and the QI/PI has confirmed they are qualified.
- **Fellows and rotating residents** cannot conduct research independently or serve as QI/PI backup. All medical acts they perform must be co-signed by a QI/PI or qualified Sub-Investigator with Privileges.
- In Quebec, **<Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">IP</Tooltip> management** (storage, dispensing, accountability) is delegated to a trained pharmacist. The Investigator retains responsibility for ensuring IP is used per the approved protocol.
- If the **QI/PI changes** during a study, both outgoing and incoming QI/PI must sign the Task Delegation Log, the Sponsor and <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> must be notified, and a <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip> amendment (Form F1) must be submitted without delay.
