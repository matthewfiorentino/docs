# Ongoing REB Review and Reportable Events

<Note>
This SOP describes the required communication for ongoing study submissions between the Sponsor-Investigator or QI/PI and the MUHC REB throughout the duration of human research studies. It covers amendments, reportable events, protocol and GCP deviations, suspension/termination, study closure, and public registry updating. For Initial REB Review and Annual Continuing Review, refer to [SOP-CR-007](/sops/cr-007).

## Key points

- Submit **amendments** to the MUHC REB via Nagano **before** implementing any changes, except for emergency deviations to protect participant safety (implement immediately, then notify REB ASAP). Do not implement until written REB approval **and** Sponsor's Go Letter are received.
- **Protocol deviations** are categorized as Minor (no immediate risk, may be reported annually at Continuing Review), Major (may result in health risk or data invalidity — report within 15 days), or Critical (fatal/life-threatening/unsafe conditions — report within 7 days). All major and critical deviations require a CAPA and must be sent to QAclinicalresearch@muhc.mcgill.ca.
- **SAEs and MDIs** that are unexpected, possibly related to IP or study conduct, and pose greater risk of harm must be reported to the REB within **7 days** (life-threatening) or **15 days** (all other). Always remove participant nominative information; use participant study number only → [SOP-CR-012](/sops/cr-012), [SOP-CR-024](/sops/cr-024).
- **Other reportable events** include: interim analysis results, unexpected AE frequency/severity, regulatory labeling changes, unanticipated problems, privacy breaches, DSMB reports, and audit/inspection findings relevant to participant safety.
- Report **study completion** to the REB within 30 days of the Close-Out visit. Once the study status is "Closed" in Nagano, no further submissions are permitted; use the Discussion feature for any subsequent documents.
- If the REB **suspends or terminates** ethics approval, no further study activities can take place (other than submission of amendments or reportable events) until corrective actions are completed to the REB's satisfaction.
- Sponsor-Investigators are responsible for maintaining public trial registry records (e.g., clinicaltrials.gov) throughout the study. Ensure MUHC-RIMUHC is entered as "Sponsor" for insurance purposes; the Sponsor-Investigator is the "Responsible Party."
- **Multi-centrique studies:** each Quebec site reports its own events to the MUHC REB (if lead) via Nagano. For sites without Nagano access, the MUHC Sponsor-Investigator submits on their behalf.
