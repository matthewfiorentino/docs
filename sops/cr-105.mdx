## Purpose and scope

This SOP describes the management and maintenance of the study database from database launch until database lock to ensure accurate, reliable, complete, and secure electronic data.

This SOP is applicable to all study databases managed at the RI-MUHC and to those personnel responsible for management and maintenance of study databases, unless otherwise stated in the study contract or Data Management Plan.

---

## Responsibilities

The **Sponsor-Investigator or <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/Investigator, Data Management personnel, and IT Systems Support personnel** (when applicable) are responsible for ensuring that all database maintenance and management processes meet all applicable regulatory, ICH-<Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, Sponsor, and local requirements.

## Procedure

### 5.1 Routine database backups

**5.1.1** The database must be backed up at a frequency sufficient to prevent unacceptable data loss (as specified in the DMP → [SOP-CR-103](/sops/cr-103)). Minimum recommended frequency is daily for active studies.

**5.1.2** Backups must use a method that ensures data are recoverable. Test backup restoration periodically to confirm recoverability.

**5.1.3** Maintain a backup log documenting: date of backup, backup method, storage location, person responsible, and any issues encountered.

**5.1.4** Store backups in a separate, secure location from the primary database.

### 5.2 User access management

**5.2.1** Review user access at regular intervals (at a minimum, at each annual renewal and whenever team members change) and update promptly when team members join, change roles, or leave the study.

**5.2.2** Remove user access immediately when a team member is undelegated from the <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip> or leaves the study. Document the date of access removal.

**5.2.3** Maintain a user access log documenting: user name, role, access level, date access was granted, and date access was removed (if applicable).

**5.2.4** Shared user accounts are prohibited. Each user must have a unique login.

### 5.3 Data entry and query management

**5.3.1** Monitor data entry timelines per the DMP. Identify and address data entry backlogs promptly.

**5.3.2** Run data validation checks routinely to identify:
- Missing data or incomplete CRFs
- Out-of-range values
- Cross-field inconsistencies
- Protocol deviation flags

**5.3.3** Raise queries for all data issues identified. Queries must include: the field in question, the issue identified, and a request for the site/user to verify and correct.

**5.3.4** Resolve all outstanding queries within the timeframes specified in the DMP. Document query status and closure.

**5.3.5** All data corrections must be made through the audit trail — overwriting original data without an audit trail entry is prohibited.

### 5.4 Handling database issues

**5.4.1** Any system issue that could affect data integrity must be documented immediately, including: description of the issue, date/time of occurrence, potential data affected, and corrective action taken.

**5.4.2** Assess the impact of any system issue on all data entries made during the affected period. Review and verify all potentially affected data.

**5.4.3** Report significant database issues to the QI/PI and, if participant safety could be affected, to the <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> → [SOP-CR-026](/sops/cr-026).

### 5.5 Preparation for database lock

**5.5.1** Prior to database lock → [SOP-CR-107](/sops/cr-107), ensure:
- All participant data are entered and complete
- All outstanding queries are resolved and closed
- All protocol deviations are documented on the Deviation Log
- All required data validation checks are completed and issues resolved
- The Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) is finalized → [SOP-CR-101](/sops/cr-101)
- A final audit trail review has been completed
