# Study Analysis and Reporting

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-101_05</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 05</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 Sep 2018</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Sponsor-Investigator</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the analysis and reporting of results from a clinical study. It applies to Sponsor-Investigator studies where study analysis and reporting is the responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator. It covers the Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP), interim analyses, final analysis, and Clinical Study Report (CSR) preparation.
</Note>

## Key points

- The **Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP)** must be finalized and dated before the database is locked ([SOP-CR-107](/sops/cr-107)) and before any unblinding occurs.
- The SAP must include, at minimum: the primary and secondary statistical analyses, the handling of missing data, the intent-to-treat and per-protocol populations, and any pre-specified subgroup analyses.
- The study analysis reporting activities list (per N2 SOP101, section 5.1.1) includes: **"Revisions within the Analysis Plan"** as an activity requiring documentation — added by RI-MUHC Addendum.
- Protocol-specified interim analyses must follow a pre-specified stopping rule or statistical adjustment to protect the overall study alpha.
- The **Clinical Study Report (CSR)** must follow ICH E3 format and include all required sections: synopsis, introduction, study objectives, design, results, safety data, and conclusions.
- The final report must be submitted to the <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> as the study completion report and made available to Health Canada upon request → [SOP-CR-016](/sops/cr-016).
