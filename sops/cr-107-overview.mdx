# Database Lock and Archiving

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-107_04</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 04</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 Sep 2018</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Sponsor-Investigator</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the procedures for locking, unlocking, and archiving a study database. Database lock is a prerequisite for final statistical analysis and study reporting. It applies to all clinical or research studies at the RI-MUHC and to those research and Data Management personnel responsible for data management.
</Note>

## Key points

- **Database lock criteria:** all data entered, all queries resolved, all protocol deviations documented, all required data validation checks completed, and the Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) finalized → [SOP-CR-101](/sops/cr-101).
- The database lock must be documented with a **formal lock memo** signed by the data manager and the <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI.
- After lock, the database must be made **read-only**. Any post-lock data correction requires a formal unlock procedure with full documentation of the reason, correction, and re-lock.
- The **locked database, audit trail, validation documentation, data dictionary, and DMP** must all be archived together for the regulatory retention period (**25 years** under Canadian <Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip> regulations for Health Canada-regulated studies) → [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015).
- Maintain the archived database in a format that allows future access for audits, inspections, and follow-up analyses. Use file formats that will remain readable over the retention period.
- Database lock is a prerequisite for final analysis; no statistical analysis using the final dataset may begin before lock is confirmed and documented.
