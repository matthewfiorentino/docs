## Purpose and scope

This SOP describes the process for notifying the subject's treating physician and the transmittal of clinically useful information in the context of continued care.

This SOP is not linked to any N2 SOPs and is specific to the RI-MUHC institution.

This procedure concerns the research community (employees, investigators, physicians, management, consultants, students, volunteers, or other persons) involved in conducting research with human subjects at the MUHC.

---

## Responsibilities

The **Qualified Investigator (QI)/Investigator** is responsible for ensuring continuance of care by informing the subject's physician of the subject's participation in a research study, if the patient has consented, and transmitting clinically useful information in the context of continued care.

---

## Procedure

### 5.1 Notification of participation in research

**5.1.1** When a participant has consented to notification of their treating physician, inform the treating physician of the participant's enrollment in the study using the standard notification letter (Appendix 1).

**5.1.2** The notification letter must include:
- Name of the participant's treating physician (To)
- Name and contact information of the QI/PI (From)
- The participant's name
- The title of the protocol
- The ClinicalTrials.gov study ID number (NCT number), if applicable
- A request that the physician not prescribe new medication without informing the QI/PI
- Contact information for the QI/PI and study coordinator

**5.1.3** Send the notification letter only if the participant has given consent for their physician to be notified. Document the consent in the participant's source documents.

**5.1.4** File a copy of the notification letter in the participant's source documents and in the ISF.

### 5.2 Transmittal of clinically useful information

**5.2.1** When clinically important information arises during the study that could affect the participant's continuing care (e.g., abnormal laboratory results, new diagnoses, study-related findings), transmit this information to the treating physician using the standard transmittal letter (Appendix 2).

**5.2.2** The transmittal letter must include:
- Name of the participant's treating physician (To)
- Name and contact information of the QI/PI (From)
- The participant's name
- The protocol title
- A description of the clinically important information in the context of continuing care
- A list of attachments (e.g., copies of relevant test results)
- Contact information for the QI/PI and study coordinator

**5.2.3** Send the transmittal only with participant consent. File a copy in the participant's source documents and in the ISF.

### 5.3 Documentation

**5.3.1** Maintain a record of all physician notifications and information transmittals in the participant's source documents, including:
- Date of each communication
- Recipient (name and address of treating physician)
- Summary or copy of the content sent
- Any response received from the treating physician
