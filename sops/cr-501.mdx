## 1.0 Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for notifying the subject’s treating physician and the transmittal of clinically useful information in the context of continued care.

This SOP is not linked to any N2 SOPs and is specific to the RI MUHC institution.

## 2.0 Scope

This procedure concerns the research community (employees, investigators, physicians, management, consultants, students, volunteers or other persons) involved in conducting research with human subjects at the MUHC.

## 3.0 Responsibilities

The Qualified Investigator/Investigator is responsible for ensuring continuance of care by informing the subject’s physician of the subject’s participation in a research study, if the patient has consented, and transmitting clinically useful information in the context of continued care.

See [Glossary of Terms](/kb/foundations/glossary)

## 5.0 Procedure

<a id="5-1"></a>

### 5.1 Obtaining consent to inform patient’s physician

<a id="5-1-1"></a>**5.1.1** The patient’s consent is obtained to inform their treating physician of their participation in a human research study via the Informed Consent Form (<Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#icf">ICF</Tooltip>). Refer to [SOP-CR-006](/sops/cr-006) and the Centre for Applied Ethics (CAE) webpage for ICF templates, https://muhc.ca/cae/page/templates-consent-forms.

<a id="5-1-2"></a>**5.1.2** Ensure the ICF contains the following wording:

“I hereby authorize the researcher to inform my treating physician of my participation in this project”. There should be boxes to check off either “YES”, “NO” or “I do not have a treating physician actively following me.”

<a id="5-1-3"></a>**5.1.3** Ensure the ICF contains an area where the name and contact information of the treating physician can be documented. If there is no treating physician, the person obtaining informed consent should indicate N/A in these spaces.

<a id="5-1-4"></a>**5.1.4** Ensure that there is a paragraph in the ICF informing the subject that clinical information useful to the subjects continued care will be sent to the subject’s treating physician as per Standard of Care.

<a id="5-2"></a>

### 5.2 Informing the subject’s treating physician of subject’s participation in research

<a id="5-2-1"></a>**5.2.1** The template in [Appendix 1](#appendix-1): Notification to Subject’s Physician of Participation in Research should be used to inform the subject’s treating physician of a subject’s participation in a human research study.

<a id="5-3"></a>

### 5.3 Informing the subject’s treating physician of clinically useful information in the context of continued care

<a id="5-3-1"></a>**5.3.1** The template in [Appendix 2](#appendix-2): Transmittal of Clinical Information to Subject’s Physician in the Context of Continued Care, should be used to inform the subject’s treating physician of any clinically useful information in the context of continued care (whether an incidental finding or not).
