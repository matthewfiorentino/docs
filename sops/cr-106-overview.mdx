# File Transfer

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-106_04</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 04</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 Sep 2018</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Sponsor-Investigator</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the steps required during the transfer of external files to and from the study database to preserve the integrity of transmitted data and the study database. It applies to all data files that are part of the study data set which are transmitted from another site to be included in the study database, and to all Data Management and IT personnel responsible for transmissions.
</Note>

## Key points

- **A Data Sharing/Transfer Agreement must be in place** before any transfer or sharing of information (partial or entire data) to or from a non-MUHC site. Obtain the template Agreement from the RI-MUHC Contracts Office at contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca.
- All data transfers must use **secure, encrypted methods**. Unencrypted transfer of identifiable or study data by email or unencrypted file sharing is prohibited.
- Every transfer must be **documented**: what was transferred, to whom, by what method, on what date, and who received and confirmed the transfer.
- **Recipient confirmation** of receipt and data integrity must be obtained and filed in the <Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip>.
- After transfer, verify the integrity of the received data against the source (checksums, record counts, or other validation methods as specified in the DMP → [SOP-CR-103](/sops/cr-103)).
- For transfers to regulatory authorities (Health Canada), use only the submission formats and channels specified by the regulator.
