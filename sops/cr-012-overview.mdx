# AE, SAE, and SUSAR Reporting

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-012_08</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 08</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 19 Jun 2023</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Levels I–IV</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the process for assessing, documenting, and reporting adverse events (AEs), serious adverse events (SAEs), and Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reactions (SUSARs) in clinical research studies at the RI-MUHC, in compliance with ICH-<Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, Health Canada regulations, and <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> requirements.
</Note>

## Key points

- All AEs must be collected, assessed by the <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI, and documented on the <Tooltip tip="Any untoward medical occurrence in a participant, whether or not related to the investigational product." headline="Adverse Event (AE)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ae">AE</Tooltip> Assessment Log (Appendix 1) from the time of the participant's <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#icf">ICF</Tooltip> signature date to end of study. The QI/PI must initial and date the log for every entry.
- An AE becomes an <Tooltip tip="An adverse event meeting seriousness criteria: death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability, or congenital anomaly." headline="Serious Adverse Event (SAE)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#sae">SAE</Tooltip> when any one of these criteria is met: hospitalization or prolonged hospitalization; congenital malformation; persistent or significant disability; life-threatening or death; medically important event as per protocol.
- All SAEs must be reported to the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator within **24 hours** of the QI/PI Awareness date and documented on the SAE Reporting Log (Appendix 2). A complete Follow-Up SAE report must be sent within 8 calendar days of the initial report.
- **Reportable SAEs to the REB** must be: (1) unexpected, (2) related or possibly related to study participation, AND (3) suggest greater risk of harm than previously known. Report **within 7 days** if life-threatening or fatal; **within 15 days** otherwise. Submit via <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip> (Form 3H).
- Sponsor-Investigators must submit fatal or life-threatening SUSARs to Health Canada **within 7 calendar days** (with written follow-up within 8 days); all other SUSARs **within 15 calendar days**. Use a CIOMS-I form (Appendix 5).
- External SUSARs received from the Sponsor must be reviewed and initialled by the QI/PI; those requiring protocol or ICF amendments or immediate participant notification must be reported to the REB within **15 days** via Nagano.
- The QI/PI must establish a written procedure (e.g. a Working Instruction) for receiving and processing <Tooltip tip="Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reaction — unexpected in nature, severity, or frequency; must be reported to Health Canada and the REB." headline="SUSAR" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#susar">SUSAR</Tooltip> reports from the Sponsor.
- For studies without an <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">investigational product</Tooltip>, the same AE/SAE collection and REB reporting procedures apply; part of the REB reporting criterion is that the SAE be at least possibly related to the study (not necessarily to an IP).
- For medical device studies, use the Mandatory Medical Device Problem Reporting form on the Health Canada website for device incidents.
