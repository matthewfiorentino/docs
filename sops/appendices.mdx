The following resources are provided as part of the SOP Appendices to support the preparation and conduct of clinical research at The Institute. They include essential document templates for Investigator Site Files (ISFs), Sponsor-Investigator Trial Master Files (TMFs), and preparation for Health Canada inspections.

<Note>
Only the forms that need to be printed are available for download here. All other appendices remain accessible in the SOPs via TalentLMS.
</Note>
