## Purpose and scope

This SOP describes the analysis and reporting of results from a clinical study.

This SOP is applicable to investigator-initiated human subject research whereby study analysis and reporting is the responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator.

---

## Responsibilities

The **Sponsor-Investigator or QI/PI** is responsible for ensuring that the study analysis and reporting processes meet all applicable regulatory, ICH-GCP, Sponsor, and local requirements.

Any or all parts of this procedure may be delegated to appropriately trained study team members, but remain the ultimate responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator or QI/PI.

---

## Procedure

### 5.1 Study analysis reporting activities

**5.1.1** Study analysis reporting activities include:
- Development and finalization of the Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP)
- Conduct of interim analyses (if specified in the protocol)
- Database lock → SOP-CR-107
- Unblinding (if applicable)
- Statistical analysis per the SAP
- **Revisions within the Analysis Plan** (RI-MUHC Addendum addition)
- Preparation of the Clinical Study Report (CSR)
- Submission of the CSR to the REB and Health Canada (as applicable)
- Distribution of the CSR summary to participating sites and participants

### 5.2 Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP)

**5.2.1** The SAP must be a separate, formal document that specifies all analyses that will be performed on the study data. The SAP must be finalized and dated before the database is locked (SOP-CR-107) and before any unblinding occurs.

**5.2.2** The SAP must include, at a minimum:
- Primary and secondary efficacy analyses
- Safety analyses (AEs, SAEs, laboratory parameters)
- Handling of missing data
- Definition of the intent-to-treat (ITT) and per-protocol (PP) populations
- Any pre-specified subgroup analyses
- Statistical methods for each analysis
- Any planned interim analyses and their associated stopping rules

**5.2.3** Any revisions to the SAP must be documented and version-stamped prior to implementation. If revisions are made after database lock, provide a full justification and document separately from the original SAP.

### 5.3 Interim analyses

**5.3.1** Conduct interim analyses only as specified in the protocol and SAP.

**5.3.2** Interim analyses must follow pre-specified stopping rules or statistical adjustments to protect the overall type I error rate (study alpha).

**5.3.3** Document interim analysis results in a formal report. Ensure that access to unblinded interim results is restricted to authorized personnel only (e.g., DSMB members) to protect the integrity of the ongoing trial.

### 5.4 Final statistical analysis

**5.4.1** Conduct the final statistical analysis after database lock per the finalized SAP.

**5.4.2** Document all deviations from the pre-specified SAP, including the reason for the deviation and whether the deviation was pre- or post-database lock.

**5.4.3** Retain all analysis datasets, programming code, and output files in the TMF/ISF for the regulatory retention period.

### 5.5 Clinical Study Report (CSR)

**5.5.1** Prepare the CSR following ICH E3 format. The CSR must include all required sections:
- Title page and synopsis
- Introduction
- Study objectives
- Investigational plan (study design, participants, treatments, efficacy and safety variables)
- Statistical methods
- Study participants (disposition, protocol deviations)
- Efficacy evaluation results
- Safety evaluation results (AEs, deaths, discontinuations, clinical laboratory evaluations)
- Discussion and overall conclusions

**5.5.2** The final CSR must be submitted to the REB as the study completion report → SOP-CR-016, and made available to Health Canada upon request within the required timelines.

**5.5.3** Distribute a CSR summary to all participating sites and inform research participants about study results and publications when available → SOP-CR-016 section 5.4.12.
