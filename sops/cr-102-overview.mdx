# Protocol Development

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-102_05</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 05</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 Sep 2018</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Sponsor-Investigator</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the process for developing study protocols for human subject research. It applies to Sponsor-Investigator studies at the RI-MUHC where protocol development is the responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator. It covers the content requirements for the protocol and Investigator's Brochure, the internal review and approval process, and management of protocol amendments.

## Key points

- In the case of clinical studies initiated by a Sponsor, a **Confidentiality Disclosure Agreement (CDA)** between the Sponsor, the Institution, and the Investigator must be signed and dated prior to obtaining confidential study documents. The RI-MUHC Office of Contracts must review and approve the CDA before signing. File the signed CDA in Nagano → SOP-CR-007.
- The protocol must contain all **ICH E6(R2) and applicable Health Canada required elements** before submission for REB review and Health Canada authorization (CTA/ITA).
- The protocol must be reviewed by a qualified biostatistician, the RI-MUHC Research Hub, and (where applicable) a pharmacist before finalization.
- All protocol amendments must go through the same verification process as the original protocol before submission to the REB and Health Canada.
- The **Reference for Verification of Protocol/Amendment** (Appendix 1) and **Confirmation of Verification of Protocol/Amendment** (Appendix 2) checklists must be completed for the protocol and every subsequent amendment.
- If the study uses an Investigator's Brochure (IB), complete the **Reference for Verification of IB/Amendment** (Appendix 3) and **Confirmation of Verification of IB/Amendment** (Appendix 4).
- The date and version must be clearly indicated on each page of the protocol and Investigator's Brochure.
- A copy of the signed and dated CDA must be kept with the essential study documents in the ISF/TMF.
