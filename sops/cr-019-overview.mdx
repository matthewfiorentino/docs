# Confidentiality and Privacy

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-019_07</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 07</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 Sep 2018</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All levels</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the procedures to ensure the integrity and confidentiality of clinical data collected within the framework of a clinical study, and the privacy of clinical study participants. It applies to all clinical studies undertaken at the RI-MUHC and to all clinical research personnel responsible for ensuring the security and confidentiality of data and participant information.
</Note>

## Key points

- The <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI is responsible for ensuring that confidentiality and privacy aspects of the study meet all applicable regulatory, ICH-<Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, sponsor, and local requirements. Responsibilities may be delegated but remain the QI/PI's ultimate accountability.
- **Quebec law (RSQ A-2.1):** A public body may not release nominative (personally identifiable) information without the person's consent, except as authorized by the Commission d'accès à l'information for study, research, or statistical purposes, or for reportable diseases under the Quebec Public Health Act.
- **MUHC Medical Records Services** is responsible for maintenance of and access to all health records of MUHC research participants. An **MUHC Confidentiality Agreement** (Appendix 1) must be signed and submitted to Medical Records Services before any individual is granted access to patient records.
- Inform Medical Records Services of each participant's enrollment in a study involving an <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">Investigational Product</Tooltip> by providing a copy of the signed <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#icf">ICF</Tooltip> with the Confirmation of Participation in Research Form → [SOP-CR-009](/sops/cr-009).
- Inform Medical Records Services of the conclusion of each participant's involvement in research with an Investigational Product by providing an electronic copy of the "End of Participation in Research" form → [SOP-CR-009](/sops/cr-009).
- MUHC health records of research subjects must be maintained for a minimum of **25 years of inactivity**, unless otherwise specified. Records may be stored off-site.
- All research team members who access patient records must comply with the MUHC Confidentiality and Security Agreement, which covers password protection, authorized use of informational assets, prohibition on unauthorized record access, secure destruction of information, and mandatory reporting of security incidents.
