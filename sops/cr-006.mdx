## Key points

- Always submit ICFs and participant-facing materials to the REB **in editable Word format** — not PDFs. The REB will not accept PDF-only submissions.
- The ICF must be **available in both English and French**. Translation must be done by a qualified/certified translator (or documented on a translation certificate if done by the team). The REB will generally not approve a study without bilingual ICFs.
- For studies involving investigational products (drugs, devices, NHPs), the ICF must be printed on **MUHC hospital letterhead with the FMU-9997 bar code** so it appears in the participant's OACIS electronic record.
- The ICF must include MSSS-mandated paragraphs (shown in red in the REB ICF templates) covering: voluntary participation and right to withdraw, confidentiality, harm compensation, contact information, and signatures. **Without these paragraphs, the REB will reject the ICF.**
- Verify ICF content against the **Verification of an ICF Document checklist** (Appendix 1) before submission.
- Do **not** consent participants before receiving written REB approval, <Tooltip tip="Institutional authorization allowing a study to proceed at the RI-MUHC. Issued after ethics approval and feasibility review." headline="MUHC Authorization" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#muhc-auth">MUHC Authorization</Tooltip>, the <Tooltip tip="Monitoring visit to train the site team on the protocol and confirm site readiness, after REB approval and MUHC Authorization." headline="Site Initiation Visit (SIV)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#siv">SIV</Tooltip>, and the Sponsor's/Sponsor-Investigator's Go Letter (if applicable).
- Use **eighth-grade reading level** language; avoid dense paragraphs; use headings, bullets, and tables to improve comprehension.
- The ICF must include the **clinical trial registration number** (registry name, URL, unique code) as standard information per TCPS2.
- For electronic ICFs with eSignature, a documented remote ICF procedure describing the software must be submitted to the REB with the ICF, confirming validation and compliance with electronic records guidance.

## 1 · Purpose {#purpose}

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the activities involved in
preparing the Informed Consent Form (ICF) in compliance with the requirements
applicable to a given research project (e.g., Declaration of Helsinki, Tri-Council
Policy Statement 2 (TCPS2), Good Clinical Practice (GCP), Health Canada (HC)
regulations, MSSS requirements and Research Ethics Board (REB) requirements).

Note: This procedure also applies to other written participant-facing material supplied
to the study participant, which may contain some of the same information, as the ICF
and must also be reviewed and approved by the REB.

---

## 2 · Scope {#scope}

This procedure concerns the research community (employees, investigators,
physicians, management, consultants, students, volunteers or other persons)
involved in preparing (drafting, adapting, revising, reviewing or translating) study
ICFs (hard copy or electronic) for research with human participants at the
MUHC/RI-MUHC. This SOP is applicable to all human research studies (unless
in cases where informed consent has been waived by the REB).

---

## 3 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

**Sponsor / Sponsor-Investigator:** Responsible for providing sites with a master ICF that complies with the Sponsor's SOPs, study protocol, Health Canada regulatory requirements, GCP, the Declaration of Helsinki, and TCPS2.

**<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip> / <Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip>:** Responsible for ensuring that after revision of the Sponsor's master ICF (if applicable), the site-specific ICF continues to meet all applicable international guidelines, federal regulations, provincial MSSS requirements, and local REB requirements.

Any or all parts of this procedure may be delegated to appropriately qualified and trained study team members, but remain the ultimate responsibility of the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator and QI/PI.

## 5 · Procedure {#procedure}
### 5.1 General overview

**5.1.1** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator provides the site with a study-specific master ICF that requires customization to meet site and/or local REB requirements.

**5.1.2** The ICF is developed, adapted, or revised by the designated research team at the site based on the MUHC REB ICF template.

**5.1.3** The ICF is reviewed by the QI/PI and Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator.

**5.1.4** The Investigator submits the site-specific ICF to the REB via <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip> for REB review/comments, site revisions, and REB approval. The approved ICF is filed in the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip> along with documented approval. **Submit ICFs and participant-facing material to the REB in editable Word format — not PDFs.**

**5.1.5** The Investigator sends the approved ICF to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator for their <Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tmf">Trial Master File</Tooltip> (TMF) with the documented approval.

**5.1.6** Once REB approval for the ICF is obtained, the ICF should be sent for translation. The translated ICF must also be submitted to the REB.

### 5.2 ICF guidelines

**5.2.1** For studies involving investigational products (drug, medical device, or natural health product), the ICF must be printed on **MUHC hospital letterhead** with the **MUHC Medical Records bar code FMU-9997** (already on the REB ICF templates). This bar code ensures the ICF is stored in the Alert section of the participant's electronic medical record (OACIS). Other studies need not meet the bar code requirement. Users of the official letterhead or logo must adhere to the brand guidelines at https://muhc.ca/muhc-logo-guidelines.

**5.2.2** Use language that is as non-technical as possible, understandable to the participant, without compromising content. An **eighth-grade reading level** is recommended.

**5.2.3** Define all medical and technical terms (random assignment, probability, blinding, placebo) and all acronyms and abbreviations when first used.

**5.2.4** Use headings, bulleted lists, and tables (e.g. visit-specific schedule of procedures, table of risks). Avoid dense paragraphs to improve readability and comprehension.

**5.2.5** For printed copies, select a font size appropriate to the target population (e.g. font 12), considering age and underlying conditions, unless otherwise specified by the REB.

**5.2.6** Clearly identify each ICF version with the **version number and/or date on every page**, and ensure the file name also reflects the version number/date. Health Canada inspection findings have included inaccurate file names and versions that were not updated.

**5.2.7** Have someone unfamiliar with the study — preferably a layperson — read the ICF and participant-facing materials to assess comprehension before finalizing. Adjust content as needed.

**5.2.8** Per TCPS2, Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigators must register all clinical trials before recruiting the first participant in a recognized, publicly accessible registry. The **registry name, URL, and unique registration code** must appear as standard information in the REB-approved ICF. Contact QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca if you need an account in ClinicalTrials.gov.

**5.2.9** If using an **electronic ICF with eSignature**, ensure a documented remote ICF procedure is submitted to the REB with the ICF. It must identify the software used and confirm its installation is validated and complies with electronic records guidance. → [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015) regarding electronic records.

### 5.3 ICF content

**5.3.1** If no master ICF is provided by the Sponsor, use the [Consent/Assent document templates on the CAE templates page](https://muhc.ca/cae/templates-consent-forms). These templates contain **mandatory MSSS wording in red** required for clinical trials.

**5.3.2** If a master ICF is provided by the Sponsor, ensure the **MSSS-required paragraphs** (shown in red in the REB ICF templates) have been inserted into the site-specific ICF. These mandatory paragraphs cover:
- Voluntary participation and the right to withdraw
- Confidentiality
- Should you suffer any harm
- Contact information
- Signatures

Verify content using the **Verification of ICF Document** (Appendix 1). Without these MSSS paragraphs, the REB will reject the ICF.

**5.3.3** Have the revised ICF quality-controlled (reviewed by a second person at the site) before REB submission.

**5.3.4** For clinical trials, if the participant agrees, obtain consent to contact the participant's primary caregiver regarding study involvement and any clinically useful information for continued care. → [SOP-CR-501](/sops/cr-501)

**5.3.5** Ensure the ICF also includes/complies with:
- Consistency with the protocol (objectives, number of visits, study procedures, expected risks, alternative treatments)
- Sponsor's and Investigator's name
- Page numbers on all pages (e.g. "page 1 of 15")
- All required signature, initial, and date fields (participant, person obtaining consent, QI/PI if applicable; and where needed: legally appointed representative, witness, translator)

**5.3.6** All research advertisements (posters, flyers, press releases, electronic/social media, videos) must comply with the MUHC Communication policy and must be submitted to the REB as part of the initial Nagano application. → [SOP-CR-007](/sops/cr-007) and [SOP-CR-009](/sops/cr-009)

**5.3.7** ICFs and all participant-facing materials must be available in **both official languages (English and French)**. Translation must be done by a qualified/certified translator; obtain a translation certificate. A Sponsor-Investigator without professional translation funds may translate the ICF and document it on the translation certificate (Appendix 2). Forward translations to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator and QI/PI for approval before REB submission.

**5.3.8** Do **not** consent participants before receiving: written REB approval, <Tooltip tip="Institutional authorization allowing a study to proceed at the RI-MUHC. Issued after ethics approval and feasibility review." headline="MUHC Authorization" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#muhc-auth">MUHC Authorization</Tooltip>, the <Tooltip tip="Monitoring visit to train the site team on the protocol and confirm site readiness, after REB approval and MUHC Authorization." headline="Site Initiation Visit (SIV)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#siv">SIV</Tooltip>, and the Sponsor's/Sponsor-Investigator's Go Letter (if applicable).

### 5.4 Ongoing amendments

**5.4.1** Throughout the study, revise the ICF as needed based on safety reports/issues, protocol or <Tooltip tip="Compilation of clinical and nonclinical data on the investigational product relevant to the study." headline="Investigator's Brochure (IB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ib">Investigator Brochure</Tooltip> amendments, REB recommendations, <Tooltip tip="Independent committee that reviews interim safety and efficacy data and recommends whether to continue, modify, or stop the study." headline="Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#dsmb">DSMB</Tooltip> reports, Health Canada/regulatory requests, or Sponsor requests.

**5.4.2** Update the ICF version number and/or date on every page and in the electronic file name when revising.

**5.4.3** Submit ICF revisions to the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator for review before REB submission (when applicable).

**5.4.4** Submit the revised ICF (with tracked changes) to the REB for approval.

**5.4.5** Do not begin using the amended ICF until receipt of written REB approval and a Go Letter/email from the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator (if applicable). → Section 5.7.1 and [SOP-CR-008](/sops/cr-008) for exceptions.

**5.4.6** Print the REB submission, approval, and all relevant emails. Maintain an audit trail of all communications related to the ICF amendment (Sponsor, regulatory authorities, REB). File in the ISF.

**5.4.7** Best practice: maintain a **Document Approval Tracking Log** to track all REB submissions, approvals, and implementations. → Appendix 3 — ICF Approval Tracker.

### 5.5 Waiver of legal rights

**5.5.1** Do not use any language in the ICF that causes the participant or their legally appointed representative (LAR) to waive, or appear to waive, any legal rights.

**5.5.2** Do not use any language that releases, or appears to release, the Investigator, institution, site, Sponsor, or their agents from liability for negligence. Statements such as "we are not responsible for…" must be avoided.

### 5.6 Cultural considerations

**5.6.1** Ensure that the language and research activities respect the culture, priorities, and knowledge base of the cultural group being invited to participate. Use **inclusive language** throughout (e.g. "they," "their" instead of "his/her"; name participant groups according to their self-denomination). A translator should be available when required. See the Equity, Diversity and Inclusion page of the RI Portal for inclusive writing guides.

**5.6.2** Anyone conducting research with First Nations communities should understand the **FNIGC OCAP® principles**:
- **O**wnership — First Nations communities own their data collectively
- **C**ontrol — First Nations communities control all aspects of data collection and governance that affect them
- **A**ccess — First Nations must have access to data regardless of where it is stored
- **P**ossession — First Nations must have physical control of data

For guidance, contact RI-MUHC Equity, Diversity and Inclusion Specialists at edi.ri@muhc.mcgill.ca.

### 5.7 Approval

**5.7.1** Obtain approval from the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator and QI/PI (as applicable) and from the REB before implementing any change to the protocol, ICF, or participant materials. The only exception is an emergency situation involving an immediate safety hazard to participants. In such a case, a documented conversation with the participant or LAR is required. → [SOP-CR-008](/sops/cr-008)

**5.7.2** Depending on the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator, a documented approval (e.g. in the form of a Go Letter) from the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator may also be required before implementing the revised ICF at the site.
