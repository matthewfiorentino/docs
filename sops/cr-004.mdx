## 1.0 Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the processes for determining human research feasibility, and describes the processes to be followed prior to, during, and following the site selection visit by a Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator.

## 2.0 Scope

This procedure concerns the research community (employees, sponsor-investigators, investigators, physicians, management, consultants, students, volunteers or other persons) involved in hosting a feasibility visit for a human research study.

## 3.0 Responsibilities

The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator and Qualified Investigator (<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>)/Principal Investigator (<Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip>) are responsible for ensuring that the study Feasibility meets all of the applicable regulatory, International Council on Harmonisation (ICH) Good Clinical Practice (GCP)/ International Standards Organization (ISO) Good Clinical Practice (GCP), Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator, and local requirements.

## 4.0 Definitions

See [Glossary of Terms](/kb/foundations/glossary)

## 5.0 Procedure

<a id="5-1"></a>

### 5.1 Preparation for the sponsor’s/Sponsor-investigator’s/monitor’s site feasibility visit (SFV)

<a id="5-1-1"></a>**5.1.1** A confidentiality agreement between the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator and the QI/PI, should be signed and dated prior to receiving any confidential study information such as the study protocol. The review of the confidentiality agreement by a legal advisor is often required before signing.

<a id="5-1-2"></a>**5.1.2** Assess the feasibility of the protocol, to determine the organizational needs of the site. The main issues to consider may include, but are not limited to, the technical and ethical feasibility of the protocol, compatibility with local healthcare practice, access to the target population for screening and accrual, time and resource requirements as well as the availability and commitment of the research team and support services involved in study conduct.

<a id="5-1-3"></a>**5.1.3** The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator is to ensure that all electronic software used in a study has been validated and is compliant with [21CFR Part11](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-11). Study team should contact IT via “IT rescue” on the RI-MUHC Portal in order for evaluation of any study specific software and assessment of e-connectivity issues.

<a id="5-1-3-b"></a>**5.1.3** The site should conduct a preliminary feasibility assessment using [Appendix 1](#appendix-1) Study Feasibility Checklist in preparation for the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor’s SFV.

<a id="5-1-4"></a>**5.1.4** Secure, adequate and temperature-controlled space for storage of investigational products, <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#ip">IP</Tooltip> (drug, biological products, natural health product, medical devices), under the appropriate conditions, should be identified. A qualified person responsible for the management of IP(s) and procedures for their destruction (if required) must be available.

<a id="5-1-5"></a>**5.1.5** The facilities must be adequate for conduct of all protocol procedures as well as having secure space for local data management activities.

<a id="5-2"></a>

### 5.2 Budget and financial contract

<a id="5-2-1"></a>**5.2.1** The site must be aware of the financial implications of any research in which it is participating. It is the Institution/QI/PI’s responsibility to specify RI-MUHC policies regarding all agreements and contracts to be submitted to [Nagano](/apps/nagano/overview) and the RI-MUHC Contracts Department and be aware of the reimbursement of direct and indirect costs associated with the use of their facilities Please note:

- Contracts for new studies are submitted through Nagano.

- Once the contract has been submitted in Nagano, the investigator is instructed to email contracts group at contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca notifying them that a contract was submitted via Nagano.

- Refer to SOP CR-007 for details.

<a id="5-2-2"></a>**5.2.2** Regulatory Agreements: The contractual documents may include, but are not limited to, the following:

- Confirmation that the Institution/QI/PI will conduct the clinical study according to the protocol approved by the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, and to which the <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> and any necessary, regulatory authorities have given their approval or a favourable opinion.

- Confirmation that the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator and QI/PIs will respect compliance with the [Declaration of Helsinki](https://www.wma.net/policies-post/wma-declaration-of-helsinki/)/[TCPS2](https://ethics.gc.ca/eng/policy-politique_tcps2-eptc2_2022.html) and ICH-GCP/ISO-GCP as well as Health Canada regulations (where applicable); and
- Dated signatures of the QI/PI/Institution, as well as the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator on the contract or other document, to confirm the agreement. For investigator-initiated studies the process is still via Nagano for new studies. The investigator asks the contract group contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca for the latest contract template to be submitted via Nagano. Refer to SOP CR-007.

<a id="5-2-3"></a>**5.2.3** Financial agreements: Agreements between Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, QI/PI, Institutions, and/or CRO may include details of the financial aspects, as follows:

- Fees for the Sponsor-Investigator, QI/PI/Sub-Investigators (Sub-Is) or other research team members;
- Payment per participant or visit;
- Payment for participants having completed the study;
- Payment schedule;
- Reimbursement of expenses to participants;
- Reimbursement of costs incurred for patient referrals or remote trial conduct by Sub-Is at satellite sites;
- Reimbursement of supporting service providers/institutional departments involved in the conduct of the study;
- Dated signatures on these documents or other contract, to confirm the agreement, prior to each separate human research study.

<a id="5-3"></a>

<a id="5-3-site-feasibility-visit-sfv-and-site-selection"></a>

### 5.3 Site feasibility visit (SFV) and site selection

<a id="5-3-1"></a>**5.3.1** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator (or CRO): Conducts a Site Feasibility Visit (SFV) to evaluate the site to ensure that the QI/PI has access to the appropriate study population, the appropriate facilities and equipment and suitably qualified and experienced staff available to enroll the specified number of eligible participants within the defined timeframe, as per protocol, Health Canada regulations and GCP standards.

<a id="5-3-2"></a>**5.3.2** Sponsor-Investigators and Monitors should refer to [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for an SFV Report template for Sponsor-Investigators and/or Monitors to document this visit. Once conducted and completed, a copy should be placed in the Trial Master File (<Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip>).

<a id="5-3-3"></a>**5.3.3** Clinical Research Coordinator or designate:

- Compiles the requested information, upon receiving the Sponsor’s/Sponsor-Investigator’s request for a SFV.

- Assists the QI/PI with completion of the Study Feasibility Checklist ([Appendix 1](#appendix-1)) to ensure all aspects of feasibility have been assessed prior to agreement to participate.

- Facilitates the SFV meeting format and ensures that follow-up requirements are met.

<a id="5-3-4"></a>**5.3.4** Items assessed by Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor may include, but are not limited to the following:

- Qualifications and experience of the QI/PI/Sub-Is;
- Capabilities of the human research team and supporting departments/service providers to conduct the human research study;
- Site team’s knowledge of the therapeutic area, Good Clinical Practice (GCP), Health Canada regulations, Tri-Council Policy Statement, and FDA regulations (if applicable);
- Availability and qualifications of the site research team members;
- Requirement for 3rd party/Vendor involvement (e.g. external nursing agency for nights)
- Investigator workload and conflicting studies;
- Potential population eligible for the study enrollment, including the projected number of participants needed to be screened to achieve the site accrual target;
- Site team communication and responsiveness (e.g., return of telephone calls, provision of documents in a timely manner);
- Clinical research management procedures and processes (SOPs, and WIs);
- Documents confirming training;
- Ability to meet the required timelines for study activities;
- Storage of confidential records.

<a id="5-3-5"></a>**5.3.5** Ensure that the documentation or information to support the above requirements is available to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator and/or Monitor at the SFV.

<a id="5-3-6"></a>**5.3.6** Once the SFV is conducted and the study is deemed feasible to be conducted at the site, a signed and dated copy of the agreement must be kept with the study-related essential documentation.

<a id="5-3-7"></a>**5.3.7** As per [SOP-CR-007](/sops/cr-007), the contract/agreements and REB required study documents (protocol, <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#icf">ICF</Tooltip>, <Tooltip tip="Compilation of clinical and nonclinical data on the investigational product relevant to the study." headline="Investigator's Brochure (IB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#ib">IB</Tooltip>, patient-facing documents) must then pass through an RI-MUHC/MUHC feasibility and ethical review.
