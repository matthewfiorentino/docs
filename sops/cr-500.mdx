## Key points

- **Human Research Privileges** are required for MDs and Dentists listed on the MUHC Council of Physicians, Dentists and Pharmacists (CPDP). Privileges are required for QI/PIs conducting Health Canada-regulated studies with an <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">investigational product</Tooltip> (drug, medical device, or natural health product).
- **Researcher Status** is required for non-MDs/Dentists (e.g., PhDs, nurses, physiotherapists, psychologists, pharmacists) who wish to be a <Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip> conducting research that does not require medical oversight. Researcher Status **does not** permit conducting research involving human medical oversight.
- **Fellows, residents, and medical students** may not conduct research independently and are not eligible for Privileges or Researcher Status. They cannot be listed as QI/PI or QI/PI backup.
- All other research team members listed on the <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip> (other than the QI/PI and Sub-Investigator backup) are **not** required to obtain Privileges or Researcher Status.
- To apply, log onto **TalentLMS** (via the RI Portal → Applications → TalentLMS) and complete the Privileges/Researcher Status questionnaire. Eligibility typically requires a McGill Faculty appointment; others are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
- Upon acceptance, the **commitment certificate** is automatically generated in TalentLMS and is valid for **3 years**. File a copy in the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip> of each study.
- A **reminder email** is automatically sent 30 days before expiration for renewal via TalentLMS.
- Privileges/Researcher Status may be **revoked** by the RI-MUHC ED/CSO for misconduct, fraud, or loss of CPDP rights.
- **MNI exception:** MNI PIs conducting research at the MNI follow McGill/MNI procedures. If conducting research at other MUHC sites, MNI PIs must follow RI-MUHC SOPs.

## 1 · Purpose {#purpose}

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for QI/PIs to
obtain MSSS required Privileges or Researcher Status from the RI-MUHC in
order to conduct human research at the MUHC/RI-MUHC.

This procedure describes how researchers (who are, in the context of this SOP,
Qualified or Principal Investigators, Sub-Investigators delegated QI/PI backup
tasks on the Task Delegation Log, and Sponsor-Investigators, as defined in the
Glossary of Terms) obtain and maintain either Human Research Privileges (for
MDs and Dentists listed on the MUHC CPDP) or Researcher Status (for non-
MDs/Dentists). Privileges and Researcher Status are mandatory prior to
accessing training to conduct clinical research (see SOP-CR-003 for the
procedure on human research training) and conducting a human research study.

---

## 2 · Scope {#scope}

This procedure applies to any MUHC physicians or dentists (and their backup)
who wish to obtain Privileges to be a QI/PI in order to conduct MUHC/RI-MUHC
studies requiring medical oversight.

This procedure also applies to non-MDs e.g. PhDs, nurses, physiotherapists,
radiotherapists, psychologists, pharmacists and other health care professionals





(and their backup) who wish to obtain Researcher Status in order to be a PI
conducting a human research study that does not require medical oversight.

The QI/PI or Sponsor-Investigator (and their backup) is required to obtain Human
Research Privileges or Researcher Status to conduct any study that involves
human participants’ participation, information or materials at the MUHC (including
biological material human embryos, fetuses, fetal tissue, reproductive materials
and stem cells from living and deceased individuals).

The aim of this SOP covers all studies, whether or not these require the
submission of a CTA/ITA to Health Canada including observational,
epidemiological and human health records research.

Montreal Neurological Institute (MNI) exception:

• Although the MNI is part of the MUHC, MNI PIs who plan to conduct
studies at the MNI must obtain McGill Research Privileges and
institutional training as per MNI and McGill SOPs.

• MNI PIs who plan to conduct studies at other MUHC sites (e.g., RVH,
MGH, MCH) must obtain RI-MUHC Research Privileges and institutional
training as per RI-MUHC SOPs.


As per the 2022 MUHC Regulatory Framework, for the purposes of a study, a
fellow, resident or medical student cannot conduct research independently and
hence, cannot be Sponsor-Investigator, Principal/Qualified Investigator, nor Sub-
Investigator delegated QI/PI responsibilities (i.e. cannot apply for Privileges or
Researcher Status).


Note: All other research team members listed on the Task Delegation Log, other
than the QI/PI and Sub-Investigator backup, are not required to obtain
Privileges/Researcher Status for the same study.

## 3 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

**Investigators** are required to obtain either Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status to submit a study to the MUHC <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> and conduct human research. In addition to overall study conduct, Investigators are responsible for:
- Obtaining appropriate institutional training → [SOP-CR-003](/sops/cr-003) and completing the SOP Reader prior to study submission
- Ensuring research staff who will be delegated protocol tasks have adequate institutional and study-specific training and documented task delegation prior to any trial/study activities
- Ensuring that a Sub-Investigator designated as QI/PI backup has also obtained the same Privileges/Researcher Status and training as the QI/PI or better

**The RI-MUHC Executive Director and Chief Scientific Officer (ED/CSO)** is responsible for:
- Ensuring that ICH-<Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, ISO 14155 GCP, RI-MUHC SOPs, and federal/provincial regulatory authority requirements are available to the MUHC/RI-MUHC research community
- Ensuring each QI/PI or Sponsor-Investigator has valid Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status
- Revoking Privileges or Researcher Status when required

**The RI-MUHC Performance Office** is responsible for defining, implementing, and maintaining the process to attribute RI-MUHC Privileges and Researcher Status, and for processing and issuing certificates.

**The RI-MUHC <Tooltip tip="All planned and systematic actions to ensure trial data are generated, documented, and reported in compliance with GCP and SOPs." headline="Quality Assurance (QA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qa">Quality Assurance</Tooltip> department** is responsible for confirming the QI/PI or Sponsor-Investigator has Privileges or Researcher Status during the QA feasibility portion of the study submission process for new studies.

## 4 · Definitions {#definitions}

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Human Research Privileges** | Authorization for a member of the CPDP (physicians or dentists) to conduct research on humans at the MUHC/RI-MUHC. Required for QI/PIs conducting Health Canada-regulated studies with <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">IP</Tooltip> (drug, medical device, or natural health product). |
| **Researcher Status** | Authorization for individuals who are not members of the CPDP (e.g., PhDs, nurses, physiotherapists, psychologists, pharmacists) to conduct research on humans at the MUHC/RI-MUHC that does not require medical oversight. Individuals with Researcher Status cannot conduct research involving human medical oversight. |

The MSSS, via the *Cadre de référence ministériel pour la recherche avec des participants humains* (2020), defines these two ways for a researcher to obtain authorization to conduct research on human participants.

## 5 · Procedure {#procedure}
### 5.1 Evaluation of eligibility for Research Privileges or Researcher Status

**5.1.1** Before initiating human research, researchers must receive their human research commitment certificate confirming their Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status.

To receive this certificate, log onto **TalentLMS** (via the RI Portal → Applications → TalentLMS) and select the Privileges/Researcher Status "course" from the course catalog. Complete the questionnaire:
- For physicians/dentists: **Demande de privilèges de recherche — Research Privileges Request**
- For non-MDs/Dentists: **Demande de statut de chercheur — Researcher Status Request**

**5.1.2** To be eligible for either Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status, the individual must typically hold a McGill Faculty appointment and plan to conduct research at the MUHC. The eligibility of individuals requesting Privileges/Researcher Status who do not hold a McGill Faculty appointment will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis by the RI-MUHC Performance Officer.

### 5.2 Privileges/Researcher Status commitment certificate

**5.2.1** Upon acceptance by the RI-MUHC Performance Office, the commitment certificate (Privileges or Researcher Status) is automatically generated and available for download in TalentLMS. The certificate:
- Outlines all commitments
- Is valid for **3 years**

**5.2.2** It is the Investigator's responsibility to ensure this certificate is filed in the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">Investigator Site File</Tooltip> (ISF) of each of their studies. A copy is maintained in TalentLMS as a backup.

### 5.3 Renewal

**5.3.1** Thirty days before the expiration of Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status, a reminder email is automatically sent to the Investigator to renew via TalentLMS.

**5.3.2** Ongoing eligibility will be verified by the Performance Office before the certificate is renewed.

### 5.4 Revocation

**5.4.1** Research Privileges or Researcher Status will be revoked by the RI-MUHC ED/CSO for the following reasons (without being limited to this list):
- Suspicion of misconduct that puts participant safety at risk and requires further investigation
- Evidence of fraud
- Inspection and audit activities have identified fraud or misconduct by the Investigator
- Loss of CPDP rights or end of MUHC employment

### 5.5 Reattribution of Research Privileges or Researcher Status

**5.5.1** Depending on the situation, Research Privileges or Researcher Status may be reattributed by the RI-MUHC ED/CSO following resolution of the issue that led to revocation.
