## 1.0 Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for the development/revision, Subject Matter Expert (SME) review, CIM Compliance review, RI-MUHC Management approval/authorization, RI-MUHC launch, maintenance and storage of written RI-MUHC SOPs intended for use by the clinical research community as well as the associated SOP reader, training &amp; quiz revisions for RI-MUHC institutional training.

## 2.0 Scope

This procedure concerns the research community (employees, investigators, physicians, management, consultants, students, volunteers or other persons) who play a role in RI-MUHC SOP Management as well as Subject Matter Expert and Training Advisor contributions.

All human research processes should be documented in writing. Department-specific or protocol-specific SOPs or Working Instructions (WIs) fall outside the scope of this SOP as they are approved by their department/<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/<Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip>/Sponsor-Investigator.

## 3.0 Responsibilities

RI-MUHC Management: The RI-MUHC must ensure that it provides, to the research community, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that are in compliance with all of the applicable Health Canada regulations, International Council on Harmonisation (ICH) Good Clinical Practice (GCP), International Standards Organization (ISO) Good Clinical Practice (GCP), and local requirements and that these SOPs are available for Health Canada (or FDA/EMA) review, if inspected.

Operational Group Improver/Identifier: MUHC/RI-MUHC teams who conduct human research using the RI-MUHC SOPs and are able to identify the need for process improvement and SOP revision. It is the Operational Group’s responsibility to communicate a need for process improvement to <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's Quality Assurance department, which checks the credentials and training of each investigator who submits a study, audits research and maintains the SOPs. In GCP, quality assurance is the planned, systematic actions that ensure a trial complies with GCP and the applicable regulatory requirements." headline="Quality Assurance (QA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qa">QA</Tooltip>.

SOP Writer: A person who knows the process well and possesses technical writing skills. It should be the Operational group who identifies the need for process improvement and who then delegates the writing or revision of the SOP to a qualified person.

QA Process Controller: A Quality Assurance employee who can guide operational groups/ Subject Matter Experts (SMEs), review SOPs and manage the administrative aspect of SOP management. Quality Assurance should not write nor approve SOPs, in order to preserve independency between QA and Operations, however, QA will provide recommendations based on process audit observations, which may be the foundation for SOP review. Once SOPs have undergone revision by SMEs and QC by CIM Compliance, QA provides final review prior to submitting to upper management for review and approval.

Subject Matter Experts (SMEs): Any qualified person who uses the process and is able to contribute to the writing and review of one or more SOPs. Note that the Quality Control SOP Reviewers are generally part of these discussions as well. Quality Control SOP Reviewer: Any qualified person who is able to QC the SOP after SME review and interacts with QA to create a final draft. The CIM Compliance Specialist typically executes this role.

## 5.0 Procedure

<a id="5-1"></a>

### 5.1 Decision to create or revise a new procedure

<a id="5-1-1"></a>**5.1.1** The RI-MUHC QA department is currently making a format transition from N2 copy written SOPs with attached RI-MUHC addenda to RI-MUHC SOPs, which combine N2 and RI-specific processes in a more user-friendly manner.

<a id="5-1-2"></a>**5.1.2** MUHC/RI-MUHC Human Research Operational groups should identify a need for new or revised procedures, which may originate from staff information, a need for more clinical research tools, specific study requirements, changes to administrative policy or processes, changes in other departments etc. The need for a revision to the process in question should be communicated to the QA department.

<a id="5-1-3"></a>**5.1.3** Additionally, the QA department conducts process audits across RI-MUHC human research groups in order to promote and facilitate process improvement. Process audits identify a need for process improvement and provide recommendations for revision to specific SOPs. These recommendations are communicated to the Operational groups who participated in the process audit(s).

<a id="5-2"></a>

### 5.2 Verify if the process in question belongs in an SOP or WI

<a id="5-2-1"></a>**5.2.1** It is important to determine if the process should be documented as an SOP or a WI. As soon as the process involves detailing responsibilities, timelines, communication, or resources, the document should be an SOP. If the document only describes a sequence of technical steps and instructions, the documents should to be a WI. SOPs can however contain technical steps as well as helpful tools as appendices. Refer to [Appendix 1](#appendix-1) for an SOP template and [Appendix 2](#appendix-2) for a WI template.

<a id="5-3"></a>

### 5.3 Designate an SOP writer

<a id="5-3-1"></a>**5.3.1** The Operational group who identifies a need for SOP creation or SOP review should delegate the writing/revision of a process to a qualified individual who uses and knows the process well. Other contributors may be brought in, depending on the complexity of the process.

<a id="5-4"></a>

### 5.4 Request a new SOP number

<a id="5-4-1"></a>**5.4.1** If there is a need for a new RI-MUHC SOP for an RI-wide process, the SOP writer must request via QAclinicalresearch.muhc.mcgill.ca email, a new SOP number from the QA Process Controller.

<a id="5-5"></a>

### 5.5 Grant the SOP number (if applicable)

<a id="5-5-1"></a>**5.5.1** The QA Process Controller confirms the justification for a request for a new or revised SOP, determines where the process change belongs in the set of current SOPs and then issues a new SOP number, where appropriate.

<a id="5-5-2"></a>**5.5.2** The QA Process Controller tracks the use of the new SOP in a revised SOP Table of Contents (TOC).

<a id="5-5-3"></a>**5.5.3** If the request for process revision does not warrant creating a new RI-MUHC SOP, the QA Process Controller will confirm which current RI-MUHC SOP can be revised and provide a revision number or suggest a department-specific SOP/WI.

<a id="5-6"></a>

### 5.6 Draft a new or revised SOP

<a id="5-6-1"></a>**5.6.1** The delegated SOP writer takes sufficient time in order to draft a process that all contributors will deem adequate.

<a id="5-6-2"></a>**5.6.2** It is important to consider the weight of the terminology used in SOPs and WIs:

- The wording “must”, “will”, “have to” involve mandatory actions. Any excursions from these requirements must be documented in a SOP deviation.

- The wordings “should”, “typically”, “usually” involve that by default, it is expected that the process will be followed. However, it is anticipated that the process might not always be applicable to all situations. In this case, a comment on the record itself, or a separate note-to-file will be enough to justify an excursion and document its impact (if the reason is not obvious).

<a id="5-7"></a>

### 5.7 Circulate the new or revised RI-MUHC SOP for review by SMEs

<a id="5-7-1"></a>**5.7.1** The SOP writer circulates the draft of the SOP to a team of SMEs who know the process well and can contribute.

<a id="5-7-2"></a>**5.7.2** For more complex revisions, meetings may be held with the writer, the SMEs, CIM Compliance and RI-MUHC QA in order to facilitate the revisions.

<a id="5-7-3"></a>**5.7.3** Revisions are documented via change tracks in Word.

<a id="5-7-4"></a>**5.7.4** SOP versions are tracked by ensuring revision date is at the end of the electronic file name.

<a id="5-7-5"></a>**5.7.5** Best practice is to have one person collect all SME contributions (e.g. CIM Compliance Specialist) and then hold a meeting to review with QA and SMEs to discuss revisions.

<a id="5-7-6"></a>**5.7.6** The SME review by all parties is captured on an SOP Review and Approval Log (refer to [Appendix 4](#appendix-4)).

<a id="5-8"></a>

### 5.8 Quality control and Quality Assurance of the SOP(s)

<a id="5-8-1"></a>**5.8.1** Once all SMEs have provided their input, the final draft is sent to CIM Compliance for quality control of the content.

<a id="5-8-2"></a>**5.8.2** Once CIM Compliance is satisfied with the content, the final SME draft is sent to QA for a final review to ensure compliance with regulations, guidelines and local regulations and policies. The CIM Compliance QC as well as QA review are documented on the SOP Review and Approval Log.

<a id="5-8-3"></a>**5.8.3** Drafts of the SOP are filed by QA electronically with date of draft at the end of the file name.

<a id="5-8-4"></a>**5.8.4** The QA Process Controller inserts the Effective Date of the SOP. This date should be at least 2 months after the projected launch date of an SOP. (Ideally, an English and French SOP version and associated training and quizzes should be launched simultaneously therefore, the Effective Date will also depend on the projected timelines for SOP Upper Management approval, translation services and RI-MUHC Training Advisor services.)

<a id="5-8-5"></a>**5.8.5** QA updates the SOP Table of Contents (TOC) with the revised SOP version numbers.

<a id="5-9"></a>

### 5.9 Review and approval by RI-MUHC upper management

<a id="5-9-1"></a>**5.9.1** Once the final SME draft has been reviewed by Compliance and QA, QA sends a track change version and a clean version of the electronic SOP to RI-MUHC Upper Management for review and approval along with the SOP Review and Approval Log detailing the contributions to the particular SOP revision.

<a id="5-9-2"></a>**5.9.2** Once Upper Management review is complete and each SOP and corresponding Appendices have been assembled into PDF format, the final SOP version is signed by RI-MUHC Upper Management and returned to QA for electronic filing.

<a id="5-9-3"></a>**5.9.3** RI-MUHC Upper Management can conduct an annual review of RI-MUHC SOPs and submit any recommendations to QA.

<a id="5-10"></a>

### 5.10 Secure filing of the final SOP

<a id="5-10-1"></a>**5.10.1** The signed paper copy of the SOP is filed at the RI-MUHC in a QA binder stored at the administrative building. If electronically signed, only an electronic version will be kept as per [5.10.2](#5-10-2).

<a id="5-10-2"></a>**5.10.2** The QA Process Controler electronically files the approved SOP in electronic QA files and the RI portal Document Repository (refer to [Appendix 3](#appendix-3)).

<a id="5-11"></a>

### 5.11 Updating of SOP training and SOP reader by the RI-MUHC training and learning solutions advisor

<a id="5-11-1"></a>**5.11.1** QA communicates the SOP revisions to the RI-MUHC Training and Learning Solutions Advisor and requests revisions to the [TalentLMS](/apps/talentlms) SOP training, where required.

<a id="5-11-2"></a>**5.11.2** A draft version of the revised training module(s) is sent to QA for review and approval.

<a id="5-11-3"></a>**5.11.3** Additionally, the Training and Learning Solutions Advisor replaces the superseded SOP with a revised version in the SOP Reading application on TalentLMS.

<a id="5-11-4"></a>**5.11.4** QA and the Training and Learning Solutions Advisor prepare quiz questions related to the revised SOP(s). These same questions will be part of the course renewal quiz as well.

<a id="5-11-5"></a>**5.11.5** Once approved and translated, the revised training with quiz and [SOP Reader](/training/compliance-requirements#sop-reader-and-assessment) with quiz is made available to the research community via the TalentLMS platform.

<a id="5-12"></a>

### 5.12 SOP translation

<a id="5-12-1"></a>**5.12.1** Ensure that the QA budget does cover translation services.

<a id="5-12-2"></a>**5.12.2** QA contacts a translation vendor (e.g. previously used translation services included Beyond Words and TransPerfect).

<a id="5-12-3"></a>**5.12.3** QA sends the final version of SOP to the translation vendor, providing required timeline.

<a id="5-12-4"></a>**5.12.4** QA files the final version of the French SOP electronically and uploads it into the RI portal Document Repository along with the updated TOC.

<a id="5-12-5"></a>**5.12.5** QA then sends the French SOP version to the Training and Learning Solutions Advisor for French SOP Reader updating and French SOP Training and quiz updating.

<a id="5-13"></a>

### 5.13 SOP reader &amp; SOP training module launch to the MUHC/RI-MUHC research community

<a id="5-13-1"></a>**5.13.1** Once a new or revised SOP is uploaded into TalentLMS for SOP reading, an email is sent to notify the research community that QA has issued a new/revised SOP that should be read and a short quiz passed by the human research community prior to the SOP effective date. Best practice is to ensure the SOP is uploaded at least one month prior to its effective date, giving the research community time to read and do the quiz prior to it being in effect.

<a id="5-13-2"></a>**5.13.2** If a trainee’s SOP training certificate has not yet expired, in lieu of redoing the entire course, the trainee can read the revised SOP(s) and correctly answer the SOP-related quiz questions in order to update their training requirements. Note: this does not change the certificate expiry date. If however, the SOP is launched close to the trainees certificate expiry date, best practice would be to renew the training.

<a id="5-13-3"></a>**5.13.3** Once an SOP module in SOP training has been revised, translated and approved, the research community will receive another TalentLMS email notification regarding the updated training module(s) for upcoming course renewal (due prior to the 2-year training certificate expiry date).

<a id="5-14"></a>

### 5.14 Maintenance of the effective SOP life-cycle

<a id="5-14-1"></a>**5.14.1** An SOP is effective at any time during its life-cycle. Typically, a life-cycle is less than 5 years. Revision and re-approval and launch should be flexible and occur any time during the 5 year period in order to adapt to required changes (e.g. regulatory, international guidance, MSSS requirements, organizational structure changes, RI policy, MUHC Regulatory Framework, software).

<a id="5-14-2"></a>**5.14.2** When an SOP is removed from circulation (e.g. a process is no longer used or a process is incorporated into another SOP), the SOP is retired.

<a id="5-15"></a>

### 5.15 SOP retirement

<a id="5-15-1"></a>**5.15.1** The QA Process Controller verifies any request for SOP retirement and obtains any clarifications.

<a id="5-15-2"></a>**5.15.2** The QA Process Controller documents the reason for SOP retirement in a note-to-file and the SOP Table of Contents.

<a id="5-15-3"></a>**5.15.3** The QA Process Controller notifies the Training and Learning Advisor who ensures that the SOP Reader is updated and that there are no references to a retired SOP in TalentLMS learning modules and quizzes.

<a id="5-15-4"></a>**5.15.4** Once TalentLMS has been updated, the SOP is officially retired.
