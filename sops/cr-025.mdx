## Purpose and scope

This SOP describes the process for institutions to demonstrate calibration and routine preventative maintenance of equipment being used in clinical research.

This procedure applies to all trials conducted by Qualified Investigator/Sponsor-initiated research conducted for drugs, natural health products, medical devices, and other trials involving human participants.

---

## Responsibilities

Management of SOPs for equipment maintenance, as well as program oversight, will vary by institution, and will depend on the institutional structure (e.g., Director/Manager Biomedical Engineering, VP Research, or other).

If equipment maintenance is the responsibility of another party/group (e.g., a pharmaceutical partner for a study-specific instrument), ensure that this responsibility is documented in writing and that the relevant maintenance records are available to the QI/Investigator for the ISF.

---

## Procedure

### 5.1 RI-MUHC institutional responsibilities for equipment

**5.1.1** The **MUHC Biomed Department** is responsible for MUHC hospital equipment. The **RI-MUHC Biomed Department** is responsible for CIM (Centre for Innovative Medicine) equipment.

### 5.2 Critical equipment identification and record-keeping

**5.2.1** Using a risk-based approach, the QI/Investigator must identify critical equipment used in the clinical trial and its specifications.

**5.2.2** All service, maintenance, cleaning, and calibration records, as well as the product manual for critical pieces of equipment used in the trial, must be retained for **25 years** for Health Canada-regulated studies. This includes:
- Certificates
- Calibration data
- Records of faults, breakdowns, and misuse of the equipment

**5.2.3** In addition to dates of calibration, device details, and the person who performed calibrations, the QI/Investigator must also document the **person responsible for each instrument** on the Equipment Log, per Health Canada Guide 0100, Medical Instrument Records (section 1183).

**5.2.4** After maintenance and calibration, the maintenance responsible person must apply a **sticker** on the equipment indicating the date inspected and calibrated.

**5.2.5** Upon request, the QI/Investigator must make available all maintenance, safety, and calibration certificates.

### 5.3 Refrigerators, freezers, and rooms used for storage of biological specimens and IP

**5.3.1 QI/Investigator responsibilities:**

The QI/Investigator must ensure that refrigerators, freezers, and rooms used for storage of biological specimens and investigational product:
- Are adequate and undergo regular maintenance
- Are equipped with a single calibrated Min/Max thermometer or other centralized monitoring system
- Are linked to back-up power in case of power outage
- Are equipped with an alarm or linked to the central alarm system, allowing quick detection in case of malfunction and temperature excursion
- Inform the appropriate Biomed department of any equipment malfunction

**5.3.2** The QI/Investigator or designee is responsible for written procedures covering:
- What to do when the alarm activates
- What to do in case of power outage
- Procedures for transfer of samples to a back-up storage location

The type of back-up system used is based on the facility's equipment.

### 5.4 Temperature monitoring

**5.4.1** Record daily temperature readings (and humidity, where required) in the **Refrigerator/Freezer/Room Temperature Log** (Appendix 1). For each entry, record:
- Date and time
- Actual temperature (and humidity, if applicable)
- Min and Max temperatures
- Initials of the recorder
- Whether a temperature deviation occurred (Yes/No) and comments if yes

**5.4.2** Document any temperature deviation, including the duration of the excursion, corrective actions taken, and whether any specimens or IP were affected. Report to the Sponsor per the protocol requirements → SOP-CR-010, SOP-CR-011.

### 5.5 Study equipment inventory and maintenance documentation

**5.5.1** Maintain the **Study Equipment Inventory and Maintenance Routine Documentation** (Appendix 2) for all study equipment. For each instrument, document:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| Equipment type, brand, model, serial number | Device identification details |
| Location | Physical location at site |
| Regular maintenance frequency | As specified in equipment manual (yearly, 6 months, or other) |
| Maintenance done (yes/no) and date | Date maintenance was completed |
| Calibration/Inspection frequency | Measuring instruments: as per manual (yearly, 6 months, or other) |
| Calibration done (yes/no) and date | Date calibration was completed |
| Sticker present | Whether calibration sticker is affixed |
| Report present | Whether maintenance report/certificate is on file |
| Expiry date | Calibration/inspection expiry date |
