# Equipment Calibration and Maintenance

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-025_03</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 03</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 Sep 2018</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All levels</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the process for institutions to demonstrate calibration and routine preventative maintenance of equipment being used in clinical research. It applies to all trials conducted at the RI-MUHC involving drugs, natural health products, medical devices, and other trials involving human participants.

## Key points

- The **MUHC Biomed Department** is responsible for MUHC hospital equipment. The **RI-MUHC Biomed Department** is responsible for CIM (Centre for Innovative Medicine) equipment. If equipment maintenance is the responsibility of another party (e.g., pharmaceutical partner for a study-specific instrument), that responsibility must be documented in writing.
- Using a **risk-based approach**, the <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/Investigator must identify critical equipment used in the clinical trial and its specifications. All service, maintenance, cleaning, and calibration records — as well as the product manual — must be retained for **25 years** for Health Canada-regulated studies. This includes certificates, calibration data, and records of faults, breakdowns, and misuse.
- In addition to calibration dates and device details, the QI/Investigator must document the **person responsible for each instrument** on the Equipment Log, per Health Canada Guide 0100, Medical Instrument Records (section 1183).
- After maintenance and calibration, the responsible person must apply a **sticker** on the equipment indicating the date inspected and calibrated.
- Refrigerators, freezers, and rooms used for storage of biological specimens and <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">IP</Tooltip> must: undergo regular maintenance; be equipped with a **calibrated Min/Max thermometer or centralized monitoring system**; be linked to back-up power; and be equipped with an alarm or linked to the central alarm system. The QI/Investigator or designee must maintain written procedures for alarm events and power outages.
- All maintenance, safety, and calibration certificates must be made available upon request.
- Use the **Refrigerator/Freezer/Room Temperature Log** (Appendix 1) to record daily temperatures and deviations. Use the **Study Equipment Inventory and Maintenance Routine Documentation** (Appendix 2) to track all study equipment calibration and maintenance activities.
