Before you enrol the first participant, a series of administrative, regulatory, and logistical steps must be completed and documented. These SOPs define the required sequence of activities from initial feasibility assessment through site activation and ongoing equipment readiness. They apply to all interventional study types (Levels I–IV).

<Warning>
**Controlled documents — always use the online version.** Do not rely on downloaded or printed copies. Your first reading of each SOP must be completed through the SOP Reader on TalentLMS to count toward your training record.
</Warning>

## SOPs in this group

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-004 — Protocol Feasibility and Site Selection · v19-Jun-2023 · Levels I–IV">
    This SOP defines the process for evaluating whether the RI-MUHC site has the capacity, resources, and qualified personnel to conduct a proposed clinical study. Feasibility assessment must be completed and documented before any commitment is made to a Sponsor or before the study is submitted for REB review.

    **Key points:**
    - You must assess patient population availability, investigator qualifications, site infrastructure, equipment, pharmacy capacity, and competing trials before agreeing to participate in a study.
    - The **Study Feasibility Checklist** (Appendix 1) is the required documentation tool. A completed checklist must be filed in the Investigator Site File.
    - For industry-sponsored studies, the Sponsor's own feasibility questionnaire may supplement but does not replace the RI-MUHC checklist.
    - Feasibility concerns identified during assessment must be communicated to the Sponsor before the site agreement is signed. Do not commit to enrolment targets that the site cannot realistically meet.

    **Downloadable forms:**
    - [Appendix 1 — Study Feasibility Checklist](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=52840213683651482)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-005 — Study Initiation and Activation · v19-Jun-2023 · Levels I–IV">
    This SOP covers all the steps that must be completed between receiving final REB approval and enrolling the first participant. It defines what constitutes "site activation" and what documents must be in place before study activities begin.

    **Key points:**
    - Site activation requires: written REB approval, a fully executed site agreement (for industry-sponsored studies), Health Canada authorization where required, a completed and signed Task Delegation Log, and documented training for all team members.
    - A Site Initiation Visit (SIV) conducted by the Sponsor or CRO is common for industry-sponsored trials. You must ensure that all action items from the SIV are resolved before activating.
    - The Nagano study record must be in "Active" status before any study-specific procedures are performed on participants.
    - For internally sponsored studies, confirm with the Research Hub that all institutional requirements are met before proceeding.

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP. Initiation documentation is maintained in the Investigator Site File per SOP-CR-015.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-007 — Initial REB Review, COI and Trial Registration · v01-May-2025 · Levels I–IV">
    This SOP describes the requirements for submitting a study for initial Research Ethics Board (REB) review at the RI-MUHC, managing conflict of interest (COI) declarations, and completing mandatory trial registration before the first participant is enrolled.

    **Key points:**
    - All human research studies at the RI-MUHC must receive written REB approval before any study-related activity begins. No exceptions apply, including for amendments to already-approved studies.
    - All submissions are made through **Nagano**, the RI-MUHC's study management system. Paper submissions are not accepted.
    - Conflict of interest declarations are required from the QI/PI and all Sub-Investigators at submission and must be updated if circumstances change during the study.
    - Clinical trials meeting the ICMJE or Health Canada definition must be registered in a recognized registry (e.g., ClinicalTrials.gov) **before** the first participant is enrolled. The registration number must be included in your REB submission and on the ICF.
    - The **REB Approval Tracking Log** (Appendix 1) must be maintained and updated for every REB approval, renewal, and amendment throughout the study.

    **Downloadable forms:**
    - [Appendix 1 — REB Approval Tracking Log](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=249041588157044407)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-025 — Equipment Calibration and Maintenance · v01-Sep-2018 · Levels I–IV">
    This SOP defines requirements for the calibration, maintenance, and monitoring of equipment used in clinical research studies, including refrigerators, freezers, centrifuges, blood pressure monitors, scales, and any other study-specific device. It ensures that measurements taken during a study are accurate and traceable.

    **Key points:**
    - All study-specific equipment must be identified in the Investigator Site File with calibration and maintenance records before the equipment is used for any participant.
    - Temperature-sensitive materials (investigational products, biological specimens) must be stored in monitored equipment. Temperature excursions must be documented, assessed, and reported to the Sponsor.
    - Calibration must be performed at the frequency specified by the manufacturer or the protocol, whichever is more frequent. Calibration records must remain in the ISF for the required retention period.
    - The **Temperature Log** (Appendix 1) must be completed for any storage unit holding temperature-sensitive study materials.

    **Downloadable forms:**
    - [Appendix 1 — Temperature Log](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=105488036820738888)
    - [Appendix 2 — Study Equipment Inventory and Maintenance](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=123007490059292474)
  </Accordion>

## Setup sequence overview

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assess feasibility">
    Complete the Study Feasibility Checklist (SOP-CR-004, Appendix 1) and confirm the site has the necessary capacity, personnel, and infrastructure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit for REB review">
    Prepare and submit your study package through Nagano (SOP-CR-007). Include COI declarations and trial registration details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Obtain all authorizations">
    Receive written REB approval. Obtain Health Canada CTA/ITA/NOL if required. Execute site agreement for industry-sponsored studies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete team setup">
    Build your Task Delegation Log (SOP-CR-002). Ensure all team members complete TalentLMS SOP Reader and Competency Assessment training (SOP-CR-003).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up equipment and site">
    Inventory and document all study equipment. Establish temperature monitoring and calibration schedules (SOP-CR-025).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate in Nagano">
    Confirm all conditions are met and set the Nagano record to "Active" status (SOP-CR-005) before enrolling the first participant.
  </Step>
</Steps>
