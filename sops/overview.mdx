The RI-MUHC Clinical Research Hub maintains a complete set of Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) that govern how clinical research is conducted at our site. These documents set the minimum requirements for Good Clinical Practice (GCP) compliance, regulatory conformance, and participant protection across all study types — from interventional drug trials to retrospective chart reviews.

<Warning>
**Controlled documents — always use the online version.** Do not rely on downloaded or printed copies of any SOP. Only the version on this site is guaranteed to be current. If a version has been updated since you last read it, your printed copy may no longer reflect the approved requirements.
</Warning>

<Note>
**Training requirement — TalentLMS SOP Reader.** Reading SOPs on this site does not satisfy your institutional training requirement. Your first reading of each SOP must be completed through the SOP Reader in TalentLMS, where your acknowledgement is recorded. Only TalentLMS completion counts toward your training record and Task Delegation Log eligibility. See the [SOP Reader guide](/training/talentlms) for instructions on accessing your level-specific course.
</Note>

## How SOPs are organized

The 33+ RI-MUHC clinical research SOPs are grouped into eight topic areas. Each group page lists the relevant SOPs with version information, key points, and direct download links for all appendices and forms.

Every SOP entry shows:
- **SOP number and title** — the unique identifier and full document name
- **Effective date** — the date the current version came into force
- **Training levels** — which study types require this SOP (All, Levels I–IV, Sponsor-Investigator, etc.)
- **Downloadable appendices and forms** — direct links to portal.rimuhc.ca

## Training levels at a glance

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Level I — Drug">
    GCP + Division 5 · Interventional drug, biologic, gene/cell therapy, and radiopharmaceutical trials. Covers all 33 SOPs (excludes SOP-CR-023 NHP and SOP-CR-024 Devices). Competency Assessment certificate valid 2 years.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Level II — Devices">
    GCP + ISO 14155 · Medical device and surgical intervention trials. Covers all 33 SOPs (excludes SOP-CR-018 Drug CTA, SOP-CR-010 IP, and SOP-CR-023 NHP). Competency Assessment certificate valid 2 years.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Level III — NHP">
    Natural health product trials. Covers all 33 SOPs (excludes SOP-CR-018 Drug CTA, SOP-CR-010 IP, and SOP-CR-024 Devices). Competency Assessment certificate valid 2 years.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Level IV — Observational">
    Prospective observational studies, educational and non-invasive interventions. Covers 18 SOPs (SOP-LR path). Competency Assessment certificate valid 5 years.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Level V — Retrospective">
    Retrospective and secondary data studies, previously anonymized biobanked samples. Covers 9 SOPs (SOP-LR path). Competency Assessment certificate valid 5 years.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Sponsor-Investigator">
    Additional obligations for investigators who also act as sponsor. Requires the SOP-CR-100 through 107 series in addition to the applicable Level I–III SOPs.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
