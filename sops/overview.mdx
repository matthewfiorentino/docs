The RI-MUHC Clinical Research SOPs set the minimum requirements for GCP compliance, regulatory conformance, and participant protection across all study types conducted at The Institute. They cover everything from how to structure a research team to how to manage investigational products, report safety events, and close out a study.

Browse SOPs using the sidebar. Each SOP has an overview page with key points and a full document page with the complete text, appendices, and forms.

  **Controlled documents — always use the online version.** Do not rely on downloaded or printed copies. Only the version on this site is guaranteed current. If a version has been updated since you last read it, your printed copy may no longer reflect the approved requirements.
</Warning>

## How the SOPs are organized

The RI-MUHC clinical research SOPs are grouped into three series:

**Core Series (SOP-CR-001 through 026)** — applies to all human research conducted at the RI-MUHC. Organized into topic groups: team and governance, study setup, participants and safety, data and records, investigational products, and oversight.

**Sponsor-Investigator Series (SOP-CR-100 through 107)** — additional obligations for investigators who also act as Sponsor. Required in addition to the applicable Core Series SOPs.

**500 Series** — institutional administrative SOPs covering research privileges and related institutional requirements.

## Training levels

Which SOPs apply to you depends on your study type, not your role. Every member of the Task Delegation Log must be trained at the level required for the study.

| Level | Study type | SOPs required |
|---|---|---|
| Level I | Interventional drug / biologic / gene therapy / radiopharmaceutical | All Core Series SOPs |
| Level II | Medical device / surgical intervention | All Core Series SOPs |
| Level III | Natural health product | All Core Series SOPs |
| Level IV | Prospective observational / non-invasive interventions | 18 SOPs (SOP-LR path) |
| Level V | Retrospective / secondary data / anonymized biosamples | 9 SOPs (SOP-LR path) |
| Sponsor-Investigator | Any study where PI also acts as Sponsor | Applicable level plus SOP-CR-100–107 |
