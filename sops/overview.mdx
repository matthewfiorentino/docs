The RI-MUHC Clinical Research SOPs set the minimum requirements for <Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip> compliance, regulatory conformance, and participant protection across all study types conducted at The Institute. They cover everything from how to structure a research team to how to manage investigational products, report safety events, and close out a study.

<Warning>
  **Controlled documents — always use the online version.** Do not rely on downloaded or printed copies. Only the version on this site is guaranteed current. If a version has been updated since you last read it, your printed copy may no longer reflect the approved requirements.
</Warning>

<Note>
  **Reading this site does not fulfill your training requirement.** Your first reading of each SOP must be completed through the SOP Reader in TalentLMS, where your acknowledgement is recorded. Only TalentLMS completion counts toward your training record and <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip> eligibility. See [TalentLMS](/apps/talentlms) for access instructions.
</Note>

## Training levels

Which SOPs apply to you depends on your study type, not your role. Every member of the Task Delegation Log must be trained at the level required for the study.

| Level | Study type | SOPs required |
|---|---|---|
| Level I | Interventional drug / biologic / gene therapy / radiopharmaceutical | All Core Series SOPs |
| Level II | Medical device / surgical intervention | All Core Series SOPs |
| Level III | Natural health product | All Core Series SOPs |
| Level IV | Prospective observational / non-invasive interventions | 18 SOPs (SOP-LR path) |
| Level V | Retrospective / secondary data / anonymized biosamples | 9 SOPs (SOP-LR path) |
| Sponsor-Investigator | Any study where <Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip> also acts as Sponsor | Applicable level plus [SOP-CR-100](/sops/cr-100)–107 |
