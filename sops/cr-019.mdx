## 1 · Purpose {#purpose}

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the procedure to ensure the
integrity and confidentiality of clinical data collected within the framework of a clinical
study, and the privacy of clinical study participants.

---

## 2 · Scope {#scope}

This SOP is applicable to all clinical studies undertaken at the site, and to those clinical
research personnel responsible for ensuring the security and confidentiality of data, and
participant information.

---

## 3 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

The **Sponsor-Investigator or <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">Qualified Investigator</Tooltip> (QI)/Investigator** is responsible for ensuring that the confidentiality and privacy aspects of the study meet all applicable regulatory, ICH-<Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, sponsor, and local requirements.

Any or all parts of this procedure may be delegated to appropriately trained study team members, but remain the ultimate responsibility of the Sponsor-Investigator or QI/Investigator.

## 5 · Procedure {#procedure}
### 5.1 General confidentiality principles

**5.1.1** Participant data must be kept confidential in accordance with applicable privacy legislation, including:
- Quebec Act respecting Access to documents held by public bodies and the Protection of personal information (RSQ A-2.1)
- Quebec Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector
- Federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
- ICH-GCP E6(R2) section 4.8 (informed consent) and section 2.11 (confidentiality)
- MUHC institutional policies

**5.1.2** All study data must be stored securely. Access to participant data must be restricted to authorized personnel only.

**5.1.3** Participant data shared with Sponsors, monitors, auditors, or regulatory inspectors must be de-identified or shared only as permitted by the signed <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#icf">Informed Consent Form</Tooltip> and applicable law.

### 5.2 Quebec law on nominative information

**5.2.1** A public body may not release nominative (personally identifiable) information without the consent of the person concerned.

**5.2.2** Notwithstanding the foregoing, a public body may release nominative information without consent in the following cases only:
- To a person authorized by the Commission d'accès à l'information (in accordance with RSQ A-2.1, article 59) to use the information for study, research, or statistical purposes
- In the case of intoxications, infections, or other reportable diseases, to the regional director of public health in accordance with the Quebec Public Health Act (LRQ S-2.2, articles 79 to 82)

### 5.3 MUHC Medical Records Services

**5.3.1** MUHC Medical Records Services is responsible for the maintenance of, and access to, all information contained in the health records of users of MUHC health care and services who participate as subjects of research conducted at the MUHC.

**5.3.2** An **MUHC Confidentiality Agreement** (Appendix 1) must be signed and sent to Medical Records Services **prior** to an individual being granted access to patient medical records.

**5.3.3** Medical Records Services must be informed of a participant's enrollment in a clinical study involving an <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">Investigational Product</Tooltip> (drug, natural health product, or medical device) by receiving a copy of the signed ICF with the Confirmation of Participation in Research Form → [SOP-CR-009](/sops/cr-009).

**5.3.4** Medical Records Services must be informed of the conclusion of a participant's involvement in research with an Investigational Product by receiving an electronic copy of the "End of Participation in Research" form → [SOP-CR-009](/sops/cr-009).

**5.3.5** MUHC health records of research subjects must be maintained for a minimum of **25 years of inactivity**, unless otherwise specified. Records may be stored off-site.

### 5.4 MUHC Confidentiality and Security Agreement obligations

All team members who access patient health records or the MUHC network must comply with the MUHC Confidentiality and Security Agreement (Appendix 1), which includes the following obligations:

- **Password protection:** Each individual is responsible for their assigned identification code and password for MUHC Intranet access; passwords must not be shared.
- **Authorized use:** Informational assets must be used only for authorized purposes in support of MUHC-approved activities.
- **Access restriction:** Browsing patient records or accessing records not required for one's duties is strictly prohibited.
- **Non-disclosure:** Patient information and MUHC data must not be revealed or transmitted in any way that could compromise confidentiality or data security.
- **Secure destruction:** Information must be destroyed in a secure manner in accordance with current industry standards once no longer required.
- **Log-off:** Team members must log off workstations when work is completed or when the workstation is left unattended.
- **Incident reporting:** Improper or illegal activities involving informational assets must be reported immediately to one's supervisor or to the Security and Confidentiality coordinator (extension 58484).
- **Audit awareness:** MUHC informational assets will be audited for security reasons.

**5.4.1** Any breach of the Confidentiality and Security Agreement may result in measures deemed appropriate by Human Resources or the Council of Physicians, Dentists and Pharmacists.
