These SOPs establish how the RI-MUHC clinical research program is governed, how research teams must be structured and documented, and what training all team members must complete before participating in study activities. You must be familiar with these SOPs regardless of your role or study type — every training level requires them.

<Warning>
**Controlled documents — always use the online version.** Do not rely on downloaded or printed copies. Your first reading of each SOP must be completed through the SOP Reader on TalentLMS to count toward your training record.
</Warning>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-002 — Research Team Roles and Responsibilities · v19-Jun-2023 · All levels">
    This SOP defines who does what in a clinical research study at the RI-MUHC — from the Sponsor down to third-party vendors. It governs how research teams are structured, what each role is responsible for, and how task delegation must be documented. The **Task Delegation Log** is the central compliance document this SOP governs.

    **Key points:**
    - The **QI/PI** holds ultimate responsibility for all study activities at the site and must hold RI-MUHC Institutional Privileges or Researcher Status before any study begins.
    - No person may perform any study task before they appear on the **Task Delegation Log**, their training is documented, and the QI/PI has confirmed they are qualified.
    - **Fellows and rotating residents** cannot conduct research independently or serve as QI/PI backup. All medical acts they perform must be co-signed by a QI/PI or qualified Sub-Investigator with Privileges.
    - In Quebec, **IP management** (storage, dispensing, accountability) is delegated to a trained pharmacist. The Investigator retains responsibility for ensuring IP is used per the approved protocol.
    - If the **QI/PI changes** during a study, both outgoing and incoming QI/PI must sign the Task Delegation Log, the Sponsor and REB must be notified, and a Nagano amendment (Form F1) must be submitted without delay.

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-003 — Human Research Training · v19-Jun-2023 · All levels">
    This SOP specifies the mandatory training requirements for all personnel involved in RI-MUHC clinical research. It defines what training must be completed before study activities begin, how training is documented, and what constitutes an acceptable training record for GCP and regulatory purposes.

    **Key points:**
    - Training must be completed and documented **before** you perform any study-related task. Being on the Task Delegation Log without a completed training record is a GCP violation.
    - The SOP Reader (TalentLMS) and Competency Assessment are the primary institutional training components. External certifications (ICH-GCP, TCPS2, etc.) are required in addition, not instead of, RI-MUHC institutional training.
    - The **Training Log** (Appendix 2) must be maintained in your Investigator Site File and updated whenever a team member completes new training or a new team member joins.
    - When an SOP is revised, all team members at the relevant level must re-read and re-acknowledge the updated version in TalentLMS and update the Training Log.

    **Downloadable forms:**
    - [Appendix 2 — Training Log](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=51237730353589814)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-500 — Human Research Privileges and Researcher Status · v01-May-2025 · All levels">
    This SOP defines the institutional credentialing requirements that a QI/PI must satisfy before conducting clinical research at the RI-MUHC. It covers the distinction between Institutional Privileges and Researcher Status, how each is obtained and maintained, and what happens when privileges lapse or are not renewed.

    **Key points:**
    - A QI/PI must hold valid RI-MUHC Institutional Privileges or formal Researcher Status before any study-related activity can begin at the site — there are no exceptions and no grace periods.
    - Researcher Status is obtained through the RI-MUHC Office of Research and must be renewed on the schedule set by that office. Lapsed status must be resolved before study activities resume.
    - If a QI/PI's privileges or status lapses during an active study, all study activities must be paused and the Research Hub must be notified immediately.

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP. Credentialing forms are managed through the RI-MUHC Office of Research and the relevant clinical department.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-501 — Notification of Participant's Physician · v01-Sep-2018 · All levels">
    This SOP describes when and how the research team must notify a study participant's family physician or treating specialist about the participant's enrolment in a clinical research study. It covers the circumstances requiring notification, the required content of the notification, and how to handle situations where the participant does not have a primary physician or declines notification.

    **Key points:**
    - For interventional studies, you must make reasonable efforts to notify the participant's treating physician of their enrolment and of any study-related findings that may affect their clinical care.
    - Notification must be documented in the participant's research file. Where the participant objects to physician notification, the objection must be documented and the clinical risks assessed.
    - The forms below provide standardized language for participant notification and for transmitting clinical information to a treating physician.

    **Downloadable forms:**
