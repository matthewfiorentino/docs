Before you can administer an investigational product to a participant, or collect and ship biological specimens, a set of regulatory authorizations and institutional controls must be in place. These SOPs define how investigational drugs, devices, and natural health products are managed at the site, and what Health Canada authorizations are required for Sponsor-Investigator studies. Training level eligibility varies by SOP — check the header of each entry carefully.

<Warning>
**Controlled documents — always use the online version.** Do not rely on downloaded or printed copies. Your first reading of each SOP must be completed through the SOP Reader on TalentLMS to count toward your training record.
</Warning>

## Health Canada authorization types

Before a Sponsor-Investigator can administer any investigational product to a human participant in Canada, the appropriate Health Canada authorization must be obtained. The type of authorization depends on the product.

<Note>
For industry-sponsored trials, the Sponsor holds these authorizations and you receive copies for your ISF. For Sponsor-Investigator studies, you are responsible for obtaining and maintaining the authorization yourself. See [Sponsor-Investigator SOPs](/sops/sponsor-investigator) for the additional obligations this role creates.
</Note>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-010 — Management of Investigational Products · v01-Sep-2018 · Levels I–III">
    This SOP defines the requirements for receiving, storing, dispensing, returning, and destroying investigational products (IPs) at the RI-MUHC site. In Quebec, IP management is the responsibility of a trained pharmacist to whom the QI/PI delegates this function; the investigator retains overall accountability.

    **Key points:**
    - IP must be received, logged, and stored according to the Sponsor's specifications and the study protocol before it can be used for any participant.
    - All IP dispensing must be documented with participant identifier, amount dispensed, lot number, expiry date, and date/time. Dispensing records must match accountability records exactly.
    - Unused, returned, or expired IP must be segregated, documented, and either returned to the Sponsor or destroyed per the Sponsor's instructions and applicable regulations. Do not dispose of IP through general waste.
    - Temperature excursions affecting stored IP must be documented immediately, assessed for impact on product integrity, and reported to the Sponsor. Do not administer IP that has experienced an unqualified excursion without Sponsor authorization.
    - IP accountability records must be available for inspection at all times and filed in the ISF.

    <Info>
      No downloadable appendices are associated with this SOP. IP accountability documentation is typically provided by the Sponsor. Site pharmacy records serve as the primary accountability log.
    </Info>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-011 — Management of Biological Specimens · v01-Sep-2018 · Levels I–IV">
    This SOP defines the requirements for collecting, processing, labeling, storing, shipping, and tracking biological specimens collected during clinical research studies at the RI-MUHC. It covers blood, urine, tissue, and other biological samples, whether collected for central lab analysis or local processing.

    **Key points:**
    - All specimen collection, processing, and shipping must follow the Sponsor's laboratory manual and the protocol exactly. Deviations (wrong tube, wrong processing time, incorrect storage temperature) must be documented and reported to the Sponsor.
    - Specimens must be labeled with the participant's study ID (never name or MUHC health record number), visit code, collection date/time, and any other Sponsor-required identifiers.
    - Chain of custody must be maintained from collection through shipment. The **Human Sample Collection, Processing and Shipping Log** (Appendix 1) must be completed for every specimen batch.
    - Biological specimens being shipped internationally are subject to IATA regulations and may require dangerous goods certification for certain biological categories. Contact the Research Hub for shipping support.

    **Downloadable forms:**
    - [Appendix 1 — Human Sample Collection, Processing and Shipping Log](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=105481077864637878)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-018 — Clinical Trial Application (Drugs) · v01-Sep-2018 · Level I only">
    This SOP defines the process for preparing and submitting a Clinical Trial Application (CTA) to Health Canada for an investigational drug, biologic, or radiopharmaceutical. It applies only to Sponsor-Investigator studies at Level I. For industry-sponsored trials, the Sponsor holds the CTA and provides a copy for your ISF.

    **Key points:**
    - A CTA must be submitted to Health Canada and a 30-day review period must elapse (or a No Objection Letter received) before the first participant is administered the investigational product.
    - CTA preparation requires a complete Investigator's Brochure (IB), protocol, ICF, REB approval, and supporting Chemistry and Manufacturing Controls (CMC) documentation.
    - The **Drug Clinical Trial Application Checklist** (Appendix 1) must be used to verify that all required components are included before submission.
    - Amendments to an approved CTA (Protocol Amendments, IB updates, safety updates) must be submitted to Health Canada. Some amendments require a 30-day review; others can be implemented after notification.

    **Downloadable forms:**
    - [Appendix 1 — Drug Clinical Trial Application Checklist](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=105486240523703628)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-023 — Clinical Trial Application (Natural Health Products) · v01-Sep-2018 · Levels I–III">
    This SOP defines the process for preparing and submitting a Clinical Trial Application to Health Canada for an investigational natural health product (NHP). It applies to Sponsor-Investigator studies where the investigational product is classified as a natural health product under the Natural Health Products Regulations.

    **Key points:**
    - An NHP CTA must be submitted to Health Canada and authorization received before any participant is administered the investigational NHP.
    - The NHP CTA process differs from the drug CTA process. Key submission components include: a Product License Application or evidence of product quality, the study protocol, ICF, and REB approval.
    - The **NHP Clinical Trial Checklist** (Appendix 1) must be used to verify that all required components are included before submission.
    - For NHPs that already hold a product licence, a simplified authorization pathway (Notice of Authorization) may be available. Consult the Research Hub regulatory team for guidance.

    **Downloadable forms:**
    - [Appendix 1 — NHP Clinical Trial Checklist](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=105487408579727514)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-024 — Investigational Testing Authorization (Medical Devices) · v01-Sep-2018 · Levels I–II">
    This SOP defines the process for preparing and submitting an Investigational Testing Authorization (ITA) application to Health Canada for an investigational medical device. It applies to Sponsor-Investigator studies where the device does not hold a current Health Canada Device Licence or is being tested for an indication outside its licensed use.

    **Key points:**
    - An ITA must be obtained from Health Canada before any participant is exposed to the investigational device. Health Canada has 45 days to review an ITA application.
    - ITA submission requirements include: device description and technical documentation, study protocol, ICF, REB approval, and investigator qualifications.
    - All devices must be tracked using the **Medical Device Accountability Log** (Appendix 1). This log records device receipt, assignment to participants, return, and final disposition.
    - The **Medical Device ITA Checklist** (Appendix 2) must be used to verify that all ITA submission components are complete before filing.

    **Downloadable forms:**
    - [Appendix 1 — Medical Device Accountability Log](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=105488324837746166)
    - [Appendix 2 — Medical Device ITA Checklist](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=105487744873734143)
  </Accordion>
