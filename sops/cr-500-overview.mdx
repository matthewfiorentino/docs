# Human Research Privileges and Researcher Status

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-500_06</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 06</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 May 2025</Badge> <Badge color="blue">QI/PI · Sponsor-Investigator</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the process for QI/PIs to obtain MSSS-required Privileges or Researcher Status from the RI-MUHC in order to conduct human research at the MUHC/RI-MUHC. Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status are mandatory prior to accessing training and conducting any human research study. This SOP applies to Qualified Investigators, Principal Investigators, Sub-Investigators delegated QI/PI backup tasks, and Sponsor-Investigators.
</Note>

## Key points

- **Human Research Privileges** are required for MDs and Dentists listed on the MUHC Council of Physicians, Dentists and Pharmacists (CPDP). Privileges are required for QI/PIs conducting Health Canada-regulated studies with an investigational product (drug, medical device, or natural health product).
- **Researcher Status** is required for non-MDs/Dentists (e.g., PhDs, nurses, physiotherapists, psychologists, pharmacists) who wish to be a PI conducting research that does not require medical oversight. Researcher Status **does not** permit conducting research involving human medical oversight.
- **Fellows, residents, and medical students** may not conduct research independently and are not eligible for Privileges or Researcher Status. They cannot be listed as QI/PI or QI/PI backup.
- All other research team members listed on the Task Delegation Log (other than the QI/PI and Sub-Investigator backup) are **not** required to obtain Privileges or Researcher Status.
- To apply, log onto **TalentLMS** (via the RI Portal → Applications → TalentLMS) and complete the Privileges/Researcher Status questionnaire. Eligibility typically requires a McGill Faculty appointment; others are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
- Upon acceptance, the **commitment certificate** is automatically generated in TalentLMS and is valid for **3 years**. File a copy in the ISF of each study.
- A **reminder email** is automatically sent 30 days before expiration for renewal via TalentLMS.
- Privileges/Researcher Status may be **revoked** by the RI-MUHC ED/CSO for misconduct, fraud, or loss of CPDP rights.
- **MNI exception:** MNI PIs conducting research at the MNI follow McGill/MNI procedures. If conducting research at other MUHC sites, MNI PIs must follow RI-MUHC SOPs.
