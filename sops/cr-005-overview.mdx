## Key points

- Study initiation occurs **only after** Health Canada approval (<Tooltip tip="Letter from Health Canada confirming a CTA is satisfactory and authorizing the sponsor to proceed." headline="No Objection Letter (NOL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nol">NOL</Tooltip> or <Tooltip tip="Health Canada authorization to conduct a clinical investigation of a medical device. The device equivalent of a CTA." headline="Investigational Testing Authorization (ITA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ita">ITA</Tooltip>, where applicable), full REB approval, and <Tooltip tip="Institutional authorization allowing a study to proceed at the RI-MUHC. Issued after ethics approval and feasibility review." headline="MUHC Authorization" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#muhc-auth">MUHC Authorization</Tooltip> have all been received. These documents must be filed in the <Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip> and/or <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>.
- The **<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI and all team members with delegated protocol tasks** should be present at the SIV. When attendance is not possible, document the reason and provide subsequent documented training.
- For all drug studies where MUHC Pharmacy Services will be used, **Pharmacy must be informed and invited to the SIV** (or a separate pharmacy SIV must be arranged).
- All team members trained at the SIV must be **entered on the <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip>** before performing any study task. The QI/PI's signature on the log confirms training and qualification.
- **Participant enrollment must not begin** until the site has received the Sponsor's/Sponsor-Investigator's Go Letter confirming site activation. The Go Letter must be filed in both the TMF and ISF.
- SIV participation must be **documented** — attendance sign-in sheets (or virtual meeting attendee lists for remote SIVs) must be retained in the ISF and TMF.
- For **Sponsor-Investigator and Monitor templates** (SIV confirmation letters, monitoring logs, SIV report, Go Letter), see [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013).
