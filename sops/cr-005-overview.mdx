# Study Initiation and Activation

<Note>
This SOP covers all the steps that must be completed between receiving final REB approval and enrolling the first participant. It defines what constitutes "site activation" and documents the Site Initiation Visit (SIV) process — including preparation, conduct, and the Go Letter requirement before participant enrollment may begin.

## Key points

- Study initiation occurs **only after** Health Canada approval (NOL or ITA, where applicable), full REB approval, and MUHC Authorization have all been received. These documents must be filed in the TMF and/or ISF.
- The **QI/PI and all team members with delegated protocol tasks** should be present at the SIV. When attendance is not possible, document the reason and provide subsequent documented training.
- For all drug studies where MUHC Pharmacy Services will be used, **Pharmacy must be informed and invited to the SIV** (or a separate pharmacy SIV must be arranged).
- All team members trained at the SIV must be **entered on the Task Delegation Log** before performing any study task. The QI/PI's signature on the log confirms training and qualification.
- **Participant enrollment must not begin** until the site has received the Sponsor's/Sponsor-Investigator's Go Letter confirming site activation. The Go Letter must be filed in both the TMF and ISF.
- SIV participation must be **documented** — attendance sign-in sheets (or virtual meeting attendee lists for remote SIVs) must be retained in the ISF and TMF.
- For **Sponsor-Investigator and Monitor templates** (SIV confirmation letters, monitoring logs, SIV report, Go Letter), see SOP-CR-013.
