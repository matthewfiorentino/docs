# Study Monitoring and Communication

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-013_08</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 08</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 19 Jun 2023</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Levels I–IV</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the monitoring and communication requirements for clinical research studies at the RI-MUHC/MUHC. It covers development of the monitoring plan, preparation for and conduct of monitoring visits (feasibility, <Tooltip tip="Monitoring visit to train the site team on the protocol and confirm site readiness, after REB approval and MUHC Authorization." headline="Site Initiation Visit (SIV)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#siv">SIV</Tooltip>, ongoing, close-out), OACIS access setup for external monitors, and documentation standards for all monitoring communications. Read this SOP together with [SOP-CR-004](/sops/cr-004) (feasibility), [SOP-CR-005](/sops/cr-005) (SIV), and [SOP-CR-016](/sops/cr-016) (close-out).

## Key points

- The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator must develop a written, risk-based **Monitoring Plan (MP)** before the study begins. The MP describes the monitoring strategy (on-site, remote, or combined), responsibilities of all parties, rationale, and processes for addressing non-compliance. → Appendix 3.
- A **monitoring report** must be written and a **follow-up Monitoring Letter** sent to the site after every monitoring visit or communication. The <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI must initial and date every monitoring letter to document medical oversight. File in the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>.
- The **Monitor is the Sponsor-Investigator's team member**, not the site team member — the Monitor is not listed on the site <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip> and must remain independent of site operations.
- The QI/PI must be available at the time of the monitoring visit. Ensure all documentation (CRFs, ISF, pharmacy records, deviation log) is up-to-date before each visit.
- Protocol deviations observed by the Monitor must be reported to the Sponsor within **24 hours**; violations are reported to the <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> within **7 days**; exceptions may be submitted at Annual Continuing Review.
- External monitors require temporary restricted OACIS access from MUHC Medical Records. Requests must be submitted **at least 5 working days** in advance with a signed Confidentiality and Security Agreement, DPS Chart Request form, QI/PI letter, and copies of REB and <Tooltip tip="Institutional authorization allowing a study to proceed at the RI-MUHC. Issued after ethics approval and feasibility review." headline="MUHC Authorization" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#muhc-auth">MUHC Authorization</Tooltip> letters.
- No study document with patient identifiers may leave the institution. De-identify documents before providing copies to the Monitor.
- The **Go Letter** from the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, issued after the SIV report is approved, provides written authorization for the site to commence the study. → Appendix 7.
