# Investigator Study Files and Essential Documents

This SOP describes the set-up, maintenance, archiving, and destruction of Investigator Site Files (ISF) and Trial Master Files (TMF) for clinical research studies at the RI-MUHC. It covers essential document management from study initiation through long-term archiving at Iron Mountain.

## Key points

- The **QI/PI** maintains the Investigator Site File (ISF); the **Sponsor-Investigator** maintains the Trial Master File (TMF). For single-site Sponsor-Investigator studies, the ISF and TMF are one and the same. For multi-site studies, the site ISF mirrors the Sponsor's TMF except for a few documents.
- The ISF must be created as soon as the first Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator contact occurs or a protocol outline is available. Create a Training Log and Task Delegation Log first; then build out the ISF per Appendix 4 structure.
- All documents with **wet signatures** (original penned signatures) must be kept in paper: Agreements, Protocol and IB signature pages, ICFs, paper CRFs, and all documents initialled and dated by the QI/PI, team member, or participant.
- Until the RI-MUHC has a validated 21 CFR Part 11-compliant document management system, the primary ISF/TMF must be paper-based in binders. Electronic backups (without participant source data) may be kept on a share drive.
- The QI/PI must initial and date all essential documents pertaining to patient safety and data integrity in a timely fashion, including safety reports (local and external SAE/SUSAR), Sponsor newsletters, protocol amendments, and procedure clarifications.
- **Retention periods**: 15 years for Health Canada-regulated studies (all participants, including screen failures); 7 years from publication date for non-regulated studies. The EMA requires 25 years — confirm Sponsor requirements in the study budget before study start. The RI-MUHC recommends Iron Mountain for long-term archiving.
- All essential documents must be audit/inspection-ready at all times. For a For-Cause Inspection, records must be available immediately. For routine inspections, 7–14 calendar days notice is standard.
- Do not destroy any study files without written authorization from the QI/PI and Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator. Retain destruction documentation on site.
