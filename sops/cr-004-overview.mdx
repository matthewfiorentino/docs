# Protocol Feasibility and Site Selection

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-004_08</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 08</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 19 Jun 2023</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Levels I–IV</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP defines the process for evaluating whether the RI-MUHC site has the capacity, resources, and qualified personnel to conduct a proposed clinical study. Feasibility assessment must be completed and documented before any commitment is made to a Sponsor or before the study is submitted for <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> review.

## Key points

- **Complete the Study Feasibility Checklist (Appendix 1) before agreeing to participate in any study.** The checklist covers scientific/ethical aspects, participant population, personnel, resources, facilities, and financial considerations across 7 parts. A completed checklist must be filed in the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">Investigator Site File</Tooltip>.
- A **confidentiality agreement** between the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator and the <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI should be signed and dated before receiving any confidential study information such as the protocol. Legal review is often required before signing.
- Assess **IT infrastructure** needs early — contact IT via "IT rescue" on the RI-MUHC Portal for evaluation of any study-specific software and assessment of e-connectivity requirements (21 CFR Part 11 compliance for electronic systems).
- Identify **secure, temperature-controlled storage** for investigational products and ensure a qualified <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">IP</Tooltip> manager and destruction procedures are in place before committing.
- For industry-sponsored studies, the Sponsor's own feasibility questionnaire may supplement but does **not replace** the RI-MUHC checklist.
- Once the SFV is conducted and the study is deemed feasible, **a signed and dated copy of the site agreement** must be kept with the essential study documentation.
- Do not commit to **enrolment targets** the site cannot realistically meet. Document any feasibility concerns in writing before signing the site agreement.
