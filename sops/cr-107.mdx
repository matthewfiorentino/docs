## Key points

- **Database lock criteria:** all data entered, all queries resolved, all protocol deviations documented, all required data validation checks completed, and the Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) finalized → [SOP-CR-101](/sops/cr-101).
- The database lock must be documented with a **formal lock memo** signed by the data manager and the <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI.
- After lock, the database must be made **read-only**. Any post-lock data correction requires a formal unlock procedure with full documentation of the reason, correction, and re-lock.
- The **locked database, audit trail, validation documentation, data dictionary, and DMP** must all be archived together for the regulatory retention period (**25 years** under Canadian <Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip> regulations for Health Canada-regulated studies) → [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015).
- Maintain the archived database in a format that allows future access for audits, inspections, and follow-up analyses. Use file formats that will remain readable over the retention period.
- Database lock is a prerequisite for final analysis; no statistical analysis using the final dataset may begin before lock is confirmed and documented.

## 1 · Purpose {#purpose}

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the procedures for locking,
unlocking, and archiving a study database.

---

## 2 · Scope {#scope}

This SOP is applicable to all clinical or research studies undertaken at the site, and to
those research and Data Management personnel responsible for data management,
unless otherwise stated in the study contract or Data Management Plan.

---

## 3 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

The **Sponsor-Investigator or <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/Investigator and Data Manager** are responsible for ensuring that the database lock and archiving processes meet all applicable regulatory, ICH-<Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, Sponsor, and local requirements.

Any or all parts of this procedure may be delegated to appropriately trained study team members, but remain the ultimate responsibility of the Sponsor, Sponsor-Investigator, and/or QI/Investigator.

## 5 · Procedure {#procedure}
### 5.1 Pre-lock criteria

**5.1.1** Before initiating database lock, confirm that all of the following criteria are met:
- All participant data are entered and complete
- All outstanding data queries are resolved and closed
- All protocol deviations are documented on the Protocol/GCP/SOP Deviation Log
- All required data validation checks (range checks, cross-field consistency, completeness) are completed and all identified issues resolved → [SOP-CR-105](/sops/cr-105)
- The Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP) is finalized, dated, and signed → [SOP-CR-101](/sops/cr-101)
- The final audit trail review is complete
- A final data extract has been reviewed by the data manager and QI/PI

### 5.2 Database lock procedure

**5.2.1** Confirm all pre-lock criteria are met and documented.

**5.2.2** Prepare a formal **database lock memo** documenting:
- Study name and protocol number
- Database version and date of lock
- Confirmation that all pre-lock criteria have been met (with references to supporting documentation)
- Name and date of lock authorization (QI/PI signature)
- Name and date of lock execution (data manager)

**5.2.3** The lock memo must be signed and dated by the **data manager** and the **QI/PI**.

**5.2.4** Change the database status to **read-only** (no further data entry, editing, or deletion permitted) and document the date and time of lock.

**5.2.5** Notify the Sponsor-Investigator and statistician that the database is locked and the final analysis dataset is available.

**5.2.6** No statistical analysis using the final dataset may begin before database lock is confirmed and documented.

### 5.3 Post-lock data corrections (unlock procedure)

**5.3.1** If a data error is identified after database lock that requires correction, initiate a formal unlock procedure:
- Document the reason for the data correction in a memo approved by the QI/PI
- Request authorization for unlock from the QI/PI (and, if applicable, from the Sponsor)
- Perform only the specific, documented correction
- Re-lock the database following the lock procedure in section 5.2
- Update the lock memo with the reason for unlock, correction made, and re-lock date

**5.3.2** Post-lock corrections must be minimized. If a post-lock correction changes any primary outcome data, document the impact on the analysis and update the SAP as needed.

### 5.4 Database archiving

**5.4.1** After database lock and completion of the final analysis, archive the database and all associated documentation together. The archived set must include:
- The locked database (all tables and fields)
- Complete audit trail
- Validation documentation (UAT records) → [SOP-CR-104](/sops/cr-104)
- Data dictionary (field names, variable descriptions, coding conventions)
- Data Management Plan (DMP) → [SOP-CR-103](/sops/cr-103)
- Database lock memo
- Any unlock/re-lock documentation
- Final analysis datasets and programming code

**5.4.2** Retain the archived database for the required regulatory retention period:
- **25 years** for Health Canada-regulated studies
- **7 years** from publication for non-regulated studies
- → [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015) for full archiving requirements and timelines

**5.4.3** Archive in a format that will remain readable over the retention period. Include a data dictionary that documents all field names, variable definitions, and coding conventions to enable future interpretation.

**5.4.4** Store archives in a secure location with controlled access. Maintain a record of the archive location in the <Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip>.

**5.4.5** Inform the Sponsor-Investigator of the archive location → [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015) section 5.3.
