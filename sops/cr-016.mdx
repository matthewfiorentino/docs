## 1.0 Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the processes to be followed by the site prior to, during, and following study Close-Out, by a Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator or their qualified delegated Monitor. The same study Close-Out procedures should be followed if the Sponsor or Sponsor- Investigator discontinues a study in its entirety, or at an individual site.

## 2.0 Scope

This procedure concerns the research community (employees, sponsor-investigators, investigators, physicians, management, consultants, students, volunteers or other persons) involved in close-out procedures for human research studies at the MUHC/RI-MUHC. Sponsor-Investigators and Monitors should consult [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) on Monitoring regarding a monitor’s close-out activities.

## 3.0 Responsibilities

The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator and Qualified Investigator (<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>)/Principal Investigator (<Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip>) are responsible for ensuring that the study Close-Out meets all of the applicable regulatory, International Council on Harmonisation (ICH) Good Clinical Practice (GCP)/ International Standards Organization (ISO) Good Clinical Practice (GCP), Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator, and local requirements. Site procedures may be delegated to appropriately trained human research team members, but remain the ultimate responsibility of the Sponsor, Sponsor-Investigator, or QI/PI, as defined in the GCP guidelines and regulations.

## 5.0 Procedure

<a id="5-1"></a>

### 5.1 Site preparation for the sponsor’s study close-out visit

<a id="5-1-1"></a>**5.1.1** Review the Investigator Site File (<Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>) for completeness, resolve any outstanding items remaining and document any discrepancies (e.g., by memo, note to file or Deviation Log and CAPA form, as applicable).

<a id="5-1-2"></a>**5.1.2** Confirm that the last study participant has completed the last scheduled study visit, and the associated Case Report Forms (CRFs) have been completed.

<a id="5-1-3"></a>**5.1.3** Ensure that other remaining CRFs are accurately completed, and available for final review/monitoring, along with the corresponding source documents.

<a id="5-1-4"></a>**5.1.4** Ensure that any outstanding <Tooltip tip="Document designed to record all protocol-required information to be reported to the sponsor for each participant." headline="Case Report Form (CRF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#crf">CRF</Tooltip> corrections, additions, or deletions are made, dated, explained (if necessary), initialed by the QI/PI or delegate, and ready for review, along with the corresponding source documents.

<a id="5-1-5"></a>**5.1.5** Review the monitoring follow-up letters and ensure that all data queries received, to date, have been resolved, all monitoring follow-up letters are on file (corresponding to the Monitoring Log visit dates) and all monitoring follow-up letters are initialed and dated by the QI/PI to show QI/PI oversight.

<a id="5-1-6"></a>**5.1.6** Arrange for the QI/PI to review, sign and date the completed CRFs and initial and date the monitoring follow-up letters, if not already done.

<a id="5-1-7"></a>**5.1.7** Review the study budget and, if necessary, pay any amounts owing to participants, site(s), etc., in accordance with the contract.

<a id="5-1-8"></a>**5.1.8** Ensure all relevant departments are notified of the upcoming study close-out visit e.g., Pharmacy.

<a id="5-1-9"></a>**5.1.9** Ensure that all logs are complete, including the <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip>, which should now document all team member end dates (except for the QI/PI and Clinical Research Coordinator (<Tooltip tip="The team member who handles day-to-day administration and coordination of the clinical trial." headline="Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#crc">CRC</Tooltip>) answering to queries) and QI/PI signature and date to indicate undelegation of team members.

<a id="5-2"></a>

### 5.2 Study close-out visit (COV) by Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/monitor

<a id="5-2-1"></a>**5.2.1** Compile all study documents needed for the Monitor to complete the close-out procedures. Note: Sponsor-Investigators should refer to the COV Monitoring report template in [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013).

<a id="5-2-2"></a>**5.2.2** Facilitate the Monitor’s Close-Out visit, as the Monitor verifies the following items, as/if required:

- Adverse events (AEs)/ serious adverse events (SAEs) and adverse drug reactions /serious adverse drug reactions (ADR/SADRs) and MDIs (if applicable) recorded in the source documents and CRFs;
- SAEs, SADRs and MDIs (if applicable) reported to the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> and/or regulatory agencies (if Sponsor-Investigator). Refer to [SOP-CR-012](/sops/cr-012);
- Expedited external reports (e.g., reportable Suspect Unexpected Serious Adverse Event Reports, SUSARs or Medical Device Incident, MDI reports reported by the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator), are initialed and dated by the QI/PI and submitted to the local Research Ethics Board (REB). Refer to [SOP-CR-012](/sops/cr-012) and [SOP-CR-026](/sops/cr-026);
- CRFs completed in accordance with the source documents;
- Corrections completed to the source documents and/or CRFs;
- Outstanding questions from previous monitoring, verification/audit or inspections completed;
- Essential study documents complete and;
- All study documents requiring the QI/PI documented oversight have been signed/initialed and dated.

<a id="5-2-3"></a>**5.2.3** Respond to final queries regarding source data verification and CRFs.

<a id="5-2-4"></a>**5.2.4** Discuss with the Monitor any ongoing follow-up activities that may be required, such as:

- Outstanding issues or action items arising from the Close-Out visit;
- Participant follow-up requirements, including post-study SADRs/SAEs from MDIs Note: Follow-up procedures for ongoing adverse events (AEs), adverse drug reactions (ADRs), serious adverse drug reactions (SADRs) and serious adverse events (SAEs) from MDIs must be in compliance with the protocol and <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#icf">ICF</Tooltip>;
- Procedures for handling data clarifications that may arise;
- Record retention/storage requirements.

<a id="5-2-5"></a>**5.2.5** Once all queries are resolved and finalized the eCRF should then be locked and signed by the QI/PI.

<a id="5-3"></a>

### 5.3 Reconciliation and return of study-related supplies

<a id="5-3-1"></a>**5.3.1** Ensure completion of the accountability log(s) for all investigational products (<Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#ip">IP</Tooltip>) used and unused. Retain this documentation with the essential study documents for the required archiving period (15 years for Health Canada regulated studies and 7 years for non-Health Canada regulated studies) as per [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015).

<a id="5-3-2"></a>**5.3.2** Arrange the return or destruction of all study-related supplies (e.g., any unused test kits, hard copy forms or recruitment materials), including the IP, with the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator.

<a id="5-3-3"></a>**5.3.3** Arrange the destruction of the IP by the local pharmacy, (if requested). Ensure that the Pharmacy SOP destruction procedures are followed. Provide destruction documentation to the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, with a copy for the Investigator Site File (ISF).

<a id="5-3-4"></a>**5.3.4** Return/handle the randomization code envelopes or lists to the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, according to the protocol, or other written instructions.

<a id="5-3-5"></a>**5.3.5** Return unused CRFs and other used/unused study-related paper/electronic material to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator at the end of the study, unless instructed to destroy them on-site, in accordance with local procedures for destruction of confidential documents. Refer to [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015).

<a id="5-3-6"></a>**5.3.6** Return any study equipment and supplies to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/vendors, unless otherwise arranged.

<a id="5-3-7"></a>**5.3.7** Return all laboratory specimens (blood, tissues, etc.) to the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator for evaluation and storage, unless otherwise described in the protocol, or other written instructions.

<a id="5-4"></a>

### 5.4 Post study close-out visit (COV) activities

<a id="5-4-1"></a>**5.4.1** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor writes a COV Report. Sponsor-Investigators/Monitors should refer to [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for a template of a COV Report.

<a id="5-4-2"></a>**5.4.2** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor sends a COV Notification which consists of a summary of key discussions and conclusions made during the study COV to the site for filing in the ISF. Sponsor-Investigators/Monitors should refer to [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for a template of a Monitor’s Close-Out Notification to sites.

<a id="5-4-3"></a>**5.4.3** Resolve any subsequent data queries that arise.

<a id="5-4-4"></a>**5.4.4** Request information from the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator, such as:

- Anticipated timing to receive any outstanding payments;
- Anticipated timing for availability of the final report;
- Publication plans;
- Feedback regarding site performance in the study and;
- Plans for audits or knowledge of upcoming regulatory inspections.

<a id="5-4-5"></a>**5.4.5** The QI/PI should inform the Sponsor/Sponsor- Investigator/Monitor, REB, and regulatory authorities, if applicable, of any unexpected SAEs or MDIs (reasonably thought to be associated with the IP), following the study closure.

<a id="5-4-6"></a>**5.4.6** Check the ISF for completeness, and prepare to archive the files according to regulatory requirements, sponsor agreement, and local policies (usually after database closure). Refer to [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015) regarding archiving.

<a id="5-4-7"></a>**5.4.7** Inform Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator of the file archive location (e.g., Iron Mountain). Refer to [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015) for more information on archiving and timelines.

<a id="5-4-8"></a>**5.4.8** Ensure that the archiving service is aware of the required storage period.

<a id="5-4-9"></a>**5.4.9** Once all post close-out activities have been reconciled and all team members have been undelegated on the Task Delegation Log, ensure that the REB is informed of study close-out via the appropriate form in the study submission platform (e.g., [Nagano](/apps/nagano/overview) Closure of Research Project form). Refer to section [5.5](#5-5). The site should send a copy of the correspondence with the REB to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor.

<a id="5-4-10"></a>**5.4.10** The site should inform all members of the human research team and all relevant departments (e.g., pharmacy, laboratory, diagnostic imaging) of the study closure.

<a id="5-4-11"></a>**5.4.11** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator should ensure that the Clinical Study Report (CSR), required for all human studies, is written after data are analyzed. For the purposes of writing a CSR refer to the ICH-GCP guidance document, ICH E3. Distribute the CSR summary, when available (produce interim reports, if interim analysis was performed). Sites may receive the CSR from either the Sponsor or the Clinical Research Organization (CRO).

<a id="5-4-12"></a>**5.4.12** Publication of study results (as per study contract or other agreement): the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator confirms that all participating sites are closed, and all data analyzed. The site should inform the human research team and participants about study results and publications, once available.

<a id="5-4-13"></a>**5.4.13** If a Clinical Trial Application (CTA) or Investigational Testing Authorization (<Tooltip tip="Health Canada's authorization for a manufacturer or importer to sell a Class II, III or IV medical device to qualified investigators for testing." headline="Investigational Testing Authorization (ITA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#ita">ITA</Tooltip>) was submitted to Health Canada, the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator should inform the agency that the study has ended.

<a id="5-5"></a>

### 5.5 Reporting completion or early termination/discontinuation to the MUHC REB

<a id="5-5-1"></a>**5.5.1** As per [SOP-CR-026](/sops/cr-026), the completion or early termination/discontinuation of research is a change in human research activity that must be reported to the REB. Submission of the Closure of Research Project form allows the REB to close its files in addition to obtaining information that may be used in the evaluation and approval of related studies.

<a id="5-5-2"></a>**5.5.2** Determining when a human research study can be “Closed” in Nagano: The Sponsor-Investigator or QI/PI should submit a Closure of Research Project form to the REB when

- there is no further participant involvement at the site;
- participants are no longer exposed to immediate research risks;
- all data collection is complete;
- all study supplies and IP have been returned/destroyed on site (if applicable) and;

- the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator, Investigator and Monitor’s Close-Out activities, if applicable, have been completed. Refer to [Appendix 1](#appendix-1) Decision Tree regarding submitting the Closure Report to the REB which summarizes the below points:

For a research project that is “Multi-centrique” (The Framework, where any research project conducted at more than one public institution in the RSSS undergoes only one ethics review by a research ethics board in the RSSS, in the province of Quebec):

- If the MUHC is the lead site (and MUHC REB is reviewing), each participating RSSS site in Quebec is responsible for completing the Closure of Research Project form in the study submission platform (e.g., Nagano). Only once all closure reports for all RSSS centers in Quebec have been submitted, should the MUHC lead site submit their study Closure of Research form. Once the lead site submits the form, the sites can no longer create new forms or submit additional documents.

- If the Lead site is from the RI-MUHC/MUHC with participating sites across Quebec and Canada, all other participating sites must submit their closure reports to their respective REBs prior to the Lead site submitting the Closure of Research form.

- If the RI-MUHC/MUHC site is a participating site (not lead site); the MUHC site is responsible for submitting the Closure of Research Project form for their site using the lead site’s form in a timely manner for external REB acknowledgement.

If the Research Project is not “Multi-centrique” (only 1 Quebec site whether or not there are other sites across Canada):

- Submit a Closure of Research Project form in the study submission platform (e.g., Nagano) as per [SOP-CR-026](/sops/cr-026).

- If the Sponsor-Investigator is from the RI-MUHC/MUHC with participating sites across Canada, other participating sites must submit their closure reports to their respective REBs prior to the Lead site submitting the Closure of Research form.

The responsible REB office personnel will review the Closure of Research Project form and request any outstanding information, clarification or documentation from the Sponsor-Investigator or QI/PI, if needed.

- The REB Chair or designee or designated member of the REB Support Staff will review the submission and issue an acknowledgement of receipt to the Sponsor-Investigator or QI/PI. The evaluation status will change from “Approved” to “Closed” within the study submission platform (e.g., Nagano).
- Once a research project is “Closed” with the REB, no further submissions for that research will be permitted. At this point it is no longer possible to open a new form in Nagano, however, if required, the Sponsor-Investigator or QI/PI may submit relevant documents for acknowledgement via Nagano Discussion for traceability and, if applicable, further investigation and/or action may be undertaken by the REB.

- If the Sponsor requests additional data following the closure of the research project, a request for approval shall be made to the REB via Nagano Discussion and the conditions of this request will be determined at the time of the review.

<a id="5-5-3"></a>**5.5.3** Certain elements should be addressed in the Closure of Research Project form. Refer to the appropriate study submission platform (e.g., Nagano) form.

<a id="5-5-4"></a>**5.5.4** For studies with investigational product (drug, medical device or natural health product), as per [SOP-CR-009](/sops/cr-009), an End of Participation in Research Form must be completed for every participant and sent to Medical Records for scanning into the patient’s electronic medical chart (e.g., [OACIS](/apps/oacis)). The original form is filed with source documents.

<a id="5-5-5"></a>**5.5.5** Once the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator has written the Clinical Study Report (CSR), this report should also be submitted to the REB. In most cases, the study status in the study submission platform (e.g., Nagano) will already be set to “Closed”, however the CSR can be submitted via Nagano Discussions.

<a id="5-6"></a>

### 5.6 Verification/audit or regulatory inspection readiness

<a id="5-6-1"></a>**5.6.1** Even after the study close-out, the study may be selected by an auditor or regulatory authority for inspection. The study team should have a plan on how the auditor/inspector may have access to study data and essential documents after the study closure.

<a id="5-6-2"></a>**5.6.2** Prepare for Sponsor audits or regulatory inspections, as required. Refer to [SOP-CR-017](/sops/cr-017).

<a id="5-6-3"></a>**5.6.3** If the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator is informed of an upcoming audit or inspection, they must inform the QI/PI, and research team.

<a id="5-6-4"></a>**5.6.4** If there is notification of an audit or inspection received by QI/PI, they must inform the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator and in the case of a regulatory inspection, should inform the REB and the Quality Assurance Department (if applicable).
