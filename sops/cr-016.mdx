## 1 · Purpose {#purpose}

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the processes to be
followed by the site prior to, during, and following study Close-Out, by a Sponsor
or Sponsor-Investigator or their qualified delegated Monitor. The same study
Close-Out procedures should be followed if the Sponsor or Sponsor- Investigator
discontinues a study in its entirety, or at an individual site.

---

## 2 · Scope {#scope}

This procedure concerns the research community (employees, sponsor-
investigators, investigators, physicians, management, consultants, students,
volunteers or other persons) involved in close-out procedures for human
research studies at the MUHC/RI-MUHC. Sponsor-Investigators and Monitors
should consult SOP-CR-013 on Monitoring regarding a monitor’s close-out
activities.

---

## 3 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

The **Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator** and the **<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI** are responsible for ensuring that the study Close-Out meets all applicable regulatory, ICH-<Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>/ISO-GCP, Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator, and local requirements.

Site procedures may be delegated to appropriately trained team members, but remain the ultimate responsibility of the Sponsor, Sponsor-Investigator, or QI/PI.

## 5 · Procedure {#procedure}
### 5.1 Site preparation for the sponsor's Close-Out Visit

**5.1.1** Review the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip> for completeness; resolve any outstanding items and document any discrepancies (e.g. by memo, Note to File, or Deviation Log and <Tooltip tip="Actions taken to resolve a compliance issue and prevent recurrence: identify, correct, prevent." headline="Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#capa">CAPA</Tooltip> form, as applicable).

**5.1.2** Confirm that the last study participant has completed the last scheduled study visit and the associated CRFs have been completed.

**5.1.3** Ensure that remaining CRFs are accurately completed and available for final review/monitoring, along with corresponding source documents.

**5.1.4** Ensure all outstanding <Tooltip tip="Document designed to record all protocol-required information to be reported to the sponsor for each participant." headline="Case Report Form (CRF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#crf">CRF</Tooltip> corrections, additions, or deletions are made, dated, explained (if necessary), and initialled by the QI/PI or delegate, and ready for review with corresponding source documents.

**5.1.5** Review monitoring follow-up letters; ensure all data queries received to date have been resolved, all monitoring follow-up letters are on file (corresponding to Monitoring Log visit dates), and all are initialled and dated by the QI/PI to show oversight.

**5.1.6** Arrange for the QI/PI to review, sign, and date completed CRFs and initial and date monitoring follow-up letters, if not already done.

**5.1.7** Review the study budget and, if necessary, pay any amounts owing to participants, sites, etc., per the contract.

**5.1.8** Ensure all relevant departments are notified of the upcoming study <Tooltip tip="Final monitoring visit to verify all study closure requirements have been addressed before database lock." headline="Close-Out Visit (COV)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#cov">close-out visit</Tooltip> (e.g. Pharmacy).

**5.1.9** Ensure all logs are complete, including the <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip>, which must now document all team member end dates (except for the QI/PI and <Tooltip tip="The team member who handles day-to-day administration and coordination of the clinical trial." headline="Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#crc">CRC</Tooltip> answering queries) with QI/PI signature and date to indicate undelegation.

### 5.2 Study Close-Out Visit (COV) by Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor

**5.2.1** Compile all study documents needed for the Monitor to complete the close-out procedures. → [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for the COV Monitoring Report template.

**5.2.2** Facilitate the Monitor's COV, as the Monitor verifies (as/if required):
- AEs/SAEs/MDIs (if applicable) recorded in source documents and CRFs → [SOP-CR-012](/sops/cr-012)
- SAEs/SADRs/MDIs reported to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator, <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip>, and/or regulatory agencies → [SOP-CR-012](/sops/cr-012)
- Expedited external reports (SUSARs/MDIs from the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator) initialled and dated by the QI/PI and submitted to the REB → [SOP-CR-012](/sops/cr-012) and [SOP-CR-026](/sops/cr-026)
- CRFs completed in accordance with source documents
- Corrections completed to source documents and/or CRFs
- Outstanding questions from previous monitoring, verification/audit, or inspections
- Essential study documents complete
- All documents requiring QI/PI oversight have been signed/initialled and dated

**5.2.3** Respond to final queries regarding source data verification and CRFs.

**5.2.4** Discuss with the Monitor any ongoing follow-up activities required:
- Outstanding issues or action items from the COV
- Participant follow-up requirements, including post-study SADRs/SAEs/MDIs (follow-up must comply with the protocol and <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#icf">ICF</Tooltip>)
- Procedures for handling data clarifications that may arise
- Record retention/storage requirements

**5.2.5** Once all queries are resolved and finalized, the eCRF should be locked and signed by the QI/PI.

### 5.3 Reconciliation and return of study-related supplies

**5.3.1** Ensure completion of all <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#ip">IP</Tooltip> accountability log(s) for all used and unused IP. Retain this documentation with essential study documents for the required archiving period (15 years for Health Canada-regulated studies; 7 years for non-regulated studies) per [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015).

**5.3.2** Arrange the return or destruction of all study-related supplies (unused test kits, hard copy forms, recruitment materials, IP) with the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator.

**5.3.3** Arrange IP destruction by the local pharmacy (if requested); follow Pharmacy SOP destruction procedures. Provide destruction documentation to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator, with a copy for the ISF.

**5.3.4** Return or handle randomization code envelopes or lists to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator per the protocol or other written instructions.

**5.3.5** Return unused CRFs and other used/unused study materials to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator at study end, unless instructed to destroy on-site per local procedures for confidential document destruction. → [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015).

**5.3.6** Return any study equipment and supplies to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/vendors unless otherwise arranged.

**5.3.7** Return all laboratory specimens (blood, tissues, etc.) to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator for evaluation and storage, unless otherwise described in the protocol or written instructions.

### 5.4 Post-COV activities

**5.4.1** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor writes a COV Report. → [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for the COV Report template.

**5.4.2** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor sends a COV Notification (summary of key discussions and conclusions) to the site for filing in the ISF. → [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for the Monitor's Close-Out Notification template.

**5.4.3** Resolve any subsequent data queries.

**5.4.4** Request information from the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator:
- Anticipated timing for outstanding payments
- Anticipated timing for the final report
- Publication plans
- Feedback on site performance
- Plans for audits or knowledge of upcoming regulatory inspections

**5.4.5** The QI/PI should inform the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor, REB, and regulatory authorities (if applicable) of any unexpected SAEs or MDIs reasonably associated with the IP, following study closure.

**5.4.6** Check the ISF for completeness and prepare to archive files per regulatory requirements, Sponsor agreement, and local policies (usually after database closure). → [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015).

**5.4.7** Inform the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator of the file archive location (e.g. Iron Mountain). → [SOP-CR-015](/sops/cr-015) for archiving information and timelines.

**5.4.8** Ensure the archiving service is aware of the required storage period.

**5.4.9** Once all post-COV activities are reconciled and all team members are undelegated on the Task Delegation Log, inform the REB of study close-out via the appropriate <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip> form (Closure of Research Project form). → Section 5.5. Send a copy of the REB correspondence to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor.

**5.4.10** Inform all research team members and all relevant departments (pharmacy, laboratory, diagnostic imaging) of the study closure.

**5.4.11** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator must ensure the Clinical Study Report (CSR) is written after data analysis, per ICH-GCP guidance document ICH E3. Distribute the CSR summary when available; produce interim reports if interim analysis was performed.

**5.4.12** Publication of results: the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator confirms all participating sites are closed and all data analyzed. Inform the research team and participants about study results and publications when available.

**5.4.13** If a <Tooltip tip="Application to Health Canada for authorization to conduct a drug trial under Division 5 of the Food and Drug Regulations." headline="Clinical Trial Application (CTA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#cta">CTA</Tooltip> or <Tooltip tip="Health Canada authorization to conduct a clinical investigation of a medical device. The device equivalent of a CTA." headline="Investigational Testing Authorization (ITA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#ita">ITA</Tooltip> was submitted to Health Canada, the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator must inform Health Canada that the study has ended.

### 5.5 Reporting completion or early termination to the MUHC REB

**5.5.1** Per [SOP-CR-026](/sops/cr-026), the completion or early termination/discontinuation of research is a change in human research activity that must be reported to the REB. The Closure of Research Project form allows the REB to close its files and provides information useful in evaluation of related studies.

**5.5.2** Submit the Closure of Research Project form to the REB when:
- There is no further participant involvement at the site
- Participants are no longer exposed to immediate research risks
- All data collection is complete
- All study supplies and IP have been returned/destroyed on site (if applicable)
- The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator, Investigator, and Monitor's close-out activities have been completed

**Closure for multi-centrique studies (>1 Quebec <Tooltip tip="Quebec's public health and social services network (Réseau de la santé et des services sociaux), including the RI-MUHC." headline="RSSS" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#rsss">RSSS</Tooltip> site):**
- If MUHC is the lead site: each participating RSSS Quebec site submits their Closure Report in Nagano; only after all Quebec RSSS closure reports are submitted does the MUHC lead site submit its Closure Report. Once the lead site submits, no further forms can be created.
- If the lead site is RI-MUHC/MUHC with sites across Quebec and Canada: all other participating sites must submit to their respective REBs before the lead site submits.
- If RI-MUHC/MUHC is a participating site (not lead): submit the Closure form in Nagano for external REB acknowledgement.

**For non-multi-centrique studies (1 Quebec site, whether or not there are other Canadian sites):**
- Submit the Closure of Research Project form via Nagano per [SOP-CR-026](/sops/cr-026).
- If the Sponsor-Investigator is from RI-MUHC/MUHC with participating sites across Canada, other sites must submit to their respective REBs before the lead submits.

The REB Chair or designee reviews the submission and issues an acknowledgement; the study status changes from "Approved" to "Closed" in Nagano. Once "Closed," no further submissions are permitted — submit subsequent relevant documents via Nagano Discussion.

**5.5.3** Include all required elements in the Closure of Research Project form per the Nagano form.

**5.5.4** For studies with an investigational product (drug, medical device, or NHP): complete an **End of Participation in Research form** (Appendix 2, [SOP-CR-009](/sops/cr-009)) for every participant and submit to Medical Records for scanning into OACIS. File the original with source documents.

**5.5.5** Once the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator has written the CSR, submit it to the REB. In most cases, the study will already be "Closed" in Nagano — submit the CSR via Nagano Discussions.

### 5.6 Audit or inspection readiness

**5.6.1** Even after study close-out, the study may be selected for audit or regulatory inspection. Maintain a plan for how auditors/inspectors may access study data and essential documents after closure.

**5.6.2** Prepare for Sponsor audits or regulatory inspections as required. → [SOP-CR-017](/sops/cr-017).

**5.6.3** If the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator is informed of an upcoming audit or inspection, they must inform the QI/PI and research team.

**5.6.4** If the QI/PI receives notification of an audit or inspection, they must inform the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator and, in the case of a regulatory inspection, inform the REB and the <Tooltip tip="All planned and systematic actions to ensure trial data are generated, documented, and reported in compliance with GCP and SOPs." headline="Quality Assurance (QA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qa">Quality Assurance</Tooltip> Department (QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca).
