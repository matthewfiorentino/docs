# CRF Design

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-100_05</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 05</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 Sep 2018</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Sponsor-Investigator</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP defines the standards for designing Case Report Forms (CRFs) for Sponsor-Investigator studies. CRFs are the primary data collection instruments for the study database and must be designed before the study begins to ensure all protocol-required data points are captured accurately. It applies to Sponsor-Investigators and data management personnel responsible for CRF design.

## Key points

- CRFs must capture all primary and secondary outcome measures defined in the protocol, plus safety data, medical history, and concomitant medications as required. **Only data defined in the protocol** will be entered in the CRF.
- Each CRF must include site and participant identification, visit number and date. Standard CRF sections may include: Randomization/Registration, Demographic, Eligibility, Baseline/Screening, Treatment, Vital Signs, ECG, Physical Exam, Laboratory Evaluation, Imaging, Medical History, Prior/Concomitant Medications, Adverse Events, SAE Reporting, Follow-up, End of Study, and Investigator Sign-off.
- Each CRF field must map to a source document or source data entry point. Fields that cannot be sourced must be justified.
- CRF design must be reviewed against the protocol and the Data Management Plan ([SOP-CR-103](/sops/cr-103)) before database setup begins ([SOP-CR-104](/sops/cr-104)).
- Version control applies to CRFs: every change to a CRF after study start must be documented, version-stamped, and the ISF/TMF updated.
