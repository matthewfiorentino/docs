# Study Close-Out

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-016_08</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 08</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 May 2025</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All levels</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the processes to be followed by the site prior to, during, and following study Close-Out by a Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator or their qualified delegated Monitor. The same procedures apply if the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator discontinues a study in its entirety or at an individual site. Read in conjunction with [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for Monitor close-out activities.
</Note>

## Key points

- Before the <Tooltip tip="Final monitoring visit to verify all study closure requirements have been addressed before database lock." headline="Close-Out Visit (COV)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#cov">Close-Out Visit</Tooltip> (COV), review the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip> for completeness and resolve all outstanding items; ensure all CRFs are complete, corrections are done and initialled by the <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI, all monitoring follow-up letters are on file and initialled/dated by the QI/PI, and the <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip> documents end dates for all team members except the QI/PI and <Tooltip tip="The team member who handles day-to-day administration and coordination of the clinical trial." headline="Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#crc">CRC</Tooltip>.
- Facilitate the Monitor's COV: the Monitor verifies AEs/SAEs/MDIs, <Tooltip tip="Document designed to record all protocol-required information to be reported to the sponsor for each participant." headline="Case Report Form (CRF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#crf">CRF</Tooltip> accuracy, source data, external <Tooltip tip="Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reaction — unexpected in nature, severity, or frequency; must be reported to Health Canada and the REB." headline="SUSAR" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#susar">SUSAR</Tooltip>/MDI initials and dating by QI/PI, outstanding queries, and completeness of essential documents. Once all queries are resolved, the QI/PI signs and locks the eCRF.
- All <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">IP</Tooltip> accountability logs must be completed and retained for 15 years (Health Canada-regulated) or 7 years from publication (non-regulated). Arrange return or destruction of all IP and study supplies with the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator through the MUHC pharmacy.
- Submit the **Closure of Research Project form** via <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip> when: there is no further participant involvement; participants are no longer exposed to immediate research risks; all data collection is complete; all IP and supplies have been returned/destroyed; and all close-out activities are complete. For multi-centrique studies, participating sites must submit their closure reports before the lead site submits.
- Once a research project is "Closed" in Nagano, no further submissions are permitted. Submit the Clinical Study Report (CSR) to the <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> via Nagano Discussion once available.
- For studies with an investigational product, an **End of Participation in Research form** must be submitted to Medical Records for every participant.
- Even after study close-out, the study may be selected for audit or regulatory inspection. Maintain an accessible record of archived document locations at all times. Notify <Tooltip tip="All planned and systematic actions to ensure trial data are generated, documented, and reported in compliance with GCP and SOPs." headline="Quality Assurance (QA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qa">QA</Tooltip> (QAclinicalresearch@muhc.mcgill.ca) if an inspection notification is received.
