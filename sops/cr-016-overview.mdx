# Study Close-Out

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-016_08</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 08</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 May 2025</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All levels</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the processes to be followed by the site prior to, during, and following study Close-Out by a Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator or their qualified delegated Monitor. The same procedures apply if the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator discontinues a study in its entirety or at an individual site. Read in conjunction with [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for Monitor close-out activities.

## Key points

- Before the Close-Out Visit (COV), review the ISF for completeness and resolve all outstanding items; ensure all CRFs are complete, corrections are done and initialled by the QI/PI, all monitoring follow-up letters are on file and initialled/dated by the QI/PI, and the Task Delegation Log documents end dates for all team members except the QI/PI and CRC.
- Facilitate the Monitor's COV: the Monitor verifies AEs/SAEs/MDIs, CRF accuracy, source data, external SUSAR/MDI initials and dating by QI/PI, outstanding queries, and completeness of essential documents. Once all queries are resolved, the QI/PI signs and locks the eCRF.
- All IP accountability logs must be completed and retained for 15 years (Health Canada-regulated) or 7 years from publication (non-regulated). Arrange return or destruction of all IP and study supplies with the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator through the MUHC pharmacy.
- Submit the **Closure of Research Project form** via Nagano when: there is no further participant involvement; participants are no longer exposed to immediate research risks; all data collection is complete; all IP and supplies have been returned/destroyed; and all close-out activities are complete. For multi-centrique studies, participating sites must submit their closure reports before the lead site submits.
- Once a research project is "Closed" in Nagano, no further submissions are permitted. Submit the Clinical Study Report (CSR) to the REB via Nagano Discussion once available.
- For studies with an investigational product, an **End of Participation in Research form** must be submitted to Medical Records for every participant.
- Even after study close-out, the study may be selected for audit or regulatory inspection. Maintain an accessible record of archived document locations at all times. Notify QA (QAclinicalresearch@muhc.mcgill.ca) if an inspection notification is received.
