# Human Research Training

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-003_10</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 10</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 19 Jun 2023</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All training levels</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP specifies the mandatory training that every member of a clinical research team must complete before performing any study task at the RI-MUHC. It defines the seven study-level training tiers available through TalentLMS, the external certifications required (CITI <Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, Health Canada Division 5), how training is documented, and what happens when a team member goes on extended leave.
</Note>

## Key points

- **Training must precede delegation.** No team member may be listed on the <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip> or perform any study task before their training is documented. Being on the log without a completed training record is a GCP violation.
- There are **7 levels of SOP training** on TalentLMS — one for each study risk tier. Only one level is required per person, but it must match the highest-risk study they are involved in. All team members on the same study train at the same level.
- The **SOP Reader** (TalentLMS) and **Competency Assessment** are the primary institutional training components. External certifications (CITI GCP, Health Canada Division 5) are required *in addition to*, not instead of, RI-MUHC institutional training.
- **Level I** (drug/biologic/gene therapy/radiopharmaceutical studies) requires CITI GCP + CITI Health Canada Division 5. Level II (medical device/surgery) requires CITI GCP. ISO 14155 training is required for anyone involved in medical device studies.
- Training certificates are valid for **2 years**. TalentLMS sends a reminder 30 days before expiry. At renewal, learners may take a refresher quiz in lieu of repeating the full course.
- If a team member is on leave for **more than 6 months**, SOP training must be renewed before they can return to study tasks.
- The **Training Log** (Appendix 2) must be maintained in the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">Investigator Site File</Tooltip>. It must be updated for every new training completion and whenever a new team member joins.
- **Protocol-specific training** must also be documented — including sign-in sheets, slide decks, and meeting summaries from Site Initiation Visits or sponsor briefings.
