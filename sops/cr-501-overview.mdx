# Notification of Participant's Physician

<Badge color="gray">SOP-CR-501_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 01 Sep 2018</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All levels</Badge>

<Note>
This SOP describes the process for notifying a subject's treating physician of their participation in a research study, and for transmitting clinically useful information in the context of continued care. This SOP is RI-MUHC specific and is not linked to any N2 SOPs. It applies to all research community members involved in conducting research with human subjects at the MUHC.

## Key points

- The **<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/Investigator is responsible** for ensuring continuance of care by informing a participant's physician of the participant's involvement in a research study — **only if the patient has consented** to this notification.
- Use **Appendix 1** (Notification to Subject's Physician of Subject Participation in Research) to formally notify the treating physician of study enrollment. The letter asks the physician not to prescribe new medications without first informing the QI.
- Use **Appendix 2** (Transmittal of Clinical Information to Subject's Physician) to communicate clinically important information that could affect the participant's continuing care (e.g., copies of test results, findings, or changes in participant health status relevant to ongoing medical management).
- Both letters must be sent only with participant consent and must identify the protocol name, ClinicalTrials.gov NCT number (if applicable), and QI/PI and study coordinator contact information.
- File copies of all correspondence with the participant's treating physician in the source documents/<Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>.
