Protecting study participants begins before the first conversation with a potential volunteer and continues through to study close-out. These four SOPs govern every interaction between the research team and participants: how consent documents are prepared and verified, how consent is obtained and documented, how participants are recruited and tracked, and how their personal information is kept confidential. All apply to Levels I–IV; SOP-CR-019 also applies at Level V.

<Warning>
**Controlled documents — always use the online version.** Do not rely on downloaded or printed copies. Your first reading of each SOP must be completed through the SOP Reader on TalentLMS to count toward your training record.
</Warning>

## SOPs in this group

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-006 — Informed Consent Forms · v01-May-2025 · Levels I–IV">
    This SOP defines the requirements for preparing, reviewing, and managing Informed Consent Form (ICF) documents. It covers what the ICF must contain, how the RI-MUHC verifies that an ICF meets regulatory and ethical requirements before use, how translations must be handled, and how ICF versions are tracked throughout the study.

    **Key points:**
    - The ICF must be written at an appropriate reading level, include all ICH-GCP required elements, and carry the current REB approval stamp and version date before any participant reads it.
    - When a translated ICF is used, a **Certificate of Translation** (Appendix 2) confirming the accuracy of the translation must be filed in the ISF.
    - Use the **Reference for Verification of Consent Document** (Appendix 1) checklist to confirm that each ICF version meets all requirements before submitting it to the REB.
    - The **ICF Tracking Log** (Appendix 3) must be maintained to document which version of the ICF was in use at each point in time. This log is critical for demonstrating that participants were consented on the correct version.

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-008 — Informed Consent Process · v01-May-2025 · Levels I–IV">
    This SOP describes how to obtain and document informed consent for human research studies at the RI-MUHC. It covers written, electronic, and remote consent, special populations (minors, incapacitated adults, non-English/French speakers), and ongoing re-consent requirements. The **Informed Consent Process Checklist (Appendix 3)** is the central compliance document.

    **Key points:**
    - Only team members on the Task Delegation Log who are trained on SOP-CR-006, SOP-CR-008, and the study protocol may obtain consent. Where possible, the consent obtainer should not be the participant's treating physician.
    - All required signatures must be obtained **before** any study-related screening procedure or enrolment begins — no exceptions. The ICF must be the most recently REB-approved version; verify the version number before each use.
    - For **minors and incapacitated adults**, a legally authorized representative must sign. When a participant reaches age 18 or recovers capacity during a study, obtain consent from them directly as soon as practicable.
    - For **investigational product studies** (drug, device, NHP), file MUHC Form FMU-9997 and a copy of the signed ICF in the Alert section of the participant's OACIS medical record, and provide the participant with an REB-approved wallet card.
    - Participants affected by an **amended ICF** must be re-consented at their earliest visit after written REB approval is obtained. Document re-consent using Appendix 3 and provide participants with the new ICF version.

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-009 — Participant Recruitment, Screening and Enrolment · v01-May-2025 · Levels I–IV">
    This SOP defines the procedures for recruiting, screening, and enrolling participants in clinical research studies at the RI-MUHC. It covers acceptable recruitment methods, how to screen candidates against eligibility criteria, how to document screening failures, how to assign participant identification numbers, and how to track participation over time.

    **Key points:**
    - All recruitment materials (advertisements, letters, scripts) must be REB-approved before use. You may not recruit participants using unapproved materials, including social media posts.
    - Eligibility criteria must be assessed systematically and documented for every screened individual, whether or not they are enrolled. Record each candidate on the Screening Log regardless of outcome.
    - Participant identification numbers must be assigned in the sequence specified by the protocol or Sponsor. Never use a participant's name or health record number as their study identifier.
    - The **Confirmation of Participation** (Appendix 1) and **End of Participation** letters (Appendix 2) are bilingual and must be provided to participants at enrolment and upon completion or withdrawal.
    - The **Telephone and VC Contact Log** (Appendix 6) is used to document all remote recruitment contacts and screening interactions conducted by phone or video.

  <Accordion title="SOP-CR-019 — Confidentiality and Privacy · v01-Sep-2018 · Levels I–V">
    This SOP defines the requirements for protecting the confidentiality and privacy of research participants in accordance with Quebec privacy legislation (Law 25 / Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector), the MUHC institutional privacy policy, and applicable research ethics requirements. It applies at all training levels, including Level V retrospective studies.

    **Key points:**
    - All personnel with access to identifiable participant data — including students, volunteers, and observers — must sign the **MUHC Confidentiality and Security Agreement** (Appendix 1) before accessing any study records.
    - Participant data must be stored on authorized systems only. Transfer of identifiable data outside MUHC systems requires explicit authorization and must use approved secure methods.
    - Data minimization applies: collect only the personal information that is strictly necessary for the research objectives. Avoid collecting more than what the protocol requires.
    - When participants withdraw consent, handle their existing data in accordance with the withdrawal terms specified in the approved ICF and REB approval letter.

    **Downloadable forms:**

## Consent process at a glance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare and verify the ICF">
    Use the Reference for Verification of Consent Document (SOP-CR-006, Appendix 1) to confirm the ICF contains all required elements and carries the current REB approval stamp.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recruit and pre-screen">
    Use only REB-approved recruitment materials. Log all screened candidates on the Screening Log (SOP-CR-009, Appendix 3).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide the ICF and allow adequate time">
    Give the potential participant sufficient time to read the ICF, ask questions, and consult family or a physician before deciding. Document the date the ICF was provided.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Obtain signatures">
    A trained team member (not the treating physician where possible) witnesses and documents the consent discussion. All signatures must be obtained before any study procedure.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Informed Consent Process Checklist">
    Complete Appendix 3 of SOP-CR-008 for each participant. For IP studies, file FMU-9997 in OACIS and provide the participant with their wallet card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track ICF versions">
    Record the ICF version used for each participant on the Participant ICF Tracking Log (SOP-CR-008, Appendix 4). Re-consent participants when a new ICF version is approved.
  </Step>
</Steps>
