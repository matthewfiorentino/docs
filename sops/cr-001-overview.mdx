## Key points

- **Only the online version is current.** The signed PDF is on file but the authoritative version for compliance purposes is the one published on the Clinical Research Hub. All references to "current SOP" mean the online version.
- An **SOP** governs processes that involve responsibilities, timelines, communication, or resources. A **Work Instruction (WI)** describes only a sequence of technical steps. If in doubt, write an SOP.
- Department-specific WIs must be **compatible with and subordinate to** the relevant RI-MUHC SOP. They cannot contradict or lower the standard set by the SOP.
- The effective date of a new or revised SOP is set **at least 2 months after QA approval**, to allow time for translation, TalentLMS training development, and community notification.
- SOPs have a typical **5-year lifecycle**. Revision can occur at any time within that period in response to regulatory changes, process audits, or organizational requirements.
- When an SOP is revised, **all team members at the relevant training level must re-read and re-acknowledge** the updated version in TalentLMS before their next study activity.
- The **SOP Review and Approval Log** (Appendix 4) must document all SME, CIM Compliance, QA, and Upper Management contributions to each revision.
