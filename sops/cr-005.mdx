## Purpose and scope

This SOP covers all the steps that must be completed between receiving final <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> approval and enrolling the first research participant. It applies to QIs/PIs, Sub-Investigators, CRCs, and all research team members involved in the initiation and activation of a human research study at the MUHC/RI-MUHC. It also applies to Sponsors, Sponsor-Investigators, and Monitors conducting Site Initiation Visits (SIVs).

---

## Procedure

### 5.1 Preparation for the Site Initiation Visit (SIV)

**5.1.1** Study initiation (in the form of an on-site or virtual <Tooltip tip="Monitoring visit to train the site team on the protocol and confirm site readiness, after REB approval and MUHC Authorization." headline="Site Initiation Visit (SIV)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#siv">SIV</Tooltip>) occurs after:
- Health Canada approval (<Tooltip tip="Letter from Health Canada confirming a CTA is satisfactory and authorizing the sponsor to proceed." headline="No Objection Letter (NOL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nol">No Objection Letter</Tooltip>/NOL or <Tooltip tip="Health Canada authorization to conduct a clinical investigation of a medical device. The device equivalent of a CTA." headline="Investigational Testing Authorization (ITA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ita">Investigational Testing Authorization</Tooltip>/ITA), where applicable
- Full REB board approval
- <Tooltip tip="Institutional authorization allowing a study to proceed at the RI-MUHC. Issued after ethics approval and feasibility review." headline="MUHC Authorization" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#muhc-auth">MUHC Authorization</Tooltip>

These documents must be filed with the essential documents in the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator's <Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip> and/or the site <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>.

**5.1.2** Once all approvals are obtained, the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator (or Monitor) sends a pre-visit confirmation letter confirming the date, time, and location of the SIV. → [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for monitoring templates. The confirmation letter must be filed in the TMF and/or ISF.

**5.1.3** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor ships study materials to the site in preparation for the SIV. Ensure the site has appropriate (temperature-controlled), adequate, and secure space for all study materials.

**5.1.4** For all drug studies (industry and <Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip>-initiated) where MUHC Pharmacy Services will be used, Pharmacy must be informed and asked to attend the SIV meeting, or a separate SIV for pharmacy staff must be arranged.

**5.1.5** The study team should review the protocol and all other study-related materials before the SIV and prepare a list of questions.

**5.1.6** The site should ensure the SIV meeting is held in a confidential area. For guidance on virtual/remote SIVs, refer to "Clinical Research Conduct during the COVID-19 Pandemic" on the RI-MUHC Portal.

### 5.2 Site Initiation Visit (SIV)

**5.2.1** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor conducts the SIV as a final assessment of the site's readiness to start the study and to provide training on study procedures and processes.

**5.2.2** The <Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>/PI and all research team members with delegated protocol tasks should be present at the SIV. When team members are unable to attend, document the reason, provide subsequent documented training, and append confirmation to the SIV documentation.

**5.2.3** Items for review at the SIV meeting include, but are not limited to:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Study content">
    - Study protocol, objectives, and procedures
    - Participant inclusion and exclusion criteria
    - Screening and enrollment procedures
    - <Tooltip tip="A drug, medical device, or natural health product being tested or used as a reference in a clinical trial." headline="Investigational Product (IP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#ip">Investigational product</Tooltip> / medical device / radiopharmaceutical
    - Management of investigational products (if applicable)
    - <Tooltip tip="Document designed to record all protocol-required information to be reported to the sponsor for each participant." headline="Case Report Form (CRF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#crf">Case report form</Tooltip> completion
  </Card>
  <Card title="Operations and compliance">
    - <Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip>, legal, and regulatory obligations
    - Virtual visits and off-site working procedures
    - Management of AEs, SAEs, adverse reactions, medical device incidents
    - Monitoring and inspection activities
    - Essential document management in the ISF
    - <Tooltip tip="The written document providing the participant with information essential to making an informed decision. Both parties must sign." headline="Informed Consent Form (ICF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#icf">Informed consent form</Tooltip> procedures
    - Data management and biological sample management
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

**5.2.4** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor should reconfirm that the facilities and research team are suitable for the study, and that all procedures have been reviewed and understood by the QI/PI and all team members with delegated tasks.

**5.2.5** The site study team should request clarification or additional information regarding any logistical, medical, or procedural aspect of the study. If time allows, it is best practice to design visit-specific source document forms before the SIV to examine and clarify protocol ambiguities with the Monitor.

**5.2.6** SIV participation must be documented (e.g. on a signed Training Log) and retained with the essential documents in the ISF and TMF. For virtual meetings, in lieu of wet-ink signatures, it is acceptable to download the attendee list from the virtual meeting platform. The Monitor must also sign the study-specific Monitoring Log. → [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013)

**5.2.7** All team members with delegated protocol tasks who were trained at the SIV must now be entered on the <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip>. The QI/PI's signature confirms that persons are qualified and appropriately trained. Pharmacists dedicated to the study must also be trained and delegated pharmacy tasks.

### 5.3 SIV Report and Go Letter

**5.3.1** After the SIV, the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor writes the SIV report confirming that the site has been trained on all study aspects and GCP requirements. → [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013), Appendix 7 for the SIV report template.

**5.3.2** The completed and signed SIV Report is sent to the site for review, follow-up action, and filing in the ISF along with training materials (SIV training slides) and the Training Log, as per GCP and Health Canada GUI-0100.

**5.3.3** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor retains a copy of the SIV Report, Training Log, and all SIV training slides in the TMF.

**5.3.4** Once the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor is satisfied with site readiness, the study is activated by sending a **Go Letter** to the site. Only after the Go Letter has been received and REB and institutional approvals are in hand may participant enrollment begin at that site. The Go Letter must be filed in both the TMF and ISF. → [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for a Go Letter template.

## Activation checklist

| Requirement | Notes |
|---|---|
| Health Canada NOL / ITA (if applicable) | Filed in TMF and ISF |
| Full REB written approval | Filed in TMF and ISF |
| MUHC Authorization letter | Filed in TMF and ISF |
| Site agreement (industry studies) | Executed and filed |
| Pre-visit confirmation letter | Filed in TMF and ISF |
| SIV conducted and documented | Attendance log, training slides, SIV report |
| Task Delegation Log complete | All trained team members listed; QI/PI signed |
| Training Log complete | All SIV training documented |
| Pharmacy trained and delegated | For all drug studies using MUHC Pharmacy |
| Go Letter received from Sponsor | Filed in TMF and ISF — enrollment may begin |
