## 1.0 Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the process for the Study Initiation Visit (<Tooltip tip="Monitoring visit to train the site team on the protocol and confirm site readiness, after REB approval and MUHC Authorization." headline="Site Initiation Visit (SIV)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#siv">SIV</Tooltip>) and activation of a human research study.

## 2.0 Scope

This procedure concerns the research community (employees, sponsor-investigators, investigators, physicians, management, consultants, students, volunteers or other persons) involved in conducting research with human participants at the MUHC/RI-MUHC.

## 3.0 Responsibilities

The Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator is responsible for ensuring that a Site Feasibility Visit (SFV) has been conducted and that the study has received HC(<Tooltip tip="Letter from Health Canada confirming a CTA is satisfactory and authorizing the sponsor to proceed." headline="No Objection Letter (NOL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#nol">NOL</Tooltip>/<Tooltip tip="Health Canada's authorization for a manufacturer or importer to sell a Class II, III or IV medical device to qualified investigators for testing." headline="Investigational Testing Authorization (ITA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#ita">ITA</Tooltip>) and <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">REB</Tooltip> approval prior to shipping study materials and conducting the Sponsor’s/Sponsor-Investigator’s/Monitor’s Site Initiation Visit (SIV) and subsequent activation of the study at the site.

The Qualified Investigator (<Tooltip tip="The physician responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of a clinical trial at the site. One QI per study per Canadian site." headline="Qualified Investigator (QI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qi">QI</Tooltip>)/ Principal Investigator (<Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip>) is responsible for ensuring that the site meets all study feasibility requirements, that the research has been approved by the REB and authorized by the MUHC prior to SIV and that the research team under their supervision complies with this procedure.

## 5.0 Procedure

<a id="5-1"></a>

### 5.1 In preparation for the sponsor’s/Sponsor-investigator’s/monitors SIV

<a id="5-1-1"></a>**5.1.1** Study Initiation (in the form of an on-site or virtual SIV) occurs after Health Canada study approval, (where applicable) i.e. receipt of No Objection Letter, NOL or Investigational Testing Authorization, ITA, REB full board approval and subsequent MUHC authorization. These documents should be filed with other essential documents in the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator’s <Tooltip tip="The sponsor's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance across all study sites." headline="Trial Master File (TMF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#tmf">TMF</Tooltip> and/or in the site <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip>.

<a id="5-1-2"></a>**5.1.2** Once all approvals have been obtained, the Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator (or Monitor) will send a pre-visit confirmation letter confirming the date, time and location of the SIV visit (Sponsor-Investigators/Monitors should refer to [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for monitoring templates). The confirmation letter should be filed in the Trial Master File (TMF) and/or the Investigator Site File (ISF).

<a id="5-1-3"></a>**5.1.3** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor will then ship study materials to the site in preparation for SIV. Ensure that the site has appropriate (temperature controlled), adequate and secure space for all study materials.

<a id="5-1-4"></a>**5.1.4** For all drug studies (Industry and PI-Initiated) where the MUHC Pharmacy Services will be utilized, the MUHC Pharmacy must be informed and asked to attend the SIV meeting or asked to attend separate SIV for pharmacy staff.

<a id="5-1-5"></a>**5.1.5** The study team should review the protocol and any other study-related materials before the SIV and prepare a list of questions.

<a id="5-1-6"></a>**5.1.6** The site should ensure that the SIV meeting will be held in a confidential area. For guidance on remote access refer to “Clinical Research Conduct during the COVID-19 Pandemic” available on RI-MUHC portal.

<a id="5-2"></a>

### 5.2 Site initiation visit (SIV) by Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/monitor

<a id="5-2-1"></a>**5.2.1** The Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor conducts an SIV, as a final assessment of the site’s readiness to start the human research study and provide training on the study procedures and processes.

<a id="5-2-2"></a>**5.2.2** The QI/PI and all research team members with delegated protocol tasks should be present at the SIV. When research team members are unable to attend an SIV, a reason should be documented, subsequent documented training should be provided, and confirmation appended to the SIV documentation.

<a id="5-2-3"></a>**5.2.3** Items for review at the SIV meeting may include, but are not limited to, the following:

- Study protocol, objectives and procedures,

- Requirement for subsequent training procedures on all amendments throughout the study
- Participant inclusion and exclusion criteria,
- Participant screening and enrollment procedures
- Knowledge of the investigational product, biological products, medical devices or radiopharmaceuticals,
- Management of investigational product, biological products, medical devices or radiopharmaceuticals, if applicable
- GCP, legal, and regulatory obligations of the research team,
- Virtual visits, working off-site procedures,
- Management of adverse events, serious adverse events, adverse reactions, serious adverse reactions, or medical device incidents,
- Monitoring and inspection activities,
- Case report form completion,
- Management of study essential documents in the ISF,
- Participant informed consent form procedures,
- Data management,
- Management of biological samples, and
- Any other protocol-specific item.

<a id="5-2-4"></a>**5.2.4** Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor should reconfirm that the facilities and research team are suitable for the conduct of the study, and that study procedures have been reviewed and understood by the QI/PI and research team members delegated protocol tasks.

<a id="5-2-5"></a>**5.2.5** The site study team should request clarification or additional information regarding any logistical, medical or procedural aspect of the study. Time permitting; it is best practice to design your visit-specific source document forms prior to SIV. This allows you time to examine and clarify any ambiguity in the protocol with the Monitor.

<a id="5-2-6"></a>**5.2.6** SIV participation must be documented (e.g. on a signed Training Log) and retained with the essential documents in the ISF and TMF. For virtual meetings, in lieu of wet ink signatures on SIV log, it is acceptable to download the list of attendees from the virtual meeting platform. The Monitor must also sign a study specific Monitoring Log, (Refer to [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013)).

<a id="5-2-7"></a>**5.2.7** All study team members delegated protocol tasks who were trained at this SIV, should now be delegated tasks on the study <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip>. The QI/PI’s signature on this log confirms that persons delegated protocol tasks are qualified and have been appropriately trained to conduct the tasks delegated. The pharmacists dedicated to the study should also be trained (if not at this SIV) and subsequently delegated pharmacy tasks on the Task Delegation Log (Main or Pharmacy specific).

<a id="5-3"></a>

### 5.3 Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/monitor’s SIV report

<a id="5-3-1"></a>**5.3.1** After the SIV, the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor writes the SIV report, confirming that the site has been trained on and understands all study aspects as well as GCP requirements. Refer to [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) Appendix 7 for an SIV report template for Monitors to complete.

<a id="5-3-2"></a>**5.3.2** The completed and signed SIV Report is sent to the site for review, action to take and filing in the ISF along with training materials (SIV training slides) and training log as per GCP and [Health Canada GUI 0100](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/compliance-enforcement/good-clinical-practices/guidance-documents/guidance-drugs-clinical-trials-human-subjects-gui-0100.html).

<a id="5-3-3"></a>**5.3.3** Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor retains a copy of this SIV report along with the Training Log and all SIV training slides in the TMF.

<a id="5-3-4"></a>**5.3.4** Once the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor is satisfied with site readiness, the study is then activated by sending a Sponsor’s Go Letter to the site (assuming REB and institutional approval have already been obtained). Only then should participant enrolment begin at that site. The Go Letter should be filed in the TMF and in the ISF. Refer to [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) for a GO Letter template.
