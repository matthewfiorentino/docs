CANTRAIN (the CANadian Consortium of Clinical Trial TRAINing Platforms) is a national training initiative funded by the Canadian Institutes of Health Research (CIHR). It is headquartered at the RI-MUHC.

CANTRAIN's mandate is to build a skilled, inclusive clinical trials workforce across Canada. Training is grounded in the Joint Task Force Core Competency Framework for clinical research professionals and built on adult learning principles — modular courses, microlearning, and practical application rather than passive content delivery. All courses are asynchronous and self-paced.

<Note>
  CANTRAIN is **supplementary** — it does not replace the [SOP Reader and Assessment](/training/compliance-requirements), or required [external certifications](/training/external-certifications). For other optional courses (CATALIS / N2 Dual Code, the N2 medical device course, RCQ), see [Supplementary Training](/training/supplementary-training).
</Note>

## CANTRAIN at the RI-MUHC

<Note>
**Free for RI-MUHC affiliated researchers and staff.** Because CANTRAIN is housed at the RI-MUHC and funded by CIHR, access is free for all RI-MUHC PIs, research coordinators, trainees, and other affiliated personnel. No institutional licence or purchase is required.
</Note>

CANTRAIN training is supplementary to RI-MUHC institutional requirements — it does not replace the SOP Reader and Assessment, or external GCP certifications required for the Task Delegation Log. It is a professional development resource, not a compliance requirement. That said, the Common Core Foundation modules overlap with and reinforce the content covered in the SOP Reader, and the CRP courses map directly to the competency domains in the [CRC Orientation](/roles/coordinator).

Individual CANTRAIN courses are linked throughout the orientation page's domain cards wherever they apply to a specific competency area.

## Common Core Foundation

The Common Core Foundation (CCF) is the recommended starting point for any learner. It consists of 14 asynchronous modules that establish a foundational understanding of clinical trial principles.

| # | Module |
|---|--------|
| 1 | Study Designs and Clinical Trial Phases |
| 2 | Drug Discovery and Development Process |
| 3 | Patient Engagement in Research |
| 4 | The Clinical Trial Protocol |
| 5 | Roles and Responsibilities in Clinical Research |
| 6 | Good Documentation Practices |
| 7 | Ethics and Informed Consent |
| 8 | Participant Recruitment |
| 9 | Participant Safety and Adverse Events |
| 10 | Data Management in Clinical Research |
| 11 | Study Oversight and Monitoring |
| 12 | ICH Guidance E6(R3) GCP |
| 13 | Tri-Council Policy Statement (TCPS 2) |
| 14 | Health Canada, Part C, Division 5 |

The CCF covers Canadian health regulations, Good Clinical Practice, Health Canada Division 5, TCPS 2, trial design and phases, participant safety, ethics and consent, and roles and responsibilities. Completion prepares learners to advance into any of the four specialized streams. The CCF is a prerequisite for some programs, including the Graduate and Postdoctoral Trainee career development program.

## Stream 2: Clinical Research Professionals

Stream 2 is the most relevant stream for CRCs and other research staff at the RI-MUHC. It includes the CRP Bootcamp and a growing library of domain-specific courses.

### CRP Bootcamp

The Bootcamp is a flexible, competency-based program covering three core areas: understanding the CRP role and the clinical study lifecycle, conducting externally sponsored clinical trials, and managing investigator-initiated trials (including responsibilities as a lead site). The Bootcamp provides a foundation in site-level operations, regulatory and institutional requirements, and trial conduct from planning through close-out. It includes practical resources: templates, how-to videos, and operational guides.

## SOCRA and ACRP certification credits

CANTRAIN courses qualify as continuing education (CE) hours toward maintenance of both SOCRA and ACRP professional certifications.

| Organization | Certification | CE requirement | How CANTRAIN applies |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOCRA | CCRP (Certified Clinical Research Professional) | 45 CE hours every 3 years. Minimum 22 hours must relate to clinical research regulations and policy. | Self-report CANTRAIN course hours with your certificate of completion. CANTRAIN courses covering GCP, Division 5, TCPS2, ethics, and regulatory compliance count toward the 22-hour regulatory minimum. |
| ACRP | CCRC (Clinical Research Coordinator) or CCRA (Clinical Research Associate) | 24 contact hours or points every 2 years. | Self-report CANTRAIN courses as non-accredited CE using the Exam Content Outline to confirm relevance. ACRP accepts CE from recognized educational providers covering clinical research practice topics. |

Neither SOCRA nor ACRP pre-approves CANTRAIN courses specifically, but both accept self-reported educational activities related to clinical research practice. Keep your CANTRAIN certificates of completion as documentation. Both organizations also recognize each other's certification through the TransCelerate GCP Mutual Recognition Program.

<Note>
**Not a substitute for GCP certification.** CANTRAIN's CCF Module 12 covers ICH GCP E6(R3), but this does not replace the formal GCP certification required for Levels I and II at the RI-MUHC. GCP certification must be obtained through CITI or another recognized provider. See [External Training Resources](/training/external-certifications) for details.
</Note>

## How to access

CANTRAIN courses are hosted at **cantrain.blackboard.com**. To get started:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register at wecantrain.ca">
    Visit [wecantrain.ca](https://wecantrain.ca) and navigate to Clinical Trials Training Programs. Complete the registration questionnaire through the registration portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your account credentials">
    Within 24 hours you will receive an account creation notification with login credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to the learning platform">
    Log in at [cantrain.blackboard.com](https://cantrain.blackboard.com) using Genius SSO with your assigned username and password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
**Start with the Common Core Foundation.** If you are new to CANTRAIN, begin with the 14 CCF modules before exploring the specialized streams. They are available immediately after registration and provide the baseline knowledge all streams build on. [Go to CANTRAIN](https://wecantrain.ca/home/clinical-trial-training-programs/)
</Note>
