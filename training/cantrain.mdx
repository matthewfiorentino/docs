CANTRAIN offers online clinical trials training for researchers, research staff, trainees and patient partners across Canada. The CANadian Consortium of Clinical Trial TRAINing Platform is funded by the Canadian Institutes of Health Research (CIHR) and housed at the RI-MUHC. Its courses are free for the RI-MUHC's investigators, research staff and trainees.

CANTRAIN's mandate is to build a skilled, inclusive clinical trials workforce across Canada. CANTRAIN's training is competency-based, grounded in the Joint Task Force Core Competency Framework. Its programs are built for how adults learn, with modular courses, microlearning and practical application.

## Know what CANTRAIN does not replace

CANTRAIN's courses are professional development. They do not replace the RI-MUHC's required training for your study's level: the [SOP Reader and Assessment](/training/compliance-requirements#sop-reader-and-assessment) in TalentLMS, and the [good clinical practice (GCP) and other training your level requires](/training/compliance-requirements#training-requirements-by-study-level).

The Common Core Foundation includes a module on the International Council for Harmonisation (ICH) guideline E6(R3) on GCP. GCP training counts toward your level's requirement only if it comes from the Collaborative Institutional Training Initiative (CITI) Program or from [another provider that meets the RI-MUHC's conditions](/training/external-certifications#using-another-training-provider). Ask [Quality Assurance (QA)](mailto:QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca) before you rely on a course from any other provider.

For other optional courses, such as the CATALIS courses and the N2 course on medical devices, see [Supplementary Training](/training/supplementary-training).

## Start with the Common Core Foundation

The Common Core Foundation is CANTRAIN's introductory program of 11 e-learning courses on key concepts such as ethics, trial design and documentation. CANTRAIN encourages learners to begin with its regulatory compliance course on GCP and the Common Core Foundation. Learners in the clinical research professionals stream are encouraged to complete the Common Core Foundation before its courses. The Graduate and Postdoctoral Trainee Career Development Program requires it.

## SOCRA and ACRP certification credits

CANTRAIN's courses may be eligible for maintenance of certification credits with the Society of Clinical Research Associates (SOCRA) and the Association of Clinical Research Professionals (ACRP). Keep your CANTRAIN certificates of completion as documentation.

| Organization | Certification | Maintenance requirement | How CANTRAIN courses apply |
|---|---|---|---|
| SOCRA | CCRP (Certified Clinical Research Professional) | 45 continuing education hours every 3 years. At least 22 of those hours must relate to clinical research regulations and policy. | Report the hours of your CANTRAIN courses yourself, with your certificates of completion. Courses on GCP, Division 5, TCPS 2, ethics and regulatory compliance count toward the 22 hours on regulations. |
| ACRP | CCRC (Clinical Research Coordinator) or CCRA (Clinical Research Associate) | 24 maintenance activity points every 2 years. | Report CANTRAIN courses yourself as non-accredited continuing education, using ACRP's Exam Content Outline to confirm that each is relevant. ACRP accepts continuing education from recognized educational providers on clinical research practice. |

Neither SOCRA nor ACRP pre-approves CANTRAIN's courses. Both accept educational activities on clinical research practice that you report yourself. Both organizations also recognize each other's certification through the TransCelerate GCP Mutual Recognition Program.

## Register and open your courses

You reach CANTRAIN's courses through myCourses, McGill University's learning management system, once you have registered with CANTRAIN.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register with CANTRAIN">
    On CANTRAIN's [Clinical Trials Training Programs page](https://wecantrain.ca/home/clinical-trial-training-programs/), select **Register now** and complete the CANTRAIN Learner Registration form. The form confirms your affiliation and gives you access to the learning management system.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your email">
    You should receive an automatic email once you have completed the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your courses">
    On the Clinical Trials Training Programs page, select **Access your courses**, or go directly to [myCourses](https://mycourses2.mcgill.ca/d2l/loginh/).
  </Step>
</Steps>
