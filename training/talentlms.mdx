TalentLMS is the RI-MUHC's learning management system. It is the platform where you complete the mandatory clinical research compliance program — the SOP Reader and Competency Assessment — apply for Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status, and access all records and certificates tied to your Task Delegation Log eligibility. All courses are available by self-enrollment, and the platform is available in both English and French.

## Accessing TalentLMS

Log in with your RI-MUHC credentials. Both language versions share the same course catalogue and training records — use whichever you prefer.

  <Card title="TalentLMS — English" icon="laptop" href="https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/home">
    Log in to the English instance of TalentLMS.
  </Card>
  <Card title="TalentLMS — Français" icon="laptop" href="https://francais-rimuhc.talentlms.com/accueil">
    Ouvrir la version française de TalentLMS.
  </Card>

TalentLMS is also accessible via the [RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca). If you have difficulty logging in or accessing the platform, open an IT Rescue ticket and select the **"Learning Management System (TalentLMS)"** category.

## What TalentLMS is used for in clinical research

TalentLMS hosts the four components of your RI-MUHC clinical research compliance record:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SOP Reader" icon="book-open">
    Read and acknowledge each SOP relevant to your training level. No calendar expiry — units reset only when that SOP is revised. Prerequisite for the Competency Assessment.

    [SOP Reader details](/training/compliance-requirements#sop-reader)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Competency Assessment" icon="circle-check">
    Scenario-based assessment from a shared question pool. Certificate valid 2 years (SOP-CR path, Levels I–III) or 5 years (SOP-LR path, Levels IV–V).

    [Competency Assessment details](/training/compliance-requirements#competency-assessment)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Research Privileges application" icon="user-doctor" href="https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/236">
    Request Human Research Privileges (course #236) — for CPDP members (physicians and dentists) leading studies requiring medical oversight.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Researcher Status application" icon="flask" href="https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/235">
    Request Human Researcher Status (course #235) — for non-CPDP researchers leading observational, retrospective, or non-medical-act studies.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For the full training requirements by study level — including external GCP and Health Canada Division 5 certifications managed outside TalentLMS — see [Training and Compliance Requirements](/training/compliance-requirements).

All courses in TalentLMS are available by self-enrollment.

<Tabs>

  <Tab title="Course Catalog">

To see the training requirements that apply to your specific role, consult the [Training page on the RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:3).

## Downloading a certificate

Compliance certificates for the Competency Assessment, and records for Human Research Privileges and Researcher Status, can be downloaded directly from your TalentLMS profile. You will need to file these in your study documentation (ISF/TMF).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to TalentLMS">
    Log into TalentLMS via the [RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hover over your name">
    Hover over your name at the top of the screen to open the profile menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click My Progress">
    Click **My Progress** in the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Certificates">
    Click **Certificates** to view all certificates associated with your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your certificate">
    Under the Options column (three dots icon) next to the certificate you want, click the download icon.
  </Step>
  <Step title="File your certificate">
    Print and file the certificate in your study documentation (ISF/TMF).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  GCP and Health Canada Division 5 certificates are **not** stored in TalentLMS — they are downloaded directly from your training provider account (CITI or other). See [External Certifications](/training/external-certifications) for details.
</Info>

## Viewing your training record in the Researcher Administrative Profile

Your Human Research Privileges/Status and SOP training records also appear in your Researcher Administrative Profile in the RI-MUHC Portal. This view is for reference only — certificates cannot be downloaded from here.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Portal">
    In the [RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca), click your name in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Profile">
    Select **My Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Administrative Profile">
    Under Work Details, click **Administrative Profile**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
