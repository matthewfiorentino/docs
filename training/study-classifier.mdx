---
title: "Study Type and Training Classifier"
description: "Answer a short series of questions about your study type and data practices to identify your required RI-MUHC training level, certifications, and applicable Quebec regulatory considerations."
---

The Study Type and Training Classifier helps you determine your required training level based on your study type and data practices. It identifies the SOP Reader and Competency Assessment you need to complete, and flags any applicable Quebec regulatory considerations.

If you are involved in more than one study, complete this tool for the highest-risk study type. The resulting training level covers all lower levels.

<Note>
**Regulatory basis:** Training requirements derive from SOP-CR-003, **ICH E6(R3)** (Health Canada, effective Apr 2026), **Food and Drug Regulations Part C Div. 5** (GUI-0100), **ISO 14155:2020**, **TCPS2 (2022)**, and Quebec's **LSSSS**. Training level is based on your highest-risk study across all active protocols.
</Note>

<Warning>
This tool provides guidance based on SOP-CR-003. Training level is based on your **highest-risk active study**. Results do not constitute formal compliance advice. When in doubt, contact QA.
</Warning>
