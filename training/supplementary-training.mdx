The courses on this page are **optional**. They support professional development and deepen specific skills, but none of them replace the institutional requirements for the Task Delegation Log — the [SOP Reader and Assessment](/training/compliance-requirements) in TalentLMS, or the [external certifications](/training/external-certifications) (GCP, Health Canada Division 5, ISO 14155) required for certain study levels.

<Note>
  Looking for what's **required**? See [External Certifications](/training/external-certifications) for the mandatory GCP, Division 5, and ISO 14155 training, and [Training and Compliance Requirements](/training/compliance-requirements) for the full matrix by study level.
</Note>

## CANTRAIN

The RI-MUHC is the headquarters of **CANTRAIN**, Canada's national clinical trials training platform, funded by CIHR. Its Common Core Foundation includes 14 modules covering GCP, Health Canada Division 5, TCPS2, trial design, consent, and adverse events. The CRP Bootcamp and domain-specific courses are particularly relevant for clinical research coordinators and research nurses. Access is free for RI-MUHC affiliated researchers and staff.

## CATALIS Québec / N2 Dual Code modules

CATALIS Québec offers optional modules through the **N2 Dual Code** platform, including courses on task delegation, ALCOA+ data integrity principles, study data management, and quality management. These modules are currently available **in French only**.

## N2 Canadian Medical Device course

The _N2 Canadian Medical Device_ course covers Health Canada medical device regulations. It is an optional general course — it **does not replace** the ISO 14155 GCP for Medical Devices training required for [Level II device studies](/training/external-certifications#iso-141552020-gcp-for-medical-devices) — and is a useful complement for teams running device trials.
