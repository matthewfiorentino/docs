These courses are optional, except [the certification for shipping biological specimens](#other-web-based-and-task-specific-courses), which team members involved in shipping specimens need. They build skills beyond the training your study's level requires, and none of them replaces it. That required training is the [SOP Reader and Assessment](/training/compliance-requirements#sop-reader-and-assessment) in TalentLMS and the [good clinical practice (GCP) and other training your level requires](/training/compliance-requirements#training-requirements-by-study-level), each needed before you are delegated a task on the Task Delegation Log.

## CANTRAIN

CANTRAIN, the national clinical trials training program housed at the RI-MUHC, offers online courses in four streams, from its introductory Common Core Foundation to courses for clinical research professionals, investigators and patient partners. Its courses are free for the RI-MUHC's investigators, research staff and trainees. [CANTRAIN's own page](/training/cantrain) has its streams, courses and registration.

## N2 and CATALIS Québec courses on the Dual Code platform

The Network of Networks (N2), an alliance of Canadian research networks, offers online courses on its [Dual Code platform](https://my.dualcode.com/n2canada/). They include a general course on Health Canada's medical device regulations, "Medical Devices". The course is optional and does not replace GCP training. A guide on how to access it for free is on the Training page of the RI-MUHC Portal.

CATALIS Québec, the organization the Government of Quebec mandates to coordinate clinical research services in the province, offers four courses on the same platform:

- Gestion de la qualité (quality management).
- Délégation des tâches (task delegation).
- Principes ALCOA+ et corrections de données (ALCOA+ principles and data corrections).
- Gestion des données d'étude (study data management).

These four courses are in French only.

N2's other courses on the platform include "Responsabilités des chercheurs", "Introduction to Clinical Research", "Cultural Competency Skills in Clinical Research", "Foundations of Privacy in Research" and "Source, CRFs and Good Data Management".

## Other web-based and task-specific courses

The RI-MUHC's SOPs also name these courses:

- A course on the [responsible conduct of research](/kb/governance/research-integrity#find-training-on-research-ethics-and-responsible-conduct), on the Collaborative Institutional Training Initiative (CITI) Program.
- Two options for a funder that requires a course on research ethics under the *Tri-Council Policy Statement* (TCPS 2). The first is the SOP Reader and Assessment, which incorporates all of TCPS 2's principles and concepts. The second adds [CORE-2022](https://tcps2core.ca/welcome), the online course on TCPS2core.ca.
- An introductory course for research coordinators on CITI, which the SOPs highly recommend for them.
- Certification for shipping biological specimens under the Transportation of Dangerous Goods (TDG) Regulations and the International Air Transport Association (IATA) standards, on CITI. [The RI-MUHC's SOPs require it](/kb/conduct/study-operations/biological-specimens#hold-a-valid-shipping-certificate) of RI-MUHC members and employees involved in shipping biological specimens.
- REDCap training, for staff who want to use [REDCap](/apps/redcap) to design electronic case report forms.
- An introduction to submitting studies in Nagano, in TalentLMS.
- MUHC training, such as on OACIS, through the MUHC portal.

The RI-MUHC Portal lists other web-based courses. For more information, write to the [Training Advisor](mailto:training-formation@muhc.mcgill.ca) or to [Quality Assurance (QA)](mailto:QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca). [Registering with CITI](/training/external-certifications#accessing-citi-—-first-time-registration) is with the required external training.
