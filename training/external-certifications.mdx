In addition to the SOP Reader and Competency Assessment completed in TalentLMS, certain study levels require external certifications that run on their own 2-year schedules. The RI-MUHC holds an institutional licence with the **CITI Program**, giving affiliated researchers and staff free access to GCP and Health Canada Division 5 training. This page covers which certifications you need, how to access them through CITI, and what to do if you train with another provider.

## Which certifications do you need?

## ICH E6(R3) GCP

ICH E6(R3) GCP is required for everyone on the Task Delegation Log for **Level I and Level II** studies. The RI-MUHC CITI institutional licence gives you access at no cost.

- **New to GCP:** Take the full course — _Good Clinical Practice (GCP) — Canada_
- **Renewing:** Take the refresher — _Good Clinical Practice (GCP) — Canada Refresher_

<Note>
  **E6(R3) transition.** Health Canada adopted ICH E6(R3) effective April 1, 2026. If you completed GCP before October 27, 2025, your R2 certificate has been reset — you must complete the R3 version in CITI. If you completed GCP after October 27, 2025, you have already completed R3.
</Note>

For questions about GCP certification, contact QA: [QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca)

## Health Canada Division 5

Health Canada Part C Division 5 is required for everyone on the Task Delegation Log for **Level I** studies (drug, biologic, vaccine, and advanced therapy studies).

- **Course:** _Health Canada Part C Division 5 — Drugs for Clinical Trials_
- Based on GUI-0100. Covers Health Canada's regulations for clinical trials under Part C, Division 5 of the Food and Drug Regulations.
- **Renewal:** Every 2 years

For questions about Health Canada Division 5 certification, contact QA: [QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca)

## ISO 14155:2020 GCP for Medical Devices

ISO 14155:2020 is required for **Level II** studies involving investigational medical devices and surgical interventions. This training is **not available through CITI** — teams must self-train on the copy-controlled ISO document.

- **Provider:** Purchase the document via [iso.org](https://www.iso.org) and self-train
- **Optional supplement:** The CITI _N2 Canadian Medical Device_ course covers Health Canada medical device regulations and is a useful complement
- **Renewal:** Every 2 years

<Info>
  CITI ICH-GCP is accepted in lieu of ISO-GCP where ISO training is unavailable. However, Health Canada inspectors expect ISO-GCP training for device studies.
</Info>

## Accessing CITI — first-time registration

If you have never used the CITI Program before, follow these steps to create an account and affiliate with the RI-MUHC institutional licence.

<Note>
  **Use your institutional email.** Register with your RI-MUHC or MUHC email address. This allows the RI-MUHC to identify your learning records in compliance reports. You can also enter a secondary email address for account recovery.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to citiprogram.org and register">
    Go to [citiprogram.org](https://www.citiprogram.org) and click **Register**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Affiliate with the RI-MUHC">
    Under **Select Your Organization Affiliation**, search for **Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre (RI-MUHC) (N2)** and select it from the list. Click **Create a CITI Program account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your personal information">
    Enter your first and last name and email address. Enter your name exactly as you want it to appear on your completion certificate. Use your RI-MUHC or MUHC institutional email. Adding a secondary email is recommended for account recovery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create login credentials">
    Choose a username (4–50 characters, not case-sensitive) and password (8–50 characters, case-sensitive). Select a security question and answer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your country">
    Select your country of residence (Canada). Click **Continue to Step 6** at the next screen — CITI Canada does not offer content at Step 5.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the RI-MUHC institutional profile">
    Fill in your language preference, institutional email address, highest degree, employee number (if applicable), and department. Click **Continue to Step 7**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your courses and finalize">
    Read each course enrollment question carefully and select the courses appropriate to your role and study type. Click **Finalize Registration**. Your account is now active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Accessing CITI — starting a course

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in and go to the Main Menu">
    Log into [citiprogram.org](https://www.citiprogram.org) and go to the Main Menu. Under **Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre (RI-MUHC) Courses**, click **Add a Course or Update Learner Group** to enrol in a course you haven't started yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start your course">
    Your enrolled courses appear on the Main Menu. Click **Start Now** next to the course you want to begin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read and accept the Assurance Statement">
    Read the Assurance Statement, check the box at the bottom, and click **Submit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete all modules">
    Complete all required modules and their associated quizzes. You must achieve the passing score set by the RI-MUHC. You may repeat any quiz to improve your score, but your first passing score is the one recorded.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your certificate">
    When all required modules are complete, click **View / Print** to download your completion certificate. File it in your study documentation (ISF/TMF).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Already registered with CITI?

If you already have a CITI account but have not yet affiliated with the RI-MUHC, you can add the affiliation without creating a new account.

Log into CITI, go to your Main Menu, and click **Affiliate with another institution**. Search for **Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre (RI-MUHC) (N2)** and follow the prompts. This gives you access to the RI-MUHC course catalogue under your existing account.

If your previous GCP certificate was completed under a different institution, you can transfer credit by affiliating with the RI-MUHC and contacting CITI support to link your prior completion.

## Using another training provider

GCP and Health Canada Division 5 certificates from providers other than CITI are accepted if the provider appears on the **TransCelerate list of recognized GCP training providers**. Contact QA to confirm equivalency before submitting your study: [QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca)

<Warning>
  Even if your certificate from another provider is valid for 3 years, the RI-MUHC will only recognize it for 2 years.
</Warning>

## CANTRAIN and other supplementary resources

The RI-MUHC is the headquarters of **CANTRAIN**, Canada's national clinical trials training platform, funded by CIHR. CANTRAIN training is supplementary to RI-MUHC institutional requirements — it does not replace the SOP Reader, Competency Assessment, or external GCP certifications required for the Task Delegation Log. Access is free for RI-MUHC affiliated researchers and staff.

CANTRAIN's Common Core Foundation includes 14 modules covering GCP, Health Canada Division 5, TCPS2, trial design, consent, and adverse events. The CRP Bootcamp and domain-specific courses are particularly relevant for clinical research coordinators and research nurses.

<Card title="CANTRAIN — Canada's national clinical trials training platform" icon="graduation-cap" href="https://wecantrain.ca/home/clinical-trial-training-programs/">
  Free for RI-MUHC affiliated researchers and staff. Supplementary professional development — does not replace institutional compliance requirements.
</Card>

CATALIS Québec also offers optional training modules through the **N2 Dual Code** platform, including courses on task delegation, ALCOA+ data integrity principles, study data management, and quality management. All modules are currently available in French only.
