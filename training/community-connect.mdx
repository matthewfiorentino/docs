Community Connect is a monthly session series on clinical research topics at the RI-MUHC, hosted by the Research Facilitator with subject matter experts from across the institution. Sessions are held approximately monthly and cover processes, regulations, tools, and institutional updates that affect research teams. All sessions are recorded and the archive is available below.

## Upcoming sessions

Sessions are announced through the Research Facilitator. To be notified of upcoming sessions and receive registration links, contact the Research Facilitator at [research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca).

## Topics covered

Community Connect sessions have covered a wide range of topics relevant to RI-MUHC research teams, including:

- **Nagano submission process** — Parts 1 and 2 of the study submission walkthrough, multicentre studies (three-part series), and the F11 data access form and EFVP
- **Data access and management** — accessing MUHC patient data, the data warehouse access centre, human research data management guidelines under Law 25 and ICH E6(R3), and TMF/ISF document management
- **Regulatory and policy updates** — Health Canada Medical Device Regulations and ITAs, Health Canada CTA process, and new SOPs including the Cree Community review SOP
- **Operational topics** — the new clinical trials budget template, McGill Office of Innovation and Partnerships, and the critical role of SOPs in research conduct

## Suggest a topic

Community Connect topics are driven by researcher questions and operational needs. If there is a process, regulation, or tool you would like to see covered in a future session, reach out to the Research Facilitator at [research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca).
