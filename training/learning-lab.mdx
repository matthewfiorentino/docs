The Learning Lab is a set of applied practice tools for clinical research staff at the RI-MUHC. It is designed to help you and your team work through real-world scenarios, test your recall, and sharpen your judgement on the situations that come up most in study conduct. There are five activity types, covering everything from multiple-choice knowledge checks to team discussion exercises built around actual SOP content.

<Note>
  The Learning Lab is for **informal practice only**. It does not replace the SOP Reader, Competency Assessment, or any other formal training requirement. Completion here does not count toward your Task Delegation Log eligibility or any compliance certificate.
</Note>

Access the Learning Lab through the [RI-MUHC Clinical Research Hub](https://rimuhc.ca/clinical-research).

## Team Exercises

The eight Team Exercises are case-based discussion exercises built around specific SOPs and GCP principles. Each exercise presents a realistic scenario with a study team, then walks through situations that raise questions of accountability, documentation, safety, or compliance. Key points are revealed on demand — making these well-suited for team meetings where you want people to work through the reasoning themselves before seeing the answers.

## Knowledge Checks

Knowledge checks are multiple-choice questions with immediate feedback, organized across 11 topics that map to the RI-MUHC SOPs. You can work through a topic systematically or use shuffle mode for a randomized draw of questions across all areas. Shuffle mode lets you select 5, 10, 20, or 30 questions to calibrate the session length.

Topics cover both interventional trial content (SOPs, GCP, ICH E6(R3), Health Canada) and observational/retrospective study content (TCPS2, privacy, consent).

## Inspection Prep

The Inspection Prep activity presents 40 mock inspector questions with model responses. You read the question, prepare your answer, and then reveal what a strong response looks like. This activity is designed both for individual preparation and for team readiness sessions ahead of a monitoring visit, audit, or Health Canada inspection.
