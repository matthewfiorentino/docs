## Research support and infrastructure

The Institute's research centres and platforms support your study from design through analysis — most are open to any RI-MUHC researcher, and the strongest time to engage them is during study design. Not sure who you need? The [Design Stage Support](/kb/design-support/study-design) guide maps the right specialist to each part of your study.

<div className="ri-support-cards">
<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM)" icon="hospital" href="/cim/services">
    Dedicated clinical research infrastructure and staff — specialized nursing, coordination, monitoring, Phase I capacity, and technical platforms at the Glen and Montreal General. Open to any RI-MUHC researcher.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ACT-CTU — Design Stage Support" icon="compass-drafting" href="/kb/design-support/study-design">
    Accelerating Clinical Trials at the Institute — the Novel Trial Methods Hub (protocol methodology), protocol review, seed-funding connections, and cross-provincial start-up for investigator-led trials.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Biostatistics (BCU)" icon="chart-line" href="/kb/design-support/bcu">
    A service of the Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE). Sample size, randomization, analysis plans, and DSMB support across the full study lifecycle.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Management (CORD)" icon="database" href="/kb/design-support/cord">
    A service of the Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE). REDCap database builds, CRF review, and data management. REDCap is validated for 21 CFR Part 11 compliance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="MUHC Data Warehouse" icon="server" href="/kb/design-support/data-warehouse">
    A service of the Division of Digital and Information Technology. Clinical and administrative data from 18+ MUHC hospital systems — for retrospective research and feasibility.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
</div>
