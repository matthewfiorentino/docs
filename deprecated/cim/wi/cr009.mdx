## Key points

- Study-specific source document templates must be adapted per protocol; only text or boxes marked in green may be modified — black boxes must not be removed (use "NA" if not required).
- Each study-specific source document template must include a footer with a version date and current MUHC protocol version/date; versioning is tracked using the **Source Document Template Version Tracker** (Appendix 1).
- A second qualified site staff member must perform a QC review of each study-specific source document created against the protocol; both the creator and the QC reviewer must initial and date the first page footer.
- If a protocol amendment modifies how an assessment is performed or adds data to collect, the relevant source documents must be reviewed and updated (then re-versioned); superseded versions must be moved to the archived folder in the ISF.
- Investigator or Sub-Investigator must complete the Investigator sections of visit source documents contemporaneously and sign/date reports and logs within **5 business days** of the subject visit.
- Laboratory results, pathology, and imaging reports must be printed and signed by the Investigator upon receipt or within **5 business days** of the visit.
- Medical History/Baseline Symptom, Adverse Event, and Concomitant Medication logs must be entered/updated at the time of the subject visit; the QI or Sub-I must sign the Investigator section within 5 business days of the visit.
- The CRC and RN must review each other's completed logs and source documents to ensure all required documents are complete, signed, dated, and ready for data entry within 5 business days of the subject visit.

## 1 · Purpose and scope {#purpose-and-scope}

**Purpose:** This Working Instruction (WI) describes the requirements for the creation and management of study-specific and general source documents.

**Scope:** This WI applies to all qualified trained staff involved in clinical studies and handling study documents at the Center for Innovative Medicine (CIM) of the Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre (RI-MUHC). This WI includes the requirements for creation and management of study-specific and general source documents.

---

## 2 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Qualified Investigator (QI) | The Investigator (includes Sponsor-Investigator) is responsible for: ensuring qualified site staff create, complete, sign, quality control (QC) the source documents, transfer the data electronically, and file study-specific and general source documents in each subject's research chart; completing Investigator sections contemporaneously in visit source documents and signing and dating reports and logs within **5 business days** of subject visit; QI or delegated Sub-Investigator (Sub-I) must review the inclusion/exclusion criteria, sign and date all supporting documents at screening, and confirm subject eligibility for the trial. |
| Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC) / Research Nurse (RN) | The CRC (or RN) creates/adapts study-specific source documents from existing (most recent) templates for documenting visit information required for each subject. A review/QC of the "adapted to protocol" source document template is completed by a second Qualified Site Staff to ensure all per-protocol procedures are included — if the CRC creates the source document, the RN should do the review. The CRC (or RN) shall transfer created study-specific worksheet templates, without any patient information, under the study ISF "Source document" folder on the H: drive. The RN (and/or CRC) completes information on source documents for each subject visit/activity. CRC and RN are to review each other's completed logs and source documents to ensure all required source documents — including AE and Concomitant Medication logs — are completed, signed, and dated by the Investigator and ready to be sent for data entry within **5 business days** of the subject visit. The CRC (or RN) files the documents in the subject's research chart under the specific visit tab once data has been entered into the eCRF. |

## 4 · Instructions {#instructions}

### Document Categories

**Study-Specific Source Documents** (adapted per protocol):
- Screening Visit
- Treatment Visit
- Follow-Up Visit
- Survival Follow-Up
- Additional study-specific worksheet

**General Source Documents managed by CIM Study Staff** (CIM-managed templates):
- Medical History/Baseline Symptom Log
- Adverse Event Log
- Concomitant Medication Log
- Serious Adverse Event Log
- Discontinuation Summary
- Withdrawal Summary
- Statement of Death

**General Source Documents NOT managed by CIM Study Staff** (not editable by CIM):
- Patient Informed Consent Process Form
- Patient Informed Consent Form tracking
- Sponsor/CRO questionnaires and diaries
- Clinical forms (e.g., pregnancy reporting for female subjects)
- Pathology request form
- Request for Chemotherapy/Biotherapy
- Pharmacy drug accountability logs
- MUHC Central Laboratory requisition
- Blood results, pathology, imaging reports from external facility
- Lab Sample Processing & Storage Log
- Sponsor Central Laboratory requisitions
- Recruitment Logs (non patient-specific)
- Deviation Log
- EMR Confirmation of participation in research
- EMR End of participation in research
- Progress note form
- Authorization to release information form
- Regulatory Source Documents (non patient-specific)

### 4.1 Study-Specific Source Documents {#study-specific-source-documents}

Study-specific source document templates are adapted as per protocol. **You may only adapt/modify/remove/duplicate green text or boxes.**

**4.1.1** Each visit/activity source document cover page includes an editable checklist to ensure that all protocol requirements are documented and completed. The sequence of visit tasks is to be indicated if specified by the protocol. Do not remove black boxes (different from optional green) from source documents; check the "NA" option if not required.

**4.1.2** Each visit usually requires its own source document. Information to use in source document creation can be found in:
- **4.1.2.1** Protocol Schedule of Assessments/Events: Gives a visit-by-visit overview of the study in a summary table and footnotes.
- **4.1.2.2** Protocol and protocol appendices: Describe what and how data is to be collected. Also refer to any existing central/local laboratory tables to find accurate and complete information regarding required laboratory tests.
- **4.1.2.3** Study Manuals: eCRF guidelines can help clarify data collection; imaging, laboratory, and/or biospecimen manuals can provide clarification on required assessments.

**4.1.3** Additional study-specific worksheets can be created to capture specific data not covered by any existing source document.

**4.1.4 Source Document Template Version and Footer:**
- **4.1.4.1** All site study-specific source documents used to collect data should be subject to version control by adding to the source document template footer: the version date and the current used protocol MUHC version/date (e.g., `Source Document version_MUHC Protocol version/date`).
- **4.1.4.2** Study-Specific and General Source Document Templates versioning will be tracked using the **"Source Document Template Version Tracker"** (Appendix 1). The tracker will be controlled by CIM management and kept electronically under "CIM-Research\Source Document Templates" folder on the H: drive.
- **4.1.4.3** Numbering of pages is included electronically in each template.
- **4.1.4.4** The RN, or another CRC, must perform quality control of each study-specific source document created against the protocol by the CRC. Qualified site staff creating and QC'ing the source document respectively **initial and date the first page footer** of the created visit source document.

**4.1.5** If a protocol is amended, previously created study-specific source documents are to be reviewed and updated if the new amendment modifies how an assessment is performed or adds data to be collected. Only if changes are made, the updated source document will be re-versioned by incrementing the new protocol amendment MUHC version/date in the document footer. Superseded documents should then be moved to the archived folder under the study ISF "Source document" folder on the H: drive.

### 4.2 General Source Documents {#general-source-documents}

General source documents are logs, forms, requisitions, results, and reports completed and/or printed from the EMR system called OACIS, or from the DSQ, or received from external facilities. Some general source documents are templates managed by the CIM research study staff (partially editable; see section 4.2.3) and some are templates or reports not managed by the CIM research study staff and are therefore not editable.

**4.2.1** Laboratory results, pathology, and/or imaging reports are printed and the Investigator is required to sign the results upon receipt or within **5 business days** of the visit. Results are reviewed by the QI/Sub-I at the time of the visit by logging into the EMR.

**4.2.2** Medical History/Baseline Symptom, Adverse Event, and Concomitant Medication information is to be entered/updated on their respective logs by the Qualified Site Staff at the time of the subject visit. The QI or Sub-I has to complete/sign the Investigator section on those logs within **5 business days** of the visit.

**4.2.3** On the AE log, sections in green may be modified: the "Relationship: other" column content, and adding or removing treatment columns "Tx:" according to the protocol.
