## Key points

- Before scheduling a visit, the Monitor or PM sends the Short Preliminary Site Questionnaire (Appendix 3) and a Confidentiality Agreement to potential sites via the RI-MUHC contracts portal.
- The Site Feasibility Visit Confirmation E-mail (Appendix 2) must be sent to the site; confirmation of visit timing must be received within 2 weeks.
- During the visit the monitor assesses five areas using Appendix 1: (1) subject population/recruitment, (2) investigator and staff qualifications, (3) equipment/facilities, (4) data collection capability, and (5) regulatory compliance/administrative procedures.
- The Site Feasibility Visit Report (Appendix 1) must be completed and submitted to the Sponsor within **10 working days** of the visit.
- An approval or refusal letter (Appendix 4) is sent to the site; if approved, the follow-up letter includes any required action items for the Investigator to file in the ISF.
- The Investigator must sign and date the Site Feasibility Visit Follow-Up Letter (Appendix 4) and all outstanding issues must be resolved before contract/budget negotiations begin.
- If a feasibility visit is not performed, a Note to File documenting the reason must be uploaded to the TMF.
- Each investigator must provide a current CV and valid medical licence as evidence of suitability.

## 1 · Purpose and scope {#purpose-and-scope}

This Work Instruction describes the process for conducting Site Feasibility Visits to assess potential investigator(s) and institution(s) for suitability to conduct a clinical trial. It covers preliminary site assessment, visit preparation, visit conduct, and follow-up activities.

## 4 · Instructions {#instructions}

### 5.1 Preliminary Site Assessment {#preliminary-site-assessment}

**Step 5.1.1** Potential investigator(s) and institution(s) are assessed for suitability by completing a Short Preliminary Site Questionnaire (Appendix 3), based on sponsor-approved site selection criteria. Assessments of adequate resources may include:
- Interest and willingness to participate
- Qualifications and training requirements of site staff
- Potential to recruit eligible participants
- Adequate facilities/equipment/resources

**Step 5.1.2** Potential investigator(s) must consider their availability and interest in the study, their ability to conduct the study in accordance with relevant regulations/guidelines, and confirm their willingness to enter eligible participants.

**Step 5.1.3** If a Site Feasibility Visit is not performed, the reason and decision must be documented on a Note to File and uploaded to the TMF.

**Step 5.1.4** After the Investigator confirms availability and interest, a Confidentiality Agreement is prepared by the PM or Monitor and sent through the RI-MUHC contracts office (RI portal, My Contracts application) to the Investigator, in accordance with RI-MUHC SOP-CR-019 Confidentiality and Privacy.

**Step 5.1.5** Upon receipt of notification of the fully executed Confidentiality Agreement from the Contracts office, upload it to the TMF.

**Step 5.1.6** The PM or Monitor will send the Protocol and the Short Preliminary Site Questionnaire (Appendix 3) to potential sites to confirm their interest. This form must be completed by the site PI and returned to the PM or Monitor for review.

### 5.2 Site Feasibility Visit Preparation {#site-feasibility-visit-preparation}

**Step 5.2.1** The Monitor will schedule a site feasibility visit (for internal or external sites), prepare and send the Site Feasibility Visit Confirmation E-Mail Template (Appendix 2), including a list of mandatory attendees and items to be reviewed.

**Step 5.2.2** Obtain site confirmation for the feasibility visit within 2 weeks. If confirmation is not received within 2 weeks, follow up with the site coordinator by e-mail or telephone.

### 5.3 Site Feasibility Visit Conduct {#site-feasibility-visit-conduct}

#### 5.3.1 Investigator and Staff

**Step 5.3.1.1** The Sponsor is responsible for selecting appropriate investigator(s) and institution(s) for a particular study.

**Step 5.3.1.2** Careful site selection ensures that study resources are directed towards well motivated, qualified staff with the potential to recruit eligible participants, generate high-quality study data, and conduct the study within the context of relevant regulations or guidelines.

**Step 5.3.1.3** Each investigator should be qualified by education, training, and experience and must have adequate personnel resources to properly conduct the study. Qualifications must be documented and provided to the sponsor as evidence of suitability — in the form of current CV and medical licence or other documentation as per SOP-CR-002 and SOP-CR-003.

**Step 5.3.1.4** Any other items verified or discussed in relation to staffing should be documented in section 2 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.3.2 Equipment/Facility Allocations Assessment

**Step 5.3.2.1** List all equipment needed for the study and confirm availability at the site as per Appendix 1 (section 3).

**Step 5.3.3** The monitor will ensure that all applicable facilities are available at the site or close to the site as per protocol requirements (laboratory, exercise room, stairs/corridors for testing, etc.). Other items should be documented in section 3 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.3.4 Study Subject Population/Recruitment

**Step 5.3.4.1** Discuss with the Investigator their ability to enroll subjects as per protocol.

**Step 5.3.4.2** Assess the capacity of the site to enroll subjects as per the site agreement, the anticipated date of first subject in, and completion date.

**Step 5.3.4.3** Document any other items in section 1 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.3.5 Data Collection

**Step 5.3.5.1** Discuss the site's capability to utilize electronic data collection (if applicable) and access to source documents as per protocol.

**Step 5.3.5.2** Document any other items in section 4 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.3.6 Regulatory Compliance/Administrative Procedures

**Step 5.3.6.1** Local site study approval procedures for potential sites should be discussed and clarified in the early stages of site contact. Approval or favorable opinion must be sought from ethics, competent authorities, and other relevant bodies.

**Step 5.3.6.2** Document any other items in section 5 of Appendix 1.

### 5.4 Site Feasibility Visit Follow-up {#site-feasibility-visit-follow-up}

**Step 5.4.1** Complete and sign the Site Feasibility Visit Report Template (Appendix 1) within **10 working days** after the visit.

**Step 5.4.2** Submit the report for review and approval to the Sponsor within **10 working days**.

**Step 5.4.3** Obtain the Project Manager or Sponsor's site qualification approval/refusal and file in the TMF.

**Step 5.4.4** An approval or refusal letter (Appendix 4) is sent to the site to confirm site evaluation.

**Step 5.4.5** If the site is approved, the follow-up letter to the Investigator will include the list of any actions required. The Investigator is to file the letter in the ISF.

**Step 5.4.6** Discuss any outstanding issues as a result of the feasibility visit. Follow up on the list of actions identified and document resolution of the issues.

**Step 5.4.7** Verify that the Site Feasibility Visit Follow-Up Letter Template (Appendix 4) is signed and dated by the Investigator.

**Step 5.4.8** Once the site has been approved and all outstanding issues have been resolved, contract/budget negotiations can begin with the site.
