REDCap (Research Electronic Data Capture) is a secure web platform for building and managing online databases and surveys. At the RI-MUHC, REDCap is provided and administered by **CORD** — the Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation — in partnership with the Research Institute.

## Getting access

REDCap access at the RI-MUHC is managed by the CORD team. There are two separate request types — use the correct form for your situation. Allow 2–3 working days for any request to be processed. When a request is submitted, the CORD team is automatically notified.

## What REDCap does

REDCap is designed specifically for clinical and translational research data collection. It can be used for electronic case report forms (eCRFs), participant surveys, longitudinal data collection, randomization, and more. Projects can be set up quickly and modified at any time, including after data collection has begun.

## Use policy and REB requirement

REDCap at the RI-MUHC must be used in accordance with the **REDCap Use Policy**. If you are collecting data for human subjects research, your project requires review and approval by your Research Ethics Board before data collection begins. This applies to REDCap projects in the same way as any other data collection method — REDCap is a tool, not an exemption from ethics obligations.

<Warning>
  For studies involving identifiable participant data, your REDCap project setup must be consistent with what was described in your REB submission, including any EFVP (privacy impact assessment) filed with your Nagano submission. If the data collection scope changes, an amendment may be required before the change can be implemented.
</Warning>
