REDCap is a secure web platform for building and managing online databases and surveys. The RI-MUHC and its Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE) provide REDCap to support clinical research. [Capture and Optimization of Research Data (CORD)](/kb/design-support/cord), a unit of CORE, hosts the projects and administers REDCap.

[CORD hosts REDCap projects](/kb/design-support/cord#who-can-use-cord) for teams of the RI-MUHC and of its affiliated institutions, the MUHC and McGill University. Creating and hosting a project has [fees](/kb/design-support/cord#fees).

[REDCap is meant as an electronic case report form (eCRF) that collects data transcribed from source documents](/kb/design-support/cord#use-redcap-for-a-study). [REDCap may be used for electronic consent only in a study that Health Canada doesn't regulate](/kb/recruitment/consent#obtain-consent-remotely-or-electronically), and only if the basic guidelines that [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca) has been preparing with Quality Assurance are followed.

## What REDCap can do

Study teams use REDCap for electronic case report forms, participant surveys, longitudinal data collection, randomization and other data collection. A project can be created quickly and changed at any time, even after data collection has begun. Its features include:

- Building online surveys and databases in a web browser, from any device, with no extra software.
- Instrument design with automatic validation, calculated fields, file uploads, branching and skip logic, and survey stop actions.
- Survey invitations sent with personalized messages to a list of email addresses or phone numbers, with a record of who has responded, or a link to an anonymous survey for mass distribution.
- Field validation, branching logic and missing data codes that protect data quality during data entry, and data queries that identify and resolve discrepancies as they arise.
- Custom reports with basic statistics and charts, and exports to Excel, SPSS, SAS, Stata and R.
- Alerts and notifications to the team by email, text message or phone when certain data are entered or a question has a particular answer.
- Secure file storage and sharing through the File Repository or the Send-It tool, which also work with exports and the built-in file uploads.
- An application programming interface (API) that moves data to and from a project, and custom development that connects REDCap to other systems.

## Request a project or an account

Every REDCap project and every REDCap account at the RI-MUHC starts with a request to [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca). Two forms serve the two kinds of request:

| You need | Use |
|---|---|
| A new REDCap project for a study or initiative, or CORD's paid support | The [CORD Service Request Form](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/cim/redcap/surveys/?s=LPCYP8KP4JHP8C79) |
| A REDCap account, or access to a project that already exists | The [REDCap User Account Request Form](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/cim/redcap/surveys/?s=SKbfXbvAig) |

Staff, students and trainees can submit requests, but each request must be linked to a <Tooltip tip="The investigator who leads the study team at a site and is responsible for conducting the study there." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#pi">principal investigator</Tooltip>. The CORD Service Request Form is for requests that CORD bills, not for general questions. For a general question, write to [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca).

If this is your first REDCap project and you don't have a REDCap account yet, submit the project request first, then the account request. Account requests are processed after the project has been created in REDCap.

[Every trainee, researcher and administrative staff member must complete a REDCap User Account Request Form and agree to the REDCap Use Policy before receiving access](/kb/design-support/cord#before-your-redcap-project-collects-data). Anyone who does not is denied access.

The principal investigator manages user access and roles in the project, or may designate a team member to do it after the project is created. [Give REDCap access only to team members](/kb/conduct/data-documentation/edc-query-management#get-access-to-the-edc) whose training on it is documented and who have been delegated the task on the Task Delegation Log. Make sure that measures of protection, detection and correction are in place, including a secure electronic signature with an audit trail.

To request an account, or access to an existing project, complete the REDCap User Account Request Form:

1. Give the project's full name as it appears in REDCap, or its project ID (PID). If you don't know the PID, ask the principal investigator or the person who manages user rights in the project.
2. Give the user's name, email address and role in the project. Confirm the role with the principal investigator.
3. Answer the form's two required questions on approval: whether the principal investigator has approved the request, and whether the site investigator has approved it. Each question takes "Yes", or "Not applicable" when the person it names is the one completing the form.
4. Submit the form. The new user then receives a questionnaire that confirms their identity and contact information.
5. Once the user completes it, [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca) creates the account and the user receives an email with their username. If that email doesn't arrive, check the junk mail folder before you write to [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca).

Allow 2 to 3 working days for any request to be processed. CORD is notified automatically when a request is submitted.

## Log in

Log in to the [RI-MUHC's REDCap](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/cim/redcap/) with your REDCap username and password. If you have trouble logging in, use the **Forgot your password?** link on the login page. Do not access REDCap from Vanderbilt University's website. Use only the RI-MUHC's REDCap, which [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca) manages.

REDCap must be used in accordance with the REDCap Use Policy, which the login page links.

## Before your project collects data

A REDCap project is built in a testing environment, then moves to production status to collect data. [Test the project before it moves to production](/kb/design-support/cord#before-your-redcap-project-collects-data). When it reaches production, all data entered while testing are deleted.

A research study needs approval from a <Tooltip tip="A body, not affiliated with the sponsor, whose main mandate is to approve the start of biomedical research involving human participants and to review it periodically, to protect the participants' rights, safety and well-being." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">research ethics board (REB)</Tooltip> before its REDCap project moves to production and starts collecting data.

<Warning>
Do not start any research activity, including recruitment, data collection or analysis, until you have both REB approval and MUHC Authorization.
</Warning>

If the REDCap project is the study's eCRF, make sure that it collects [only data pertinent to the REB-approved protocol](/sops/cr-014#5-7-5). In a study that uses identifiable participant data, the project's setup must be consistent with what the study's REB submission describes, including any privacy impact assessment (<Tooltip tip="The privacy impact assessment, or évaluation des facteurs relatifs à la vie privée, that Quebec's Act respecting health and social services information requires with a researcher's request for access to health and social services information without consent, including access before consent for pre-screening." headline="EFVP (privacy impact assessment)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#efvp">EFVP</Tooltip>) filed with the Nagano submission. If the data the study collects change, submit the change to the REB. [Wait for every approval the change needs before you implement it](/kb/conduct/safety-reporting/study-amendments#wait-for-every-approval-before-you-implement-the-amendment).

[Sponsors and sponsor-investigators must validate](/kb/design-support/cord#use-redcap-for-a-study) any electronic data capture system they use, REDCap included. For a Health Canada-regulated study, ask [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca) for the validation records that cover your use.

## Get training and help

[Training videos](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/cim/redcap/index.php?action=training) on the RI-MUHC REDCap's Training Resources page cover the basics, building a project, and REDCap's modules and features. [Any REDCap self-training should be documented on a Training Log](/sops/cr-014#5-8-1). [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca) also offers [training on REDCap and help with it throughout the project](/kb/design-support/cord#what-cord-offers).

For questions about REDCap, technical problems or help with project setup, write to CORD, the RI-MUHC's REDCap administrator, at [cord@rimuhc.ca](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca).
