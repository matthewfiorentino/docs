CrossLab is the RI-MUHC's online booking system, made by Agilent. Two RI-MUHC services take requests through it:

- The <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's Centre for Innovative Medicine, which provides clinical research services, facilities and technical platforms at the Glen site and the Montreal General Hospital." headline="Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#centre-for-innovative-medicine-cim">Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM)</Tooltip>, for its [technical platforms](/cim/services#use-the-technical-platforms): functional testing, ophthalmology, imaging, human specimen services and experimental procedures. Its human specimen services are at the Glen site and the Montreal General Hospital.
- The [Biostatistics Consulting Unit (BCU)](/kb/design-support/bcu), for service requests and billing.

To learn which of the CIM's platforms suit your study, write to the [CIM](mailto:clinical.research@muhc.mcgill.ca). [Plan the CIM's part in your study](/cim/planning) before you submit the study, with the cost of its services in the study budget.

## Register before your first request

Register in CrossLab before your first request. To register:

1. Open the service's page in CrossLab: the [CIM's page](https://rimuhc.corefacilities.org/service_center/show_external/3741/cim_platform) or the [BCU's page](https://rimuhc.corefacilities.org/service_center/show_external/4719).
2. Select **Register**, in the upper right corner.
3. Enter your institution email address and agree to the privacy and security policies.
4. Enter your personal information, select your <Tooltip tip="The investigator who leads the study team at a site and is responsible for conducting the study there." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#pi">principal investigator</Tooltip> or lab, and give your billing information.
5. Wait for confirmation. The CrossLab team reviews your registration. Once it is confirmed, you can submit requests.

For help with registering, see [Agilent's registration guide](https://help.ilab.agilent.com/en_US/99540-getting-started-with-ilab/325013-registering-with-ilab). If you have trouble getting access to CrossLab, write to the [CIM](mailto:clinical.research@muhc.mcgill.ca) for its platforms, or to the [BCU](mailto:bcu@rimuhc.ca) for its services.

## Book a CIM platform

On the [CIM's CrossLab page](https://rimuhc.corefacilities.org/service_center/show_external/3741/cim_platform), [request a service, book a blood draw or reserve equipment](/cim/services#book-a-platform-in-crosslab). Training is mandatory before you use the CIM's equipment on your own, and it is free. The CIM's [fees](/cim/services#fees) are set in its Technical Platform Fee Schedule.

## Request a BCU service

[Sign in to the BCU's service centre and submit your request](/kb/design-support/bcu#how-to-request-a-consultation) from its **Request Services** tab. The BCU team reviews the request. The team either agrees to do the work or asks you for more information.
