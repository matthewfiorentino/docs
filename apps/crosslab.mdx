CrossLab is the RI-MUHC's core facility management system for the Centre for Innovative Medicine. Researchers use it to request access to CIM's specialized technical platforms, including bioanalytical services, imaging, and other infrastructure available at the Glen and MGH sites.

## What you can book through CrossLab

CrossLab provides access to the CIM's technical platform portfolio. Available services include specialized instrumentation and core facility resources managed by the CIM team. Contact CIM directly to discuss which platforms are relevant to your study.

## Getting access

CrossLab uses your RI-MUHC credentials. If you encounter access issues or need to create an account, contact the CIM team.
