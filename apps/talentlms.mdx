TalentLMS is the RI-MUHC's learning management system. It hosts mandatory institutional training, role-specific training, and the clinical research SOP compliance program — including the SOP Reader, Competency Assessment, and Research Privileges and Status applications. All courses are available by self-enrollment. The platform is available in English and French.

## Logging in

TalentLMS is available in English and French. Both instances share the same course catalog and training records — use whichever language you prefer. Log in with your RI-MUHC credentials.

## Clinical research training

The clinical research compliance program in TalentLMS has two components: the **SOP Reader** and the **Competency Assessment**. Both are required of everyone listed on a study's Task Delegation Log, including the PI. Training level is determined by the highest-risk study you are involved in.

The SOP Reader requires you to read and acknowledge each SOP relevant to your training level. The Competency Assessment is a scenario-based quiz that generates a compliance certificate valid for 2 years (Levels I–III) or 5 years (Levels IV–V). The Reader must be complete before you can access the Assessment.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SOP Reader" icon="book-open">
    Read and acknowledge each SOP for your training level. No expiry — units reset only when that SOP is updated. Prerequisite for the Competency Assessment.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Competency Assessment" icon="certificate">
    Scenario-based assessment from a shared question pool. Certificate valid 2 years (CR path, Levels I–III) or 5 years (LR path, Levels IV–V).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For the full training requirements by level — including external GCP and Health Canada Division 5 certifications — see the Training and Compliance Requirements page.

## Downloading your certificate

After completing your Competency Assessment, you can download your compliance certificate directly from TalentLMS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to TalentLMS">
    Go to [english-rimuhc.talentlms.com](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/home) and log in with your RI-MUHC credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Training">
    Select **My Training** from the navigation menu to see your completed courses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the completed course">
    Click the name of the Competency Assessment course you completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Certificates tab">
    Inside the course, select the **Certificates** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your certificate">
    Click **Download** next to the certificate you need. The file downloads as a PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share as required">
    Save the certificate to your files. Submit a copy to your Research Facilitator or upload it as required by your study's training documentation process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mandatory institutional training

All members of the RI-MUHC — administrative staff, researchers, research trainees, postdoctoral fellows, laboratory personnel, and research staff — must complete the following five institutional training courses.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Let's Be Allies: Foundations in Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion">
    Strengthen your understanding of EDI in the research environment. Learn to build inclusive teams and integrate diverse perspectives into your work.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/371)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cybersecurity Basics at The Institute">
    Protect sensitive research and institutional information. Learn to recognize potential digital threats and adopt safe online practices.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/424)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Prevention of Violence and Psychological Harassment in the Workplace and Research Space">
    Recognize, prevent, and address violence and psychological harassment in the research setting.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/276)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Creating a Culture of Care: Understanding Intimate Partner, Family and Sexual Violence in the Workplace">
    Learn how to identify, respond to, and prevent workplace violence. Contribute to a safe and supportive community for everyone at The Institute.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/427)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="MUHC Emergency Measures">
    Be prepared to act safely and effectively during emergency situations within MUHC facilities. Covers the colour code emergency system and key response procedures.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/401)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
