TalentLMS is the RI-MUHC's learning management system. It hosts mandatory institutional training, role-specific training, and the clinical research SOP compliance program — including the SOP Reader and Assessment, and Research Privileges and Status applications. All courses are available by self-enrollment. The platform is available in English and French.

## Logging in

TalentLMS is available in English and French. Both instances share the same course catalog and training records — use whichever language you prefer. Log in with your RI-MUHC credentials.

## What TalentLMS is used for in clinical research

TalentLMS hosts the components of your RI-MUHC clinical research compliance record:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SOP Reader and Assessment" icon="book-open">
    One course per level: read and acknowledge each SOP for your level, then complete the assessment in the same course. Certificate valid 2 years (SOP-CR, Levels I–III) or 5 years (SOP-LR, Levels IV–V).

    [SOP Reader and Assessment details](/training/compliance-requirements#sop-reader-and-assessment)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Research Privileges application" icon="user-doctor" href="https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/236">
    Request Human Research Privileges, for physicians and dentists who are members of the MUHC's Council of Physicians, Dentists, Pharmacists and Midwives. Privileges are required to lead a Health Canada-regulated study with an investigational product, or research that requires medical oversight.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Researcher Status application" icon="flask" href="https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/235">
    Request Researcher Status, for researchers who are not members of the council, and for pharmacists acting as principal investigator or as the principal investigator's backup. Researcher Status covers only research that does not require medical oversight and is not a Health Canada-regulated study with an investigational product, such as observational and retrospective studies.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

For the full training requirements by study level — including external GCP and Health Canada Division 5 certifications managed outside TalentLMS — see [Training and Compliance Requirements](/training/compliance-requirements).

## Downloading your certificate

After completing the SOP Reader and Assessment, you can download your compliance certificate directly from TalentLMS.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to TalentLMS">
    Go to [english-rimuhc.talentlms.com](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/home) and log in with your RI-MUHC credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Training">
    Select **My Training** from the navigation menu to see your completed courses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the completed course">
    Click the name of the SOP Reader and Assessment course you completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Certificates tab">
    Inside the course, select the **Certificates** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your certificate">
    Click **Download** next to the certificate you need. The file downloads as a PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and share as required">
    Save the certificate to your files. Submit a copy to your Research Facilitator or upload it as required by your study's training documentation process.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Mandatory institutional training

All members of the RI-MUHC — administrative staff, researchers, research trainees, postdoctoral fellows, laboratory personnel, and research staff — must complete the following five institutional training courses.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Let's Be Allies: Foundations in Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion">
    Strengthen your understanding of EDI in the research environment. Learn to build inclusive teams and integrate diverse perspectives into your work.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/371)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cybersecurity Basics at The Institute">
    Protect sensitive research and institutional information. Learn to recognize potential digital threats and adopt safe online practices.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/424)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Prevention of Violence and Psychological Harassment in the Workplace and Research Space">
    Recognize, prevent, and address violence and psychological harassment in the research setting.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/276)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Creating a Culture of Care: Understanding Intimate Partner, Family and Sexual Violence in the Workplace">
    Learn how to identify, respond to, and prevent workplace violence. Contribute to a safe and supportive community for everyone at The Institute.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/427)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="MUHC Emergency Measures">
    Be prepared to act safely and effectively during emergency situations within MUHC facilities. Covers the colour code emergency system and key response procedures.

    [View course](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/401)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  If you are on the RI-MUHC payroll, log your mandatory training hours in **Logibec** using code **TRAIN** and submit supporting documents to [training-formation@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:training-formation@muhc.mcgill.ca).
</Note>

## Viewing your training record in the Researcher Administrative Profile

Your Human Research Privileges/Status and SOP Reader and Assessment records also appear in your Researcher Administrative Profile in the RI-MUHC Portal. This view is for reference only — certificates cannot be downloaded from here.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Portal">
    In the [RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca), click your name in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Profile">
    Select **My Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Administrative Profile">
    Under Work Details, click **Administrative Profile**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
