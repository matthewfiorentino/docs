TalentLMS is the RI-MUHC's training tool for human research. It holds the SOP Reader and Assessment your study's level requires, the forms to request Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status, and your certificates. TalentLMS keeps track of the courses assigned to you, your progress, your evaluations and when each certificate expires. The mandatory institutional training is in TalentLMS too.

Reading an SOP on the Hub does not count toward your compliance record. Only TalentLMS holds that record.

## Find and enrol in your courses

You enrol yourself in the RI-MUHC's courses from the Course Catalog: search for the course and enrol in it. The course then appears on your dashboard among the courses to complete. A manager, an instructor or an administrator can also assign a course to you.

- [Course Catalog](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog) shows every available course, so that you can find and enrol in yours.
- [My Training](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/my/training) shows your active courses and the courses assigned to you, with your progress, completion records and certificates.

You can also reach the SOP Reader and Assessment courses from the Clinical Research SOPs page of the RI-MUHC Portal. The training requirements for your role are on the [Training page of the RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:3).

## What TalentLMS holds for clinical research

TalentLMS holds these parts of your clinical research compliance record:

- The [SOP Reader and Assessment](/training/compliance-requirements#sop-reader-and-assessment), one course for each study level. In the course for your level, read and acknowledge each SOP, then complete the assessment in the same course. The certificate expires at the end of [your level's renewal period](/training/compliance-requirements#training-requirements-by-study-level).
- [Research Privileges application](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/236): request Human Research Privileges, for physicians and dentists who are members of the MUHC's Council of Physicians, Dentists, Pharmacists and Midwives. Privileges are required to lead a Health Canada-regulated study with an investigational product, or research that requires medical oversight.
- [Researcher Status application](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/235): request Researcher Status, for researchers who are not members of the council, and for pharmacists acting as principal investigator or as the principal investigator's backup. Researcher Status covers only research that does not require medical oversight and is not a Health Canada-regulated study with an investigational product, such as observational and retrospective studies.

Good clinical practice (GCP) and Health Canada Division 5 training is taken outside TalentLMS, on the Collaborative Institutional Training Initiative (CITI) Program or with another provider. The full requirements for each study level are on [Training and Compliance Requirements](/training/compliance-requirements).

## Downloading your certificate

Once you pass the SOP Reader and Assessment, TalentLMS issues your certificate automatically. Download it and file it with your study training records.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to TalentLMS">
    Go to [english-rimuhc.talentlms.com](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/home) and log in with your RI-MUHC credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Training">
    Select **My Training** from the navigation menu to see your completed courses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the completed course">
    Select the name of the SOP Reader and Assessment course you completed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Certificates tab">
    Inside the course, select the **Certificates** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your certificate">
    Select **Download** next to the certificate you need. The file downloads as a PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="File your certificate">
    File the certificate with your study training records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

TalentLMS also holds your Privileges or Researcher Status certificate. [Make sure that it is filed](/training/compliance-requirements#apply-for-privileges-or-researcher-status) in the <Tooltip tip="The qualified or principal investigator's collection of study documentation that demonstrates compliance with regulations and guidelines, so that the conduct and integrity of the study and the safety of participants at the site can be verified." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#isf">Investigator Site File (ISF)</Tooltip> of each of your studies.

## Mandatory institutional training

All members of the RI-MUHC, from administrative staff, researchers, research trainees and postdoctoral fellows to laboratory personnel and research staff, must complete these five institutional training courses in TalentLMS:

| Course | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Let's Be Allies: Foundations in Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/371) | Equity, diversity and inclusion in the research environment: building inclusive teams and bringing diverse perspectives into your work. |
| [Cybersecurity Basics at The Institute](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/424) | Protecting sensitive research and institutional information: recognizing digital threats and adopting safe online practices. |
| [Prevention of Violence and Psychological Harassment in the Workplace and Research Space](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/276) | Recognizing, preventing and addressing violence and psychological harassment in the research setting. |
| [Creating a Culture of Care: Understanding Intimate Partner, Family and Sexual Violence in the Workplace](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/427) | Identifying, responding to and preventing violence in the workplace, for a safe and supportive community at The Institute. |
| [MUHC Emergency Measures](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com/plus/catalog/courses/401) | Acting safely during emergencies in MUHC facilities: the colour code emergency system and key response procedures. |

If you are on the RI-MUHC payroll, log your mandatory training hours in Logibec with the code TRAIN. Send the supporting documents to the [Training Advisor](mailto:training-formation@muhc.mcgill.ca), who also answers questions about Law 90.

## Viewing your training record in the Researcher Administrative Profile

Your Privileges or Researcher Status and your SOP Reader and Assessment records also appear in your Researcher Administrative Profile in the RI-MUHC Portal. The profile is for viewing only: certificates cannot be downloaded from it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Portal">
    In the [RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca), select your name in the top right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to My Profile">
    Select **My Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Administrative Profile">
    Under Work Details, select **Administrative Profile**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
