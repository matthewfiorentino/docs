The courses listed here are in addition to the standard RI-MUHC clinical research training pathway (SOP Reader, Competency Assessment, GCP, Health Canada Division 5). Not all courses apply to all roles — your manager will confirm your required enrollment. All completion is tracked in TalentLMS under your account.

<Note>
**Note on course codes.** Codes shown are the TalentLMS course identifiers (e.g. CIM_009_01_ENG). Use these when searching TalentLMS or contacting your manager about enrollment.
</Note>

[Open TalentLMS](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com)

## CIM courses on TalentLMS

### Clinical and research staff

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="CIM_009 — CIM Work Instructions Reader">
    The primary read-and-acknowledge mechanism for all CIM Work Instructions. Staff read the WIs relevant to their role and record completion through this course. It is each employee's responsibility to stay current as WIs are updated.

    **Applies to:** All CIM staff

    **Expiry:** No expiry listed
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CIM_003 — Code Blue for the Glen Site">
    Overview of how to prepare for and manage Code Blue situations at the CIM Glen site. Covers recognition of cardiac and respiratory emergencies, the internal alert process, Pointer and Runner roles, UBEC and AED locations, and post-event documentation requirements.

    **Applies to:** All staff working in CIM clinical areas at the Glen site

    **Expiry:** No expiry listed
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CIM_002 — Code Red and Code Green for the Glen Site">
    Covers fire emergency preparation (Code Red Alert and Alarm) and evacuation procedures (Code Green) for the CIM Glen site. Includes the roles of the CIM Emergency Team Lead and Floor Monitor, patient management during active procedures, and post-evacuation study documentation requirements.

    **Applies to:** All staff working in CIM clinical areas at the Glen site

    MGH staff: confirm site-specific requirements with your manager — a separate MGH training record form exists but no equivalent TalentLMS course is currently listed.

    **Expiry:** No expiry listed
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CIM_005 — Unit-Based Emergency Cart (UBEC) Verification and Management">
    Covers the monthly verification process for Unit Based Emergency Carts at the Glen and MGH sites. Includes lock seal verification, expiry date checking, documentation requirements, deviation handling, and the CAPA process for discrepancies. Verification is performed by two CRC-Ns and overseen by a designated Safety Team Lead.

    **Applies to:** CRC-Ns assigned to UBEC verification duties

    **Expiry:** No expiry listed
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Lab and technical platform staff

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="CIM_001 — Specimen Processing Laboratory Safety Practices">
    Overview of laboratory rules at CIM covering mandatory lab attire, maintaining a safe laboratory environment, proper use of lab equipment, and use of emergency equipment and emergency procedures. Required before working in the specimen processing lab.

    **Applies to:** All staff working in the CIM specimen processing laboratory

    **Expiry:** Valid 1 year
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CIM_008 — CIM Specimen Processing Laboratory — Glen SOP and WI Reader">
    Read-and-acknowledge course for the Containment Level II Processing Laboratory SOPs and Work Instructions at the Glen site. Covers the SOP_CL series (lab attire, lab coats, biological safety cabinet, freezers and fridges, centrifuges) and the WI_TEC series for Glen lab equipment. Required for lab access.

    **Applies to:** Staff working in the Glen specimen processing lab

    **Expiry:** Valid 2 years
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CIM_010 — CIM Specimen Processing Laboratory — MGH SOP and WI Reader">
    Read-and-acknowledge course for the Containment Level II Processing Laboratory SOPs and Work Instructions at the MGH site. Same scope as the Glen SOP and WI Reader but specific to MGH lab procedures. MGH lab users are only required to complete the MGH version. Required for lab access.

    **Applies to:** Staff working in the MGH specimen processing lab

    **Expiry:** Valid 2 years
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CIM_011 — CIM Functional Testing Platform Work Instructions Reader">
    Read-and-acknowledge course for Work Instructions governing the CIM Functional Testing Platform. Covers the WI-TEC-100 and WI-TEC-200 series including onboarding, iLab booking, CPET, ECG systems, body composition assessment, plethysmography, metabolic testing, and other platform-specific instruments. It is each employee's responsibility to stay current with updates.

    **Applies to:** All staff using the CIM Functional Testing Platform

    **Expiry:** No expiry listed
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CIM_007 — CERNER/LIS User Guide">
    Covers how to navigate the CERNER LIS system to request tests and place orders in accordance with CIM guidelines and protocols, and how to identify and troubleshoot common issues to ensure accurate order submission.

    **Applies to:** Staff who use the CERNER LIS system

    **Expiry:** No expiry listed
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Lab access request

**CIM_004 — CIM Laboratory Access Request**

This is not a training course but an access request process completed on TalentLMS. Staff use it to upload the external training certificates required for their access tier and to formally request lab access. Once certificates and required training are submitted, access is granted by the CIM Laboratory and Test Center Supervisor. See [Laboratory access](#laboratory-access) below for the full requirements by tier.

**Applies to:** All staff requiring access to the CIM specimen processing laboratory

## Laboratory access

Access to the CIM specimen processing laboratory requires completion of external training certificates in addition to the TalentLMS courses above. The certificates required depend on which type of access you need. There are three access tiers.

<Warning>
**External certificates must be uploaded before access is granted.** Complete your required TalentLMS courses first, then upload your certificates through the **CIM Laboratory Access Request** on TalentLMS, then email the supervisor below to confirm your access category and site.
</Warning>

### Required certificates by access tier

| Access tier | PHAC General Safety for Containment Labs | CITI Transport of Dangerous Goods (TDG) — Valid 2 years | CIM Lab SOP and WI Reader (Glen or MGH) — Valid 2 years | Specimen Processing Laboratory Safety Practices — Valid 1 year |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Specimens processing, storage, and shipping | Required | Required | Required | Required |
| Specimens storage and shipping only | — | Required | Required | Required |
| Pneumatic Transport System usage and specimen drop-off/pick-up only | — | — | — | Required |

MGH lab users complete the MGH SOP and WI Reader in place of the Glen version. All other requirements are the same.

### How to request access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your access tier">
    Use the table above to identify which tier applies to your role.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload your certificates">
    Upload your required external certificates through the **CIM Laboratory Access Request** on TalentLMS.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete all required TalentLMS training">
    Ensure you have completed **Specimen Processing Laboratory Safety Practices** and the **Glen** or **MGH SOP and WI Reader** as applicable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email the CIM Laboratory Supervisor">
    Email the CIM Laboratory and Test Center Supervisor with your required access category and the CIM lab location needed (Glen or MGH).

    **Randy Jacinto** — CIM Laboratory and Test Center Supervisor
    [randy.jacinto@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:randy.jacinto@muhc.mcgill.ca)
  </Step>
</Steps>
