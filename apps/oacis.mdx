OACIS is the MUHC electronic medical record (EMR) system. In the context of clinical research, it is accessed for two distinct purposes by two different groups — external monitors conducting source data verification, and research teams accessing records for pre-screening or retrospective research.

## What OACIS is used for in research

**External monitors and CRAs** use OACIS to verify source data during site monitoring visits. They need temporary restricted access to confirm that study data recorded in the CRF matches what is documented in the participant's medical record, and to verify participant existence across all MUHC sites.

**Researchers and study teams** may need access to OACIS before a participant has consented — for example, to pre-screen potentially eligible patients from medical records, or to conduct retrospective health records research. This use requires a separate authorization pathway that goes through the study submission process.

<Warning>
  Every person accessing OACIS for research purposes — whether as a monitor, CRC, or investigator — must sign the **MUHC Confidentiality and Security Agreement** before access is granted. This is an institutional requirement independent of any sponsor confidentiality agreement and applies to access to any MUHC systems, patient records, or identifiable participant information.
</Warning>

## Requesting access for external monitors

Under [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013), the CRC is responsible for ensuring that external monitors receive OACIS training and appropriate access before their first on-site monitoring visit. Access is temporary and restricted to the patient list for the specific study.

<Warning>
  Allow **five working days**. Medical Records requires a minimum of five working days' notice for all OACIS access requests — whether for a new ID, access to a new patient list, or additions to an existing list. Do not schedule a monitoring visit without confirming OACIS access is in place.
</Warning>

  <Accordion title="DPS Chart Request form">

  <Accordion title="QI/PI letter requesting OACIS access">
    Must specify the reason for access, the monitor's role, the study number, and a list of patient MRN numbers. The MRN list must be updated regularly as new participants are enrolled. [SOP-CR-013](/sops/cr-013) Appendix 2 — Monitor OACIS Access Request template available on the Portal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Copy of the study's MUHC Authorization letter" />
  <Accordion title="Copy of the study's Initial REB Approval letter" />

Submit the complete package to MUHC Medical Records at the Glen site. Medical Records will notify the OACIS team, who will create the monitor's OACIS ID with a termination date. OACIS training will be arranged for untrained monitors before their first visit. Document the monitor's OACIS training in the monitoring section of the ISF.

### Ongoing maintenance

- **Annual renewal:** The CRC or delegate must renew the monitor's OACIS ID each year by submitting the study's REB Annual Renewal approval to Medical Records.
- **Adding patients:** Submit updated MRN lists to Medical Records when new participants are enrolled. Allow five working days.
- **Monitor change:** When a monitor leaves the study, notify Medical Records immediately to remove their access. Arrange access for the incoming monitor before their first visit.

## Researcher access for pre-screening and records research

If your study requires access to OACIS to identify or pre-screen potential participants before they have given written informed consent — or if you are conducting retrospective health records research — you must obtain MUHC authorization through your study submission.

This is distinct from monitor access. It is governed by Quebec's Act respecting health and social services information (Law 5) and requires a privacy impact assessment.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Answer the pre-screening questions in your Nagano submission">
    In your Nagano submission, answer the questions about access to participant information and pre-screening activities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete a short-form EFVP">
    Complete an **EFVP** (Évaluation des facteurs relatifs à la vie privée) — a privacy impact assessment required under Quebec law whenever identifiable health information is accessed without consent. The form is completed in French and signed by the QI/PI.
  </Step>
  <Step title="MUHC Access to Information Officer review">
    The MUHC Access to Information Officer reviews the EFVP and forwards it to the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI) for authorization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="DPS approval">
    The Director of Professional Services (DPS) must separately approve the resource utilization, including use of medical records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  An EFVP is required not only for pre-screening without consent, but also when personal health data is transferred outside Quebec (including to a sponsor's servers or a CRO in another province), or when a technology product or service that handles personal data is acquired or developed for the study. For more information contact [efvp@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:efvp@muhc.mcgill.ca).
</Note>

## CIM monitor access via Citrix

For monitors working on studies at CIM, OACIS is accessed remotely through the MUHC Citrix environment (IRIAS3). This applies during monitoring visits where source data review is conducted remotely or from within CIM facilities.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Citrix Workspace App">
    Install **Citrix Workspace App** from [citrix.com/downloads/workspace-app](https://www.citrix.com/downloads/workspace-app/). Use Google Chrome. When prompted to enter a server name or email address at end of installation, cancel — nothing to enter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to the MUHC Citrix portal">
    Navigate to [irias3.muhc.mcgill.ca](https://irias3.muhc.mcgill.ca) and log in with MUHC network credentials provided by the CIM audit lead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the PIN">
    Enter the 4-digit PIN sent to your mobile phone on the "Additional Information Required" screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open OACIS">
    From the Citrix desktop, open the **Clinical Applications** folder, then the **OACIS-EMR** icon. Log in with your OACIS login and password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Important restrictions for CIM remote access:

  - External users cannot be connected to the institute's server while on remote access.
  - Do not download, save, print, or take screenshots of records from OACIS or the MUHC shared drive. Request any copies from the CIM audit host.
  - The system locks silently after three failed login attempts. Contact [c2system@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:c2system@muhc.mcgill.ca) if locked out.
  - Log out at the end of every session.
</Warning>

## Access rules and restrictions

OACIS access for research purposes is subject to the following rules regardless of access type:

- Access is restricted to records of participants enrolled in, or being considered for, the specific study for which access was granted. Browsing records beyond what is required for the study is prohibited.
- All research-related access must be authorized in advance — either through the Medical Records process (monitors) or through the Nagano study submission (researchers).
- The MUHC Confidentiality and Security Agreement must be signed before access is granted and covers obligations around data use, confidentiality incidents, and system security.
- OACIS medical records are retained for a minimum of 15 years. Signed informed consent forms filed in OACIS are part of the participant's permanent medical record.
- Access is subject to revocation if confidentiality obligations are not met.

## OACIS training

Researchers and research staff who need access to patient records in OACIS for study purposes may request OACIS training through the Health Records (Archives) department. Training is site-specific — contact the appropriate site directly.
