# F11 Part 9 — Agreement and PI Signature

The final part of the F11 before submission.

## Part 9: Agreement and PI Signature

This part covers the PI's research privileges and training status, any conflict of interest, additional comments for reviewers, and the PI's formal commitment and signature. Reviewers use this section to confirm that the PI is eligible to lead human participant research at the MUHC and that the study team meets institutional training requirements.

## Research privileges, status, and training

### Medical practice privileges (MUHC)

Indicate whether the PI holds medical practice privileges at the MUHC.

### Human Research Privileges and Human Researcher Status

Obtaining Human Research Privileges or Human Researcher Status is an MSSS requirement for conducting human subject research at the MUHC or any other hospital in Québec. Indicate which applies to the PI:

- **Human Research Privileges** — for physicians and dentists on the MUHC CPDP. Note: pharmacists are members of the CPDP but are not eligible for Privileges under MSSS and MUHC frameworks — they must obtain Human Researcher Status instead.
- **Human Researcher Status** — for individuals who are not members of the CPDP (e.g., PhDs, nurses, physiotherapists, psychologists, radiotherapists). Individuals with this status cannot conduct research involving human medical oversight.

<Note>
See the [RI-MUHC portal — Privileges and Status page](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:217) for details on eligibility and the process for obtaining Privileges or Status.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Research team members:** Fellows, residents, medical students, and others listed on the Task Delegation Log are not eligible for Human Research Privileges or Human Researcher Status — these apply to PIs, Sponsor-Investigators, and Sub-Is acting as PI backup only. All research personnel are still required to complete institutional training, regardless of eligibility for Privileges or Status.
</Warning>

### Required institutional training

The PI and all team members listed on the Task Delegation Log must complete the training required for the study's risk level and type. Feasibility reviewers may check that each person's training is appropriate for their assigned tasks.

| Training | When required |
|---|---|
| SOP Reader and Assessment | Required for all studies — level varies by study risk. Includes SOP Reader designation where applicable. See the RI-MUHC Clinical Research Training Hub for the training matrix. |
| Good Clinical Practice (GCP) | Required for clinical trials. See the [Additional Training Requirements](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:221) section of the RI-MUHC portal for the training matrix. |
| Health Canada Division 5 | Required for drug studies regulated under Health Canada Division 5. See the [Additional Training Requirements](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:221) section of the RI-MUHC portal for the training matrix. |
| ISO 14155 GCP (Medical Devices) | Required for medical device studies. See the [Additional Training Requirements](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:221) section of the RI-MUHC portal for the training matrix. |

<Note>
**MNI researchers:** Human research privileges and the SOP Reader and Assessment for MNI researchers are managed by MNI administration — not through the standard RI-MUHC process. Contact MNI administration for guidance.
</Note>

## Conflict of interest

Indicate whether you or any collaborators are in a real, potential, or perceived conflict of interest in relation to this study. This includes financial, professional, or institutional conflicts, as well as any payments or relationships with sponsors or commercial interests.

If yes, the REB will communicate with you about the declaration before granting final approval.

<Note>
When in doubt, disclose. A perceived conflict — even if you believe it does not affect your judgment — should be declared. The REB will assess it with full context.
</Note>

## Additional comments

Use this space to provide key context that will help reviewers interpret your submission — clarifications or study-specific considerations that do not fit elsewhere in the form.

- This field is **optional** but can be valuable for complex or unusual submissions.
- Use it to flag anything that might otherwise prompt a question from the REB — for example, a deviation from standard practice, a dependency on another study, or a clarification on jurisdiction.
- Do not repeat information already covered elsewhere in the form.

## Commitment and PI signature

This step confirms the PI's formal responsibility for the study at this institution. There are two ways to complete it, depending on who is submitting.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="PI is submitting directly — PI completes the signature step in Nagano">
    The PI logs in with their own Nagano credentials and completes the commitment and signature step directly within the form. No separate document is required.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Coordinator or collaborator is submitting — PI must sign a separate commitment form">
    The coordinator or collaborator preparing the submission cannot sign on behalf of the PI. The PI must complete and sign the **Principal Investigator's Commitment and Signature form**. Attach the signed form as instructed in Nagano before submitting.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
**Important:** The PI's commitment and signature must be in place before the submission is complete. Do not submit without confirming this step is done — missing or incomplete PI sign-off is one of the most common reasons for submission delays.
</Warning>

## Before you submit

Confirm each item is in order before submitting the F11.

- ✓ PI holds valid Human Research Privileges (MD/dentist) or Human Researcher Status (non-MD/dentist)
- ✓ Required institutional training is complete for the PI — and for delegated staff where applicable
- ✓ Conflict of interest declaration completed
- ✓ Additional comments added if any study-specific context is needed
- ✓ PI commitment and signature step completed — either directly in Nagano or via signed attachment
