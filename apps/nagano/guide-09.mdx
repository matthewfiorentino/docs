# F11 Part 7 — Feasibility

Part 7 of the F11 covers the institutional resources and services your study will use at the MUHC and MNI.

## Part 7: Feasibility

This part asks you to document the MUHC, MNI, and Institute resources your study requires — departments, nursing, clinical staff, space and equipment, laboratory services, pharmacy, and budget and contract information. Reviewers use this information to assess institutional readiness and route your submission to the relevant feasibility reviewers. Work through each subsection in order; where a resource is not applicable, indicate "No" or "None" as prompted.

## Section 1 — Departments involved

Add all MUHC departments where the project will primarily take place, and any department where participants are likely to receive care as a result of their participation.

- Include both the primary research site and any care-delivery departments that will be involved.
- Upload a signed **Department Head Authorization letter** for each additional department involved, as required by Nagano.

---

## Section 2 — MUHC Nursing resources

Indicate whether MUHC nurses will be involved in the research project.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Yes — MUHC nursing services required">
    Nagano will ask which aspects of the project nurses will be involved in, the estimated total hours for the entire project, and which setting(s) they will work in.

    You will also be asked whether the project involves **research product administration**. Your answer determines which Nursing Feasibility Review form to download, complete, have signed by the Nurse Manager, and upload.

    - **Investigational drug(s) or products involved** — Download the **Nursing Feasibility Review — Research involving INVESTIGATIONAL DRUG(S) OR PRODUCTS** form from the link in Nagano. Have it signed by the Nurse Manager, then upload it before submitting.
    - **No investigational products** — Download the **Nursing Feasibility Review — Research NOT involving investigational drug products** form from the link in Nagano. Have it signed by the Nurse Manager, then upload it before submitting.

    A pricelist for MUHC resources used in research studies is available in Nagano. For questions or services not listed, email the Research Resources office.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No — not required">
    If you have made arrangements for **CIM services** or have your own dedicated research nurse, and you will not require MUHC nursing services, answer No.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---

## Section 3 — Other MUHC human resources (clinical)

Document any MUHC-affiliated clinical staff involved in the study other than nurses — for example, technicians, psychologists, social workers, medical records staff, or administrative agents.

For each site involved:

1. Select the site.
2. Add each type of human resource.
3. Describe their role in the study.
4. Estimate the total hours required.

If no additional MUHC human resources are involved, select **"none"** as prompted.

---

## Section 4 — Material resources

Detail the material resources of the institution your study will use, per site. Use the **Add** button in Nagano if more than one site is involved.

For each site, select from the following options:

- Glen site (adult)
- Glen site — pediatric
- Montreal General Hospital
- Montreal Neurological Hospital
- Lachine Hospital
- Other affiliated hospitals or centres
- **There is no use of material resources of the institution for the research project** — select this if no material resources are required.

Once a site is selected, indicate which resource types apply:

- Exam or interview room
- Patient room (overnight hospitalization)
- Specialized equipment (e.g., CT scan, ECG, OR, PFT, CPET, etc.)

<Warning>
**Important — CIM resources:** If you plan to use CIM space, equipment, or services, confirm availability with CIM directly before completing this section. Do not assume CIM resources are available — availability must be verified before you indicate their use in Nagano.
</Warning>

A pricelist for MUHC resources used in research studies is available in Nagano. Use it to estimate budgetary needs before completing this section. For questions or services not listed in the pricelist, email the Research Resources office. Fees are reviewed annually; invoices are issued quarterly.

---

## Section 5 — Laboratory services

Indicate whether the project requires the use of MUHC clinical laboratory services — for example, testing and analyses, sample centrifugation or storage, retrieval of stored samples, out-of-hours services, or LIS/CERNER data extraction.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Yes — laboratory services required">
    Nagano will first ask whether the expected tests and analyses are also part of usual standard of care (SOC), or whether they are needed for study purposes only.

    - **All tests are also standard of care** — no further study-specific lab details are required.
    - **Tests needed for study purposes only** — Nagano will ask whether a request for lab services has already been transmitted.
      - **Request already transmitted:** indicate the site(s) where samples will be sent, provide the LIS lab number, and attach a copy of the Request for Laboratory Services and the list of tests submitted to the laboratory.
      - **Request not yet transmitted:** indicate the site(s), the services required (testing, blood procurement, centrifugation/storage, evening/weekend services, or other), attach a list of required tests, and indicate how samples will be collected and identified.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No — not required">
    If the project does not require MUHC clinical laboratory services, select No and proceed to the next section.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---

## Section 6 — Pharmacy services

Indicate whether the study involves a drug or natural product.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Yes — drug or natural product involved">
    Complete the pharmacy questions as prompted:
    - Study context (e.g., interventional trial, observational)
    - Drug supply source
    - Services required from the pharmacy
    - Dispensing details and site(s)

    Upload the **Pharmacy Manual** or other pharmacy-related documents as prompted.

    <Note>
      The Pharmacy Manual is distinct from the Investigator's Brochure. Upload pharmacy-specific handling documents here — not in the Project Files section.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No — not applicable">
    If the study does not involve a drug or natural product, select No and proceed to the next section.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

---

## Section 7 — MNI resources

Indicate whether the project will use MNI resources (PET, MRI, MEG).

If Yes, upload the required approval letter(s) from the appropriate MNI review committees for each resource type used.

---

## Section 8 — Budget aspects

Indicate whether the project requires financial resources and specify which institution will manage the funds.

If a budget is requested but not yet finalized, upload the most current available version in Project Files. Include known or negotiated costs wherever possible.

<Note>
Use the pricelist link in Nagano to estimate costs when confirming that funding covers all planned study activities. The REB service fee schedule (**Barème de facturation**) is available in the Documentation section of Nagano under "Billing" — account for these fees in your budget.
</Note>

### Industry-sponsored studies

- The **30% overhead calculation** in this section applies to industry-sponsored studies only.
- Budget negotiation with an industry sponsor or sponsor-investigator is the responsibility of the study team — it is not the role of the Research Agreements Office to prepare or negotiate the budget.
- For budget templates and guidance, consult the [Pre-Awards Office](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:477) on the RI-MUHC Portal (Resources and Templates page).

### No funding

If the project has no funding, the Pre-Awards Office can be consulted regarding possible funding opportunities.

---

## Section 9 — Contractual aspects

Indicate whether the project requires a contract or agreement — for example, arrangements involving external collaborators, transfers of samples, data or funds, devices or software, or service providers.

If Yes, Nagano will ask you to select the relevant contracts office. To decide between the **RI-MUHC Research Agreements Office** and the **McGill University Contracts Office**, consider: which institution manages the funds, where the investigator holds research privileges, where data is housed, and where the research takes place.

### RI-MUHC Research Agreements Office

Upload your contract(s) in Nagano, then also submit through **MyContracts** on the RI-MUHC Portal.

<Warning>
**Dual submission required:** Uploading a contract to Nagano does _not_ start the RI-MUHC contract review process. You must submit in **both Nagano and MyContracts** to trigger review. Submitting in only one place will result in delays.
</Warning>

### McGill University Contracts Office

Upload your contract(s) in Nagano and follow the prompts for billing address details.

<Note>
Questions about contracts? Email the Research Agreements Office.
</Note>

## Before you leave this section

Review the following before moving to Project Files:

- ✓ All departments involved are listed, with Department Head Authorization letters uploaded where required.
- ✓ Nursing involvement is answered; if Yes, activities, hours, and setting are complete, the correct Nursing Feasibility Review form (investigational products or standard) is signed by the Nurse Manager and uploaded.
- ✓ All other MUHC human resources are documented by site with role descriptions and hour estimates.
- ✓ CIM availability has been confirmed directly before indicating use of CIM space or equipment.
- ✓ Laboratory services requirements are described and options selected.
- ✓ If drugs or natural products are involved, pharmacy details and the Pharmacy Manual are complete and uploaded.
- ✓ MNI approval letters are uploaded for any PET, MRI, or MEG resources used.
- ✓ Budget is indicated, with costs estimated using the pricelist; REB service fees are accounted for.
- ✓ If a contract is required, it has been submitted in both Nagano and MyContracts.
