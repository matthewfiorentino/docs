# Creating a New Project in Nagano

Creating a project is your first action in Nagano. The selections you make here determine which F11 form is generated and how your submission is routed for review.

## Start from the My Projects dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to My Projects">
    Select **My Projects** in the navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new project">
    Click **New project**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Project creation

### Nagano identifier and project language

- **Nagano identifier (acronym)** — Enter a short label or acronym for your own tracking. This helps you find the right project quickly in My Projects, especially if you manage multiple studies.
- **Project language** — Select the language that best matches your submission materials: protocol, consent forms, and participant-facing documents.

## Ethics evaluation location

This field determines who is responsible for scientific and ethical evaluation. Select the option that matches your situation.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="MUHC is the lead site (or only site) — select the MUHC REB">
    Use this for a single-site study at the MUHC, or when the MUHC is the lead site for other Québec RSSS participating sites.

    <Note>
      **Single-site note:** If the MUHC is the only Québec RSSS site — even if sites outside Québec are also participating — the study is treated as single-site for MUHC REB purposes.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="MUHC is a participating site only — do not select the MUHC REB">
    The MUHC is a participating site in a study led by another Québec institution. The reviewing REB (RREB) of the lead institution handles scientific and ethical evaluation.

    <Note>
      Guidance on participating-site (MEO) submissions will be covered in a separate section of this user guide. Contact the Research Facilitator in the meantime.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Project type

This is the most important selection on this screen — it determines which F11 form Nagano generates. The menu may appear in French.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="F11-H — Clinical Trial / Clinical Research">
    Use this for **interventional trials and most forms of clinical research** submitted through the standard F11 pathway — drug trials, device studies, surgical interventions, process-of-care changes, and more. This is the form used as the example throughout the F11 sections of this user guide.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="F11-BR — Bank / Registry">
    Use this when **the bank or registry itself is what you're submitting** — an organized system for collecting and storing data or biospecimens for future research use. If you're using an existing bank rather than creating one, do not select this type.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="F11-NIR — Fundamental research">
    Use this for **experimental or theoretical research** aimed at generating new knowledge, without a primary focus on immediate clinical application.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="F11-NIR — Psychosocial / Qualitative / Nursing sciences">
    Use this when your main approach is **psychosocial, qualitative, or nursing-science oriented** — studies exploring lived experience, behaviour, communication, or care environments.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="F11-HRR — Retrospective chart review">
    Use this when the study **uses information already documented in health records**, with no direct participant contact and no new data collection.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="F11-NIR — Epidemiology / Health services / Global health">
    Use this when the study's main lens is **populations, health systems, or services** — including how care is organized and delivered, and impacts on access, quality, and outcomes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Varies — Imaging">
    Use this when **imaging is the primary focus** of the research question or when the study depends heavily on imaging resources such as PET, MRI, or MEG. The form generated may vary by local routing — confirm with the Research Facilitator if unsure.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
The F11 sections of this user guide use **F11-H (Clinical Trial / Clinical Research)** as the reference example. If your project type differs, the sections and questions in your form may vary.
</Note>

## Research field, sub-study, and team access

- **Main study / sub-study** — Leave blank for most submissions. If your study should be formally linked to a parent project, contact the Research Facilitator before selecting a parent.
- **Research field** — Select the closest match to your study's primary discipline. If nothing fits, choose _Other_ and clarify later in the submission.
- **Users with access** — Add team members who need access now: the PI, coordinator(s), and any collaborators involved in preparing the submission.

<Warning>
Ensure the PI has **administrator-level access** to the project. If the original creator later leaves the team, the PI must be able to manage access independently. Roles can be adjusted from the project dashboard after creation.
</Warning>

## Create the project and open your F11

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Create">
    You'll be returned to My Projects, where your new project appears under **My projects to follow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the F11 form">
    Open the generated **F11 form** to begin completing your submission.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Project ID formats

| Project type | ID format |
|---|---|
| Single-site MUHC | YYYY-##### |
| MUHC-led multi-site | MP-37-YYYY-#### |
| MUHC participating-site | MEO-XX-YYYY-#### |

<Note>
Always include the project ID when contacting support — it allows your request to be traced quickly and accurately.
</Note>

## Before you continue

Confirm each item is complete before opening the F11 form.

- ✓ Nagano identifier entered
- ✓ Project language selected
- ✓ Ethics evaluation location confirmed (MUHC REB or other)
- ✓ Correct project type selected — determines the F11 form
- ✓ PI has administrator-level access to the project
