# F11 Part 6 — Data Access (F17/F18/EFVP)

Part 6 of the F11 covers your project's access to health and social services information and determines whether a privacy process (F17, F18, or EFVP) is required.

## Part 6: Data Access (F17/F18/EFVP)

This part asks whether your project involves accessing health and social services information, under what consent conditions that access occurs, and whether supplementary privacy forms are required. Reviewers use this information to determine whether a Privacy Impact Assessment (EFVP) process needs to be initiated before authorization. Answering accurately here prevents delays later in feasibility review.

## Access to health and social services information

The first question asks whether any part of your project involves accessing health and social services information.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Yes — access to health and social services information">
    Answer **Yes** when any part of the project involves accessing health and social services information — especially if you will access medical records before consent or without consent.

    Answering Yes activates the three scenarios described in the next section. Identify which scenario applies to your project and complete the corresponding form in Nagano.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="No — no such access">
    Answer **No** only when your project collects research data with consent, without accessing health and social services information — for example, surveys, questionnaires, or focus groups — and you are not accessing identifiable information in records to determine eligibility.

    If you are unsure, consult the scenarios below or contact the Research Facilitator before answering No.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## If Yes — identifying your scenario

When you answer Yes, Nagano asks about the conditions under which access occurs. Three scenarios apply. Identify the one that fits your project — then take the corresponding action in Nagano.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Scenario A — Access before consent (pre-screening)">
    Any research team member accesses identifiable personal information in institutional records **before consent** to determine participant eligibility.

    **What this triggers:** Create, complete, and submit an **F17** in the project's Forms to begin the required privacy process.

    <Note>
      A guide for completing the F17 is available directly in Nagano.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Scenario B — Access without consent">
    Any research team member accesses and collects identifiable personal information **without first obtaining participant consent** — even if this applies to only a portion of the project.

    **What this triggers:** Create, complete, and submit an **F18** in the project's Forms to begin the EFVP (full privacy impact assessment) process.

    <Note>
      **Data Warehouse path:** If prompted, confirm whether you consulted the [RI-MUHC Data Warehouse (DW) Service](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:360). If all requested data can be obtained from DW in anonymized or aggregated form, an EFVP may not be required. If not, be ready to provide your DW ticket number and proceed with the EFVP requirements.
    </Note>

    <Warning>
      **IT Rescue request — separate step:** Even if you confirm it is possible to obtain data from the Data Warehouse, if you do require DW services you must also submit a separate [IT Rescue request](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:360) through the RI-MUHC Portal. This is in addition to what you indicate in Nagano.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Scenario C — Access only with consent">
    Access to health and social services information occurs **only after consent is obtained**, and the consent covers that access.

    No additional privacy form is triggered. Indicate this scenario in Nagano and ensure your consent documentation accurately reflects the scope of access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## RAMQ data access

If your project will access data held by the **Régie de l'assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ)**, an authorization specific to RAMQ data is required in addition to your standard ethics approval.

- You must obtain and upload an authorization letter giving you permission to access RAMQ data.
- More information on the RAMQ data access request process is available on the [RAMQ website](https://www.ramq.gouv.qc.ca/en/node/89631) (French only).

<Warning>
**Separate authorization required:** RAMQ data access is not covered by standard ethics approval. The RAMQ authorization is a distinct process and must be completed independently.
</Warning>

## Sharing data outside your organization

If the information covered by this data access request will be shared with a researcher or collaborator outside your organization, Nagano will direct you to complete the **Contractual aspects** section.

<Warning>
**Agreement may be required:** An agreement may need to be concluded before data can be shared externally. Do not assume data sharing can proceed without a completed agreement. Contact the Research Agreements Office if you have questions about what is required.
</Warning>

## Before you move on

Confirm the following before leaving this section.

- ✓ You have answered the health and social services information question (Yes or No).
- ✓ If Yes: you have identified the correct scenario (A, B, or C) and taken the corresponding action in Nagano.
- ✓ If Scenario A applies: an F17 has been created in the project's Forms.
- ✓ If Scenario B applies: an F18 has been created; Data Warehouse path has been considered; an IT Rescue request has been submitted if DW services are needed.
- ✓ If RAMQ data will be accessed: the separate RAMQ authorization process has been initiated.
- ✓ If data will be shared outside your organization: the Contractual aspects section has been addressed.
