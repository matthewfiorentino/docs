Nagano is the single point of entry for submitting research studies at the MUHC. The F11 form starts the institutional feasibility review and, where one applies, the ethics review by the Research Ethics Board (REB). The REB also carries out the [scientific review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#scientific-and-ethics-review-by-the-reb), and may recognize a peer review already done by a recognized committee, such as a granting agency's. The institutional feasibility review starts at the same time as the REB's review. Both REB approval and MUHC Authorization from the Personne mandatée must be in place before any research activity begins.

<Warning>
  **MUHC Authorization is not the same as REB approval.** A study cannot commence until the Personne mandatée grants final MUHC Authorization, which follows REB approval. Both approvals must be in place before any research begins.
</Warning>

## Access Nagano

How you get into Nagano depends on whether you already have an account:

- If you already have access, log in at [nagano.muhc.mcgill.ca](https://nagano.muhc.mcgill.ca). For access issues, write to [info.nagano@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:info.nagano@muhc.mcgill.ca).
- If you need access, your institutional training must be complete before you request it. Once your training is confirmed, complete the [Nagano Access Request Form](https://forms.office.com/r/Uau6LgNbNL). You will receive a username and temporary password after approval.

## What Nagano does

A single F11 submission triggers whichever of the three review streams apply to your study. You do not need to manage each review separately — Nagano routes your submission automatically based on your project type and the information you provide.

A submission can start three reviews:

- The scientific review confirms that the study is scientifically sound.
- In the ethics review, the Research Ethics Board reviews the protocol, consent documents, recruitment materials and all participant-facing content.
- The institutional feasibility review confirms that MUHC resources, services and infrastructure are in place to support the study, and leads to MUHC Authorization by the Personne mandatée.

Researchers and coordinators also use Nagano throughout the study lifecycle to submit amendments, report protocol deviations and reportable events, request OACIS access for external monitors, and manage annual renewals and study close-out.

## Before you create a project

Confirm all three of the following before opening Nagano. Missing any of them will stall the submission at feasibility review.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="QI/PI level — Research Privileges or Researcher Status">
    The Qualified or Principal Investigator must hold **RI-MUHC Privileges or Researcher Status** and it must be current. This designation confirms the PI is authorized to conduct human research at the MUHC.

    - **Human Research Privileges** — for physicians and dentists on the MUHC CPDP
    - **Human Researcher Status** — for researchers who are not physicians or dentists (e.g., PhDs, nurses, physiotherapists, psychologists)

    Who is eligible for Privileges or Researcher Status, and how to apply, is under [Apply for Privileges or Researcher Status](/training/compliance-requirements#apply-for-privileges-or-researcher-status).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Team level — Institutional training complete">
    **Institutional training must be complete and current** for everyone who will be on the Task Delegation Log, at the level required for the study type. During the institutional feasibility review, [Quality Assurance](mailto:QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca) checks that each investigator who submits the study holds Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status and the level of institutional training the study requires.

    Training level is determined by the highest-risk study you are involved in. Both the SOP Reader and Assessment must be completed through TalentLMS. See [TalentLMS](/apps/talentlms) for access and [Training and Compliance Requirements](/training/compliance-requirements#training-requirements-by-study-level) for the full requirements by level.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Documents — Core study documents ready for upload">
    **Core study documents should be ready for upload** as part of the F11 submission. At minimum, you should have:

    - A finalized protocol (or the version you are ready to submit)
    - Informed consent form(s)
    - Recruitment materials
    - Health Canada's letter, for a trial that needs one. [For some trials, the letter can come after the submission](/kb/submission/submission-overview#health-canada-authorization).

    For the full list of documents to upload with the F11, see [Prepare the documents](/kb/submission/preparing#prepare-the-documents).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
