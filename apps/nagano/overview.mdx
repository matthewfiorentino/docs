---

Nagano is the single point of entry for submitting research studies at the MUHC. A single F11 form routes your project through scientific review, REB ethics review, and institutional feasibility review simultaneously — and both REB approval and MUHC Authorization from the Personne mandatée must be in place before any research activities can begin.

<Warning>
  **MUHC Authorization is not the same as REB approval.** A study cannot commence until the Personne mandatée grants final MUHC Authorization, which follows REB approval. Both approvals must be in place before any research begins.
</Warning>

## What Nagano does

A single F11 submission triggers whichever of the three review streams apply to your study. You do not need to manage each review separately — Nagano routes your submission automatically based on your project type and the information you provide.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Scientific review" icon="flask">
    Confirms the study is scientifically sound before ethics review proceeds. Applied where required based on study type.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ethics review (REB)" icon="shield-halved">
    The Research Ethics Board reviews the protocol, consent documents, recruitment materials, and all participant-facing content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Institutional feasibility" icon="building-columns">
    Confirms that MUHC resources, services, and infrastructure are in place to support the study. Leads to MUHC Authorization by the Personne mandatée.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Researchers and coordinators also use Nagano throughout the study lifecycle to submit amendments, report protocol deviations and reportable events, request OACIS access for external monitors, and manage annual renewals and study close-out.

## Before you create a project

Confirm all three of the following before opening Nagano. Missing any of them will stall the submission at feasibility review.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="QI/PI level — Research Privileges or Researcher Status">
    The Qualified or Principal Investigator must hold **RI-MUHC Privileges or Researcher Status** and it must be current. This designation confirms the PI is authorized to conduct human research at the MUHC.

    - **Human Research Privileges** — for physicians and dentists on the MUHC CPDP
    - **Human Researcher Status** — for researchers who are not physicians or dentists (e.g., PhDs, nurses, physiotherapists, psychologists)

    See [Human Research Privileges and Researcher Status](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:217) on the RI-MUHC Portal for eligibility and the application process.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Team level — Institutional training complete">
    **Institutional training must be complete and current** for everyone who will be on the Task Delegation Log, at the level required for the study type. Feasibility reviewers check training records as part of the review.

    Training level is determined by the highest-risk study you are involved in. Both the SOP Reader and Competency Assessment must be completed through TalentLMS. See [TalentLMS](/apps/talentlms) for access and [Training and Compliance Requirements](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:221) for the full requirements by level.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Documents — Core study documents ready for upload">
    **Core study documents should be ready for upload** as part of the F11 submission. At minimum, you should have:

    - A finalized protocol (or the version you are ready to submit)
    - Informed consent form(s)
    - Recruitment materials
    - Any Health Canada authorization documents (e.g., No Objection Letter), if applicable

    For the full checklist by study type, consult the [Study Submission page](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:220) on the RI-MUHC Portal.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
