## What a CTMS does

A Clinical Trial Management System (CTMS) is the operational hub for running a study once it is approved and activated. It centralizes the day-to-day information that research teams need to manage their work:

- **Participant tracking** — enrollment status, eligibility, consent dates, and visit history
- **Visit scheduling** — upcoming visits, completed visits, missed or rescheduled appointments
- **Protocol adherence** — protocol deviations, waivers, and the events that trigger them
- **Team and delegation records** — who is authorized to perform which study tasks
- **Site-level reporting** — enrollment metrics, data completion rates, and study status summaries

At sites that use a CTMS, the system complements other tools: Nagano handles regulatory submissions and REB review; REDCap handles data collection and eCRF; the CTMS handles the operational layer in between.
