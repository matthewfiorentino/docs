Health Canada regulates the studies conducted under its drug, natural health product and medical device regulations, whether or not a study needs its authorization. [Check whether Health Canada regulates your study, and whether the study needs its authorization](/paths#check-whether-health-canada-must-authorize-the-study).

Like any other study, a study regulated by Health Canada follows the Knowledge Base pages that apply to its training level. For a study at Levels I to III, the Knowledge Base gives the steps by stage of the study, starting from [The Study Lifecycle](/kb/foundations/study-lifecycle). If Health Canada inspects your study, [prepare your team, documents and facility, host the inspector and respond to the inspection report](/kb/conduct/safety-reporting/hc-inspection-guide).

If you can't tell which kind of study yours is, the [Research Facilitator](mailto:research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca) can help.
