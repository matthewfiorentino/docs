<Tip>
  **Before you start:** make sure your project is research and not a QA/QI initiative. The screening questions on [Is It Research?](/is-it-research) help you decide this. The MUHC REB retains the right to make the final determination, whatever the screening questions indicate. Quality assurance and quality improvement projects used only for assessment, management or improvement [fall outside REB review](https://ethics.gc.ca/eng/tcps2-eptc2_2022_chapter2-chapitre2.html) and follow a different path.
</Tip>
