# Double Identification of Research Subjects and Independent Double Check of Medication Prior to Administration

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-CR001_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective — First approved version</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All CIM Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI defines the mandatory procedures for verifying research subject identity using two approved identifiers before any study-related activity, and for performing an independent double check (IDC) of medication — covering the five rights of safe administration — prior to every infusion or drug administration.
</Note>

## Key points

- All qualified trained staff (QTS) must verify the identity of any unfamiliar research subject using at least two approved identifiers before starting any study-related activity (consent, treatment, specimen collection, imaging, exams).
- The first identifier is always the research subject's **full legal name**, confirmed verbally or from an approved source; the second identifier is one of: MRN, date of birth, or home address.
- When a patient cannot self-identify (e.g., pediatric), identification may be performed with a family member, legal guardian, or by reference to MRN/Medicare card/driver's licence.
- Never ask "Is your name Mr. John Doe?" — always ask the subject to state their information.
- An **Independent Double Check (IDC)** must be performed before every medication administration, verifying the five rights: right patient, right medication, right dose/concentration, right route, and right time — plus the correct nursing assessment.
- IP labels must be retained and attached to source documents after each administration.
- Errors in subject identification or in any of the five rights must be investigated and rectified immediately; corrective and preventive actions must be implemented where applicable.
- Extra precautions (e.g., alert flags) are required when research subjects share similar or identical names.
