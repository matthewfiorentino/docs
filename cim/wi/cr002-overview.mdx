# External Safety Report (SUSAR) Review by Investigators

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-CR002_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 13 May 2022</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Qualified Investigators / CIM Research Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This work instruction defines how Qualified Investigators at the CIM must review and process external safety reports — specifically Suspected Unexpected Serious Adverse Reactions (SUSARs) — received from sponsors during the conduct of a clinical study.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Controlled document.** The full signed WI document is available from CIM management. Contact **research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca** for access.
</Warning>

Effective 13-May-2022 · WI-CIM-CR002_01

---

## Key points

- Qualified Investigators are responsible for reviewing all SUSARs distributed by the sponsor and taking any required actions.
- SUSAR review must be documented — the signed training record form confirms the investigator has read and understood each report.
- Unblinding decisions following a SUSAR must follow the protocol-specified procedure and be documented in the study records.
- All SUSAR communications and investigator responses must be retained in the Investigator Site File (ISF).
- Investigators must assess whether a SUSAR affects the risk-benefit assessment for ongoing participants and determine if protocol or consent form amendments are required.
