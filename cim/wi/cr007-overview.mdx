# Investigator Site File and Essential Documents

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-CR007_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 24 Oct 2022</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All CIM Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI describes the requirements for the creation, maintenance, annual renewal, and archival of the study Investigator Site File (ISF), including its structure and content. It applies to all qualified trained staff involved in clinical studies and handling study documents at the CIM, RI-MUHC, and covers both the paper-based and electronic sections of the hybrid ISF.

## Key points

- A study-specific ISF is required for every clinical study protocol; it must be prepared once site selection has been confirmed by the Sponsor.
- The ISF is maintained as a **hybrid** consisting of handwritten original records and electronic records (scanned originals and electronic documents), stored in a secure location with access limited to research personnel.
- Essential documents must be filed within **5 business days** of receipt to ensure successful management of the study.
- The **CIM ISF Index** (Appendix 1) tracks 15 sections covering all essential document categories and their location (paper binder or electronic folder); no section may be removed from the binder.
- File naming in the electronic ISF must avoid special characters and spaces; keep filenames under 31 characters where possible; use the convention `DocumentName_ProtocolName_SiteID_YYYYMMDD`.
- The Regulatory Coordinator (REG) is responsible for creating and maintaining the electronic ISF folder structure and the paper-based binder; the CRC files communications and updated study-specific documents; the RC-E handles REB submission filings.
- Pharmacy records (IP shipping, receipt, accountability logs, pharmacy training records) are maintained by the delegated pharmacist during the study and must be archived as part of the ISF at study closure.
- Once the REB acknowledges the termination report and it is sent to the Sponsor/CRO, no further ISF activities (paper or electronic) are permitted. Archiving follows the Record Retention and Archival Working Instruction.
