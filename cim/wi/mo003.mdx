## 1 · Purpose and scope {#purpose-and-scope}

Site monitoring fulfills the Sponsor's obligation to oversee clinical trials (ICH E6 R2, Section 5.18). The site monitor is the primary line of communication between the Sponsor and the site. This WI covers preparation, conduct, and follow-up of Site Monitoring Visits (SMVs), as well as remote monitoring and regular site contact.

---

## 2 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Monitor (CRA) | Prepares for and conducts the SMV, performs SDV, completes the visit report, issues follow-up letter, documents issue resolution |
| Site Personnel (Study Coordinator, PM) | Signs Monitoring Visit Log, provides access to source documents, resolves action items |
| Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator | Authorizes additional/unscheduled visits; reviews and approves visit reports |

## 4 · Instructions {#instructions}

### 5.1 Site Monitoring Visit Preparation {#site-monitoring-visit-preparation}

**Step 5.1.1** Determine the site's level of performance and areas of concern based on conduct of the current trial.

**Step 5.1.2** Review the most recent monitoring visit reports and ensure all open issues have been addressed prior to the visit.

**Step 5.1.3** Determine if subject screening data can be reviewed; timing is based on the amount of screening data required by the protocol and the extent of remote review performed.

**Step 5.1.4** Ensure trial worksheets and source documents have version dates and have been reviewed/finalized prior to use.

**Step 5.1.5** Identify the Protocol (including any amendments), ICF, and IB versions currently in use; ensure the most recent REB-approved versions are in use at the site.

**Step 5.1.6** Bring a list of documents to assist in review of the ISF or electronic version.

**Step 5.1.7** Review study documents (Protocol, ICF, IB, Monitoring Plan, Data Entry Guidelines, Laboratory and Pharmacy Manuals, etc.) and ensure the site has received the most current versions.

**Step 5.1.8** Identify the anticipated number of subjects to be reviewed.

**Step 5.1.9** Contact the site to confirm the date of the monitoring visit, followed by a Site Monitoring Visit Confirmation E-mail (Appendix 2).

**Step 5.1.10** Confirm with the site that access to subjects' charts and source documents (paper and/or electronic) will be provided.

### 5.2 Site Monitoring Visit Conduct {#site-monitoring-visit-conduct}

During the SMV, both monitor and site personnel must sign the Monitoring Visit Log or equivalent.

#### 5.2.1 Subject Recruitment

(Refer to SOP-CR-009 Subject Recruitment and Screening)

**Step 5.2.1.1** Evaluate subjects' screening and enrollment; confirm plan and timelines for recruitment and enrollment of additional subjects. Refer to section 1 of the Site Monitoring Visit Report (Appendix 1).

**Step 5.2.1.2** Verify that all screening procedures are done and documented.

**Step 5.2.1.3** Verify that a Subject Identification Log or equivalent is maintained at the site.

**Step 5.2.1.4** Document any other items in section 1 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.2 Subject Consent and Eligibility

(Refer to SOP-CR-006 and SOP-CR-008)

**Step 5.2.2.1** Verify subjects are consented prior to any study procedures (including screening) and that the current REB-approved ICF version is used.

**Step 5.2.2.2** Verify original signed ICFs are maintained in the ISF/patient binder and that a signed copy was given to the subject or legal representative.

**Step 5.2.2.3** If the study involves IP, verify a copy of the signed ICF has been provided to medical records.

**Step 5.2.2.4** Verify eligibility criteria are met and documented before randomization/enrollment.

**Step 5.2.2.5** Verify all randomized/enrolled subjects are captured on an Enrollment Log.

**Step 5.2.2.6** Document any other items in section 2 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.3 Study Conduct

**Step 5.2.3.1** Verify facilities, resources, and study-specific materials remain adequate throughout the duration of the study.

**Step 5.2.3.2** Verify changes in the research team or delegation of tasks have been documented in the Tasks Delegation Log (SOP-CR-002). Document any staff changes in Appendix 1.

**Step 5.2.3.3** Verify all required qualifications/training are obtained and documented (SOP-CR-003).

**Step 5.2.3.4** Verify the Investigator follows the most recent REB-approved protocol version and any deviations are captured on a Protocol Deviation Log (SOP-CR-007).

**Step 5.2.3.5** Verify only authorized and trained personnel record data on source forms and make corrections as documented in the Tasks Delegation Log.

**Step 5.2.3.6** Verify the Investigator provides all required reports, notifications, applications, and submissions in a timely, complete, accurate, legible, signed, and dated manner.

**Step 5.2.3.7** Verify the Investigator documents medical oversight by initialing and dating (in a timely fashion) all test reports, monitoring letters, SUSAR reports, and any other documents requiring medical oversight.

**Step 5.2.3.8** Verify the Investigator assesses adverse events for intensity, seriousness, incidence, and causality; verify AEs and SAEs are recorded and reported as per SOP-CR-012 and WI-CIM-MO006.

**Step 5.2.3.9** Document any other items in section 3 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.4 Research Ethics Board (REB)

(Refer to SOP-CR-007)

**Step 5.2.4.1** Verify all required documentation has been submitted to the REB in a timely fashion and that approval letters and correspondence are filed in the ISF.

**Step 5.2.4.2** Verify REB continuing review has been submitted in a timely manner. Confirm no subjects were seen or recruited during any lapse in REB approval; if they were, verify this was documented as a violation and reported to the REB.

**Step 5.2.4.3** Verify that serious, unexpected AEs possibly related to the trial have been reported to the REB within **7 days** (10 days for medical devices).

**Step 5.2.4.4** Verify all protocol deviations are captured on the Protocol Deviation Log and submitted to the REB appropriately (violations submitted ASAP; exceptions submitted with REB continuing review).

**Step 5.2.4.5** Verify that any changes to the conduct of the trial have been provided to the REB.

**Step 5.2.4.6** Verify appropriate follow-up of adverse events is conducted and documented for subjects no longer participating.

**Step 5.2.4.7** Document any other items in section 4 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.5 Case Report Form (CRF) and Data Clarification Form (DCF) Processing

(Refer to SOP-CR-014)

**Step 5.2.5.1** Verify only authorized and trained personnel perform data entry and corrections as documented in the Tasks Delegation Log.

**Step 5.2.5.2** Verify all protocol-required data are reported accurately on the CRF/eCRF and are consistent with source documents.

**Step 5.2.5.3** Verify missing/out-of-window procedures, visits, subject withdrawals, and discontinuations are reported and explained.

**Step 5.2.5.4** Verify source documents are adequately maintained and SDV is performed as per the monitoring plan. Refer to the SDV list in Appendix 1.

**Step 5.2.5.5** For paper CRFs, verify data entry, correction, and DCF are done per study protocol/instruction and Good Documentation Practice.

**Step 5.2.5.6** Verify paper CRF pages have been reviewed and sent to the Sponsor as per study requirements.

**Step 5.2.5.7** Document any other items in section 5 of Appendix 1.

**Step 5.2.5.8** For electronic CRFs, verify documentation is on file showing the system is validated, password-protected, backed up, and has an audit trail.

#### 5.2.6 Investigational Product (IP)/Device and Randomization

Refer to WI-CIM-MO005 Site Monitoring of Investigational Product Management for further instructions.

**Step 5.2.6.1** Document any other items in section 6 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.7 Laboratory

**Step 5.2.7.1** Ensure biological specimens are collected, processed, stored, and shipped as per protocol/laboratory manual (SOP-CR-011). Document any changes in section 7 of Appendix 1.

**Step 5.2.7.2** Document any other items in section 7 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.8 Equipment Inspection/Calibration and Maintenance

**Step 5.2.8.1** Verify there is an inventory of study equipment, proper documentation of inspection/calibration and maintenance, as per SOP-CR-025. Document any equipment changes or malfunctions in section 8 of Appendix 1.

**Step 5.2.8.2** Document any other items in section 8 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.9 Investigator Study Files

**Step 5.2.9.1** Verify the site has all pertinent essential regulatory documents on file as per ICH-GCP section 8 (SOP-CR-015, Appendix 1).

**Step 5.2.9.2** Verify the site has received the current version of the Protocol and Amendments, Investigator's Brochure, and all study documents needed for proper trial conduct.

**Step 5.2.9.3** Verify the ISF is appropriately maintained and kept in a secure location. Refer to the Verification of Regulatory Documents checklist in Appendix 1.

**Step 5.2.9.4** Document any other items in section 9 of Appendix 1.

At the conclusion of the monitoring visit, the monitor discusses findings and appropriate course of action with the Investigator/Study Coordinator.

### 5.3 Site Monitoring Visit Follow Up {#site-monitoring-visit-follow-up}

**Step 5.3.1** Complete the Site Monitoring Visit Report within **10 working days** after the visit.

**Step 5.3.2** Monitoring reports must not include specific trial data or subjective observations and opinions.

**Step 5.3.3** Send the Site Monitoring Visit Report to the Project Manager, Sponsor, or Sponsor-Investigator for review and approval.

**Step 5.3.4** The Project Manager, Sponsor, or Sponsor-Investigator reviews and approves the report and Follow-up Letter Template (Appendix 3) as necessary.

**Step 5.3.5** It is the monitor's responsibility to:
- Take action(s) on significant issues identified
- Follow up with the trial site on issues and/or corrective actions
- Document resolution of issues
- Communicate trial progress or issues to local management and/or clinical team

**Step 5.3.6** After report review is completed, the monitor sends the follow-up letter (Appendix 3) to the site for filing in the ISF.

### 5.4 Regular Contact with Site {#regular-contact-with-site}

**Step 5.4.1** The monitor will maintain a site contact list and document all contacts with each site as per the study agreement.

**Step 5.4.2** The monitor will visit sites based on the schedule outlined in the agreement and/or monitoring plan. If additional visits are needed (concerns about data integrity or subject safety), the monitor must request Sponsor authorization before performing the visit.

### 5.5 Remote Monitoring {#remote-monitoring}

Remote monitoring will be performed if specified in the Study Monitoring Plan.

**Step 5.5.1** The monitor should review the EDC for:
- Missing data, inconsistent data, data outliers, unexpected lack of variability, and protocol deviations
- Data trends (range, consistency, variability) within and across sites
- Systematic or significant errors in data collection and reporting; potential data manipulation or integrity problems
- Site characteristics and performance metrics
- Selection of sites and/or processes for targeted on-site monitoring
- AEs or suspected ADRs/ADEs on a regular basis to confirm if AEs have lessened, remain stable, or escalated to SAEs
- Query generation and closure
- Follow-up with site on outstanding items
