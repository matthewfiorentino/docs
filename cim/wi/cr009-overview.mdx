# Study-Specific and General Source Documents

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-CR009_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 18 Oct 2022</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All CIM Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI describes the requirements for the creation and management of study-specific and general source documents at the CIM, RI-MUHC. It applies to all qualified trained staff involved in clinical studies and covers study-specific visit worksheets (screening, treatment, follow-up, survival follow-up) and general CIM-managed documents (adverse event log, concomitant medication log, discontinuation summary, withdrawal summary, statement of death, and medical history/baseline symptom log), as well as those not managed by CIM study staff.
</Note>

<Warning>
**Controlled document.** Always refer to this page for the current version. Do not rely on downloaded or printed copies.
</Warning>

## Key points

- Study-specific source document templates must be adapted per protocol; only text or boxes marked in green may be modified — black boxes must not be removed (use "NA" if not required).
- Each study-specific source document template must include a footer with a version date and current MUHC protocol version/date; versioning is tracked using the **Source Document Template Version Tracker** (Appendix 1).
- A second qualified site staff member must perform a QC review of each study-specific source document created against the protocol; both the creator and the QC reviewer must initial and date the first page footer.
- If a protocol amendment modifies how an assessment is performed or adds data to collect, the relevant source documents must be reviewed and updated (then re-versioned); superseded versions must be moved to the archived folder in the ISF.
- Investigator or Sub-Investigator must complete the Investigator sections of visit source documents contemporaneously and sign/date reports and logs within **5 business days** of the subject visit.
- Laboratory results, pathology, and imaging reports must be printed and signed by the Investigator upon receipt or within **5 business days** of the visit.
- Medical History/Baseline Symptom, Adverse Event, and Concomitant Medication logs must be entered/updated at the time of the subject visit; the QI or Sub-I must sign the Investigator section within 5 business days of the visit.
- The CRC and RN must review each other's completed logs and source documents to ensure all required documents are complete, signed, dated, and ready for data entry within 5 business days of the subject visit.
