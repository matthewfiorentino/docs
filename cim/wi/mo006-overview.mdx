# Monitoring of Safety Assessment & Adverse Events / Serious Adverse Events Reporting

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-MO006_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 06 Feb 2023</Badge> <Badge color="blue">CIM Monitors / CIM Research Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI describes the review of patient safety assessments and reporting during remote and site monitoring activities to ensure that AEs, SAEs, Adverse Drug Reactions (ADRs), Medical Device Incidents (MDIs), Serious Adverse Drug Reactions (SADRs), and Serious Medical Device Incidents (SMDIs) are complete, accurate, and reported to the Sponsor, REB, and regulatory authorities (Health Canada, FDA, EMA) within required timeframes.
</Note>

## Key points

- AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI monitoring may be conducted centrally and remotely via signal surveillance as well as during on-site monitoring visits through source data verification.
- The QI/PI is responsible for patient assessment and reporting of all AEs, SAEs, MDIs, and SMDIs to the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator, and if reportable, to the REB.
- At the SIV, the monitor trains site staff on AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI management, reporting procedures, and timelines per SOP-CR-012 and SOP-CR-024.
- If an SAE/SMDI occurs, the QI/PI or delegate must inform the Sponsor-Investigator within **24 hours** of the site's knowledge of the event; the relatedness assessment for each IP must be completed as soon as possible.
- Reportable unexpected SAEs and MDIs must be reported to the REB within **7 days** for drugs and **10 days** for medical devices (SOP-CR-012, SOP-CR-024).
- Reportable unexpected SADRs/SMDIs must be reported to Health Canada within **7 days** from when the Sponsor-Investigator became aware of the event, using the CIOMS-1 form.
- During monitoring visits, source data verification of AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI logs confirms completeness, consistency, and accuracy; any unreported SAEs/MDIs must be escalated and a deviation documented and submitted to the REB.
- At the Close-Out Visit, the monitor verifies all AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI documentation is complete, follow-up is performed for all ongoing events, and all documents are archived with the ISF and the Sponsor's TMF.
