# Site Feasibility Visit

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-MO001_05</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 05</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective Refer to WI Header</Badge> <Badge color="blue">CIM Monitors / CIM Research Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI describes the end-to-end process for conducting site feasibility visits, from preliminary site assessment and distribution of questionnaires, through the on-site visit, to the follow-up letter and sponsor qualification decision. It applies to both internal and external investigational sites.

## Key points

- Before scheduling a visit, the Monitor or PM sends the Short Preliminary Site Questionnaire (Appendix 3) and a Confidentiality Agreement to potential sites via the RI-MUHC contracts portal.
- The Site Feasibility Visit Confirmation E-mail (Appendix 2) must be sent to the site; confirmation of visit timing must be received within 2 weeks.
- During the visit the monitor assesses five areas using Appendix 1: (1) subject population/recruitment, (2) investigator and staff qualifications, (3) equipment/facilities, (4) data collection capability, and (5) regulatory compliance/administrative procedures.
- The Site Feasibility Visit Report (Appendix 1) must be completed and submitted to the Sponsor within **10 working days** of the visit.
- An approval or refusal letter (Appendix 4) is sent to the site; if approved, the follow-up letter includes any required action items for the Investigator to file in the ISF.
- The Investigator must sign and date the Site Feasibility Visit Follow-Up Letter (Appendix 4) and all outstanding issues must be resolved before contract/budget negotiations begin.
- If a feasibility visit is not performed, a Note to File documenting the reason must be uploaded to the TMF.
- Each investigator must provide a current CV and valid medical licence as evidence of suitability.
