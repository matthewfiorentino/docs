# Code Red Emergency Preparation and Code Green Evacuation Plan (Glen Site)

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-AD002_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="red">Emergency Preparedness</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All CIM Staff — Glen Site</Badge>

<Note>
This work instruction covers the Code Red (fire) and Code Green (evacuation) emergency procedures for CIM personnel and users at the Glen site (Blocks B4 and C4). It defines the responsibilities of all personnel on CIM premises, the CIM Emergency Team Lead, and CIM Emergency Floor Monitors.

## Key points

- **All CIM personnel must never put their lives in peril.** If the situation becomes dangerous, leave the area regardless of where you are in the emergency process.
- **Code Red Alert** = fire not yet confirmed; fire alarm does not sound. Listen to MUHC public address system.
- **Code Red Alarm** = fire confirmed; alarm sounds; elevators return to ground floor; fire doors close; key-card doors deactivate.
- **Code Green** = order to evacuate; may be horizontal (to adjacent block beyond fire doors) or vertical (emergency stairs to RC assembly point at "Havre" sculpture).
- If with a patient during an interrupted procedure, stop the procedure if possible, document the interruption in study records, and prioritize patient safety.
- The **CIM Emergency Team Lead** coordinates evacuation decisions and communicates via the CIM public address system.
- **CIM Emergency Floor Monitors** prepare personnel lists, guide evacuees, and confirm headcount at the assembly point.
- Ensure any study records are stored to prevent water damage (e.g., in filing cabinets) in the event of sprinkler activation.
- Notify the QI and Sponsor of any study-related interruptions upon return.
