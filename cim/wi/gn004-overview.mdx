# Preparing and Administering Intravenous Infusions Other Than Chemotherapy and Biotherapy; Primary, Secondary (mini-bag) and Syringe Infusion

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-GN004_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 10 May 2022</Badge> <Badge color="blue">CIM Nursing Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI standardizes the preparation and administration of intravenous infusions (other than chemotherapy and biotherapy) for clinical trials at the CIM, covering primary IV line setup, secondary mini-bag piggyback administration, syringe infusion by pump or IV push, inline filter use, and infusion set replacement schedules.

## Key points

- Use ANTT (aseptic non-touch technique) throughout all IV procedures; protect critical parts (catheter material, hub, insertion site) from beginning to end.
- Label all IV bags/syringes with protocol number, subject number, subject initials, medication name and dosage, date and time prepared, and the QTS's initials/date. Do not write directly on the IV bag with a pen/marker — use a label.
- For primary IV line setup: inspect bag for damage and particles, close roller clamp before spiking, fill drip chamber 2/3 full, prime tubing via pump function or gravity, and cap the end with a sterile end-cap once primed.
- For secondary (mini-bag) infusion: confirm compatibility with the primary solution, use the Y port **above** the pump segment, back prime by lowering the secondary bag below the primary bag, and hang the secondary bag at least 8 inches higher than the primary bag during infusion.
- For syringe infusion by pump: connect to the microbore tubing, install per pump instructions, and rinse with primary compatible solution at the end; for IV push, scrub the Y port with chlorhexidine-alcohol swab, verify blood return before injecting, and rinse at the same rate as the drug administration.
- If a secondary medication infuses at greater than 300 mL/hour, clamp (slide clamp) the primary tubing above the Y port where the secondary line is connected.
- Filter selection: 0.2 micron for standard solutions (do not invert during priming unless package specifies); 1.2 micron for lipid emulsions or medications with larger particles (do not invert unless protocol specifies).
- Replace infusion sets every 96 hours (primary, secondary, microbore, add-on devices); sets used for lipids must be replaced at every bag change (minimum every 24 hours).
