# Site Initiation Visit

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-MO002_05</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 05</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective Refer to WI Header</Badge> <Badge color="blue">CIM Monitors / CIM Research Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI describes the end-to-end process for the Site Initiation Visit (SIV), from pre-visit preparation and monitoring plan finalization through the visit itself (covering 14 topic areas) to post-visit reporting. It includes the RI-MUHC/CIM Monitoring Plan Template as Appendix 3. Subject recruitment must not start until the SIV is complete.

## Key points

- A Monitoring Plan must be finalized and signed before an SIV is scheduled; if the Sponsor lacks a template, the RI-MUHC/CIM template (Appendix 3) may be used.
- If sites upload certified copies to a cloud repository, the Monitoring Plan must be updated to reflect remote monitoring and include a privacy risk assessment; the informed consent must be updated accordingly.
- The monitor must confirm REB, Health Authority, and any other site-specific approval is in place before the visit.
- All study personnel present at the SIV must sign the Training Log (SOP-CR-003, Appendix 1).
- The SIV covers 14 mandatory topic areas: staffing/equipment/facilities, study protocol, study procedures, AE/SAE reporting, staff roles, data collection, source documentation, data management, regulatory documents, record retention, laboratory procedures, IP/clinical supplies management, site monitoring overview, and unblinding (if applicable).
- The final SIV report must be completed and submitted within **10 working days**; the Investigator must initial and date the final report for filing in the ISF.
- Subject recruitment activities **may not start** until the SIV process has been completed.
- In some cases the SIV may be performed remotely if the Investigator and clinical staff attended the relevant Investigator Meeting.
