## 1 · Purpose and scope

This WI applies to the preparation, conduct, and follow-up of site monitoring activities concerning investigational product (IP) and is meant to be followed jointly with WI-CIM-MO001 through WI-CIM-MO004 for overall on-site monitoring procedures. It covers IP monitoring at Site Initiation Visits, Site Monitoring Visits, and Close-Out Visits.

---

## 2 · Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Monitor (CRA) | Monitors IP-related activities during SIV, SMV, and COV; reviews logs and documentation; trains site staff as needed |
| QI / PI | Ultimately responsible for IP Management at their site regardless of task delegation; provides direct access to all drug IP facilities and essential documents |
| Site Pharmacy / IP Custodian | Performs IP receipt, storage, dispensing, and return/destruction under delegation from the QI |

## 4 · Instructions

### 5.1 Monitoring Reference Documents

A Site Monitoring Visit of the site pharmacy or IP storage area will be carried out according to the protocol, product information, pharmacy manual (if applicable), and trial-specific monitoring plans. Monitoring details will be captured in the trial-specific Monitoring Plan.

### 5.2 Preparation of the Site Monitoring Visit

**Step 5.2.1** At the Initiation Visit, ensure that prior to subject recruitment all site staff who will dispense IP have been trained on randomization procedures (if applicable), and their delegated tasks regarding IP, confidentiality, storage, accountability (receipt, dispensation, and return/destruction), and security. Any new site staff delegated these tasks will be trained at the next monitoring visit prior to their documented delegation on the Task Delegation Log.

**Step 5.2.2** Ensure the Investigator/delegate has relevant and updated information on the IP, such as current protocol, Investigator's Brochure (IB), commercially available Product Monograph (PM)/Information, and Pharmacy Manual; ensure any new amendments are sent to the site prior to the visit.

**Step 5.2.3** Schedule a Site Monitoring Visit per WI-CIM-MO003, prepare and send the monitoring agenda including a list of mandatory attendees, along with the confirmation letter.

**Step 5.2.4** Schedule a pharmacy facility tour with site staff and clinical trial pharmacy or other institutional pharmacies (as applicable). If IP is kept at a clinic or doctor's office, ensure it is securely stored at the appropriately controlled temperature, that a backup fridge (if applicable) exists, and that the storage is in a locked location per protocol and pharmacy manual requirements.

**Step 5.2.5** Confirm IP dispensing, storage, accountability, and return/disposition are only performed by appropriately trained staff documented on the Task Delegation Log.

### 5.3 During the On-Site Monitoring Visit

**Step 5.3.1** Conduct an opening meeting to go over the agenda, any pending queries, training needs, and any new information.

**Step 5.3.2** Ensure all study IP-related documents pertinent to storage, handling, and safety information (protocol, IB, PM, Pharmacy Manual, and any additional IP information) are up-to-date and filed in a Pharmacy binder. Superseded versions must be appropriately indicated as such.

**Step 5.3.3** Review the Task Delegation Log and Training Log to ensure that site staff carrying out IP-related activities were trained prior to their delegation and that both training and delegation are appropriately documented.

**Step 5.3.4** Ensure IP is handled/prepared as per instructions, manufacturer's instructions (IB/PM), protocol, and Pharmacy Manual requirements.

**Step 5.3.5** Ensure IP is stored according to manufacturer's instructions (IB/PM), protocol, and Pharmacy Manual requirements.

**Step 5.3.6** Ensure subject compliance with prescribed IP usage.

**Step 5.3.7** Ensure there is sufficient stock for dispensation to subjects in accordance with anticipated recruitment rate and IP dose/device.

**Step 5.3.8** Ensure that expired IP (if applicable) has been placed in quarantine (at appropriate temperature and labeled as expired/quarantined) until further instruction from the Sponsor regarding continued use, return, or destruction.

**Step 5.3.9** Ensure shipping documents are on file if IP was sent from manufacturer/Sponsor, including shipping documents and downloaded shipment temperature alert logs.

**Step 5.3.10** Review receipt logs to ensure they are consistent with shipping documents.

**Step 5.3.11** Ensure temperature excursions (during both shipment and on-site storage) are correctly documented and escalated appropriately, and that affected IP stock is correctly handled (e.g., quarantined) and documented.

**Step 5.3.12** Review the IP Dispensing/Accountability Logs and ensure IP is correctly randomized/dispensed/returned/destroyed for all enrolled patients only, accurately documented using study identifiers in chronological order.

**Step 5.3.13** Ensure appropriate documentation, including correspondence, is in place for any return or destruction of stock — such as shipment confirmation or a destruction certificate.

**Step 5.3.14** If applicable to the study, ensure masking of treatment assignment is maintained.

**Step 5.3.15** If applicable, conduct a training session for new site staff members and document training on the Training Log prior to their delegation on the Task Delegation Log.

**Step 5.3.16** Conduct an end-of-visit meeting to go over any findings with the study staff.

### 5.4 During the Close-Out Visit

**Step 5.4.1** Verify that the accountability logs are completed.

**Step 5.4.2** Verify that the documentation demonstrates that IP was appropriately used in accordance with the protocol.

**Step 5.4.3** Ensure that IP is destroyed/returned as per protocol or Sponsor's request and/or pharmacy WIs, and that documentation is kept in the ISF.

**Step 5.4.4** Follow up with the site to ensure all pharmacy documents have been returned to the site, reviewed by the QI, and archived with all study documents.
