## 1 · Purpose and scope {#purpose-and-scope}

This work instruction describes the procedures for identifying a Code Red fire emergency and managing a Code Green evacuation for CIM personnel and users at the Glen site, Blocks B4 and C4. All personnel on CIM premises must be familiar with and follow these procedures.

**CIM personnel and CIM users must never put their lives in peril. If the situation becomes dangerous, they must leave the area, regardless of where they are in the emergency preparation and evacuation process.**

---

## 2 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **All Personnel on CIM Premises** | Know procedures for identifying a Code Red, activating a fire alarm, assisting others, and locating fire extinguishers, manual pull stations, and emergency exits. Follow instructions of the CIM Emergency Team Lead and CIM Emergency Floor Monitor. If with a patient, remain with them and call for assistance if needed. Notify the CIM Emergency Floor Monitor of any visitors present. |
| **CIM Emergency Team Lead** | Ensure assignment of CIM Emergency Floor Monitors and delegates; replace assignees as needed. Assess the situation and determine horizontal or vertical evacuation approach. Announce evacuation instructions on the CIM public address system. Notify MUHC security of any missing personnel. |
| **CIM Emergency Floor Monitor** | Prepare a list of all personnel, users, and patients/visitors using Appendix 2 CIM Emergency Action Cards. Evacuate assigned rooms and guide evacuees to the assembly point. Confirm all actions on the checklist are complete. Notify CIM Emergency Team Lead of any missing persons. Assign helpers if required. |
| **Managers** | Ensure their staff are covered under an appropriate evacuation plan if located outside CIM premises within the scope of this WI (Glen, Blocks B4 and C4). |

## 3 · Terms and definitions {#terms-and-definitions}

| Term / Acronym | Definition |
|---|---|
| CIM Public Address System | The public announcement system within the CIM only. |
| Code Red Alert | Code red announced on the MUHC public address system; fire alarm does not sound. Fire not yet confirmed. Magnetic door locks do not release; fire doors close. |
| Code Red Alarm | Code red "confirmed" announced on the MUHC public address system; fire alarm rings at 120 beats per minute where activated, 20 beats per minute on floors immediately above and below. Elevators return to ground floor; fire doors close in the affected block; key-card doors deactivate. |
| Code Green Horizontal Evacuation | Personnel are moved to another section on the same floor, beyond a set of fire doors. |
| Code Green Vertical Evacuation | Applied when horizontal evacuation is insufficient or not possible. Typically involves floors at least two levels below or above the disaster area. |
| Fire Door | Specialized doors purpose-built to withstand fires and compartmentalize the building. Typically grey-coloured. |

## 4 · Instructions {#instructions}

### 4.1 Witnessing an incident {#witnessing-an-incident}

A witness discovering fire, heavy smoke, or a potential fire threat must immediately:

- **Step 1 — Save all persons in immediate danger.** Take reasonable actions to remove or shield personnel from the danger zone. Call for help if needed.
- **Step 2 — Alert personnel.** Activate a fire alarm (manual pull station or call). Notify the CIM Emergency Team Lead or CIM Emergency Floor Monitor as soon as possible.
- **Step 3 — Intervene.** If comfortable and safe, attempt to extinguish the fire using a nearby fire extinguisher (see Appendix 1 for locations). Never put yourself in peril — leave the area if the situation becomes dangerous.

### 4.2 Code Red Alert {#code-red-alert}

Fire is not yet confirmed. Listen carefully to the MUHC public address system for further instructions. No evacuation at this stage unless directed.

### 4.3 Code Red Alarm {#code-red-alarm}

Fire has been confirmed. Alarm sounds.

- If the incident is in close proximity to the CIM (e.g., floors above or below your block), note the closest fire exits and applicable route, and/or ask the designated CIM Emergency Floor Monitor for instructions.
- Remain or return to your workstation without using the elevators in the affected block. If with a patient, remain with them and await instructions. If with a visitor, notify the CIM Emergency Team Lead or Emergency Floor Monitor of their location.
- Ensure study records are safely stored to prevent water damage from potential sprinkler activation (e.g., in filing cabinets).
- If with a patient undergoing an interrupted procedure (e.g., drug administration, infusion, blood draw), stop the procedure if possible and document the interruption in study records, including activities completed at the point of interruption.

**Roles during Code Red Alarm:**

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **All Personnel** | Remain or return to workstation; stay with patients; listen to MUHC and/or CIM public address system. |
| **CIM Emergency Floor Monitor** | Prepare for potential evacuation using Appendix 2 CIM Emergency Action Cards. Ensure hallways are unobstructed. Close all doors leading to the affected area if in close proximity. Determine if patients require assistance to evacuate. |
| **CIM Emergency Team Lead** | Initiate evacuation preparations. Ensure CIM Emergency Floor Monitors are available. If a decision is made to evacuate before Code Green is announced, communicate via the CIM public address system. |

### 4.4 Code Green (Evacuation) {#code-green-evacuation}

An order to evacuate. Applies to Code Red (fire), Code Silver (active shooter), Code Black phase 2 (suspicious package), Code Brown (internal toxic spill), flood, electrical shutdown, or any other circumstance called for by MUHC or the CIM Emergency Team Lead.

**Evacuation types:**

1. **Vertical evacuation** — Use emergency stairs to reach the assembly point at the RC level.
   - For patients or personnel with limited mobility, place the individual on a blanket and, with assistance, carry them down the emergency stairs. If assistance is required, call via the CIM public address system or call specifying the precise location.
   - Notify the CIM Emergency Floor Monitor and/or Team Lead of any mobility-limited persons.

2. **Horizontal evacuation** — Transport persons with limited mobility to an adjacent block beyond the fire doors, then use the elevator if necessary.

**Assembly point:** The "Havre" curving sculpture at the RC level (exit at RC level). CIM Emergency Team Lead announces the evacuation type and assembly point via the CIM public address system.

**Roles during Code Green:**

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| **All Personnel** | Do not use elevators in the affected block. Keep emergency exits clear. Avoid picking up personal belongings. Avoid moving patients in beds to prevent congestion. Report to assembly point and remain there until CIM Emergency Team Lead advises it is safe to return. |
| **CIM Emergency Floor Monitor** | Evacuate all assigned rooms; shut any medical gas (e.g., oxygen); close and post-it each cleared room door. Verify the presence of all personnel at the assembly point using Appendix 2 CIM Emergency Action Cards. Report headcount and any missing persons to the CIM Emergency Team Lead immediately. |
| **CIM Emergency Team Lead** | Assess situation and determine evacuation approach. Announce instructions via CIM public address system. Coordinate with emergency personnel (fire department, security, Emergency Measures Coordination Center). Advise CIM Emergency Floor Monitors when it is safe to return. |

**After the emergency:** Upon return, notify the QI and Sponsor of any study-related interruptions, detailing the cause and impact (e.g., incomplete dose) and documenting any required actions as instructed by the QI and/or Sponsor.
