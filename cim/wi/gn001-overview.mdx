# UBEC Verification and Management (Glen and Montreal General Hospital)

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-GN001_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 25 Apr 2022</Badge> <Badge color="blue">Safety Team Lead / CIM Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI defines the procedures for verifying and maintaining Unit Based Emergency Carts (UBECs) at the CIM Glen and Montreal General Hospital sites, including monthly verification schedules, documentation requirements, ordering of expired or missing items, and QC review by the Safety Team Lead.
</Note>

## Key points

- UBECs must be verified monthly by two CRC-Ns, completed within 2 business days from the first business day of the month; any excursions must be documented as a deviation.
- Verification uses Appendix 1 (Glen) or Appendix 2 (MGH) checklists — one CRC-N dictates each item (name, quantity, expiry) while the second documents on the form.
- Column codes: **A** = Available and not expired; **E** = Expired/expiring within the month and ordered; **M** = Missing and ordered; **R** = Replaced.
- Items expiring before the next verification must be ordered and replaced; the quantity in column A must equal the Quota column; missing items trigger a root-cause investigation (CAPA).
- Medication orders must be faxed to Pharmacy (not on Fridays or before public holidays); the fax confirmation must be retained and placed in the UBEC Verification Binder.
- Once verified and restocked, the cart is re-sealed with a new numbered tag lock; the new tag number is recorded on the checklist.
- The Safety Team Lead conducts a bimonthly QC review of the Verification Binder, ensures document currency, and arranges archival of checklists one year after completion.
- The AED must contain two non-expired pad boxes (adult + pediatric at Glen; adult at MGH) and Biomed verification must be current on a quarterly basis.
