## 1 · Purpose and scope {#purpose-and-scope}

**Purpose:** This Working Instruction (WI) describes the requirements for the creation, maintenance, annual renewal, and archival of the study investigator site file (ISF), including its structure and content.

**Scope:** This WI applies to all qualified trained staff involved in clinical studies and handling study documents at the Center for Innovative Medicine (CIM) of the Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre (RI-MUHC). This WI includes the requirements for creating and maintaining both the paper and electronic sections of the ISF.

---

## 2 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Qualified Investigator (QI) | Responsible for ensuring an ISF is established prior to the start of a study and ensuring it is maintained up-to-date with all relevant and applicable records throughout the conduct of the study, including archival at the end. These activities may be delegated on the study task delegation log to qualified site staff, but the overall responsibility for the ISF remains with the Investigator. |
| Research Ethics Assistant (REA) / Research Coordinator – Ethics (RC-E) | At start-up, the REA is responsible for creating the folder system (in the CIM Administration folder) used by the ethics team until Institutional MUHC authorization is obtained and for document filing for the initial REB submission. The RC-E is responsible for saving and filing required essential documents for Ethics submission of amendments once received. |
| Regulatory Coordinator (REG) | If applicable, responsible for creating the electronic section of the ISF on the shared drive, setting up and maintaining the paper-based ISF and the electronic section not under the RC-E's responsibility. |
| Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC) | Responsible for filing communications and updated study-specific working documents that do not require REB approval into the proper ISF electronic folders throughout the course of the trial. A CRC may also carry out the duties of a REG if required and delegated by the study team. |

## 4 · Instructions {#instructions}

### 4.1 General Requirements {#general-requirements}

**4.1.1** A study-specific ISF is required for every clinical study in order to store essential documents (i.e., one ISF for each clinical study protocol).

**4.1.2** The ISF should be prepared once site selection has been confirmed by the Sponsor.

**4.1.3** The responsibility for maintaining and updating the ISF must be delegated on the task delegation log to appropriate qualified site staff by the QI.

**4.1.4** The ISF will be maintained as a hybrid consisting of handwritten original records and electronic records (e.g., records obtained in electronic format and/or scanned original records), and will remain in a secure location with access limited to research personnel.

**4.1.5** Essential documents, including critical correspondence (e.g., relevant to the conduct of the study needed to reconstruct the trial), should be filed upon receipt — up to within **5 business days** — to ensure the successful management of the study. Refer to the corresponding Working Instruction specific to each team.

**4.1.6** Added as Appendix 1 is the **CIM ISF Index** to track essential documents by showing the location of files contained within the folder structure (paper-based ISF binder or electronic section of the ISF). This will help in identifying where essential documents are located throughout the study.

**4.1.7** All essential study documentation is required to be made available for monitoring, Sponsor audit(s), and inspections by regulatory authorities. It is the responsibility of the QI and delegated study team to ensure records are securely shared and readily available, while maintaining privacy and confidentiality as appropriate.

**4.1.8** The electronic section of the ISF must be created using the approved ISF template consisting of the appropriate folder structure. Files must be named using the following convention to avoid incorrect filing and readily identify purpose:
- Do not use symbols and punctuation: `#`, `%`, `&`, `$`, `@`, `^`, `{`, `}`, `|`, `\`, `<`, `>`, `*`, `?`, `!`, blank spaces, `"`, `,`, `:`, `+`, `=`, `/`
- Do not start or end the name with a space, period, hyphen, or underline
- Keep filenames under 31 characters as much as possible
- Suggested naming convention: `DocumentName_ProtocolName_SiteID#_DocumentDate(YYYYMMDD)`

**4.1.9** If a study is terminated prematurely — even if occurring prior to initiating the study — the ISF must be archived in accordance with the Record Retention and Archival Working Instruction.

### 4.2 Hybrid ISF Organization {#hybrid-isf-organization}

**4.2.1** A study-specific folder will be created by the REG after the completion of the site selection visit in the appropriate internal shared folder network location (e.g., under the QI folder), named using the protocol number and/or shorthand name.

**4.2.2** The REG will then create the electronic section of the ISF folder structure using the approved template within that study-specific folder once the site selection letter and other materials (e.g., welcome package) have been received from the Sponsor. For Investigator-Initiated studies, this will be done as soon as an outline of the protocol is available. The electronic and paper sections will comprise the sections outlined in **Appendix 1 CIM ISF Index**. If the Sponsor or CRO provides their own binders or index, the REG will inform the Sponsor/CRO that the CIM ISF Index will be used in accordance with this WI.

**4.2.3** The paper-based ISF binder will consist of, but is not limited to, the following original records and/or hardcopies:
- QIU
- FDF(s)
- Protocol Signature Pages
- IB Acknowledgement Forms
- Training logs
- Delegation log
- Agreement — Team on rotation or MD/Specialist (if applicable)
- Monitoring visit log
- Note-to-File(s)
- Miscellaneous

**4.2.4** Paper study records will be filed in reverse chronological order — the most recent version of documents within the section will remain on top.

**4.2.5** All sections per the CIM paper-based ISF Table of Contents must be maintained in the binder; no section should be removed. If a section is not used or records are solely located in the electronic section, a Note-to-File stating where those documents can be found must be filed in the respective tab.
- As an additional measure of protection, original paper records must be scanned and retained as PDF documents in the appropriate folder in the electronic section of the ISF. Ensure all pages have been scanned; the file is readable (PDF can be opened and is not corrupt); the scan is clear and legible; and any characteristics necessary to reconstruct the activities are present (e.g., color codes preserved).

### 4.3 Maintaining Hybrid ISF {#maintaining-hybrid-isf}

**4.3.1** All essential documents within the paper-based ISF must be maintained in a secure environment such as a locked office for paper-based records or restricted access folders on the internal network (e.g., all CIM read-only, study team read-write access).

**4.3.2** The qualified site staff is responsible for maintaining records under their responsibility up to date. They must save pertinent study-related communications as PDF documents (e.g., emails regarding enrollment hold, study termination, IP stability issues, monitoring follow-ups, newsletters, data lock activity memos, etc.) between study personnel and all stakeholders (REB, Sponsor, CRO, Health Canada, Central Lab) in the applicable folder of the electronic section of the ISF — within **5 business days**.

**4.3.3** At start-up, the REA files documents for REB submission. Subsequently, the RC-E is responsible for filing and updating essential documents related to Ethics submissions for the following subfolders:
1. Protocol and Amendments
2. Investigator's Brochure
3. Consent
4. Regulatory Documents (NOL only)
7. Research Ethics Board
8. Ethics Document Tracking Logs
13. Safety Reports and Committees
14. Communications (REB and/or Sponsor; Go (implementation) Letters are managed by the REG)

**4.3.4** The CRC or REG is responsible for filing the Pre-Study Visit Checklist under the Site Visit section.

**4.3.5** The REG is responsible for maintenance of the paper-based ISF and the electronic 6-Training folder. The REG is also responsible for scanning and filing pertinent study documentation in the applicable folder of the electronic section.

**4.3.6** The CRC is responsible to file electronically: study-related safety reports, monitoring confirmation and scanned signed follow-up letters/emails, communications, newsletters, study-specific source document templates, and amended sponsor study manuals that do not require REB approval.

### 4.4 Sponsor/CRO Monitor Access {#sponsor-cro-monitor-access}

Sponsor/CRO monitor access to the electronic section of the hybrid ISF is delineated in the Remote Monitoring Visit WI.

### 4.5 Records From MUHC Departments Outside the CIM {#records-from-muhc-departments-outside-the-cim}

**4.5.1** The ISF also includes Pharmacy records (e.g., investigational product shipping, receipt and accountability logs, pharmacy training records for staff involved in the study, staff not listed on the Delegation of Authority log). During the study, these are kept within the local pharmacy and maintained by the delegated pharmacist. The Pharmacy records must be archived as part of the ISF and will be provided to the CRC or REG at the closure of the study.

### 4.6 ISF Closure and Archival {#isf-closure-and-archival}

**4.6.1** Upon reception of the close-out letter from the Sponsor/CRO, the CRC or REG will forward the letter to the RC-E at `cim.regulatory@muhc.mcgill.ca`.

**4.6.2** The assigned RC-E completes and submits the F10 to the REB. The RC-E is also responsible for saving NAGANO forms.

**4.6.3** No further ISF (paper or electronic) activities are permitted once the termination report has been acknowledged by the REB or mandated person and sent to the Sponsor/CRO by the study team.

**4.6.4** Archiving of the ISF and patient charts should be initiated once the Sponsor/CRO close-out letter/report is received, reviewed, and acknowledged by the QI. Archiving, as well as handling requests for the destruction of records, will be performed in accordance with the Record Retention and Archival Working Instruction.
