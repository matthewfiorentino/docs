# Management of Monitoring Visits and Sponsor Audits

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-CR003_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 20 May 2022</Badge> <Badge color="blue">All CIM Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI describes the instructions for preparing and conducting monitoring visits and sponsor audits at the CIM, RI-MUHC. It covers scheduling, approved technology platforms, remote and onsite audit conduct, record preparation, follow-up procedures, and the forms required to grant and revoke external auditor access to MUHC systems.
</Note>

## Key points

- Regulatory agency inspections (e.g., Health Canada) are excluded from this WI — forward such requests to the RI-MUHC QA Manager and CIM Director before responding.
- All IT access requests (CITRIX/IRIAS3, CIM Study shared folder, OACIS) must be submitted at least **5 business days** before the scheduled audit.
- Only secure, approved platforms may be used: WebEx (for OACIS and certified copies), the `\\wnetvmri54\cim_study` file share, and approved video conferencing tools for screen sharing. Records must never be downloaded, printed, or uploaded via video conferencing tools.
- Each auditor must sign the **Appendix 7 Security Pledge** (MUHC Information and Information Systems) before access is granted, and the **Appendix 8 OACIS Confidentiality and Security Agreement** before OACIS access is provided.
- A pre-audit QC review of study records must be completed in advance; the **Appendix 6 Study Record Review Checklist** supports this review.
- All copies provided to auditors must be logged (Appendix 3) and scanned copies verified and logged as certified copies (Appendix 4).
- Sponsor audit responses must document the finding, root cause, immediate corrective action (with date), and preventive action (with target implementation date).
- All user access to MUHC systems must be removed promptly at the end of the audit; the shared folder must be renamed with the `_ARCHIVED` suffix.
