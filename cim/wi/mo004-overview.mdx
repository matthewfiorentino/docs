# Site Close-Out Visit

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-MO004_05</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 05</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective Refer to WI Header</Badge> <Badge color="blue">CIM Monitors / CIM Research Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI describes the preparation, conduct, and post-visit follow-up for the Site Close-Out Visit (COV). It ensures that all clinical trial activities are appropriately recorded, reported, and reconciled at study end — covering final subject recruitment status, investigator responsibility, IP/device disposition, study materials, SAEs/AEs, laboratory specimens, monitoring, and essential document review.
</Note>

## Key points

- The COV is triggered when all subjects have completed all protocol-required visits and CRFs are complete, or when the Sponsor decides to close the study early (safety issues, low enrollment, lack of efficacy, non-compliance, PI withdrawal).
- Before the visit, the monitor reviews the TMF for completeness, all open monitoring issues, outstanding SDV and CRF completeness, and prepares the clinical supply return form (if applicable).
- During the visit, seven areas must be assessed using the Close-Out Visit Report (Appendix 1): final subject recruitment status, investigator responsibility, IP/device, study materials, SAEs/AEs, monitoring, and ISF/essential documents.
- The Close-Out Visit Report must be completed within **10 working days** and the follow-up letter (Appendix 3) sent within 20 business days.
- Record retention: **25 years** for Health Canada-regulated studies; **7 years from publication date** for non-HC regulated studies.
- The QI must notify the Sponsor/CIM team within **24 hours** of any Health Canada, FDA, or regulatory inspection request.
- The QI must report all unexpected SADRs to the Sponsor and REB even after study closure.
- If all outstanding action items from the COV are resolved, the monitor issues a Post Close-Out Visit Letter (Appendix 4) confirming the site may proceed to archive.
