## 1 · Purpose and scope {#purpose-and-scope}

**Purpose:** This Working Instruction (WI) describes the requirements for telephone communication between CRCs, CRC-RNs, managers, supervisors, and study participants.

**Scope:** This WI applies to CRCs, CRC-RNs, Managers, and Supervisors involved in clinical studies at the Center for Innovative Medicine (CIM) of the Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre (RI-MUHC). This WI includes the requirements for telephone communication between CRC/CRC-RN/managers/supervisors and study participants. **Personal cell phones are not to be used by CRC/CRC-RN/managers/supervisors to contact study participants.**

---

## 2 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| CRC / CRC-RN | Must not provide personal cell phone numbers to study participants; must use CIM work phones and business cards; must cancel call forwarding at the end of each work day. |
| Managers / Supervisors | Must not provide personal cell phone numbers to study participants. |
| PI/QI or Sub-Investigator providing coverage | May, at their discretion, provide study participants with a personal/professional cell phone number. |

---

## 3 · Issues {#issues}

### 3.1 Why CRCs and CRC-RNs may be tempted to provide personal cell phone numbers {#why-crcs-and-crc-rns-may-be-tempted-to-provide-personal-cell-phone-numbers}

**3.1.1** Families who are hospitalized with their children use their personal cell phone to communicate with CRCs/CRC-RNs by SMS.

**3.1.2** During patient visits, CRCs/CRC-RNs are not at their desks to answer the phone and/or to check emails.

**3.1.3** Patients usually notify CRCs/CRC-RNs by SMS when they are lost in the hospital or if they have issues with certain internal vendors (e.g., Physiology, Ophthalmology, Audiology, or Radiology Departments).

**3.1.4** Patients who have an SAE or safety issues outside of CIM working hours.

**3.1.5** Patients who call or text at the last minute to request a change or cancel a visit date due to an unforeseen situation.

### 3.2 Issues that can occur if CRC/CRC-RN provides their personal cell phone number to a study participant {#issues-that-can-occur-if-crc-crc-rn-provides-their-personal-cell-phone-number-to-a-study-participant}

**3.2.1** By providing a personal cell phone, the CRC/CRC-RN could compromise study participant confidentiality (e.g., participant phone number or name appearing on the phone screen).

**3.2.2** Study participants calling CRCs/CRC-RNs outside of CIM working hours in the event of a medical emergency. A CRC/CRC-RN cannot provide care remotely and cannot recommend treatment, as this falls within the purview of a physician (PI/QI or Sub-Investigator authorized to provide coverage during PI/QI absence).

**3.2.3** CRC/CRC-RN could be on a leave of absence and in such cases would not legally be able to assist the study participant or even follow up with other study team members.

**3.2.4** CRC/CRC-RN leaving the RI-CIM could still be contacted by the study participant.

## 4 · Instructions {#instructions}

### 4.1 Communication with participants without providing a personal cell phone number {#communication-with-participants-without-providing-a-personal-cell-phone-number}

**4.1.1** Study participants must be provided with a CRC/CRC-RN business card with CIM coordinates such as phone number/extension and email.

**4.1.2** During CIM/CRC/CRC-RN working hours, calls to their CIM phone can be forwarded to their cell phone, with the proviso that the CRC/CRC-RN ensures to **stop call forwarding at the end of their work day**.

**4.1.3** Study participants must be provided with a **patient alert card** indicating that they are part of a clinical trial taking a study medication. The patient alert card indicates the medication type. These cards receive REB approval. Participants are instructed to carry this card and to inform emergency room staff or any other health care providers that they are part of a clinical trial.

**4.1.4** The CRC/CRC-RN **may not** provide the study participant with their personal cell phone number.

### 4.2 PI/QI and Sub-Investigator coverage {#pi-qi-and-sub-investigator-coverage}

**4.2** The PI/QI or Sub-Investigator providing coverage during PI/QI absence may, at their discretion, provide study participants with a personal/professional cell phone number.

### 4.3 For study participants who already have a CRC/CRC-RN's personal cell phone number {#for-study-participants-who-already-have-a-crc-crc-rns-personal-cell-phone-number}

**4.3.1** CRC/CRC-RN must inform the study participant to start using the work extension during working hours. During working hours, the CRC/CRC-RN may transfer calls from the work extension to another extension or personal cell phone (e.g., if they are not at their desk on a specific day).

**4.3.2** CRC/CRC-RN must remind the study participant to carry their study participation card.

### 4.4 Outside working hours {#outside-working-hours}

**4.4** The need for study participants to contact the CRC/CRC-RN outside of working hours should be assessed on a case-by-case basis.

### 4.5 Call transfer procedure (AVAYA phone system) {#call-transfer-procedure-avaya-phone-system}

1. Press **Fwd** on the AVAYA phone.
2. Enter the **5-digit extension** or **10-digit cell phone number**.
3. Confirm that the call is forwarded (confirmation will display on phone).
4. **At the end of the work day, press Fwd again to cancel call forwarding** and confirm that call forward is cancelled.
