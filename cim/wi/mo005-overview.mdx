# Site Monitoring of Investigational Product Management

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-MO005_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective Refer to WI Header</Badge> <Badge color="blue">CIM Monitors / CIM Research Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI describes the monitoring activities related to investigational product (IP) management during Site Initiation Visits, Site Monitoring Visits, and Close-Out Visits. It is used in conjunction with WI-CIM-MO001 through WI-CIM-MO004 and applies to IP preparation, storage, dispensing, accountability, and return or destruction at the site pharmacy or storage area.

## Key points

- IP monitoring is performed in accordance with the protocol, product information, pharmacy manual (if applicable), and the trial-specific monitoring plan.
- Before the SIV, the monitor must ensure all IP-dispensing staff have been trained on randomization procedures, IP confidentiality, storage, accountability (receipt, dispensation, and return/destruction), and security.
- During the monitoring visit, the monitor reviews the Task Delegation Log and Training Log to confirm that all IP-related activities are delegated only to trained staff.
- IP must be stored according to manufacturer's instructions (IB/PM), protocol, and pharmacy manual requirements; expired IP must be quarantined (at appropriate temperature, labeled as expired/quarantined) pending Sponsor instructions.
- All temperature excursions during shipment and on-site storage must be documented, escalated appropriately, and affected IP stock handled and documented correctly (e.g., quarantined).
- The IP Dispensing/Accountability Log must be reviewed at each visit to confirm all dispensing, returns, and destruction are accurately documented with study identifiers in chronological order.
- At the Close-Out Visit, verify accountability logs are complete, IP was used only per protocol, and that return or destruction documentation is maintained in the ISF.
- The QI/PI is ultimately responsible for IP management at their site, regardless of task delegation.
