## Key points

- **All personnel** must immediately inform their supervisor or manager of any incident or accident and complete the appropriate form.
- Use **form AH-223** (Rapport de déclaration d'incident ou d'accident) when a patient is the victim.
- Use the **Work-related Incident/Accident and Occupational Disease** form when an employee is involved; the completed form must be sent to the RI Occupational Health and Safety (OHS) department within 24 hours.
- For **exposures to blood or body fluids**: collect the accident-reporting package from CIM reception or your manager and go immediately to the hospital emergency department — you must be seen within 1 hour. Report the exposure to your manager/supervisor.
- An **incident** is an unusual situation without direct health consequences but which could have had consequences under different circumstances.
- An **accident** is a situation where a risk event occurs that has or could have consequences.

## 1 · Purpose and scope {#purpose-and-scope}

This work instruction defines how to declare an incident or accident that occurs on CIM premises, covering three scenarios: incidents or accidents involving a patient, work-related incidents or accidents involving an employee, and exposures to blood or body fluids. It applies to all personnel on CIM premises.

## 4 · Instructions {#instructions}

Follow the flowchart below when an incident or accident occurs on CIM premises. Forms are available at both CIM receptions and from managers/supervisors.

### 4.1 Incident or accident involving a patient — Form AH-223 {#incident-or-accident-involving-a-patient-form-ah-223}

- Complete form AH-223 (*Rapport de déclaration d'incident ou d'accident*) for any incident or accident involving a patient.
- Submit the completed AH-223 report (white and yellow copies) to your reporting supervisor or manager.
- The supervisor or manager must review the AH-223 report and ensure appropriate follow-up actions are taken.

### 4.2 Work-related incident or accident involving an employee {#work-related-incident-or-accident-involving-an-employee}

- Complete the Work-related Incident/Accident and Occupational Disease form.
- The completed form must be signed and sent to the RI department of Occupational Health and Safety within **24 hours** of the event.

### 4.3 Exposures to blood and/or body fluids {#exposures-to-blood-and-or-body-fluids}

- Complete this procedure for all exposures to blood and/or body fluids.
- Collect the accident-reporting package at either CIM reception or from your manager/supervisor and go **immediately** to the hospital emergency department — you must be seen within **1 hour**.
- It is recommended to be accompanied by a colleague if assistance is required. All necessary forms, including the physician's requisition for a blood test, are included in the package.
- Ensure the exposure has been reported to your immediate manager and/or supervisor.
- The patient-source will need to be assessed.
