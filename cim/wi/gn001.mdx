## 1 · Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** This Work Instruction (WI) describes the instructions to verify and maintain a Unit Based Emergency Cart (UBEC) at the Center for Innovative Medicine (CIM) of the Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre (RI-MUHC) at the Glen and Montreal General Hospital (MGH) sites.

**Scope:** This WI applies to nurses at the CIM assigned the responsibility for verifying and maintaining UBECs.

---

## 2 · Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|

| Team Supervisors or Managers | Ensures assignment of Safety Team Lead and compliance with the Work Instruction and scheduled assignments. |

## 4 · Instructions

### 4.1 General

**4.1.1** Management will appoint a Safety Team Lead to oversee the scheduling and oversight of UBEC verifications, and will maintain a current list of CRC-Ns. This assignment must be documented.

**4.1.2** Each UBEC must be equipped with a UBEC Verification Binder containing the following current and effective versions of documents:
- Appendix 1 CIM UBEC Verification Checklist (Glen) or Appendix 2 CIM UBEC Verification Checklist (MGH).
- MUHC Pediatric CODE BLUE RUNNER CART Order Form and MCH Unit Based Emergency Cart (UBEC) OFFICIAL EQUIPMENT / MEDICATION LIST, or UBEC Adult Medication Ordering Sheet (adults) and ADULT UBEC Medication Quick Reference.
- WI-CIM-AD001 Code Blue Preparation and Management Appendix 3 Code Blue Emergency Event Form.
- UBEC Order Number List.
- MUHC Prescription Form.
- Nursing notes (part of Appendix 3 Code Blue Emergency Event or other appropriate form from the MUHC).
- Resuscitation Drug List from the MUHC Montreal Children's Hospital for pediatric UBECs.

**4.1.3** The UBEC must only be used in case of an emergency (e.g., code blue, anaphylactic shock). If opened, the Safety Team Lead must be informed so they can replace the used items and complete the verification requirements in order to seal the cart again. For all other supply needs, the appropriate nursing supply stock must be used.

### 4.2 Planning and Scheduling

**4.2.1** The Safety Team Lead, or appropriate delegate, will oversee a schedule of assigned CRC-Ns and their backup, ensuring their assigned UBECs are verified on a monthly basis, to be completed within 2 business days from the first business day of the month. Any excursions must be documented as a deviation to this WI.

**4.2.2** Appropriate planning will be required to account for statutory holidays and personnel absences such as paid time off or sick days. A backup must be assigned to cover unscheduled absences or scheduling conflict.

**4.2.3** It is the responsibility of the CRC-Ns to manage their assigned task and notify the Safety Team Lead of any changes to the schedule.

**4.2.4** It is the responsibility of the Safety Team Lead to find a replacement before the scheduled due date if an assigned CRC-N is not available due to an unplanned absence or scheduling conflict.

### 4.3 Performing and Documenting the Verification

**4.3.1** The verification must be documented using Appendix 1 CIM UBEC Verification Checklist (Glen) or Appendix 2 CIM UBEC Verification Checklist (MGH) found in the UBEC Verification Binder and performed by two CRC-Ns.

**4.3.2** Verify that the numbered lock (tag) on the UBEC matches the number recorded on Appendix 1 (Glen) or Appendix 2 (MGH) from the last verification and document this number on the checklist. If this number does not match, notify the Safety Team Lead who will initiate an investigation. The investigation will be documented as a CAPA in accordance with the applicable procedure on CAPA management. The CAPA must be routed to the appropriate personnel (e.g., compliance specialist, manager) and a copy must be placed in the UBEC Verification Binder.

**4.3.3** Proceed with the verification of the quantity and expiry dates for each item listed on the checklist found in the cart. The first CRC-N will go through each item in the UBEC and dictate the verification (i.e., name, quantity, and expiry), while the second CRC-N will confirm this verification by documenting it on Appendix 1 (Glen) or Appendix 2 (MGH).

**4.3.4** The verification will be documented as follows:
- Enter the quantity number in column **"A"** if the item is available and, if applicable, not expired. Note: if the item is expiring within a month (i.e., prior to the next scheduled UBEC verification), the item must be ordered and replaced. In such cases, document this as "E" expired and ordered (see next bullet).
- Enter the quantity number in column **"E"** if the item is expired or due to expire within the month, prior to the next verification. Expired items are removed once replaced, and discarded appropriately (i.e., medication in the biohazard bins).
- Enter the quantity number in column **"M"** if the item is missing and ordered. The item must be added to the cart once the order is received. If an item that was available during the previous verification is missing, the root cause must be investigated and documented (e.g., corrective and preventive action, CAPA).

**4.3.5** The CRC-Ns should confirm if any of the items can be replaced with the available stock within the CIM prior to placing a new order.

**4.3.6** The quantity in column "A" must equal the number in the "Quota" column. If not, the applicable items must be ordered on the same day the verification is performed and restocked as soon as possible.

**4.3.7** Verify that the oxygen tank is full by opening the tank valve. In doing so the black needle should read full. Open the flow meter to 15L to ensure flow, close the flow meter, and then close the tank valve. Re-open the flow meter to empty it completely. If the tank needs replacement, contact the Platform manager.

**4.3.8** Verify that the AED box contains two (2) pad boxes, if applicable (one adult and one pediatric), and they are not expired or due to expire before the next verification. Also, confirm the defibrillator verification by Biomed has been performed on a quarterly basis (every 3 months). For any required maintenance and/or replacement of used or expired pads contact Biomed.

**4.3.9** Once the verification is complete, lock the cart using the applicable numbered tag locks and record the new tag lock number on Appendix 1 (Glen) or Appendix 2 (MGH).

**4.3.10** Fill out the order form(s) using the appropriate form for any medications missing, expired or expiring prior to next month's verification. It is recommended to replace medication at least one month in advance of its expiry when possible (e.g., for UBEC carts at the CIM located at the MGH).

**4.3.11** Order missing/expired items with the assistance of a CIM administrative assistant, providing the applicable cost center, order number, and the quantity needed.

**4.3.12** Orders for medication must be sent to the Pharmacy by fax. Medication orders should not be placed on Fridays or prior to public holidays, as staff will not be available at the reception to receive the orders. Ensure the fax was received correctly by obtaining the confirmation and verifying that the form was scanned correctly as the Pharmacy will not be able to reach you if the fax was not submitted correctly. Orders placed with Pharmacy are typically fulfilled within 24 hours; therefore, if not received within this timeline the CRC-N must follow-up to ensure the order is fulfilled as soon as possible.

**4.3.13** Inform the receptionist that an order was placed so they will know who is expecting the order (e.g., use plasticized card in the UBEC binder with your name and contact).

**4.3.14** Place a copy of the fax sent to Pharmacy and the order form in the applicable section of the UBEC Verification Binder. Once received, write "Received" with your initials/date on the fax so it is clear the order was fulfilled and no further action is required.

**4.3.15** When the ordered items are received, they must be added to the UBEC. Open the UBEC to replace the items and document the replacement by completing the following information in the section where the identified missing or expired items were documented during the verification on Appendix 1 (Glen) or Appendix 2 (MGH). If no items were restocked these sections will not be completed:
- Enter the quantity of the item being restocked in the appropriate row for the item under column **"R."** The quantity in columns "A" and "R" must equal the "Quota" column.
- Enter the previous tag number.
- Enter the new tag number.
- Both the nurse responsible for the verification and the second nurse completing the form for the restocking of items will initial and date in the applicable section for restocking.

**4.3.16** When the UBEC verification has been completed, the CRC-Ns must inform the Safety Team Lead.

### 4.4 QC Review by Safety Team Lead

**4.4.1** The review of the UBEC Verification Binder will comprise of:
- A QC review of Appendix 1 (Glen) or Appendix 2 (MGH) on a bimonthly basis and document this review by signing the QC Review section of the form to ensure the UBEC verification was performed and appropriately documented following good documentation practice and in accordance with this WI. Any discrepancies will be investigated.
- Ensure there are sufficient, up-to-date copies (i.e., current version) of the required documents listed in section 4.1.2. This verification must include confirmation the appendices conform to the latest MUHC forms available on MyMUHC. If revisions are required the appendices may be updated by the Safety Team Lead and version controlled accordingly.
- Remove checklists completed one year from the completion date and prepare the documents for archiving in accordance with the applicable procedure on record retention and archiving.
