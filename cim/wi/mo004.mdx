## 1 · Purpose and scope

The purpose of a Close-Out Visit (COV) is to ensure that all clinical trial-related activities are appropriately recorded, reported, and reconciled at the end of a trial in accordance with the protocol, SOPs, GCP, and applicable regulatory requirements. This WI describes the preparation, conduct, and follow-up of the COV.

---

## 2 · Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Monitor (CRA) | Prepares for and conducts the COV, completes the COV report, issues follow-up and post-COV letters |
| Project Manager | Reviews and approves the COV report; communicates completion to Sponsor |
| RI-MUHC Qualified Investigator | Attends the visit, responds to queries, signs final documents, ensures ongoing post-study obligations |
| Sponsor | Triggers close-out; confirms study end to Health Canada (if applicable) |

## 4 · Instructions

### 5.1 Close-Out Visit Preparation

**Step 5.1.1** In preparation for the COV, the monitor must:
- Schedule the visit with the QI and staff
- Confirm with the site that the last subject has completed the last scheduled study visit and all associated CRFs are completed
- Review the Sponsor's TMF for completeness, ensuring all applicable documents are on file per ICH-GCP section 8
- Review outstanding source document verification, eCRF completeness, and administrative issues
- Review previous monitoring visit reports for any open issues
- Prepare the clinical supply return form when applicable
- Gather tamper-proof materials
- Prepare to reconcile IND Safety Reports generated from receipt of study drug until final drug accountability, if applicable
- Review the study budget to ensure outstanding amounts are reconciled

### 5.2 During the Close-Out Visit

When the monitor arrives, they must meet with the QI and staff (and pharmacy if applicable) and sign the Monitoring Visit Log or equivalent.

#### 5.2.1 Final Subject Recruitment Status

**Step 5.2.1.1** Complete the Final Subject Recruitment Status Log in section 1 of Appendix 1.

**Step 5.2.1.2** Document any other items in section 1 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.2 Investigator Responsibility

**Step 5.2.2.1** The Investigator must be present to respond to the monitor's questions, queries, requests for additional information, NTFs, and last signatures.

**Step 5.2.2.2** Verify that all SADRs will continue to be reported to Health Canada and REB as per protocol.

**Step 5.2.2.3** Verify all patients with AEs/SAEs have received and will receive appropriate follow-up medical care.

**Step 5.2.2.4** Verify all SUSAR reports have been initialed and dated by the QI and submitted to the REB in a timely fashion.

**Step 5.2.2.5** Verify changes in the research team and staff responsibilities have been documented in the Tasks Delegation Log (SOP-CR-002) and all required training recorded in the Training Log (SOP-CR-003).

**Step 5.2.2.6** Confirm documented QI review is evident by the QI's initials and date on each monitoring report/letter.

**Step 5.2.2.7** Verify remaining action items from previous monitoring visit reports have been reconciled.

**Step 5.2.2.8** Verify the site meets ICH-GCP, Health Canada, and FDA requirements and is informed of the possibility of an audit or inspection (SOP-CR-016, SOP-CR-017). Ensure the site is aware of the importance of informing the Sponsor within **24 hours** of any Health Canada, FDA, or other regulatory agency inspection request.

**Step 5.2.2.9** Verify all documents requiring medical oversight have been initialed and dated by the QI (test result printouts, appropriate source forms, safety-related newsletters).

**Step 5.2.2.10** Verify the QI has signed off on all CRFs/eCRFs.

**Step 5.2.2.11** Inform the Investigator about the final study report (SOP-CR-007).

**Step 5.2.2.12** Discuss storage retention requirements: **25 years** for Health Canada-regulated studies; **7 years from publication date** for non-HC regulated studies.

**Step 5.2.2.13** Document any other items in section 2 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.3 Investigational Product (IP) / Device

Refer to WI-CIM-MO005 Site Monitoring of Investigational Product Management for further instructions.

**Step 5.2.3.1** Document any other items in section 3 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.4 Study Materials

**Step 5.2.4.1** Describe final disposition of unused study materials.

**Step 5.2.4.2** Return all Sponsor equipment and supplies, unless otherwise arranged, and document it.

**Step 5.2.4.3** Document any other items in section 4 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.5 Serious Adverse Events / Adverse Events

Refer to SOP-CR-012 and WI-CIM-MO006 for further instructions.

**Step 5.2.5.1** Document any other items in section 5 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.6 Laboratory Specimens

**Step 5.2.6.1** Return all lab specimens (blood, tissues, urine, etc.) to the Sponsor for evaluation and storage as per protocol/written instructions.

#### 5.2.7 Monitoring

Document any other items in section 6 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.8 Review of Investigator Study File and Essential Documents

**Step 5.2.8.1** Review Investigator Study Files to ensure all required documents are maintained and filed properly (SOP-CR-015, ICH-GCP).

**Step 5.2.8.2** Retrieve Investigator Trial Documents to be collected, as applicable.

**Step 5.2.8.3** Document any other items in section 7 of Appendix 1.

### 5.3 After the Close-Out Visit

**Step 5.3.1** Complete the Site Close-Out Visit Report within **10 working days** after the visit.

**Step 5.3.2** Send the Site Close-Out Visit Report to the Project Manager/Sponsor or Sponsor-Investigator or designee for review and approval.

**Step 5.3.3** The Project Manager/Sponsor reviews and approves the report in a timely manner and follows up as necessary.

**Step 5.3.4** In a timely manner, it is the monitor's responsibility to:
- Take action(s) on significant issues identified during the COV
- Follow up with the trial site on issues and/or corrective actions
- Document resolution of outstanding issues
- Communicate to the PM/Sponsor that all COV procedures are complete and no further visits are needed

**Step 5.3.5** After review is completed, send a copy of the Site Close-Out Visit Report to the trial site for filing in the ISF.

**Step 5.3.6** Discuss anticipated timing of outstanding payments (if applicable) per the contract and CIM agreement.

**Step 5.3.7** Discuss anticipated timing of availability of the Final Study Report.

**Step 5.3.8** Discuss publication plan as per the study contract or agreement.

**Step 5.3.9** Remind the QI of their responsibility to report all unexpected SADRs to the Sponsor and REB after study closure.

**Step 5.3.10** Obtain documentation regarding archiving of the study (date, time period, and location).

**Step 5.3.11** Provide feedback regarding the site's performance and discuss plans for audit or inspection (if applicable).

**Step 5.3.12** Request confirmation that the REB has been informed the clinical study has ended and that a final study report/summary has been submitted to the REB.

**Step 5.3.13** If under a Clinical Trial Application (CTA), ensure that Health Canada has been informed the study has ended by either the Sponsor, Sponsor-Investigator, or CIM Project Manager.
