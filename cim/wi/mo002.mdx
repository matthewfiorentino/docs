## 1 · Purpose and scope {#purpose-and-scope}

Once a site is approved by the Sponsor and monitoring has been delegated to RI-MUHC/CIM, this WI describes the preparation, conduct, and follow-up of the Site Initiation Visit (SIV). The SIV assesses the site's readiness to start the trial and trains site personnel on the protocol, informed consent, AE/SAE reporting, GCP, IP management, and any other items required for trial conduct. Subject recruitment must not start until the SIV is complete.

As per ICH-GCP 5.18.7, the Sponsor must develop a monitoring plan tailored to the specific human subject protection and data integrity risks of the trial.

## 2 · Responsibilities {#responsibilities}

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Monitor (CRA) | Prepares for and conducts the SIV, completes the SIV report, trains site personnel, follows up on action items |
| RI-MUHC Qualified Investigator | Attends SIV, signs training log, initials and dates the final SIV report, files the report in the ISF |
| Sponsor | Ensures adequate monitoring; provides or approves the monitoring plan; issues GO letter |

## 4 · Instructions {#instructions}

Once the Monitoring Plan is finalized and signed, a Site Initiation Visit (SIV) is held when applicable. If the Sponsor does not have a Monitoring Plan template, the RI-MUHC/CIM template (Appendix 3) may be provided.

If sites upload certified copies to a cloud-based repository, the Monitoring Plan must be updated to reflect remote monitoring, include a privacy risk assessment, and the informed consent must be updated to address sharing of personal information.

### 5.1 Site Initiation Visit Preparation {#site-initiation-visit-preparation}

**Step 5.1.1** Once the site has been qualified and agreed to conduct the trial, schedule the SIV. Send a confirmation e-mail with detailed visit information (Appendix 2).

**Step 5.1.2** Before the visit, if not already collected by the Sponsor, the monitor must:
- Obtain a copy of the signed Confidentiality Agreement / Clinical Trial Agreement between Sponsor and Investigator/Institution
- Obtain a copy of all essential regulatory documents signed by the QI
- Prepare an agenda including all mandatory attendees
- Prepare slides/handouts, a study Investigator Study Binder with Table of Contents, contact sheet and tabs (if not provided by Sponsor), and verify site's ability to project slides
- Ensure all study-related materials, including investigational product (if applicable), are received at the site and reviewed prior to the SIV

**Step 5.1.3** Ensure the site has obtained approval from the appropriate REB, Health Authorities, and any other site-specific approval body. Confirm that all recruitment materials and subject materials (questionnaires, diaries, advertisements) are REB-approved.

### 5.2 During the Site Initiation Visit {#during-the-site-initiation-visit}

The monitor and site personnel present must sign the Training Log (SOP-CR-003, Appendix 1).

#### 5.2.1 Staffing / Equipment / Facilities Allocations Assessment

**Step 5.2.1.1** Confirm staffing allocation with the QI to ensure necessary and qualified resources are available as discussed during the feasibility visit.

**Step 5.2.1.2** List all equipment needed and confirm availability at the site as per SOP-CR-025 Equipment Calibration and Maintenance.

**Step 5.2.1.3** Document any other items in section 1 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.2 Study Protocol Overview

**Step 5.2.2.1** Train all study personnel on study protocol objectives, design, and background.

**Step 5.2.2.2** Review Informed Consent/Assent procedures as per SOP-CR008. Emphasize that:
- The most recent REB-approved version of the ICF must be used
- All elements of the IC/Assent must be explained to the subject
- The IC must be signed prior to any study procedures
- The Informed Consent process must be documented in source documents

**Step 5.2.2.3** Discuss subject recruitment process as per SOP-CR-009. Ensure applicable documentation is REB-approved and maintained. Essential documents should include: Screening Log, Subject Enrollment Log.

**Step 5.2.2.4** Confirm a List of Subject Identification Codes is maintained at the site. This list is confidential and must not be provided to the Sponsor or Sponsor's representatives.

**Step 5.2.2.5** Train study personnel on specific eligibility criteria (inclusion/exclusion) as described in the protocol.

**Step 5.2.2.6** Review enrollment/randomization procedures with study personnel.

**Step 5.2.2.7** Discuss the enrollment plan and estimated timeline for including the first subject.

**Step 5.2.2.8** Reiterate the importance of protocol adherence; no amendment may be followed until REB approval is received.

**Step 5.2.2.9** Confirm the QI understands their responsibility as the person ultimately responsible for study conduct at the site, regardless of who tasks are delegated to.

**Step 5.2.2.10** Document any other items in section 2 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.3 Study Procedures Overview

**Step 5.2.3.1** Review drug administration procedures with the study pharmacist (if applicable).

**Step 5.2.3.2** Review clinical assessments required by the study protocol.

**Step 5.2.3.3** Review laboratory assessments. Ensure all assessments can be done locally or through an external facility with a signed service agreement.

**Step 5.2.3.4** Review specimen collection schedule. Emphasize special specimen-timing requirements. Refer to SOP-CR-011, Appendix 1.

**Step 5.2.3.5** Discuss protocol deviations process (exceptions and violations) as per SOP-CR-007. Introduce the Protocol Deviation Log (SOP-CR-007, Appendix 1).

**Step 5.2.3.6** Ensure the QI understands their responsibility for medical oversight (initialing and dating all test results in a timely fashion) and any document related to medical/study conduct.

**Step 5.2.3.7** Verify facilities are adequate and equipment is inspected/calibrated/maintained at least annually.

**Step 5.2.3.8** Document any other items in section 3 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.4 Adverse Event / Serious Adverse Event Reporting

Refer to WI-CIM-MO006 Monitoring of Safety Assessment & AE/SAE Reporting for further instructions.

**Step 5.2.4.1** Document any other items in section 4 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.5 Staff Roles and Responsibility

**Step 5.2.5.1** Discuss and agree on site personnel responsibilities with the QI and document on the Tasks Delegation Log (SOP-CR-002, Appendix 1). Task types include: source documentation, IP management, subject recruitment, CRF/data management, regulatory updates, specimen management, AE/SAE management, and task delegation.

**Step 5.2.5.2** Document any other items in section 5 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.6 Data Collection/Handling

**Step 5.2.6.1** Review data collection procedures. Sites should create study-specific source document forms with version control.

**Step 5.2.6.2** Train study personnel on data entry guidelines to ensure prompt and legible transcription to CRFs or e-CRFs.

**Step 5.2.6.3** Review common data collection errors. Discuss Good Documentation Practices, correction process, and disposition of forms.

**Step 5.2.6.4** Document any other items in section 6 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.7 Source Documentation Collection

**Step 5.2.7.1** Review source data management as per SOP-CR-015. Examples of source data: informed consent documentation, demographic data, medical history/concomitant medications/AEs, inclusion/exclusion and randomization criteria, objective test results reviewed and initialed by the Investigator.

**Step 5.2.7.2** Document any other items in section 7 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.8 Data Management

**Step 5.2.8.1** Review data queries/discrepancies management procedure with study personnel.

**Step 5.2.8.2** Confirm list of staff approved for data entry and document on the Tasks Delegation Log.

**Step 5.2.8.3** Document any other items in section 8 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.9 Site Regulatory Documents

**Step 5.2.9.1** Verify filing of regulatory documents using the "Verification/Collection of Regulatory Documents" checklist in Appendix 1.

**Step 5.2.9.2** Confirm the Investigator and study personnel understand the requirement for REB continuing review (annual renewal) and ongoing review of additional document/information submissions.

**Step 5.2.9.3** List all versions/dates of protocols, ICFs, and IBs presented to the REB; collect copies of approval letters.

**Step 5.2.9.4** Verify MUHC (or other institution) authorization letter was received at the site.

**Step 5.2.9.5** Discuss appropriate maintenance of subject study binders.

**Step 5.2.9.6** Confirm the Investigator's Study File was provided by the Sponsor or created by the site (for QI-initiated studies).

**Step 5.2.9.7** Ensure all study personnel roles are documented on the Tasks Delegation Log (SOP-CR-002). The QI must confirm delegation with initials.

**Step 5.2.9.8** Document any other items in section 9 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.10 Record Retention Requirements

**Step 5.2.10.1** Instruct the Investigator and study personnel on record-keeping for: Screening Log, Enrollment Log, Subject Identification Log, Tasks Delegation Log, AE Log, Concomitant Med Log, SAE Log, Monitoring Visit Log, and ICF Log.

**Step 5.2.10.2** Discuss the importance of maintaining all study-related correspondence including letters, emails, and telephone contact reports.

**Step 5.2.10.3** Document any other items in section 10 of Appendix 1.

Both paper and electronic copies of essential documents are acceptable. The site must provide the monitor access to either version of the Investigator Site Binder.

#### 5.2.11 Laboratory Procedures

**Step 5.2.11.1** Train the Investigator and site personnel on clinical laboratory assessments and procedures as required by the study protocol and/or laboratory manual. Review specimen collection, processing, shipping, storage, and disposition.

**Step 5.2.11.2** Document any other items in section 11 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.12 Investigational Product / Clinical Supplies / Device Management

Refer to WI-CIM-MO005 Site Monitoring of Investigational Product Management for further instructions.

**Step 5.2.12.1** Document any other items in section 12 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.13 Site Monitoring Overview

**Step 5.2.13.1** Confirm the Investigator agrees to monitoring/auditing/inspection visits. Highlight the importance of contacting the Sponsor immediately upon notice of an upcoming regulatory inspection.

**Step 5.2.13.2** Discuss the monitoring plan with the Investigator and clinical staff, including monitoring purpose, expectations, frequency of visits, monitoring reports, and distribution.

**Step 5.2.13.3** Document any other items in section 13 of Appendix 1.

#### 5.2.14 Unblinding Process (when applicable)

**Step 5.2.14.1** Train the Investigator and study personnel on emergency unblinding procedures required by the study protocol, if applicable.

**Step 5.2.14.2** Review management of sealed/confidential envelopes containing blinding information. Confirm envelopes are available and intact at the study site.

**Step 5.2.14.3** Instruct the Investigator and study personnel on how to document unblinding events in source documents.

**Step 5.2.14.4** Document any other items in section 14 of Appendix 1.

### 5.3 After the Site Initiation Visit {#after-the-site-initiation-visit}

**Step 5.3.1** Complete and sign the Site Initiation Visit Report Template (Appendix 1) within **10 working days** after the visit.

**Step 5.3.2** Submit the report for review and approval within **10 working days**.

**Step 5.3.3** Send a copy of the final SIV report (including the list of required actions) to the Investigator for inclusion in the ISF.

**Step 5.3.4** Follow up on the list of actions identified and document resolution of issues.

**Step 5.3.5** Verify that the final SIV report is initialed and dated by the QI. Discuss any outstanding issues, including:
- Review the monitoring plan
- Line of communication and expectations for following up on action items
- Regulatory status and clinical supplies status
- Trial calendar (confirm dates)
- Review Day-1 and Day 1 activities
- Sponsor/QI "GO" letter
- Review activities for pilot training, run-throughs, etc.
