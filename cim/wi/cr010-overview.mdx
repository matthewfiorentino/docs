# Communication Methods with Study Participants

<Badge color="gray">WI-CIM-CR010_01</Badge> <Badge color="gray">Version 01</Badge> <Badge color="green">Effective 17 Mar 2023</Badge> <Badge color="blue">CIM Research Staff</Badge>

<Note>
This WI describes the requirements for telephone communication between CRCs, CRC-RNs, managers, supervisors, and study participants. It applies to CRCs, CRC-RNs, Managers, and Supervisors involved in clinical studies at the CIM, RI-MUHC. Personal cell phones must not be used by CRC/CRC-RN/managers/supervisors to contact study participants.
</Note>

## Key points

- CRC/CRC-RN/managers/supervisors **must not** provide their personal cell phone number to study participants.
- Study participants must be provided with a CRC/CRC-RN business card containing CIM coordinates (work phone number/extension and email).
- Study participants must be provided with a **patient alert card** (REB-approved) indicating they are part of a clinical trial and the medication type — participants are instructed to carry this card at all times and show it to emergency room staff and other health care providers.
- During working hours, calls to the CIM work phone may be forwarded to the CRC/CRC-RN's cell phone, provided that call forwarding is cancelled at the end of the work day.
- If a participant already has a CRC/CRC-RN's personal cell phone number, the CRC/CRC-RN must inform the participant to use the work extension instead, and remind them to carry their study participation card.
- The PI/QI or sub-Investigator providing coverage during PI/QI absence may, at their discretion, provide study participants with a personal/professional cell phone number.
- Call transfer using the AVAYA phone: press **Fwd**, enter 5-digit extension or 10-digit cell number, confirm the display, and **cancel call forwarding at end of each work day**.
- Contact outside working hours should be assessed on a case-by-case basis.
