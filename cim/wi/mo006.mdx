## 1 · Purpose and scope {#purpose-and-scope}

This WI describes the review of site patient safety assessments and reporting during remote and site monitoring activities to ensure:
- Reporting of AEs (including ADRs and MDIs) and SAEs (including SADRs and SMDIs) is complete and accurate
- SAEs are reported to the study Sponsor, the REB (if reportable), and regulatory authorities (Health Canada, FDA, EMA) within required timeframes
- Compliance with RI-MUHC SOPs, GCP, ICH E2A, Medical Devices Regulations SOR/98-282, Health Canada incident reporting guidance, and applicable regulatory requirements

This WI applies to RI-MUHC/CIM Monitors involved in monitoring research with human participants at the MUHC/RI-MUHC and at external institution and clinic sites. It covers preparation, conduct, and follow-up of monitoring activities concerning participant assessment and AE/SAE reporting only, and is used in conjunction with other CIM Monitoring WIs.

## 4 · Instructions {#instructions}

### 5.1 Monitoring Reference Documents {#monitoring-reference-documents}

**Step 5.1.1** Site and remote monitoring of patient safety assessments and reporting of AEs, SAEs, MDIs, and SMDIs will be carried out according to the approved protocol, IB/PM (and any amendments), SUSAR reports, RI-MUHC SOP-CR-012, RI-MUHC SOP-CR-024, and current ICH-GCP and Health Canada forms, reporting criteria, and requirements.

### 5.2 Monitoring Method {#monitoring-method}

AEs, SAEs, MDIs, and SMDIs can be monitored centrally and remotely by means of signal surveillance (a safety "signal" is a report or reports of an event with unknown causal relationship to treatment that is recognized as worthy of further exploration). This allows the team to react quickly to any increase in frequency, severity, or unexpectedness of any AEs, SAEs, MDIs, or SMDIs.

Monitoring also takes place during Site Monitoring Visits where source data verification is conducted to ensure that:

**Step 5.2.1** All AEs, SAEs, MDIs, and SMDIs are captured in source forms and logs by the site.

**Step 5.2.2** All AEs, SAEs, MDIs, and SMDIs are assessed by the Sponsor-Investigator or QI/PI.

**Step 5.2.3** All reportable SAEs and SMDIs have been reported to the CIM CRA/Monitor/Sponsor and the REB by the QI/PI or delegate within the timeframe specified in RI-MUHC SOP-CR-012.

**Step 5.2.4** All reportable SAEs and SMDIs have been reported to the appropriate regulatory authorities by the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator/Monitor within the required timeframe specified in RI-MUHC SOP-CR-012 and SOP-CR-024.

### 5.3 Preparation of the SIV or Site Monitoring Visit {#preparation-of-the-siv-or-site-monitoring-visit}

**Step 5.3.1** Review the protocol, IB/PM (and any amendments), and any SUSAR reports, newsletters, or memos for safety information.

**Step 5.3.2** Send any amendments/updated documents/safety information to the study sites.

**Step 5.3.3** Prepare/update study-specific training materials as required.

### 5.4 Adverse Event Training during the Site Initiation Visit (SIV) {#adverse-event-training-during-the-site-initiation-visit-siv}

**Step 5.4.1** Train the Investigator and study staff on AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI management, reporting procedures, and timelines per RI-MUHC SOP-CR-012 and SOP-CR-024.

**Step 5.4.2** Review the REB-approved protocol and IB/PM information and any amendments related to AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI management, including AE/MDI severity grading (e.g., CTCAE in oncology), disease/indication-specific events, and follow-up with the Sponsor-Investigator/QI/PI and study staff.

**Step 5.4.3** Instruct site staff to inform the Sponsor-Investigator/QI/PI about any participant SAE/SMDI and obtain their medical assessment within **24 hours** of the site's knowledge. In addition to seriousness, the following must be assessed: outcome, action taken, severity (based on intensity), expectedness (based on IB/PM and IP information), and relatedness of the SAE/SMDI to each IP administered.

**Step 5.4.4** Instruct site staff on how to document all AEs/MDIs in source and have the Sponsor-Investigator/QI/PI assess AEs/MDIs on an AE/MDI Assessment Log (one log per participant binder), and subsequently transcribe data into CRFs.

**Step 5.4.5** Instruct site staff on reporting all SAEs and SMDIs to the Sponsor/Monitor within **24 hours** using the appropriate SAE or MDI reporting form.

**Step 5.4.6** Instruct the site on assessing, reporting, and tracking SAEs and MDIs on an SAE Reporting Log or MDI Reporting Log.

**Step 5.4.7** Instruct site staff on reporting all unexpected SAEs and/or MDIs that are at least possibly related to IP or study conduct to the REB within **7 days** for drug and within **10 days** for medical device (SOP-CR-012, SOP-CR-024).

**Step 5.4.8** Instruct the Sponsor-Investigator (if applicable) on reporting all unexpected SAEs and/or SMDIs to regulatory authorities via the CIOMS-1 reporting form. Sponsors/Sponsor-Investigators must also report to the DSMB and Health Canada "unexpected, serious" ADRs/MDIs where an increase in the rate of occurrence or severity was judged to be clinically important.

### 5.6 Remote Monitoring of AEs/SAEs/MDIs/SMDIs (if applicable) {#remote-monitoring-of-aes-saes-mdis-smdis-if-applicable}

Remote monitoring maintains oversight of a site between on-site visits through regular communication by email, telephone, or teleconference. All communication must be recorded (emails printed; telephone communications recorded on a communication log and filed in the ISF).

**Step 5.6.1** If the study uses an EDC/eCRF, review the eCRF AE/MDI sections on a regular basis (per monitoring plan) to confirm if AEs have lessened in severity and ended, remain stable, or escalated to SAEs/SMDIs.

**Step 5.6.2** If unreported SAEs/MDIs are found, immediately follow up with the site to ensure compliance with reporting criteria.

**Step 5.6.3** If the site did not report a reportable SAE/MDI within required timelines, ensure a deviation was reported on the Deviation Log and submitted to the REB along with the SAE/SMDI. Ensure that, if applicable, the reportable SAE/SMDI was reported to regulatory authorities, and arrange to re-train the site on SAE/SMDI reporting criteria as soon as possible.

### 5.7 Review of AEs/SAEs/MDIs/SMDIs during a Site Monitoring Visit (Remote or On-site) {#review-of-aes-saes-mdis-smdis-during-a-site-monitoring-visit-remote-or-on-site}

**Step 5.7.1** Ensure the site has filed all documents pertinent to safety information, including the REB-approved protocol and any amendments, the IB/PM and any amendments, and all SUSAR reports, newsletters, and memos regarding safety.

**Step 5.7.2** Ensure that all protocol and IB/PM signature pages, all SUSAR reports, newsletters, and memos regarding safety have been signed/initialed and dated in a timely fashion by the Sponsor-Investigator/QI/PI to show medical oversight. The QI/PI must ensure documentation that they shared important safety information (e.g., SUSARs) with the study team.

**Step 5.7.3** Conduct source data verification by reviewing source documents for AEs/SAEs/MDIs/SMDIs. Verify completeness and consistency of CRFs and AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI logs — including start and end dates, seriousness, severity, causality, action taken, and outcome per source documents and CRFs. Ensure any new AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI or change in severity has been acted upon and appropriately documented.

**Step 5.7.4** Ensure AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI information on source forms is consistent with other logs — e.g., "action taken" on any AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI form or log is consistent with medication listed on the Concomitant Medications Log, and the "reason for administration" mirrors the AE/MDI listed.

**Step 5.7.5** Ensure any correspondence/documentation relating to safety assessment reporting and evidence of Sponsor acknowledgement are maintained in the ISF.

**Step 5.7.6** Ensure any correspondence/documentation relating to unexpected SADRs or SADEs to and from the REB, as well as REB acknowledgement of the reportable SAE/MDI, are also maintained in the ISF.

**Step 5.7.7** If applicable, ensure current blank versions of the Sponsor's SAE reporting form and the Health Canada SAE reporting form (CIOMS form) are on file, and that information on completed forms is accurate, concise, and clear.

**Step 5.7.8** Ensure the Sponsor/Sponsor-Investigator has reported and signed off on all unexpected SADRs and SADEs on the appropriate form to regulatory authorities within reporting timelines (Health Canada: within **7 days** from when the Sponsor-Investigator/QI/PI became aware of the event). Verify the appropriate follow-up form has been completed, signed, and returned to regulatory authorities.

**Step 5.7.9** Ensure there is evidence that periodic safety updates and summaries are sent to the appropriate regulatory authorities and REB as required (SOP-CR-012, SOP-CR-024).

### 5.8 Review of AEs/SAEs/MDIs/SMDIs during the Close-Out Visit {#review-of-aes-saes-mdis-smdis-during-the-close-out-visit}

**Step 5.8.1** Verify all AEs/SAEs have been accurately documented including start and end dates, severity, causality, and action taken per protocol, source documents, and CRFs.

**Step 5.8.2** Verify the site has appropriately followed up on all ongoing AEs, SAEs, MDIs, or SMDIs. Verify AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI Logs are accurate and complete.

**Step 5.8.3** Verify documentation demonstrates SAEs/MDIs were appropriately reported to the Sponsor, REB, and/or regulatory authorities in accordance with the REB-approved protocol and RI-MUHC SOPs.

**Step 5.8.4** Follow up with the site to ensure all AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI documents have been reviewed by the Sponsor-Investigator/QI/PI and archived with all study documents in the ISF.

**Step 5.8.5** Follow up with the Sponsor (if applicable) to ensure all AE/SAE/MDI/SMDI documents have been reviewed and archived with the Sponsor's TMF.
