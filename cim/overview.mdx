The Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM) is the RI-MUHC's clinical research operations unit. It provides the infrastructure, technical platforms, and specialized staffing that make complex clinical trials possible at the Glen and MGH sites — from Phase 1 oncology trials to multi-centric device studies.

For researchers, CIM is a service provider: you engage CIM when your study requires capabilities or platforms that your own team doesn't have. For CIM staff, this section is the operational reference for conducting studies within the unit.
