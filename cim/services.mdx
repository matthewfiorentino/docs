The McConnell Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM) is the RI-MUHC's clinical research facility, a research hospital within a hospital. The CIM provides staff, facilities and technical platforms for pediatric and adult clinical research at the Glen site and the Montreal General Hospital.

Research at the CIM ranges from first-in-human (Phase I) trials to Phase III trials, industry-sponsored or investigator-initiated. The CIM runs medical device trials as well as drug trials. The CIM is located in the MUHC's hospitals, but its operations, management and services fall under the RI-MUHC.

Any researcher at The Institute can use CIM services, with no formal affiliation with the CIM. The CIM can run an industry-sponsored study from start to finish, or provide individual services for a study initiated by its <Tooltip tip="The investigator who leads the study team at a site and is responsible for conducting the study there." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#pi">principal investigator</Tooltip>. When to contact the CIM, and what to prepare, is on [Planning Your Study with the CIM](/cim/planning).

<Note>
[If your study uses CIM services, a CIM agreement must be in place](/sops/cr-002#5-5-3). For services used since June 1, 2026, the principal investigator signs a CIM service agreement, which sets out the study's details and the CIM's roles and responsibilities.
</Note>

## The team

The CIM's staff can support a study from feasibility to close-out:

| Staff | What they do |
|---|---|
| Clinical research nurses | Intravenous infusions, blood draws, specimen collection, vital signs, electrocardiograms (ECG) and clinical monitoring. Nurses who give chemotherapy and biotherapy as study drugs have completed a competency-based training program on giving them. |
| Research coordinators | Study documentation, participant coordination, regulatory compliance, consent, screening, enrolment, data capture and the management of the site file. |
| Project managers | Study timelines, budgets, vendors and reporting. For sponsor-investigator studies, the CIM's project managers coordinate the monitoring program. |
| Monitors (clinical research associates) | Site initiation, routine and remote monitoring and close-out visits, and the monitoring of the investigational product's accountability, for sponsor-investigator studies conducted through the CIM. |
| Regulatory and ethics specialists | Support for submissions to the Research Ethics Board (REB): study start-up, the development and amendment of consent forms, annual renewals and study closures. |
| Budget specialists | Cost estimates for the CIM services a study uses. For a study the CIM runs, also a review of the study budget and its negotiation with the industry sponsor. The principal investigator approves the budget. |
| Laboratory technologists and technicians | Specimen processing, biobanking and shipping, in the Containment Level 2 processing laboratory at the Glen and the Montreal General sites. |
| Medical imaging technologists | Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA), retinal imaging, fundus autofluorescence and other ophthalmic imaging, on the CIM's technical platforms. |
| Functional testing technologists | Cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET), pulmonary function testing (PFT), ECG, Holter monitoring, ambulatory blood pressure, body composition and polysomnography. |
| Data entry clerks | Clinical data management, completion of case report forms and data entry, for studies run through the CIM. |

## Get help with ethics submissions and budgets

The CIM's ethics team helps any researcher at The Institute with submissions to the REB: study start-up and initial submissions, the development and amendment of consent forms, annual renewals and study closures. Write to the CIM's Research Ethics Assistant at [cimregulatory@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:cimregulatory@muhc.mcgill.ca).

The CIM's budget specialists estimate the cost of the CIM services a study uses. For a study the CIM runs, they also review the budget and can help prepare it and negotiate it with the sponsor. The principal investigator approves the budget after their review. For a study the CIM runs, consider contacting the [CIM's Budget Supervisor](mailto:nadia.lombardo@muhc.mcgill.ca) during feasibility, before the study's agreement is signed. How a sponsor's budget is negotiated is on [Study Budgets](/kb/funding/budgets-contracts#negotiate-the-budget-with-an-industry-sponsor).

## Project management and monitoring

The CIM's project managers keep studies on time, within budget and in line with the applicable regulatory guidelines. Its monitors check data quality and participant safety against the protocol, good clinical practice and the RI-MUHC's standard operating procedures (SOPs). Monitoring services include initiation, routine, remote and close-out visits, monitoring visit reports and liaison between the sponsor and the site. Write to the CIM's [project management and monitoring lead](mailto:angie.lombardo@muhc.mcgill.ca).

A sponsor-investigator who delegates monitoring to the CIM keeps the [ultimate responsibility for it](/kb/planning/sponsor-investigator#monitoring-independence). The sponsor-investigator is responsible for ensuring that the duties the CIM carries out for the study are [specified in writing](/sops/cr-002#5-2-1), and for overseeing them.

In May 2026, the CIM was negotiating a contract with Advarra for OnCore, a clinical trial management system. Ask the [CIM](mailto:clinical.research@muhc.mcgill.ca) about its status.

### Fees

Since May 25, 2026, the CIM's service price list has two tiers of fees, one for studies initiated by a principal investigator and one for industry-initiated studies. Industry-sponsored clinical trials pay 35 per cent overhead.

The [CIM Technical Platform Fee Schedule](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=304624964744813810) (PDF), on the RI-MUHC Portal, gives the rate of each service. The schedule sets three rates:

- Equipment rental only, for RI-MUHC studies only.
- Non-industry, for studies initiated by a principal investigator that no pharmaceutical company funds.
- Industry, for every study a pharmaceutical company funds or an industry sponsor sponsors, including studies initiated by a principal investigator.

The CIM reviews its prices every year and may change them without notice. For a quote on a service the schedule doesn't list, or for a complex protocol, write to the [Technical Platforms Manager](mailto:jacinthe.baril@muhc.mcgill.ca).

## Have research bloodwork done at the CIM

<Warning>
Since June 1, 2026, the Oncology Day Centre (ODC) Blood Test Centre at the Glen no longer performs research blood tests. All research-related bloodwork at the Royal Victoria Hospital must be completed at the CIM.
</Warning>

At the Royal Victoria Hospital (Glen site):

- A participant who needs both research and standard-of-care blood tests can have all of them done at the CIM, with no extra charge for the standard-of-care tests, provided that all the standard-of-care labels are printed beforehand and sent with the participant. The CIM can train research staff to print the labels.
- The CIM's blood test service is open Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.
- Appointments are booked through CrossLab, under **Schedule Equipment**. The CIM may also accommodate last-minute requests.

At the Montreal General:

- A dedicated CIM coordinator centralizes communication and coordination across principal investigators.

### Confirm Health Canada authorization for a sponsor-supplied device

<div className="hub-scope"><span className="hub-scope-label">Applies to</span><span className="hub-scope-tag">Drug studies (Level I)</span><span className="hub-scope-tag">Medical device and surgery studies (Level II)</span><span className="hub-scope-tag">Studies regulated by Health Canada</span></div>

Before a sponsor-supplied device is used, confirm with the sponsor that Health Canada has issued an [Investigational Testing Authorization](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/medical-devices/application-information/guidance-documents/investigational-testing-authorizations-guidance/guidance-document.html) for this study if the device is:

- A Class II, III or IV device that isn't licensed in Canada, including one used in a drug trial.
- A licensed device that a study sponsored by its manufacturer tests to support a new indication.

A Class I device needs no Investigational Testing Authorization. Its manufacturer or importer must hold the records that the Medical Devices Regulations require. Institutional requirements and REB approval still apply.

Health Canada refuses a request to its [Special Access Program](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/special-access/medical-devices.html) for a device that is being used for investigational purposes.
