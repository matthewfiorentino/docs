The McConnell Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM) provides a full range of clinical research services, facilities, and technical platforms at the Glen site and Montreal General. This page covers everything available to you — from the people and expertise on the team to technical platform booking, equipment management, and support services.

<Note>
  New to CIM? See [Planning Your Study with CIM](/cim/planning) for the engagement process, budget timing, and what to do before your Nagano submission.
</Note>

## The team

The CIM brings together specialists from across clinical research disciplines. When you engage the CIM for your study, this is the expertise available to you — not just facilities and equipment, but people with the training and credentials to support your study from feasibility through close-out.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Clinical Research Nurses">
    IV infusion, blood draws, specimen collection, vital signs, ECGs, and clinical monitoring. Oncology and Phase I nurses hold additional certification for chemotherapy and biotherapy.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Research Coordinators">
    Study documentation, participant coordination, regulatory compliance, consent, screening, enrolment, data capture, and site file management.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Project Managers">
    Study timelines, budgets, vendor coordination, and reporting. For Sponsor-Investigator studies, CIM project managers coordinate the monitoring programme.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Monitors (CRAs)">
    Site initiation, routine and remote monitoring, close-out visits, and IP accountability monitoring for Sponsor-Investigator studies conducted through the CIM.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Regulatory and Ethics Specialists">
    REB submission support — study startup, ICF development and amendments, annual renewals, and study closures.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Budget Experts">
    Cost estimates, industry budget negotiation, and coordination with Research Finance to incorporate CIM costs into the Clinical Trial Agreement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Laboratory Technologists and Technicians">
    Containment Level II Processing Laboratory at the Glen and Montreal General. Specimen processing, biobanking, and shipping.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Medical Imaging Technologists">
    DEXA, retinal imaging, fundus autofluorescence, and ophthalmic imaging within the CIM technical platforms.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Functional Testing Technologists">
    Cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET), pulmonary function testing (PFT), ECG, Holter, ambulatory blood pressure, body composition, and polysomnography.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Data Entry Clerks">
    Clinical data management, CRF completion, and data entry for studies running through the CIM.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Glen and Montreal General

The CIM operates at two MUHC sites. Most services are available at both, but some capabilities are site-specific. Your CIM feasibility discussion will confirm which site is appropriate for your protocol.

| Capability | Glen (Blocks B4 and C4) | Montreal General |
|------------|------------------------|-----------------|
| Phase I unit | Dedicated Phase I capacity — negative pressure overnight pods, close monitoring | Contact CIM to confirm |
| Oncology infusion | Adult oncology (Block C) — chemotherapy, biotherapy, immunotherapy | Available — confirm with CIM |
| Paediatric studies | Paediatric side (Block B) | Contact CIM to confirm |
| Functional testing platform | Full platform — CPET, PFT, ECG, Holter, body composition, imaging, sleep lab | Limited or not available |
| Specimen processing lab | Containment Level II | Containment Level II |
| General IV infusion | Yes | Available — confirm capacity |
| Research monitoring (SI studies) | Yes | Yes |
| Ethics and budget support | Yes | Yes |

<Note>
  Site capabilities are confirmed during the CIM feasibility assessment. This table is a general guide — contact the CIM to confirm availability before making site selection decisions.
</Note>

## Equipment management

### Sponsor-supplied equipment

When a study Sponsor provides equipment, it must be registered and certified before use on research participants.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete and affix the equipment identification form">
    Download and complete the [equipment identification form](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/MUHC2.download_my_file?p_file=190945744676920178) (RI-MUHC Portal — login required) and affix it to the equipment, boxes, and associated supplies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a Planon request">
    Submit a Planon request through the RI Portal to register the item as biomedical equipment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Obtain and submit certifications">
    Before the equipment is used on any research participant, obtain the following certifications from the manufacturer or distributor and email them to [biomed.cim@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:biomed.cim@muhc.mcgill.ca):

    - Canadian Standards Association (CSA) certification
    - Electrical certification with expiration date
    - Health Canada certification
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Health Canada Special Access if required">
    If the device has not yet been approved for use in Canada, consult the [Health Canada Special Access for Medical Devices](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/special-access/medical-devices.html) process before proceeding.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Purchasing equipment

Before placing a purchase order for any research equipment, consult the CIM biomedical team. Contact [biomed.cim@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:biomed.cim@muhc.mcgill.ca) to request a consultation.

### Calibration certificates and maintenance reports

Calibration certificates and preventive maintenance reports for CIM equipment are stored in the shared folder `H:\Public\CIM Biomed`. If you do not have access, submit a C2 request through the RI Portal.

### Equipment malfunctions

If equipment is not functioning properly, submit a Planon request through the RI Portal to initiate a repair.
