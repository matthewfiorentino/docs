## CIM Work Instructions

CIM Work Instructions are version-controlled operational documents governing how clinical, research, monitoring, and safety activities are conducted within CIM. They are organized into six series by area. All WIs and their appendices are accessed via the RI-MUHC Portal. Reading and acknowledgement is tracked through the **CIM Work Instructions Reader** on TalentLMS.

<Note>
**Access requires login.** The RI-MUHC Portal and TalentLMS both require authentication. Contact your manager if you do not yet have Portal or TalentLMS access.
</Note>

[Work Instructions on the Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:420) | [CIM Work Instructions Reader (TalentLMS)](https://english-rimuhc.talentlms.com)

### WI series index

| Series | Area | What it covers | Applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| WI-CIM-AD | Administrative | Emergency preparedness and safety: Code Blue preparation and management, Code Red and Code Green fire and evacuation procedures (Glen and MGH), incident and accident declaration. | All CIM staff |
| WI-CIM-CR | Clinical Research Management | Study conduct procedures: double identification of research subjects, SUSAR review by investigators, management of monitoring visits and sponsor audits, investigator site file and essential documents, source documents, communication methods with study participants. | CRCs, CRC-Ns, monitors |
| WI-CIM-GN | General Technical and Nursing | Clinical nursing procedures: UBEC verification and management, preparing immediate-use IV medications, accessing TIVAD (noncoring needle), preparing and administering IV infusions (non-chemotherapy), management of study briefings and nurse delegation. | CRC-Ns, infusion nurses, research nurses |
| WI-CIM-ON | Oncology and Phase I | Oncology-specific clinical procedures: administration of IV chemotherapy and biotherapy as study drugs, safe handling of hazardous drugs. Requires completion of a competency-based program before performing these tasks. | Oncology-credentialled nursing staff only |
| WI-CIM-AP | Adult and Pediatrics | Procedures specific to adult and pediatric patient populations. No documents currently listed in this series. | Clinical nursing staff |
| WI-CIM-MO | Monitoring | Site monitoring procedures for Sponsor-Investigator studies: site feasibility visit, site initiation visit, site monitoring visit, site close-out visit, monitoring of IP management, monitoring of safety assessments and AE/SAE reporting. Reading is tracked via the CIM Work Instructions Reader on TalentLMS. | CRAs, project managers, monitoring staff |

Each WI may have associated appendices (templates, checklists, logs, forms) accessible from the same Portal page. Your manager will confirm which WIs and appendices apply to your role.

## Technical Platform SOPs and WIs

The CIM technical platforms — the specimen processing laboratory, imaging facilities, and functional testing platforms — have their own set of SOPs and Work Instructions separate from the WI-CIM series above. Where these documents are hosted depends on the platform.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Containment Level II Processing Laboratory — TalentLMS">
    SOPs and WIs covering lab safety, equipment use and maintenance, and specimen handling procedures. Includes the SOP_CL series (lab attire, lab coats, biological safety cabinet, freezers and fridges, centrifuges) and WI_TEC_001–021 (hemocytometer, freezer and fridge alarms, centrifuges, automated pipettes, label maker, pneumatic transport system, vortex mixer, specimen mixer, biological safety cabinet, lab balances, decontamination, air incubator, dry ice, microscope, digital cell counter, water bath, sample log, and others).

    **Access:** Glen lab SOPs and WIs are covered by the **CIM Specimen Processing Laboratory — Glen SOP and WI Reader** on TalentLMS. MGH lab SOPs and WIs are covered by the **MGH SOP and WI Reader**. Lab access also requires completion of external certificates — see [CIM Training](/apps/cim/training) for the full lab access pathway.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Imaging — Shared drive">
    SOPs, consultation forms, and safety screening forms for imaging procedures including MRI.

    **Access:** Documents are available on the MUHC shared drive at `H:\Public\CIM Platform\1-Imaging`. Internal network access required.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Functional Testing — Portal">
    Work Instructions for functional testing equipment and procedures, including ECG, pulmonary function testing (PFT), cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET), body composition assessment, and other platform-specific instruments. The WI-TEC-100 and WI-TEC-200 series cover onboarding, platform booking via iLab, and individual equipment instructions (CPET, InBody BIA, Tanita BIA, Vmax Plethysmograph, Medisoft Body Box, ActiGraph, COSMED metabolic cart, iDXA, Biodex dynamometer, SphygmoCor XCEL, Jamar Hand Grip Dynamometer, and others). Most documents are available in English and French.

    **Access:** Available on the [Technical Platform SOPs page on the RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:389). Reading and acknowledgement is tracked through the **CIM Functional Testing Platform Work Instructions Reader** on TalentLMS.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
