## Starting a new study?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm your credentials">
    Your Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status and institutional training must be current and not within 90 days of expiry. [Check requirements →](/training/compliance-requirements)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assess feasibility">
    Confirm your protocol is scientifically sound, your team is in place, and the site can support your protocol. [Feasibility guide →](/kb/planning/feasibility)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your submission">
    Assemble your protocol, ICF, recruitment materials, and Health Canada authorization documents if applicable. [Submission checklist →](/kb/submission/preparing)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit via Nagano">
    Use the F11 form to simultaneously open your study file and trigger REB review and institutional feasibility review. [Nagano guide →](/apps/nagano/overview)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A study cannot begin until the **Personne mandatée** grants MUHC Authorization. REB approval alone does not authorize research activities — both approvals must be in your ISF before any study procedures begin.
</Note>
