## Research support and infrastructure

The Institute's research centres and platforms support your study from design through analysis — most are open to any RI-MUHC researcher, and the strongest time to engage them is during study design. Not sure who you need? The [Design Stage Support](/kb/study-design) guide maps the right specialist to each part of your study.

<div className="ri-support-cards">
<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM)" icon="hospital" href="/cim/services">
    Dedicated clinical research infrastructure and staff — specialized nursing, coordination, monitoring, Phase I capacity, and technical platforms at the Glen and Montreal General. Open to any RI-MUHC researcher.
  </Card>
  <Card title="ACT-CTU — Design Stage Support" icon="compass-drafting" href="/kb/study-design">
    Accelerating Clinical Trials at the Institute — protocol review, methodology support, the Novel Trial Methods Hub, and connections to seed funding for early-stage investigator-led studies.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Biostatistics (BCU)" icon="chart-line" href="/kb/bcu">
    A service of the Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE). Sample size, randomization, analysis plans, and DSMB support across the full study lifecycle.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Data Management (CORD)" icon="database" href="/kb/cord">
    A service of the Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE). REDCap database builds, CRF review, and data management. REDCap is validated for 21 CFR Part 11 compliance.
  </Card>
  <Card title="MUHC Data Warehouse" icon="server" href="/kb/data-warehouse">
    A service of the Division of Digital and Information Technology. Clinical and administrative data from 18+ MUHC hospital systems — for retrospective research and feasibility.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
</div>

## What the site covers

Beyond Getting Started, the Hub has five more sections, each accessible from the tabs at the top of any page.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Knowledge Base" icon="book-open" href="/kb/study-lifecycle">
    The operational core — every stage of a study from feasibility and design through submission, activation, recruitment, conduct, and close-out. Plus 101 articles, a glossary, and the governance framework.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Clinical Research SOPs" icon="file-shield" href="/sops/overview">
    The full text of every RI-MUHC Clinical Research Standard Operating Procedure — organized by topic, with overviews and full documents including appendices and templates.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Training and Learning" icon="graduation-cap" href="/training/compliance-requirements">
    Compliance requirements by study level, research privileges and external certifications (ICH GCP, Health Canada Division 5, ISO 14155), and the Learning Lab — interactive practice exercises.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Centre for Innovative Medicine" icon="flask" href="/cim/services">
    Clinical trial management, research staff, and specialized infrastructure at the Glen and Montreal General. Services for researchers and resources for CIM staff.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Applications" icon="grid-2" href="/apps/nagano/overview">
    Guides for the platforms used in clinical research at the RI-MUHC — Nagano, CTMS, TalentLMS, REDCap, OACIS, and CrossLab.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

The Knowledge Base is organized around the stages of a study — see [The Study Lifecycle](/kb/study-lifecycle) for the stage-by-stage overview, or [My Study Roadmap](/my-roadmap) for a plan tailored to yours.
