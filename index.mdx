## Starting a new study?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assess feasibility">
    Before any submission, confirm your protocol is scientifically sound, your team has the required qualifications, and the facilities and patient population are available. See [Study Feasibility](/kb/planning/feasibility).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete training">
    Everyone on the Task Delegation Log must hold current compliance credentials before submission. See [Training Requirements](/training/compliance-requirements).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare your submission documents">
    Assemble your protocol, ICF, recruitment materials, and any Health Canada authorization documents. See [Preparing Your Submission](/kb/submission/preparing).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit via Nagano">
    Use the F11 form in Nagano to open your study file and simultaneously trigger REB review and institutional feasibility review. See the [Nagano User Guide](/apps/nagano/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A study cannot begin until the **Personne mandatée** grants final MUHC Authorization. REB approval alone does not authorize research activities to start — both approvals must be in place.
</Note>
