You submit a study for review at the MUHC in <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip>, with the F11 form. Incomplete submissions are a common cause of delay, so have the documents, the team's credentials and your answers ready before you submit the F11. Once it is submitted, only the <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">Research Ethics Board (REB)</Tooltip> can re-open it. [Study Submission and Review](/kb/submission/submission-overview) describes the reviews that follow.

## Choose the project type

When you create the project in Nagano, its type sets which version of the F11 you complete. The type cannot be changed once the project has its Nagano number. If you need to recreate the project because you chose the wrong type, contact the office of the <Tooltip tip="The person the MUHC has mandated to authorize research: the Director of Innovation, Performance and Quality, who issues MUHC Authorization for each study." headline="Personne mandatée" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#personne-mandatee">Personne mandatée</Tooltip>, the person the MUHC has mandated to authorize research. If you're unsure which type applies, ask the REB or the Research Facilitator before you create the project.

| Project type in Nagano | Choose it for | F11 form |
|---|---|---|
| Clinical Trial, Clinical Research | Clinical trials, which evaluate the safety or effects of one or more interventions on health outcomes, and other clinical research. | F11-H |
| Banque / Registre (bank or registry) | Creating a bank or registry: biological samples or data collected, stored and distributed for future research. A project that only uses an existing bank or registry isn't this type: it's non-interventional research, which uses the F11-NIR.{/* The Research Facilitator, through MF, Nov. 13, 2026 (FACTS "The Research Facilitator, Nov. 13, answers 14 to 34"). */} | F11-BR |
| Recherche fondamentale (fundamental research) | Experimental or theoretical research for new knowledge, with no practical application planned. | F11-NIR |
| Recherche psychosociale, recherche qualitative, sciences infirmières (psychosocial or qualitative research, nursing sciences) | Research on how psychological and social factors shape behaviour and well-being, research that collects and analyzes non-numerical data, or research that guides nursing practice. | F11-NIR |
| Recherche rétrospective sur dossier (retrospective health records research) | Using data already in patients' health records, with no contact with participants and no new data collected. | F11-HRR |
| Recherche en épidémiologie, soins et services de santé, santé internationale (epidemiology, health services or international health research) | Research on the health of populations, on health services or on health worldwide. | F11-NIR |
| Recherche en imagerie (imaging research) | Research whose main question concerns imaging, or that relies on imaging. | F11-NIR |

Submit a protocol with every F11, even for retrospective health records research.

For a study at more than one public institution in Quebec, follow [Multi-Centric Studies in Quebec](/kb/design/multi-centric), whether the MUHC leads or takes part. When the MUHC REB is to review the study for every site, submit the F0 (Request to Act as REB of Record) form with the F11.

The [Nagano guide](/apps/nagano/overview) shows each step of creating a single-site clinical research project and completing its F11-H.

## Name the principal investigator

Who can be named principal investigator, the person who leads the study at the MUHC, depends on whether the study needs medical oversight:
- A study that needs medical oversight, such as an interventional clinical trial, is led by a physician or dentist of the MUHC's Council of Physicians, Dentists and Pharmacists (CPDP) with Human Research Privileges. Privileges are required to lead a study that Health Canada regulates with an investigational drug, medical device or natural health product.
- A study that doesn't need medical oversight may be led by a researcher with Researcher Status, such as a PhD researcher or a nurse, or by a physician or dentist with Privileges.

A principal investigator at the Montreal Neurological Institute-Hospital (the Neuro) whose study runs there must obtain McGill Research Privileges and institutional training under the Neuro's and McGill's procedures instead.

A clinical trial should ideally also list at least one co-investigator in Nagano, so that medical oversight is continuous. Being named principal investigator in Nagano has no bearing on who can be the lead author. Whoever wrote the protocol can be listed as a co-investigator or collaborator if they aren't the principal investigator.

When the REB contacts a research team, it generally emails the principal investigator named in Nagano. Keep that person's contact details in Nagano accurate.

Based on: [SOP-CR-500, sections 2.0](/sops/cr-500#scope) and [4.0](/sops/cr-500#definitions).

## Check the team's credentials and training

Name a Sub-Investigator as the principal investigator's backup, with the same Privileges or Researcher Status and institutional training as the principal investigator, or better. Fellows and residents cannot be the backup, because they are not eligible for Privileges or Researcher Status.

[Training and Compliance Requirements](/training/compliance-requirements) sets out who needs Privileges or Researcher Status and which training each level needs. Before you submit, renew any credential or training that is [close to expiry](/training/compliance-requirements#validity-and-the-90-day-rule).

Before you submit, the principal investigator should be trained on Nagano, with that training documented even if self-taught, and should make sure that any team member who submits has the institutional training level the study requires. If a coordinator or another delegate submits the study for the principal investigator, the Principal Investigator's Commitment and Signature form must be completed. Submitting to the REB is one of the tasks on the Task Delegation Log.

Do not perform a study task until your training for it is documented and the task has been delegated on the Task Delegation Log.

Based on: [SOP-CR-500, sections 1.0 to 4.0](/sops/cr-500#purpose); [SOP-CR-002](/sops/cr-002#building-a-research-team) (the principal investigator's backup); [SOP-CR-003](/sops/cr-003#study-level-specific-training-tiers) (training by level); [SOP-CR-007, section 5.2](/sops/cr-007#5-2-investigator-responsibilities-for-initial-submission) (Nagano training, a delegate's submission); [SOP-CR-002](/sops/cr-002#task-delegation), Appendix 1 (the Task Delegation Log's tasks).

## Prepare the documents

Upload these documents to Nagano with the F11, with any other document required locally:
- The current protocol and a protocol summary.
- The consent form, reviewed and approved by the sponsor if there is one. [Prepare the consent form](/kb/recruitment/consent#prepare-the-consent-form) gives the rules for its English and French versions and its translation.
- Every other document participants will see, including recruitment material such as letters, phone scripts, advertisements, posters, social media posts and wording for MUHC screens, in English and French, unless the REB accepts a justification.
- The recruitment plan and methods, if the study has them, and information on participants' compensation.
- The Investigator's Brochure or its equivalent (for a phase IV trial, the product monograph) and other safety information, as applicable.
- The principal investigator's curriculum vitae.
- Health Canada's letter, for a trial that needs one, and the agreement and budget, if there are any. [Health Canada authorization](/kb/submission/submission-overview#health-canada-authorization) says when the letter can come later.

Put a version number and date on the protocol, the consent form and the Investigator's Brochure. Put the version number in the file name of every document you upload too, because the file name is what appears on the REB's approval letter.

Check [what advertisements must include](/kb/recruitment/recruitment-enrollment#have-recruitment-materials-approved).

Based on: [SOP-CR-007, sections 5.4.8 and 5.4.12](/sops/cr-007#5-4-initial-reb-and-institutional-review); [SOP-CR-006, sections 5.1](/sops/cr-006#5-1-general-overview), [5.2](/sops/cr-006#5-2-icf-guidelines) and [5.3](/sops/cr-006#5-3-icf-content) (the sponsor's review, both languages); [SOP-CR-007, section 5.2](/sops/cr-007#5-2-investigator-responsibilities-for-initial-submission) (the sponsor's approval); the MUHC REB's [REB-SOP 404.001-2](https://muhc.ca/cae/muhc-research-ethics-board-reb-standard-operating-procedures-sops), section 5.4.1 (the REB's justification).

## Answer the F11's questions on services and data access

Your answers on pharmacy, nursing and laboratory services start those reviews; [Institutional feasibility review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#institutional-feasibility-review) describes what each involves. Take particular care with these answers:
- If the Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM) or your own research nurse covers the study's nursing, answer "No" to MUHC nursing services. If you answer "Yes", upload a nursing feasibility form signed by the nurse manager; it doesn't have to be ready when you first submit.
- If a clinical trial plans to recruit participants not covered by the Régie de l'assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ), which administers Quebec's health insurance plan, such as residents of other provinces, for in-person visits at the MUHC, declare it in the F11.

If you aren't sure whether the study will use a service, ask that service or the Personne mandatée's office before you submit.

If the study will use identifiable health information held by the MUHC without consent, pre-screening included, submit the <Tooltip tip="The privacy impact assessment that Quebec's Act respecting health and social services information requires before a study accesses identifiable health information without consent, including for pre-screening." headline="EFVP (privacy impact assessment)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#efvp">évaluation des facteurs relatifs à la vie privée (EFVP)</Tooltip>, the privacy impact assessment, with the F11, together with the communication agreement (entente de communication); the principal investigator signs both. Data collected with identifiers and anonymized afterward still count as identifiable.

A missing EFVP, or the wrong EFVP form, is among the most common causes of late-stage delays in feasibility review. If the F11's data access section has a wrong answer, the F11 is re-opened for you to correct it, and the correction triggers an EFVP review.{/* The Research Facilitator, through MF, Nov. 13, 2026 (FACTS "The Research Facilitator, Nov. 13, answers 14 to 34"). */} If you're unsure, write to efvp@muhc.mcgill.ca or ask the Research Facilitator before you submit, and check [when an EFVP is needed](/kb/governance/privacy).

Based on: [SOP-CR-007, sections 5.4.3 to 5.4.5 and 5.4.9](/sops/cr-007#5-4-initial-reb-and-institutional-review); the [Act respecting health and social services information](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/R-22.1), section 44.

## Get approvals, start the agreements and declare conflicts

The head of each department the study affects must approve it. Get the signed approvals before you submit, so that other reviews don't start unnecessarily. Some types of research don't need these approvals.

A department is affected if the study recruits its patients or might use its resources. For example, if participants might need the intensive care unit after receiving a study drug, the head of the intensive care unit must approve too, even if the study isn't mainly conducted there.

Your department head also signs the submission form's MUHC Authorizing Signatures with you; if the department head is the investigator or has a conflict of interest, an academic superior signs. Also obtain feasibility agreements from the managers of the clinical services the study will use; if you haven't discussed the study with them beforehand, you may be asked to do so during the review.

Submit the study's agreements, such as a clinical trial agreement, in My Contracts on the RI-MUHC Portal and in Nagano; the upload to My Contracts starts the Research Agreements Office's review, which runs while the REB reviews the study.

The REB often reads draft agreements, so upload drafts as soon as you have them; if a draft becomes available after you submit, tell your REB panel.

[Reviewing Clinical Trial Agreements](/kb/funding/clinical-trial-agreements) covers what to look for, and [Institutional feasibility review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#institutional-feasibility-review) says when the agreements must be fully executed and who must not sign them.

Name the study's source of funding in the F11. If private enterprise funds the study wholly or partly, give the private funders' contact information and the budgets at submission; [review and authorization fees](/kb/funding/budgets-contracts) may apply.

Declare any real, potential or perceived conflict of interest in the REB submission. Read [Conflict of Interest](/kb/governance/conflict-of-interest) for that declaration and for McGill's own disclosure.

Based on: [SOP-CR-007, sections 5.2](/sops/cr-007#5-2-investigator-responsibilities-for-initial-submission) and [5.4](/sops/cr-007#5-4-initial-reb-and-institutional-review) (signatures, agreements); the MUHC REB's [REB-SOP 105B.002](https://muhc.ca/cae/muhc-research-ethics-board-reb-standard-operating-procedures-sops), section 5.2 (conflicts of interest), and [REB-SOP 350.002](https://muhc.ca/cae/muhc-research-ethics-board-reb-standard-operating-procedures-sops), section 5.1 (funding at submission).

## After you submit

Answer the REB's questions by the deadline in [Tracking Your Submission and Responding to Requests](/kb/submission/tracking-responding), or the study may be closed.

After the REB approves your recruitment material, any of it that a patient may see also needs MUHC Communications' approval.

<Warning>
Do not start any research activity, including recruitment, data collection or analysis, until you have both REB approval and MUHC Authorization.
</Warning>

Recruitment also waits for every other [start condition](/kb/submission/submission-overview#before-recruitment-and-keeping-approval-valid).

If your study doesn't fit these steps, the Research Facilitator can help you prepare it.

Based on: [SOP-CR-007, section 5.4](/sops/cr-007#5-4-initial-reb-and-institutional-review) (sections 5.4.8, the REB's deadline; 5.4.10, Communications; 5.4.11, the start); [SOP-CR-009, section 5.3](/sops/cr-009#5-3-muhc-communications-approval); the MUHC REB's [REB-SOP 401.001](https://muhc.ca/cae/muhc-research-ethics-board-reb-standard-operating-procedures-sops), section 5.4.1.
