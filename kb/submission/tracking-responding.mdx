## Follow your study in Nagano

Each form in your project shows a status. The F11 moves through these statuses as your initial submission is reviewed:

| Status | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Created | The form hasn't been submitted, and review can't start until it is. | Complete the form and submit it. |
| Re-opened | The REB has re-opened the form with comments that need your team's attention. | Answer the comments and submit the form again. |
| Submitted | The form is submitted. | Nothing yet. |
| Ready | The form has been assigned for review. It changes to Approved once the study has MUHC Authorization. | Nothing yet. |
| Approved | The study has MUHC Authorization. | Start only once you have the MUHC Authorization letter and every other [start condition](/kb/submission/submission-overview#before-recruitment-and-keeping-approval-valid) is met. |

The Statuses tab shows which reviews are still pending. Each feasibility review shows Submitted, Under review, Approved or Not required. "Not required" means the study won't need that service. Ignore the scientific review workflow, labelled "SEC". The MUHC doesn't use the SEC workflow, and it shows "Not required" even though the study does receive a scientific review.

The Discussion tab in your project is your conversation with the REB, and feasibility reviewers don't see it. Look in the Discussion tab for the REB's instructions when it re-opens a form.

The pharmacy, the Research Agreements Office and other feasibility reviewers may send updates through the Messages tab on your Nagano homepage.

If you choose the activities under "Select the activities" in your Nagano menu, Nagano emails you a nightly summary of any activity in your projects. The activity also appears in the Activities section of your Nagano dashboard. For a participating-site (MEO) file, the [Personne mandatée's office](mailto:personne.mandatee@muhc.mcgill.ca) creates and re-opens forms without an email, so check Nagano.

On forms for an approved study, such as an amendment or a renewal, the REB records its decision in the form itself. The REB's decision can differ from the form's status. Read the decision in the form.

To find your projects, their statuses and their messages, follow the step-by-step screens in the [Nagano guide](/apps/nagano/guide-12).

## Respond to the REB decision

What you do next depends on [the REB's decision](/kb/submission/submission-overview#scientific-and-ethics-review-by-the-reb):
- If the REB approves the study, [check the approval letter](#check-and-file-the-approval-letter), file it, note the renewal date and watch Nagano for MUHC Authorization.
- If the REB approves the study with modifications, it sends a conditional approval letter and its conditions in an F20 form. A conditional approval isn't yet an approval. The REB issues its final approval letter only once every condition is met.
- If the REB defers its decision, revise the study as the REB requests and resubmit it. The REB then reviews the study again.
- If the REB disapproves the study, you can [ask it to reconsider](https://muhc.ca/cae/muhc-research-ethics-board-reb-standard-operating-procedures-sops), provided you can justify your grounds. To ask for a reconsideration, start by contacting [your REB panel's coordinator](https://muhc.ca/cae/page/contact-information).

<Warning>
Do not start any research activity, including recruitment, data collection or analysis, until you have both REB approval and MUHC Authorization.
</Warning>

## Answer the REB conditions

Answer the REB's conditions in the F20 form:
1. If someone else sponsors the study, [send the sponsor the changes the REB asks for](/sops/cr-007#5-4-8) before you submit them.
2. Give each revised document a new version number and date, in its footer and in its file name. Incorrect version numbering is a common Health Canada inspection finding.
3. Upload the revised documents.
4. Answer each condition. If you give a general acknowledgement instead of answering each condition, the REB can re-open the form.{/* TO CONFIRM (the Centre for Applied Ethics): does a general acknowledgement, instead of an answer to each condition, get an F20 re-opened? Community Connect #1's clarifications say the REB re-opens an F20 when information is missing; the Research Facilitator wasn't sure (FACTS answer 20; batch 73, U6). */}
5. The REB doesn't assign your answers to a reviewer until you submit the form. Submit the form as soon as every condition is answered.

On the F20, the status Pre-approved is for the REB's internal use and needs nothing from you. The REB may re-open the F20 if information or documents are missing.

Keep a copy of your submission and all your correspondence with the REB in the investigator site file. Make sure every printout you file is clear and free of mistakes. For example, an old file name on the submission form can leave unclear what the REB approved.

If an REB request isn't clear, ask the REB in the Discussion tab or at [your panel's inbox](https://muhc.ca/cae/page/contact-information).

{/* Sources: SOP-CR-007_08, section 5.4.8 (forward REB-requested revisions to the sponsor before re-submitting; revise the footer and the file name with the version number and date, "a common Health Canada inspection finding"; file a copy of the submission and all related correspondence in the ISF; printouts placed in the ISF "clear and free of mistakes (e.g., older version of file name on the submission form making unclear what was REB approved)"); SOP-CR-026_01, section 5.3.1 (the same for ongoing submissions); FACTS "Batch 73", V10 (the F20's statuses; re-opened when information or documents are missing; answers not assigned to a reviewer until the F20 is resubmitted). */}

## Answer the REB within three months

If the REB asks for changes or more information, you have [three months from its last correspondence with you](https://muhc.ca/cae/muhc-research-ethics-board-reb-standard-operating-procedures-sops) to answer. Nagano sends reminders as the deadline approaches, but the reminders don't restart the clock. Note the date of the REB's last message that isn't a reminder.{/* The Research Facilitator, through MF, Nov. 13, 2026 (FACTS "The Research Facilitator, Nov. 13, answers 14 to 34"). */}

<Warning>
If you do not answer in time, the REB closes the file.
</Warning>

If you're short of staff or might otherwise not answer in time, ask the REB for an extension at [your panel's inbox](https://muhc.ca/cae/page/contact-information) before the three months are up.

If you follow up after the file is closed, it can be duplicated under a new Nagano number and re-opened.

{/* Sources: the MUHC REB's REB-SOP 401.001-1, sections 5.1.2 ("The Researcher has 3 months to respond to the REB, after which date the file will be closed") and 5.4.1 (three months "since the last REB correspondence to the Researcher (except for reminders)"); SOP-CR-007_08, sections 5.2.5 and 5.4.8; FACTS "The Research Facilitator, Nov. 13, answers 14 to 34", answer 20 (Nagano's reminders; the file closed, then duplicated and re-opened; extensions, for example for lack of staff); FACTS "Contacts for submission and authorization" (about a submitted study, write to the reviewing panel's inbox, or to reb@ if unsure; Community Connect #1 clarifications, 15:19; FACTS only). */}

## Check and file the approval letter

Read the REB's approval letter before you file it, and [check that it includes](/sops/cr-007#5-4-12):
- The study's identification, protocol title and protocol number.
- The name, version number and date of every document the REB reviewed.
- The date of the REB's review, and whether it was a full-board or a delegated review.
- The REB's decision, including any modifications it requires.
- The procedure for appealing the decision, unless the REB's policy gives it separately.
- The date by which the approval must be renewed, or how long it lasts.
- The Research Ethics Board Attestation (REBA) statement.

If something is wrong, such as a consent form's version number, ask the [REB coordinator](https://muhc.ca/cae/page/contact-information) to revise the letter and send it again. File the correspondence about the correction in the investigator site file.

Then [file the letter in the investigator site file](/kb/submission/submission-overview#before-recruitment-and-keeping-approval-valid). Monitors may check that the letter is on file, and Health Canada inspectors may review it. A missing or uncorrected letter is a finding in an inspection report.{/* TO CONFIRM (QA): is a missing or uncorrected REB approval letter recorded as a finding in an inspection report? ## If REB approval lapses

If REB approval lapses, do not enrol anyone, and suspend study activities unless stopping them would put participants at risk. Take [the other steps a lapse requires](/kb/submission/submission-overview#before-recruitment-and-keeping-approval-valid).

{/* Sources: SOP-CR-007_08, section 5.5 (5.5.3 and 5.5.4: no enrolment during a lapse; suspend all study activities as long as there is no risk to participants); critical rule 5 (STYLE-HUB B3). */}
