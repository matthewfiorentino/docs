## Follow your study in Nagano

Each form in your project shows a status. The F11, your initial submission, moves through these statuses:

| Status | What it means | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Created | The form hasn't been submitted, and review can't start until it is. | Complete the form and submit it. |
| Re-opened | The REB has re-opened the form with comments that need your team's attention. | Answer the comments and submit the form again. |
| Submitted | The form is submitted. | Nothing yet. |
| Ready | The form has been assigned for review. It changes to Approved once the study has MUHC Authorization. | Nothing yet. |
| Approved | The study has MUHC Authorization. | Start only once you have the MUHC Authorization letter and every other [start condition](/kb/submission/submission-overview#before-recruitment-and-keeping-approval-valid) is met. |

The Statuses tab shows which reviews are still pending. Each feasibility review shows Submitted, Under review, Approved or Not required. "Not required" means the study won't need that service. Ignore the scientific review workflow, labelled "SEC": the MUHC doesn't use it, and it shows "Not required" even though the study does receive a scientific review.

The Discussion tab in your project is your conversation with the REB, and feasibility reviewers don't see it. Look there for the REB's instructions when it re-opens a form.

Feasibility reviewers, such as the pharmacy and the Research Agreements Office, may send updates through the Messages tab on your Nagano homepage.

If you choose the activities under "Select the activities" in your Nagano menu, Nagano emails you a nightly summary of any activity in your projects; the activity also appears in the Activities section of your Nagano dashboard. For a participating-site (MEO) file, the Personne mandatée's office creates and re-opens forms without an email, so check Nagano.

On forms for an approved study, such as an amendment or a renewal, the REB records its decision in the form itself, and the decision can differ from the form's status. Read the decision in the form.

Step-by-step screens for finding your projects, their statuses and their messages are in the [Nagano guide](/apps/nagano/guide-12).

## After the REB's decision

[Study Submission and Review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#scientific-and-ethics-review-by-the-reb) explains what each decision means. What you do next depends on the decision:
- If the REB approves the study, check the approval letter, file it, note the renewal date and watch Nagano for MUHC Authorization.
- If the REB approves it with modifications, it sends a conditional approval letter, which isn't yet an approval, and its conditions in an F20 form. The REB issues its final approval letter only once every condition is met.
- If the REB defers its decision, revise the study as the REB asks and resubmit it; the REB then reviews it again.
- If the REB disapproves the study, you can ask it to reconsider its decision when you can justify your grounds. Contact your REB panel's coordinator first.

<Warning>
Do not start any research activity, including recruitment, data collection or analysis, until you have both REB approval and MUHC Authorization.
</Warning>

Based on: the MUHC REB's [REB-SOP 401.001](https://muhc.ca/cae/muhc-research-ethics-board-reb-standard-operating-procedures-sops), sections 5.1 to 5.3; [SOP-CR-007, sections 5.1](/sops/cr-007#5-1-sponsor-responsibilities) (the conditional and final approval letters) and [5.4](/sops/cr-007#5-4-initial-reb-and-institutional-review).

## Answer the REB's conditions

Answer the REB's conditions in the F20 form. On the F20, the status Pre-approved is for the REB's internal use and needs nothing from you, and the REB may re-open the F20 if information or documents are missing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send the revisions to the sponsor first">
    If someone else sponsors the study, send the sponsor the changes the REB asks for before you submit them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Revise the documents">
    Give each revised document a new version number and date, in its footer and in its file name. Incorrect version numbering is a common Health Canada inspection finding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the documents and answer every condition">
    Upload the revised documents. A general acknowledgement, instead of an answer to each condition, can get the form re-opened again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the form">
    The REB doesn't assign your answers to a reviewer until you submit the form. Submit it as soon as every condition is answered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keep a copy of your submission and all your correspondence with the REB in the investigator site file. If an REB request isn't clear, ask the REB in the Discussion tab or at your panel's inbox.

Based on: [SOP-CR-007, section 5.4.8](/sops/cr-007#5-4-initial-reb-and-institutional-review).

## Answer the REB within three months

If the REB asks for changes or more information, you have three months from its last correspondence with you to answer. Nagano sends reminders as the deadline approaches, but reminders don't restart the clock, so note the date of the REB's last message that isn't a reminder.{/* The Research Facilitator, through MF, Nov. 13, 2026 (FACTS "The Research Facilitator, Nov. 13, answers 14 to 34"). */}

<Warning>
If you don't answer in time, the REB closes the file. If you follow up later, the file can be duplicated under a new Nagano number and re-opened.
</Warning>

If you might not answer in time, for example because you're short of staff, contact the REB before the three months are up to ask for an extension.

Based on: the MUHC REB's [REB-SOP 401.001](https://muhc.ca/cae/muhc-research-ethics-board-reb-standard-operating-procedures-sops), sections 5.1.2 and 5.4.1; [SOP-CR-007, sections 5.2](/sops/cr-007#5-2-investigator-responsibilities-for-initial-submission) and [5.4.8](/sops/cr-007#5-4-initial-reb-and-institutional-review).

## Check and file the approval letter

Read the REB's approval letter before you file it, and check that it includes:
- The study's identification, protocol title and protocol number.
- The name, version number and date of every document the REB reviewed.
- The date of the REB's review, and whether it was a full-board or a delegated review.
- The REB's decision, including any modifications it requires.
- The procedure for appealing the decision, unless the REB's policy gives it separately.
- The date by which the approval must be renewed, or how long it lasts.
- The Research Ethics Board Attestation (REBA) statement.

If something is wrong, such as a consent form's version number, ask the REB coordinator to revise the letter and send it again, and file that correspondence in the investigator site file.

Then file the letter as [Study Submission and Review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#before-recruitment-and-keeping-approval-valid) describes. Monitors may check that it's on file, and Health Canada inspectors may review it; a missing or uncorrected letter is an inspection finding.

Based on: [SOP-CR-007, sections 5.4.11 and 5.4.12](/sops/cr-007#5-4-initial-reb-and-institutional-review); [SOP-CR-013, section 5.10](/sops/cr-013#5-10-site-actions-to-facilitate-the-monitoring-visit) (monitoring visits); [SOP-CR-017, Appendix 1](/sops/cr-017#appendices) (what Health Canada inspectors may review).

## If REB approval lapses

If REB approval lapses, do not enrol anyone, and suspend study activities unless stopping them would put participants at risk. [Study Submission and Review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#before-recruitment-and-keeping-approval-valid) explains what else to do.

Based on: [SOP-CR-007, section 5.5](/sops/cr-007#5-5-annual-renewal-continuing-review).
