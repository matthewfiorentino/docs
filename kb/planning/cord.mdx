The Capture and Optimization of Research Data (CORD) unit provides expertise, technical support, and methodological guidance for all data-related operations across the study lifecycle. CORD is the primary resource for REDCap database builds and ongoing data management support at the RI-MUHC. It is a service platform under the Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE).

## Services

CORD works with researchers across the full data lifecycle, from planning data collection at the protocol stage through to database maintenance and close-out. The unit works in close collaboration with the Division of Digital and Information Technology and the Maelstrom research program.

- Review data plans in study protocols or grant applications — how data will be collected, cleaned, stored, shared, and estimated costs
- Review study data collection forms (CRFs, participant questionnaires, data dictionaries) and suggest improvements such as clearer skip patterns or better response options
- Build and test REDCap databases to ensure they function correctly and meet the study's needs
- Ongoing data management support including extraction, cleaning, integration, version tracking, and database updates
- REDCap assistance throughout the project lifecycle

<Note>
REDCap is the standard eCRF platform at the RI-MUHC and is validated for 21 CFR Part 11 compliance. For studies requiring electronic source data, IT feasibility must be confirmed before submission. Contact CORD early in the design process — database architecture decisions are costly to change after data collection begins.
</Note>

## How to request support

For general inquiries, contact CORD at cord@rimuhc.ca.

For a REDCap project or specific CORD support, use the online request form — accessible via the [RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/cim/redcap/surveys/?s=LPCYP8KP4JHP8C79) (login required). Submit requests for REDCap project setup, data management support, or consultation through that form.
