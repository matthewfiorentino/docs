Equity, Diversity and Inclusion (EDI) considerations are no longer optional for most funded research. Most major Canadian and Quebec funders — CIHR, FRQS, CFI, and others — now require EDI plans as part of grant applications, and eligibility for some programs is contingent on demonstrating EDI commitments in team composition and study design.

In 2023–24, 85% of CIHR training and career-support research integrated sex and gender considerations into study design.

Engage the EDI team at the **Design & Planning stage**, before your protocol is finalized and before your grant application is submitted.

<Tip>
  Contact the EDI team early — GBA+ consultation and EDI attestation for grant proposals are time-sensitive. Don't wait until submission to confirm your requirements.
</Tip>

## What the EDI team provides

The RI-MUHC EDI team offers a range of supports for research teams at The Institute:

**GBA+ / SGBA consultation:**
Gender-Based Analysis Plus (GBA+) / Analyse comparative entre les sexes plus (ACS+) is a Tri-Agency requirement for most CIHR and FRQS grant applications. The EDI team provides consultation to help researchers integrate sex, gender, and diversity considerations into study design, eligibility criteria, and outcome measurement.

**Inclusive recruitment and retention support:**
- Guidance on designing recruitment strategies that reach underrepresented populations
- Support for developing accessible consent materials
- Retention strategies for diverse participant cohorts

**Self-assessment tools:**
- Tri-Agency-aligned EDI self-assessment for research teams
- Disability inclusion toolkit
- Neuroinclusion planning resources

**Training and awareness:**
- Online training on unconscious bias and inclusive leadership
- Team-based EDI workshops and facilitated sessions
- Resources aligned with the MUHC's institutional EDI commitments

## Programs for equity-seeking groups

The RI-MUHC EDI initiative supports researchers and research team members from equity-seeking groups, including:

- Women in research leadership
- Racialized researchers and staff
- Indigenous people and researchers
- 2SLGBTQIA+ researchers and participants
- People with disabilities
- Ethnic and linguistic minorities

The EDI team can connect researchers with institutional support programs, mentorship, and resources aligned with their specific needs.

## Funder EDI requirements

| Funder | Key EDI requirement |
|---|---|
| CIHR | GBA+ / Sex and gender considerations in research design; EDI attestation for team composition |
| FRQS | EDI plan required for most competitions; program-specific requirements vary |
| CFI | EDI considerations in team composition and research environment |
| MUHC Foundation / Private foundations | Review REB billing and EDI requirements with the Research Facilitator |

<Note>
  EDI requirements vary by program and competition year. Confirm current requirements directly with the funder and the Pre-Awards Office before submitting.
</Note>
