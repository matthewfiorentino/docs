## Plan the study

- [Study Feasibility and Site Selection](/kb/planning/feasibility): check that the site, the team, the patients and the budget can carry the study, with the RI-MUHC's feasibility checklist, before you agree to take part.
- [How Sponsored Trials Come to the RI-MUHC](/kb/planning/sponsored-trials): learn how a company or a contract research organization chooses a site, and what its feasibility questionnaire and visits ask of you.
- [Sponsor-Investigator Studies](/kb/planning/sponsor-investigator): weigh the sponsor's duties you take on when you both initiate and conduct a clinical trial, before you apply for funding.
- [Investigator-Initiated Trial Startup Checklist](/kb/planning/iit-startup): set up a drug trial you sponsor yourself, from the Clinical Trial Application to the sponsor's reports to Health Canada.
- [AI in Clinical Research](/kb/planning/ai-in-clinical-research): check what you may and may not do with AI tools in a study.

## Pages for the rest of planning

- [ACT-CTU](/kb/design-support/act-ctu): get help with the methods and the protocol of a clinical trial you lead, and with sites in other provinces.
- [Biostatistics Consulting Unit (BCU)](/kb/design-support/bcu): get statistical help with the design, the grant application and the analysis.
- [Capture and Optimization of Research Data (CORD)](/kb/design-support/cord): have a REDCap database built and get help managing the data.
- [Working with MUHC Patient Data](/kb/design-support/patient-data): explore synthetic data in MDClone, then request real patient data from the Data Warehouse.
- [Multi-Centric Studies in Quebec](/kb/design/multi-centric): plan a study at more than one institution of Quebec's health and social services network.
- [Building Your Research Team](/kb/team/building-your-team): set up the team, its roles and who may do which tasks.
- [Preparing Funding Applications](/kb/funding/funding-applications), [Study Budgets](/kb/funding/budgets-contracts), [Research Agreements](/kb/funding/clinical-trial-agreements) and [Managing Study Funds](/kb/funding/research-grants): fund the study, build its budget, have its agreements drafted and signed, and manage its account.
- [Laws, Regulations and Guidelines](/kb/governance/laws-regulations): find which laws and guidelines apply, and whether the study needs an authorization from Health Canada.
- [Preparing Your Nagano Submission](/kb/submission/preparing): gather the credentials, documents and approvals the submission needs.
- [The Study Lifecycle](/kb/foundations/study-lifecycle): see where planning sits among the stages of a study.

If you aren't sure where to start, the [Research Facilitator](mailto:research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca) can help you plan your study.
