All study team members must have an appropriate employment or affiliation agreement with the RI-MUHC before performing any study tasks. Volunteers, students without valid academic insurance, and non-affiliated individuals cannot be delegated study tasks and cannot appear on the Task Delegation Log.

Engage People & Culture early — during the Design & Planning stage, before you post a position or onboard a new team member.

<Tip>
  For study-specific questions, contact the Research Facilitator at [research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca). For HR transactions (hiring, onboarding, offboarding), go directly to the HR Service Centre.
</Tip>

## Talent Acquisition

Talent Acquisition supports bilingual recruitment and workforce planning across the RI-MUHC. The team applies EDI-informed hiring strategies to help research programs attract diverse, qualified candidates.

You lead the selection process — Talent Acquisition guides best practices and ensures compliance with institutional hiring standards. Consult Talent Acquisition **before advertising any position**, including research coordinator, research assistant, or study nurse roles.

**What Talent Acquisition can help with:**
- Job posting strategy and bilingual job descriptions
- Competitive compensation benchmarking
- EDI-aligned screening and interview practices
- Workforce planning for multi-year studies

## HR Service Centre

The HR Service Centre is the single point of contact for all employee and trainee lifecycle transactions at the RI-MUHC. All requests flow through the **HR Hub** application.

**Transactions handled by the HR Service Centre:**
- Employee onboarding and offboarding
- Role changes and contract amendments
- Leave of absence processing
- Trainee registration and affiliation agreements
- Research volunteer and observer agreements

Contact: [ri.hr.centre@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:ri.hr.centre@muhc.mcgill.ca)

<Note>
  Students, research trainees, and volunteers require a formal affiliation or volunteer agreement before accessing RI-MUHC facilities or appearing on a study's Task Delegation Log. These agreements are processed through the HR Service Centre.
</Note>

## HR Advisors

Each research program has a dedicated HR Advisor — a partner for navigating complex HR situations throughout the study lifecycle.

**HR Advisors handle:**
- Performance management and coaching
- Workplace dispute resolution
- Disability case management and return-to-work planning
- Work permit and immigration requirements for international research staff
- Accommodation requests under Quebec's Charter of Human Rights and Freedoms

Your HR Advisor is assigned based on your research program. Contact the HR Service Centre to be connected with the right advisor for your team.

## Affiliation requirements by role

| Role | Agreement required | Processed by |
|---|---|---|
| Research staff (employee) | Employment contract | HR Service Centre |
| Graduate student | Academic affiliation agreement | HR Service Centre |
| Postdoctoral fellow | Postdoc appointment agreement | HR Service Centre |
| Research trainee (non-student) | Trainee agreement | HR Service Centre |
| Research volunteer | Volunteer agreement | HR Service Centre |
| Observer | Observer agreement | HR Service Centre |
| Consultant (non-employee) | Service contract | Finance / HR |

<Warning>
  No individual may perform study tasks or appear on the Task Delegation Log without a valid employment, affiliation, or volunteer agreement in place. This includes individuals with existing clinical roles at the MUHC — a separate research agreement is still required.
</Warning>
