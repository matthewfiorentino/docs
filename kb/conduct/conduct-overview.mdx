Once a study is running, the team follows the protocol day to day and keeps the study's records, team and approvals current until close-out. Keeping them current protects the participants and makes the results of the study reliable. Each page below holds the rules for one part of it.

## Keep the study data and records

- [Data Integrity](/kb/conduct/data-documentation/data-integrity): record, correct and keep study data so that the record can be trusted, following the <Tooltip tip="The principles governing good documentation practice: Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate, plus Complete, Consistent, Enduring, and Available." headline="ALCOA+" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#alcoa">ALCOA+</Tooltip> principles.
- [Storing and Sharing Human Data](/kb/conduct/data-documentation/data-security): learn how the study's data are classified, then store, send and archive each kind securely.
- [EDC Systems and Query Management](/kb/conduct/data-documentation/edc-query-management): enter data in the sponsor's electronic data capture (EDC) system, answer its queries and reconcile serious adverse events.
- [The Investigator Site File](/kb/site-activation/isf): keep the study's essential documents current until they are archived.

## Run the study day to day

- [Investigational Products in Quebec](/kb/conduct/study-operations/investigational-products): manage the investigational product with the MUHC Research Pharmacy, from its receipt to its return or destruction.
- [Management of Biological Specimens](/kb/conduct/study-operations/biological-specimens): collect, process, store and ship participants' biological specimens.
- [Conducting a Decentralized Trial](/kb/conduct/study-operations/decentralized-trials): run the parts of a trial that happen away from the site, such as remote monitoring, electronic consent and products sent to participants.
- [Team Management During a Study](/kb/conduct/study-operations/team-management): record each change to the team on the Task Delegation Log, including a leave of absence and a change of principal investigator.
- [Recruitment, Screening and Enrolment](/kb/recruitment/recruitment-enrollment) and [Informed Consent in Quebec](/kb/recruitment/consent): recruit, screen and enrol participants, and obtain their consent again when the study changes.

## Report events and keep the study compliant

- [Reportable Events and Protocol Deviations](/kb/conduct/safety-reporting/reportable-events): find which safety events, concerns and protocol deviations to report to the sponsor, the MUHC Research Ethics Board (REB) and Health Canada, and by when.
- [Deviation Management and CAPA](/kb/conduct/safety-reporting/deviation-management): classify a deviation, find its root cause and work through the corrective and preventive action (CAPA) process.
- [Study Amendments](/kb/conduct/safety-reporting/study-amendments): get a change to the study approved before you implement it.
- [Study Submission and Review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#before-recruitment-and-keeping-approval-valid): keep the REB's approval valid with its continuing review.
- [Monitoring, Audits, and Inspections](/kb/conduct/safety-reporting/monitoring-audits): prepare for the sponsor's monitoring visits, including monitors' access to [OACIS](/apps/oacis), the MUHC's electronic medical record system, and for audits and Health Canada's inspections.
- [Hosting a Health Canada Inspection](/kb/conduct/safety-reporting/hc-inspection-guide): prepare the team, the documents and the facility for an inspection, and respond to its findings.

When the study ends, [Study Close-Out](/kb/close-out/close-out) gives the steps, from the close-out visit to archiving.

If you aren't sure which rule applies to your study, the [Research Facilitator](mailto:research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca) can help.
