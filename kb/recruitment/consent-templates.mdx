The MUHC's Centre for Applied Ethics (CAE) posts the consent form templates that studies reviewed by the MUHC <Tooltip tip="A body, not affiliated with the sponsor, whose main mandate is to approve the start of biomedical research involving human participants and to review it periodically, to protect the participants' rights, safety and well-being." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">Research Ethics Board (REB)</Tooltip> build on, and some protocol templates. The consent form templates already carry wording that Quebec's rules or the REB require, and they mark what you may change.

Build your consent form on the template for your type of study, from the CAE's [templates page](https://muhc.ca/cae/templates-consent-forms). Then follow the rules for [preparing the consent form](/kb/recruitment/consent#prepare-the-consent-form), including its content, its two languages and its translation.

## Choose the consent form template

Choose the template that fits your study:

| Your study | Template |
|---|---|
| A clinical trial with adults | “Adults - Informed consent form templates for clinical trials” (version 4.2). |
| Non-interventional research | “Informed consent form templates for non-interventional research” (version 4.1). |
| A clinical trial or multi-centre study with minors | “Pediatrics - MSSS informed consent form templates for clinical trials and/or multi-centre studies”. |
| Other research with minors | “Pediatrics - informed consent form templates for other types of studies”. |
| A Children's Oncology Group study | “Template for Children's Oncology Group (COG)”. It is for COG-affiliated studies only, contains COG-specific provisions and must be used as it is, for COG protocol compliance. |
| A Phase I study | No template is posted. Because Phase I studies have many particularities, researchers should create a consent form tailored to the study, or carefully adapt a template to represent it accurately. You can ask the REB for access to its Phase I template. |
| Biobanking | No template is posted. The requirements depend on the biobank's scope and governance. Consult the REB. |
| Data covered by the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) | The adult clinical trial template and the pediatric template for clinical trials and multi-centre studies already end with an addendum for studies the GDPR applies to. Keep its wording unchanged if the GDPR applies, and delete it if not. With another template, add the “GDPR Appendix” without changing the structure of the main form. |

The adult clinical trial template (version 4.2) must be used for every adult clinical trial not yet approved, and the updated pediatric templates for every pediatric project not yet approved. The CAE set this rule on Nov. 15, 2024, when it updated the templates for Quebec's Act respecting health and social services information.

The pediatric templates have no separate assent form. On their signature page, a minor who can understand the nature of the study signs to assent, or the person who obtains the minor's verbal assent signs to record it. To explain a study to children of different ages, use [Pediatric Research Considerations](/kb/recruitment/pediatric-research#explain-the-study-in-words-the-child-understands).

If your study doesn't fit these rows, ask the [REB](mailto:reb@muhc.mcgill.ca) which template to use.

## Edit the template

The adult clinical trial template shows, in red, the wording that Quebec's health ministry, the Ministère de la Santé, de la Prévention et des Aînés, requires for clinical trials. In that template, the red text may not be changed, and the black text may be adapted to your study or deleted. When you have finished, convert all the text to black and delete all the comments. If the sponsor provides its own form, [insert the ministry's paragraphs word for word](/kb/recruitment/consent#prepare-the-consent-form).

The pediatric templates mark their guidance differently. Their pink passages and comments guide the research team and are not meant to appear in the form you submit to the REB.

Their orange passages, such as CHU Sainte-Justine's name and privacy policy, must be adapted for your institution and the REB that reviews the study. Comments mark the clauses that may not be changed, other than in the fields shown, such as those on compensation, privacy, voluntary participation and withdrawal, and whom to call with questions.

For more on the red wording, read the ministry's guide [*Standard legal clauses for information and consent forms for clinical trials*](https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/en/document-002923/) (last updated May 26, 2021). The CAE posts it on its templates page as "MSSS-FRQ Regulatory clauses".

The CATALIS Québec network posts, in English and French, [standard harmonized clauses for clinical trial consent forms](https://catalisquebec.com/en/resources-for-health-institutions/), drawn from a consensus of its members' REBs. It also posts harmonized clauses on topics such as the secondary use of study data and the use of third-party service providers, connected devices or applications. CATALIS says that using them makes consent forms easier to draft and speeds up their approval in Quebec's health and social services network. Before you use them in a form built on the CAE's template, ask the [REB](mailto:reb@muhc.mcgill.ca).

The adult clinical trial template already carries the MUHC Medical Records bar code FMU-9997, which [a study of an investigational product needs](/kb/recruitment/consent#prepare-the-consent-form). The pediatric templates, in their versions of Sept. 30, 2024, don't carry it. If your study of an investigational product uses a template without the bar code, ask the [REB](mailto:reb@muhc.mcgill.ca) how to add it. Other studies need not carry the bar code.

## Choose a protocol template

The CAE also posts protocol templates, each for one kind of project:

- The "Use Protocol Template", for a project that uses data or samples already stored in a research biobank.
- The "Protocol template for HRR Research", for health records research (HRR).
- The "Protocol template for AI", piloted since Aug. 11, 2025, for a project that develops artificial intelligence technologies, such as algorithms, from data collected earlier.

The CAE's biobanking and registry management framework template is a different document, and it isn't posted. To use data or samples already held in a research biobank, start from the Use Protocol Template. To create a biobank or registry, consult the [REB](mailto:reb@muhc.mcgill.ca) about its management framework template. Creating one is also [its own project type in Nagano](/kb/submission/preparing#choose-the-project-type), with its own F11 form.

For the full protocol of an investigator-initiated interventional study, ask the RI-MUHC's clinical trial unit, [Accelerating Clinical Trials–Clinical Trial Unit (ACT-CTU)](/kb/design-support/act-ctu).
