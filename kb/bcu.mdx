The BCU provides consultation on biostatistics for basic, clinical, and epidemiological research across the full study lifecycle — from grant application and study design through to analysis, manuscripts, and knowledge translation. The BCU is a service platform under the Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE).

## Services

The BCU supports researchers at any stage of a project. Engaging the unit early — at the study design or grant application stage — is strongly recommended. Statistical decisions made before data collection are far less costly to change than those made after.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Study design and grants">
    - Grant proposal preparation
    - Sample size calculations
    - Randomization plans
    - Experimental design
    - Statistical Analysis Plan (SAP)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Analysis and reporting">
    - Data analysis with advanced analytical methods
    - Epidemiologic and population-based studies
    - Clinical trials (Phase II, III, IV)
    - Translational studies combining clinical and biological data
    - Statistical interpretation and manuscripts
    - <Tooltip tip="Independent committee that reviews interim safety and efficacy data and recommends whether to continue, modify, or stop the study." headline="Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#dsmb">DSMB</Tooltip> and other reports

    - Big data and large-scale cohort analyses
    - Training, education, and knowledge transfer
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to request a consultation

BCU uses iLab to manage service requests. All clients — new and returning — submit requests through the platform. The BCU team reviews each request and either confirms the work or follows up with questions.

### First time — register for an iLab account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the BCU service centre">
    Navigate to [rimuhc.corefacilities.org — BCU service centre](https://rimuhc.corefacilities.org/service_center/show_external/4719).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register">
    Click **Register** in the upper right corner. Select your <Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip>/Lab and verify your contact information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await confirmation">
    Your registration is reviewed by the iLab team. Once confirmed, you can submit requests. For registration help, see the [iLab registration guide](https://help.ilab.agilent.com/en_US/99540-getting-started-with-ilab/325013-registering-with-ilab).
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Submit a service request

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to [rimuhc.corefacilities.org — BCU service centre](https://rimuhc.corefacilities.org/service_center/show_external/4719) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate a request">
    Select the **Request Services** tab and click **Initiate request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete and submit">
    Complete the form and submit. The BCU team will review and confirm, or follow up for more information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For questions or assistance with iLab: bcu@rimuhc.ca

## Fees

<Warning>
Early Career Researchers — free consultation hours. The first four hours of consultation per grant submission are provided at no cost at the time of service, for a maximum of two grant applications per PI per year. This is reserved for Early Career Researchers and researchers who currently hold no research funding. If the grant is successfully funded, those four hours are charged to the project.
</Warning>

| Client | Rate |
|--------|------|
| RI-MUHC and MUHC researchers | $80 / hour |
| McGill and other academic or research institutions | $90 / hour |
| Industry | $125–$145 / hour |

Rates effective April 1, 2025. Subject to change. Additional fee information is available on the [RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca).
