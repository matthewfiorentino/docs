<Update label="May 2026">
  ## ICH E6(R3) GCP now in effect — certificates before Oct 27, 2025 have been reset

  Health Canada formally adopted ICH E6(R3) on April 1, 2026. GCP certificates issued before October 27, 2025 are no longer valid and must be renewed before your next submission.

  Renewal is through CITI Program. See [External Certifications](/training/external-certifications) for step-by-step renewal instructions and course codes.
</Update>

<Update label="March 2026">
  ## CATALIS FAST TRACK now available

  The RI-MUHC now participates in the CATALIS FAST TRACK program for industry-sponsored trials, offering institutional authorization in 8 weeks or less for eligible studies.

  Contact the Research Facilitator to confirm eligibility and initiate the process.
</Update>
