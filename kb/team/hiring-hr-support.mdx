## Advertise a position

Consult People and Culture's [Talent Acquisition](mailto:research.talent@muhc.mcgill.ca) team before you advertise any position, including research coordinator, research assistant and study nurse positions. You lead the selection process. Talent Acquisition advises you on good practice and checks that the hiring meets the institution's hiring standards. Talent Acquisition recruits and plans the workforce for each research setting, publishes bilingual job ads on specialized job boards and applies hiring strategies informed by equity, diversity and inclusion (EDI).{/* TO CONFIRM (People and Culture): this paragraph rests on the Meet & Greet slide (session material) and the page as it stood; the slide does not name the job titles or "the institution's hiring standards" (the b163 fact check, HR6 to HR8). */}

Talent Acquisition can help with:

- The posting strategy and bilingual job descriptions.
- Pay comparisons for the position.{/* TO CONFIRM (People and Culture): "Competitive compensation benchmarking" and "ensures compliance with institutional hiring standards" are kept from the page as it stood; the Meet & Greet slide doesn't name them. */}
- Screening and interviews that follow EDI practices.
- Workforce planning, for example for a study that runs over several years.{/* TO CONFIRM (People and Culture): the posting strategy, screening and interviews, and workforce planning items are kept from the page as it stood; no source found (the b163 fact check, HR9). */}

Every position must be advertised for at least 5 business days before an offer can be made. Advertising starts only once Talent Acquisition has reviewed and published the job posting, which takes another 3 to 4 business days after you submit it. ## Hire and onboard through the HR Service Centre

People and Culture's [HR Service Centre](mailto:ri.hr.centre@muhc.mcgill.ca) handles changes to employee and trainee records, from onboarding through role changes and leaves to offboarding. The HR Service Centre works through the HR Hub application, formerly eManpower, on the RI-MUHC Portal.{/* TO CONFIRM (People and Culture): the HR Service Centre's scope rests on the Meet & Greet slide (session material); "on the RI-MUHC Portal" is in neither lead (the b163 fact check, HR13, HR14). */}

To hire someone, wait until the candidate has accepted your offer. Then prepare a hiring form in the HR Hub application, have it signed by both parties, and upload it there with the employment contract.{/* TO CONFIRM (People and Culture): the steps. ## Get help from your HR Advisor

Each research program has its own HR partners, including an HR Advisor for situations that a routine transaction doesn't cover. Your HR Advisor helps with:

- Performance management and coaching.
- Disputes in the workplace.
- Disability cases and the return to work.
- Work permits and immigration for staff from other countries.
- Requests for accommodation under Quebec's Charter of Human Rights and Freedoms.{/* TO CONFIRM (People and Culture): "coaching", "return-to-work planning" and the accommodation requests under the Charter are kept from the page as it stood; the Meet & Greet slide names "Performance management, dispute resolution, disability case management, work permits". ## Hire summer workers and workers from other countries

Summer workers are a separate employment category for temporary hires, usually from May 1 to August 31. A dedicated standard operating procedure (SOP) and salary scale apply to them. The contract templates and details are in the [Recruiting and Hiring section of the RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:334).{/* TO CONFIRM (People and Culture; already asked, 14-open-questions.md: B129, the Portal page): is f?p=106:334 the Recruiting and Hiring section, and are the summer-worker period, SOP and salary scale right? A worker from another country must hold a valid work permit before starting any work at the RI-MUHC. The rule applies to employees and to volunteers.{/* TO CONFIRM (People and Culture; already asked, 14-open-questions.md: B130): must foreign volunteers hold a work permit? roles/volunteer says "valid work or study permit". */} Work permit renewals with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) can take up to 9 months.
