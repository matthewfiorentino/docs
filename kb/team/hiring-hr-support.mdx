Hiring for a study takes time, because a position is advertised for a set period before an offer can be made. People and Culture, the RI-MUHC's human resources (HR) division, helps at each step. Each research program has its own HR partners.

All study team members must have an appropriate employment, affiliation or volunteer agreement with the RI-MUHC before performing any study task or appearing on the Task Delegation Log.

Volunteers and other people who are not affiliated with the RI-MUHC or the MUHC may not contact or recruit research participants, or perform procedures on them, at the RI-MUHC or the MUHC, because they are not covered by professional or academic liability insurance. [Who cannot work with participants](/training/compliance-requirements#who-cannot-work-with-participants) lists everyone the rule covers.

## Advertise a position

Consult People and Culture's [Talent Acquisition](mailto:research.talent@muhc.mcgill.ca) team before you advertise any position, including research coordinator, research assistant and study nurse positions. You lead the selection process. Talent Acquisition advises you on good practice and checks that the hiring meets the institution's hiring standards. Talent Acquisition recruits and plans the workforce for each research setting, publishes bilingual job ads on specialized job boards and applies hiring strategies informed by equity, diversity and inclusion (EDI).

Talent Acquisition can help with:

- The posting strategy and bilingual job descriptions.
- Pay comparisons for the position.
- Screening and interviews that follow EDI practices.
- Workforce planning, for example for a study that runs over several years.

Every position must be advertised for at least 5 business days before an offer can be made. Advertising starts only once Talent Acquisition has reviewed and published the job posting, which takes another 3 to 4 business days after you submit it. All postings must be bilingual.

## Hire and onboard through the HR Service Centre

People and Culture's [HR Service Centre](mailto:ri.hr.centre@muhc.mcgill.ca) handles changes to employee and trainee records, from onboarding through role changes and leaves to offboarding. The HR Service Centre works through the HR Hub application, formerly eManpower, on the RI-MUHC Portal.

To hire someone, wait until the candidate has accepted your offer. Then prepare a hiring form in the HR Hub application, have it signed by both parties, and upload it there with the employment contract.

The HR Service Centre handles:

- Onboarding new employees, with the hiring form and the employment contract.
- Role changes and contract amendments.
- Leaves of absence.
- Offboarding and the end of contracts.
- Trainee registration and affiliation agreements.
- Agreements for research volunteers and observers.

Students, research trainees and volunteers need a formal affiliation or volunteer agreement before they use RI-MUHC facilities or appear on a study's Task Delegation Log. The HR Service Centre processes these agreements.

## Get help from your HR Advisor

Each research program has its own HR partners, including an HR Advisor for situations that a routine transaction doesn't cover. Your HR Advisor helps with:

- Performance management and coaching.
- Disputes in the workplace.
- Disability cases and the return to work.
- Work permits and immigration for staff from other countries.
- Requests for accommodation under Quebec's Charter of Human Rights and Freedoms.

Your HR Advisor is assigned by research program. The [HR Service Centre](mailto:ri.hr.centre@muhc.mcgill.ca) can connect you with the advisor for your team.

## Hire summer workers and workers from other countries

Summer workers are a separate employment category for temporary hires, usually from May 1 to August 31. A dedicated standard operating procedure (SOP) and salary scale apply to them. The contract templates and details are in the [Recruiting and Hiring section of the RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:334).

A worker from another country must hold a valid work permit before starting any work at the RI-MUHC. The rule applies to employees and to volunteers. Work permit renewals with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) can take up to 9 months. Start a renewal at least 6 months before the permit expires. Write to your HR Advisor early if you are hiring from another country, or if a team member's permit is close to expiry.

## Agreements by role

Each kind of team member needs its own agreement before taking part in a study:

| Role | Agreement | Processed by |
|---|---|---|
| Research staff (employee) | Employment contract | The HR Service Centre |
| Graduate student | Academic affiliation agreement | The HR Service Centre |
| Postdoctoral fellow | Postdoctoral appointment agreement | The HR Service Centre |
| Research trainee who is not a student | Trainee agreement | The HR Service Centre |
| Research volunteer | Volunteer agreement | The HR Service Centre |
| Observer | Observer agreement | The HR Service Centre |
| Consultant who is not an employee | Service contract | Finance or People and Culture |

People who already hold a clinical role at the MUHC still need a separate research agreement before they do a study task or appear on the Task Delegation Log.
