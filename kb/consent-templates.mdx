Do not build a consent form from scratch. The MUHC Centre for Applied Ethics (CAE) publishes validated ICF and assent templates that already contain the mandatory MSSS wording required by Quebec law — shown in red text in the templates. An ICF submitted to the REB without this wording will be rejected.

All templates are available for download at the CAE page:

<Card title="CAE Consent Form Templates" icon="file-word" href="https://muhc.ca/cae/page/templates-consent-forms">
  Download ICF templates, assent forms, protocol templates, and the MSSS regulatory clauses addendum. Templates are provided as editable Word documents.
</Card>

<Note>
  The templates below are listed with guidance on when to use each one. For questions about which template applies to your specific study — particularly for complex designs, registry studies, or studies involving vulnerable populations — contact the REB directly at [reb@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:reb@muhc.mcgill.ca).
</Note>

## Consent form templates

Use the table below to identify which template applies to your study. Download the correct template from the CAE page, then adapt it — inserting sponsor-specific content, study procedures, risk language, and contact details — without removing or altering the red MSSS text.

### Adult participants

| Study type | Template to use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Interventional drug, device, or NHP study | **Adult clinical trial** template (EN + FR) | Includes FMU-9997 barcode. Print on MUHC hospital letterhead for IP studies. |
| Observational, survey, or chart review | **Adult non-interventional study** template (EN + FR) | FMU-9997 barcode not required. No MUHC letterhead requirement. |
| Study collecting data subject to GDPR (EU participants or EU Sponsor) | Add the **GDPR appendix** (EN + FR) to your primary template | Attach as a separate appendix; do not modify the primary ICF structure. |
| Phase I study (healthy volunteers) | **Email the CAE directly** — template provided on request | Phase I has a separate template due to compensation and healthy volunteer provisions. |
| Study involving a biobank or biological specimens biobank | **Consult the REB** before drafting | Biobanking consent requirements depend on the scope and governance of the biobank. |

### Pediatric participants (under 18)

Pediatric consent at the RI-MUHC requires **two documents**: a parental consent form (signed by the parent or tutor) and an age-appropriate assent form (presented to the child). Submit both to the REB.

| Study population | Template to use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Minors — studies subject to MSSS Norm 9 requirements | **Pediatric MSSS template** — parental consent + child assent (EN + FR) | Use this as the default for most pediatric studies at MUHC. Includes CCQ Art. 21 provisions. |
| Minors — studies not subject to MSSS Norm 9 (e.g., PI-initiated with non-MSSS funding) | **Pediatric general template** — parental consent + child assent (EN + FR) | Confirm with the REB which template applies if uncertain. |
| Pediatric oncology studies through Children's Oncology Group (COG) | **COG template** (EN + FR) | For COG-affiliated studies only. Contains COG-specific provisions and must be used as-is for COG protocol compliance. |

<Note>
  The age-appropriate assent form included in the pediatric templates is written for children approximately 9 and older. For children 7–8, a documented verbal assent process is typically acceptable — describe this in your protocol and REB submission. For children under 7, parental consent only is generally sufficient; document the child's engagement in the source documents. See [Pediatric Research Considerations](/kb/pediatric-research) for detailed guidance on assent by age band.
</Note>

## MSSS regulatory clauses addendum

The CAE publishes a standalone **MSSS/FRQ regulatory clauses addendum** (PDF, EN + FR). This document contains the current version of the mandatory MSSS paragraphs that must appear in every Quebec REB ICF submission.

You do not need to use this addendum separately if you are starting from a CAE template — the mandatory clauses are already embedded. The addendum is useful when:

- A Sponsor provides their own master ICF and you need to identify exactly which paragraphs to insert
- You are comparing your adapted ICF against the current mandatory text
- The REB requests confirmation of clause version

The five mandatory areas covered are:
1. Voluntary participation and right to withdraw without consequence
2. Confidentiality and data protection
3. What happens if you are harmed during the study
4. Contact information for questions
5. Signatures

## Protocol templates

The CAE also provides protocol templates for common study types. These are distinct from ICF templates — they provide the structured protocol format accepted by the MUHC REB.

| Protocol template | Use when |
|---|---|
| **Biobank Use Protocol** (EN + FR) | Requesting use of existing banked samples or data from an RI-MUHC biobank |
| **Human Research Repository (HRR) Protocol** (EN + FR) | Establishing or operating a new HRR at the RI-MUHC |
| **AI and Health Data Protocol** (EN + FR) | Studies involving AI model development, training, or validation using MUHC health data |
| **Biobanking and Registry framework** | Consult the REB — requirements depend on scope |

<Tip>
  For investigator-initiated interventional studies requiring a full study protocol, consult the [ACT-CTU](/kb/study-design) for protocol template support — the CAE protocol templates above are scoped to biobank, HRR, and AI/health data studies.
</Tip>

## What the red text means

Every CAE template contains text shown in red. This is the **mandatory MSSS wording** — provincial regulatory language required in every ICF submitted to a Quebec REB. Do not delete, paraphrase, or move this text. Do not change it to match a sponsor's preferred phrasing.

If a Sponsor provides their own ICF template, you must locate each of the five mandatory areas in the Sponsor's template and replace the Sponsor's language with the MSSS text. Use the MSSS regulatory clauses addendum as your reference.

The Verification of ICF Document checklist ([SOP-CR-006](/sops/cr-006), Appendix 1) is the pre-submission quality check that confirms all required content — including the MSSS clauses — is present before the ICF is submitted via Nagano.

## FMU-9997 barcode

For studies involving an investigational product (drug, device, or natural health product), the ICF must include the MUHC Medical Records barcode **FMU-9997**. This barcode is already embedded in the adult clinical trial and pediatric MSSS templates. It ensures the signed ICF is filed in the Alert section of the participant's OACIS electronic record, making it visible to treating clinicians in an emergency.

The FMU-9997 barcode is **not required** for observational, survey, or non-interventional studies. Do not add it to the non-interventional template.
