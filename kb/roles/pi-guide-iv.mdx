Level IV studies — prospective observational, educational, and non-invasive interventions — follow a simpler regulatory pathway than interventional studies, but your personal accountability as PI for consent, team oversight, and data integrity is unchanged.

<Info>
  This guide covers day-to-day operations for Level IV studies. For your personal accountability map — prerequisites, what only you sign, and training levels — see the [PI / Qualified Investigator Pathway](/kb/roles/pi-pathway).
</Info>

<Tip>
  **Documents requiring your personal signature** — QI/PI signature obligations (Nagano commitment, delegation log countersignature, amendment sign-off, annual renewal) apply to Level IV studies. See [What only you sign](/kb/roles/pi-pathway#what-only-you-sign) on the PI Pathway.
</Tip>

## Understanding the role

As <Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip> at Level IV, you are subject to REB oversight and SOP compliance, but without Division 5, a QIU, Health Canada submission, or sponsor monitoring. You still hold personal accountability for consent, team oversight, and data integrity. The same [four-layer regulatory framework](/kb/governance/regulatory-framework) applies — including Quebec-specific requirements (Civil Code consent rules, ÉFVP, MSSS framework) and [institutional requirements](/kb/governance/institutional-structure) (MUHC Authorization, RI-MUHC SOPs) that are not covered by standard GCP training.

### Training requirements

| Level | Study type | SOP series | <Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#gcp">GCP</Tooltip> | Other certs | Renewal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IV | Prospective observational, educational, non-invasive interventions | SOP-LR (18 SOPs) | — | — | 5 years |

No external certifications required. For the full matrix, see [Training and Compliance Requirements](/training/compliance-requirements).

<Info>
**If you also lead interventional studies:** your training level is set by your highest-risk active study. A Level IV observational alongside a Level I drug trial means you hold Level I certification. See the [PI Guide — Interventional](/kb/roles/pi-guide).
</Info>

## Prerequisites

- **Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status** — Researcher Status is valid for Level IV since no medical oversight is required. CPDP members may hold either. Apply via TalentLMS. Valid for 3 years. The 90-day rule applies at submission.
- **SOP Reader and Competency Assessment** — Level IV path (SOP-LR, 18 SOPs, 5-year renewal). Complete both in TalentLMS. Certificate must not be within 90 days of expiry at submission.

## Study submission

<Note>
See also: [Site Activation and Study Setup](/kb/conduct/site-setup) | [Preparing Your Submission](/kb/submission/preparing) | [Submitting via Nagano](/kb/submission/submitting-via-nagano)
</Note>

- Submit via <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip> (F11-H or the appropriate form type). No Health Canada submission is required for Level IV studies.
- Answer the F11 accurately. Service departments (nursing, laboratory, DPS) conduct feasibility reviews based on your answers.
- **Contracts and budget:** Contracts and agreements, where applicable, must be executed via My Contracts before <Tooltip tip="Institutional authorization allowing a study to proceed at the RI-MUHC. Issued after ethics approval and feasibility review." headline="MUHC Authorization" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#muhc-auth">MUHC Authorization</Tooltip>. Most Level IV studies are grant-funded — use the [RI-MUHC Budget Tool](/kb/planning/budgets-contracts) to account for staff time, data management (CORD/REDCap), biostatistics (BCU), and participant reimbursement. Overhead is 0% for peer-reviewed grants, 15% for foundation-funded studies. Foundation-funded studies are also subject to REB billing fees. If data will be shared outside the MUHC, a Data Transfer Agreement is required. See [Contracts, Budgets, and Agreements](/kb/planning/budgets-contracts).
- MUHC Authorization from the <Tooltip tip="Individual formally authorized to issue the final written authorization for each research project at a Quebec RSSS institution." headline="Personne mandatée (PFM)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#personne-mandatee">Personne mandatée</Tooltip> is required before any participant contact. REB approval alone is not sufficient.

## Your team

- Set up the <Tooltip tip="Lists each team member, their qualifications, and specific trial tasks authorized by the QI." headline="Task Delegation Log (TDL)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#tdl">Task Delegation Log</Tooltip> before any study tasks begin. You countersign each entry. See [Building Your Research Team](/kb/planning/building-your-team).
- Appoint a Sub-I backup who holds Privileges or Researcher Status. For Level IV, Researcher Status is sufficient.
- Your team trains at Level IV (SOP-LR, 18 SOPs). No external certifications required.
- File CVs and training certificates in the study file. Monitor expiry dates.

For hiring research staff, see [Hiring and HR Support](/kb/planning/hiring-hr-support). All team members need an RI-MUHC affiliation before performing any study tasks.

## Informed consent

<Note>
See also: [Informed Consent](/kb/conduct/consent) | [SOP-CR-008](/sops/cr-008)
</Note>

The same Quebec consent obligations apply to observational studies as to interventional ones. You are accountable for ensuring consent is obtained and documented before any study procedures.

- Consent forms must be available in French (Bill 96). Use only the current REB-approved version.
- Some observational studies may qualify for a waiver or alteration of consent — for example, observation of routine care. This must be approved by the REB; you cannot make this determination independently.
- **Incapacitated adults (Art. 21 CCQ):** the same hard legal requirements apply as for interventional studies — substitute consent from the person with legal authority AND authorization from an MSSS-designated REB.
- Re-consent when amendments change what participants experience.

Some Level IV studies may qualify for alteration of consent — for example, observational studies where full disclosure of the research question would bias participant behavior. Alteration of consent is not automatic; it requires REB approval and must satisfy TCPS2 criteria (minimal risk, no adverse effect on welfare, research could not practicably be carried out without the alteration, and participants will be debriefed afterward if appropriate). If you believe your study qualifies, discuss with the REB before submission.

## Ongoing obligations

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Data oversight">
    Review collected data periodically — at minimum quarterly for multi-year studies. Check for completeness, consistency, and outliers that could indicate data entry errors or protocol non-compliance. Document each review (date, scope, findings, actions taken). Without a Sponsor's CRA checking your work, this self-monitoring is your primary quality assurance mechanism.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Safety">
    If a participant reports a health concern during a study interaction, assess whether it constitutes an unanticipated problem — an event that is unexpected, related or possibly related to the research, and suggests increased risk to participants. Not every health concern is an unanticipated problem; the assessment requires your clinical judgment. Document your assessment even if the conclusion is that no reporting is required. Report unanticipated problems to the REB regardless of causality.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Deviations">
    Document all deviations. Report major deviations to QA and the REB. See [Reportable Events](/kb/conduct/reportable-events).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Annual renewal">
    Submit via Nagano before REB approval lapses. Confirm your credentials and your team's are current. See [Tracking and Responding to Submissions](/kb/submission/tracking-responding).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Conflict of interest">
    COI disclosure operates through two independent tracks — McGill institutional (annual disclosure to your supervisor) and REB study-level (at Nagano submission and whenever a new conflict arises). Both are required. If a COI arises mid-study, disclose promptly via Nagano amendment — do not wait for renewal. See [Conflict of Interest](/kb/governance/conflict-of-interest).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Research integrity">
    You are responsible for honest, rigorous, and transparent conduct by your entire team. Research misconduct (fabrication, falsification, plagiarism) must be reported through McGill's structures. See [Research Integrity](/kb/governance/research-integrity).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Privacy and data

<Note>
See also: [Privacy and Confidentiality in Research](/kb/conduct/privacy) | [Research Data Security](/kb/conduct/data-security)
</Note>

Under <Tooltip tip="Quebec's Act to modernize personal information protection law (in force Sept 2023). Requires EFVP before sharing personal data without consent." headline="Law 25 / Loi 25" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#law-25">Loi 25</Tooltip>, the <Tooltip tip="Required under Quebec Law 25 before communicating personal information without consent for research. Non-compliance: up to $25M or 4% of worldwide turnover." headline="ÉFVP — Privacy Impact Assessment" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/glossary#efvp">ÉFVP</Tooltip> may be required if your study involves identifiable personal health information or uses software platforms for data collection. You are responsible for ensuring your team handles data in compliance with Quebec law — de-identification, access controls, and data transfer agreements for any cross-border sharing.

## Close-out

- Confirm all data collection is complete and queries resolved. See [Study Close-Out](/kb/conduct/close-out).
- Submit the Study Completion Report to the REB via Nagano.
- Record end dates on the delegation log for all team members.
- Archive the study file. The 15-year Division 5 retention requirement does not apply to Level IV. Confirm retention requirements with QA based on the funding source and institutional policy.
