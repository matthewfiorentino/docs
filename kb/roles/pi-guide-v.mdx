Accountability reference for PIs leading retrospective chart review studies and studies using previously anonymized biobanked samples (Level V). In most cases there is no participant contact. Your primary concerns are the ÉFVP consent waiver decision, data access authorization, and REB maintenance.

## Understanding the role

Level V studies use existing data or previously collected specimens. In most cases there is no participant contact, no consent process, no investigational product, no monitoring, and no SAE reporting. Your primary accountability areas are: ensuring the ÉFVP and consent waiver are properly obtained, authorizing and controlling data access, and maintaining REB compliance.

## The consent waiver decision

<Note>
See also: [Privacy and Confidentiality in Research](/kb/conduct/privacy) | [Informed Consent](/kb/conduct/consent)
</Note>

Most retrospective studies operate under a consent waiver, but under Loi 25, whether a waiver is granted depends on the ÉFVP (privacy impact assessment).

<Warning>
**The legal default is always to obtain consent.** Access to identifiable data without consent requires approval from the ÉFVP committee. A waiver is granted when the committee determines that obtaining consent is not feasible — for example, a chart review of 500 patients from 2000 to 2010, where many patients may have died, been lost to follow-up, or have outdated contact information.

In contrast, if you are reviewing records for a small number of recent patients — say, five patients from the last year — the committee may determine it is reasonable to contact those patients and obtain consent, even though the research is retrospective.
</Warning>

If consent is required, you and your team follow the same consent process described in the [Level IV PI Guide](/kb/roles/pi-guide-iv) and the corresponding consent SOPs, even though the study remains Level V in every other respect. Confirm the consent requirement before your team begins any data extraction.

## Prerequisites

- **Human Research Privileges or Researcher Status** — Researcher Status is valid for Level V. Apply via TalentLMS. Valid for 3 years. The 90-day rule applies at submission.
- **SOP Reader and Competency Assessment** — Level V path (SOP-LR, 9 SOPs, 5-year renewal). Complete both in TalentLMS.

## Study submission

<Note>
See also: [Preparing Your Submission](/kb/submission/preparing) | [Submitting via Nagano](/kb/submission/submitting-via-nagano)
</Note>

- Submit via Nagano. No Health Canada submission is required.
- The F17/F18 forms in Nagano trigger the ÉFVP if identifiable data is involved. Complete these accurately.
- The REB approval should explicitly state whether a consent waiver has been granted and specify the authorized data sources and patient population.
- MUHC Authorization from the Personne mandatée is required before your team accesses any patient records for research purposes.

## Data access and privacy

<Note>
See also: [Privacy and Confidentiality in Research](/kb/conduct/privacy) | [Research Data Security](/kb/conduct/data-security)
</Note>

Privacy is the highest-risk area for retrospective studies. You are responsible for ensuring your team accesses only the data authorized by the REB and handles it in compliance with Quebec law.

- **OACIS access** must be authorized specifically for the study. For data beyond OACIS (lab results at scale, pharmacy dispensing, administrative data), the MUHC Data Warehouse may be required.
- **De-identification is not optional.** The research database must contain participant IDs only. The identification key must be stored separately with restricted access.
- **Data transfer agreements** are required if data is shared outside the MUHC. Cross-border transfers (data leaving Quebec or Canada) have additional requirements under Loi 25.
- At study completion, determine the **disposition of identifiable data** — destroy the identification key, retain it for a specified period, or transfer responsibility. Document this decision.

<Info>
CORD can build and test REDCap databases for your study. Contact them early — database architecture decisions are costly to change after extraction begins. See [CORD](/kb/planning/cord).
</Info>

## Ongoing obligations

- **Task Delegation Log:** set up before any data access begins. See [Building Your Research Team](/kb/planning/building-your-team). Your team members must have current SOP training before being added. Countersign each entry.
- **Annual REB renewal:** submit via Nagano before approval expires. Include updated progress and any changes to data extraction scope. See [Tracking and Responding to Submissions](/kb/submission/tracking-responding).
- **Amendments:** if the data extraction plan changes — new variables, expanded population, new data sources — submit an amendment before implementing the change.
- **Deviations:** document all deviations, including unauthorized data access or extraction outside the approved scope. Report major deviations to QA and the REB. See [Reportable Events](/kb/conduct/reportable-events).
- **Conflict of interest:** disclose at submission and every renewal. See [Conflict of Interest](/kb/governance/conflict-of-interest).

## Close-out

- Confirm all data extraction is complete and the database is final. See [Study Close-Out](/kb/conduct/close-out).
- Submit the Study Completion Report to the REB via Nagano.
- Record end dates on the delegation log for all team members.
- Execute the data disposition plan — destroy or archive the identification key as documented.
- Archive the study file. The 15-year Division 5 retention requirement does not apply. Confirm retention requirements with QA based on funding source and institutional policy.
