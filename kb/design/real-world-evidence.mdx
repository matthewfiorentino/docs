Real-world evidence (RWE) is clinical evidence derived from data collected outside the setting of traditional randomized controlled trials — from electronic health records, insurance claims, registries, and other sources generated during routine care. Health Canada has signaled openness to RWE in regulatory decision-making, and CIHR funds pragmatic trials that use real-world settings. This page provides an overview of the RWE landscape in Canada, with a focus on Quebec data sources and regulatory acceptance.

## Definitions

- **Real-world data (RWD):** Data relating to patient health status or the delivery of health care routinely collected from sources other than traditional clinical trials — electronic health records, claims databases, registries, patient-generated data, wearables.
- **Real-world evidence (RWE):** Clinical evidence about the usage and potential benefits or risks of a medical product derived from analysis of RWD.
- **Pragmatic trial:** A clinical trial designed to test interventions in real-world clinical practice conditions, often with broader eligibility criteria, real-world comparators, and outcomes relevant to clinical decision-making.

## Health Canada's position

Health Canada evaluates the usefulness of RWD and RWE on a case-by-case basis. Key guidance documents:

- [**"Elements of Real World Data/Evidence Quality Throughout the Prescription Drug Product Life Cycle"**](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/health/publications/drugs-health-products/real-world-data-evidence-drug-lifecycle-report.html) — establishes principles for generating RWE consistent with the Canadian regulatory standard. Covers data quality, study design, and transparency requirements.
- [**"Optimizing the Use of Real World Evidence to Inform Regulatory Decision-Making"**](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products/drug-products/announcements/optimizing-real-world-evidence-regulatory-decisions.html) (2019) — Health Canada's notice confirming receptivity to RWE submissions.

Health Canada has accepted RWE in contexts including Notice of Compliance with Conditions (NOC/c) decisions, orphan drug approvals, and situations where traditional trials are infeasible — such as expanding indications to populations excluded from original trials (pediatric, elderly, pregnant populations) or rare diseases.

[CDA-AMC](https://www.cda-amc.ca) (formerly CADTH) and INESSS jointly published the [**"Guidance for Reporting Real-World Evidence"**](https://www.cda-amc.ca/guidance-reporting-real-world-evidence) (2023), developed in collaboration with Health Canada. This provides a reporting checklist applicable to both regulatory and health technology assessment submissions.

## Quebec administrative health data sources

Quebec has several large administrative databases useful for RWE studies. Access is governed by provincial privacy legislation (including Law 25) and requires institutional and ethics approval.

| Database | What it contains | Operated by |
|---|---|---|
| [**RAMQ**](https://www.ramq.gouv.qc.ca/en/data-statistics) (Régie de l'assurance maladie du Québec) | Registration file (demographics), medical services file (physician billing claims), pharmaceutical services file (outpatient prescriptions) | RAMQ |
| **MED-ÉCHO** (Maintenance et Exploitation des Données pour l'Étude de la Clientèle Hospitalière) | Hospitalization discharge summaries covering all Quebec acute care institutions — in-hospital procedures and diagnoses | MSSS |
| [**INESSS**](https://www.inesss.qc.ca) | Access to linked de-identified data through tripartite agreements between MSSS, RAMQ, and INESSS | INESSS |
| **SISMACQ** (Système intégré de surveillance des maladies chroniques du Québec) | Integrated dataset linking RAMQ registration, MED-ÉCHO, death registry, medical services, and pharmaceutical services | INSPQ |

RAMQ data has been extensively validated for pharmacoepidemiological studies and is one of the most commonly used administrative datasets for Canadian RWE research.

### Accessing RAMQ and MED-ÉCHO through ISQ/CADRISQ

Access to RAMQ and MED-ÉCHO data is not direct — it follows a structured pathway governed by provincial privacy legislation, institutional agreements, and the [Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI)](https://www.cai.gouv.qc.ca).

**The access pathway:**

1. **REB approval** — required before any data access request. The protocol must specify which databases are requested, the variables needed, the linkage strategy, and the privacy safeguards.
2. **CAI authorization** — a formal application to the CAI is required for access to RAMQ data and most other provincial administrative datasets. The CAI evaluates whether the research purpose justifies access and whether adequate privacy protections are in place.
3. **Data access through the ISQ** — the [Institut de la statistique du Québec (ISQ)](https://statistique.quebec.ca/en/institut/services-for-researchers) manages secure access to linked administrative data for researchers. Access is provided through the CADRISQ secure research data centres, where researchers work with de-identified linked datasets in a controlled environment.
4. **Data custodian agreements** — depending on the databases requested, individual data custodian agreements with RAMQ, MSSS, or other holders may be required.

**Timelines:** Data access timelines vary substantially depending on the complexity of the request and the number of databases involved. Simple requests (single database, standard variables) may take approximately three months. Complex requests involving multiple linked databases can take considerably longer — plan accordingly and initiate the data access process early.

<Tip>
Start the CAI and ISQ data access applications as early as possible — ideally in parallel with the REB submission. Data access delays are the most common cause of timeline slippage in Quebec RWE studies.
</Tip>

### Accessing INESSS and SISMACQ

INESSS and SISMACQ use different access routes than the RAMQ/ISQ pathway above.

**INESSS** provides access to linked de-identified data under tripartite agreements between MSSS, RAMQ, and INESSS. Access is requested directly through [INESSS](https://www.inesss.qc.ca) — not through the ISQ/CADRISQ pathway. REB approval is required before submitting a research data request to INESSS.

**SISMACQ** is operated by the [INSPQ](https://www.inspq.qc.ca) (Institut national de santé publique du Québec) and already integrates RAMQ registration, MED-ÉCHO, the death registry, medical services, and pharmaceutical services data. Researchers who need several of these sources linked together should consider requesting SISMACQ through INSPQ rather than applying for each dataset separately through ISQ — it may reduce the number of applications and linkage steps required.

### Combining MUHC Data Warehouse and provincial data

Studies that draw from both the [MUHC Data Warehouse](/kb/design-support/data-warehouse) and Quebec provincial administrative sources require two separate authorization processes. These can run concurrently — researchers often serialize them unnecessarily, which adds months to the timeline.

| Process | What it requires | When it can start |
|---|---|---|
| **MUHC DW data request** | REB approval + MUHC Authorization + EFVP (F17/F18) | EFVP filed with Nagano F11; data request submitted after both approvals |
| **CAI application (provincial)** | REB approval | Can be submitted in parallel with the REB review |
| **ISQ/CADRISQ access** | CAI authorization | After CAI approves the application |

File the EFVP alongside your Nagano F11, submit the CAI application as soon as your REB-approved protocol is ready, and initiate a DW consultation before submitting to Nagano — all three tracks can be moving simultaneously during ethics review.

## Pragmatic trials

Pragmatic trials differ from traditional explanatory trials in several ways that affect study design, ethics review, and regulatory submission.

| Feature | Explanatory trial | Pragmatic trial |
|---|---|---|
| **Setting** | Specialized research centres | Routine care settings |
| **Participants** | Narrow eligibility, highly selected | Broad eligibility, reflective of real practice |
| **Comparator** | Placebo or active control per protocol | Usual care or standard of care |
| **Outcomes** | Surrogate or biomarker endpoints | Clinically meaningful outcomes (hospitalizations, mortality, quality of life) |
| **Follow-up** | Protocol-driven, frequent visits | Routine clinical follow-up; may use administrative data |

### Funding for pragmatic trials in Canada

CIHR's **SPOR Innovative Clinical Trials (iCT) Initiative** funds innovative trial designs including pragmatic trials, adaptive designs, and platform trials. The **Clinical Trials Fund (CTF)** also supports pragmatic approaches. Both programs require patient engagement and open-access publication of results.

<Note>
Pragmatic trials still require Health Canada authorization (CTA) if they involve investigational products regulated under Division 5 of the Food and Drug Regulations. The pragmatic design does not exempt a trial from regulatory requirements. Discuss the regulatory pathway with the sponsor or the RI-MUHC QA office early in the design phase.
</Note>

## Design considerations

For investigators considering RWE or pragmatic trial approaches:

- **Data quality assessment** — RWD must meet standards for completeness, accuracy, and relevance. Health Canada's guidance emphasizes that RWD quality should be assessed against the specific research question, not assumed.
- **Confounding** — observational RWE studies must address confounding through appropriate design (e.g., active comparator, new-user design) and statistical methods. Health Canada expects transparency about residual confounding.
- **Privacy** — Quebec Law 25 applies to all research use of personal health data. Privacy impact assessments are required when personal information is involved. See [Privacy and Confidentiality](/kb/recruitment/privacy).
- **Regulatory engagement** — Health Canada encourages early engagement when planning RWE submissions. Sponsors can discuss planned RWE strategies with Health Canada before filing.
