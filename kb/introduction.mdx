The RI-MUHC Clinical Research Knowledge Base is the central reference for everyone working on a clinical study at The Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre. Whether you are planning a first-in-human drug trial, managing an observational cohort, or joining a team as a trainee, the Knowledge Base covers the regulatory frameworks, institutional processes, and operational guidance you need — organized by the stage of the study and by your role within it.

This page explains how the Knowledge Base is structured and how to find what you need.

## How the Knowledge Base is organized

The Knowledge Base is divided into four main sections, each corresponding to a distinct phase or function of clinical research at the RI-MUHC. Most topics in the Knowledge Base connect to at least one of these sections.

  <Card title="Study Planning" icon="clipboard-list" href="/kb/planning/overview">
    Everything that happens before a study is approved — feasibility, study design, regulatory submissions, budgeting, contracts, and data planning.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Submission and Review" icon="file-check" href="/kb/submission/overview">
    The institutional review and approval process — REB submission, scientific and peer review, institutional feasibility, and the authorization from the Personne mandatée.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Study Conduct" icon="stethoscope" href="/kb/conduct/overview">
    Day-to-day obligations during an active study — privacy, consent, recruitment, investigational products, data management, monitoring, and close-out.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Governance and Oversight" icon="building-columns" href="/kb/governance/institutional-structure">
    The institutional framework governing clinical research at the RI-MUHC — structure, committees, the Research Ethics Board, regulatory frameworks, and research integrity.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
