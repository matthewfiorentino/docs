The RI-MUHC Clinical Research Hub is the central resource for everyone involved in clinical research at The Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre. It brings together operational guidance, regulatory references, standard operating procedures, training tools, and application guides — all organized around the way research actually runs at this institution.

Whether you are planning a first-in-human drug trial, managing a multi-site observational study, navigating your first REB submission, or joining a team as a trainee, the Hub covers what you need to know and where to go next.

## What the site covers

The Hub is organized into five main sections, accessible from the tabs at the top of any page.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Knowledge Base" icon="book-open" href="/kb/planning-overview">
    The operational core of the site. Covers every stage of a clinical study — from feasibility and study design through submission, site activation, recruitment, conduct, close-out, and archiving. Also includes foundational 101 articles, role-based onboarding guides, a glossary, and the governance and oversight framework.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Clinical Research SOPs" icon="file-shield" href="/sops/overview">
    The full text of every RI-MUHC Clinical Research Standard Operating Procedure — organized by topic, with overview pages summarizing key points and full document pages with appendices and templates. These are the controlled reference versions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Training and Learning" icon="graduation-cap" href="/training/compliance-requirements">
    Compliance requirements by study level, external certification guidance (GCP, TCPS2 CORE), the Study Classifier tool, and the Learning Lab — interactive practice exercises for knowledge checks, team scenarios, and inspection prep.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Centre for Innovative Medicine" icon="flask" href="/cim/services">

## The study lifecycle — step by step

The Knowledge Base is structured around the stages of a clinical study. Each stage has its own group in the sidebar under **Your Study, Step by Step**, with pages covering the operational guidance for that phase.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Study Planning">
    Feasibility, protocol design, study types, regulatory frameworks, and multi-site or decentralized trial considerations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Building Your Team">
    Team composition, hiring, and equity, diversity and inclusion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Funding, Budgets and Agreements">
    Grant applications, study budgets, clinical trial agreements, and managing study funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submission and Review">
    Preparing your submission, the Nagano platform, the triple evaluation (scientific, ethical, institutional), and tracking your review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Site Activation">
    Site setup, protocol documents, and the Investigator Site File — everything between authorization and first participant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Recruitment and Enrollment">
    Consent, pediatric and vulnerable populations, recruitment procedures, privacy, and data security.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Study Conduct">
    Data integrity, decentralized trial operations, investigational products, biological specimens, reportable events, team management, and monitoring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close-Out and Archiving">
    Health Canada inspections, formal close-out, and publication and dissemination requirements.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For a detailed walkthrough of how these stages connect — and what makes the RI-MUHC and Quebec context distinct — see [The Study Lifecycle](/kb/study-lifecycle).
