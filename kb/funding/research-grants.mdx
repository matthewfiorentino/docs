Funders and sponsors pay research funds to a recognized institution, which holds them in trust for the investigator. At the RI-MUHC, the Research Grants Office opens an account for each project and manages it until it closes. The principal investigator, who leads the study at the MUHC, requests the account and holds approval authority on it.

## Request a research account

When you receive a notice of award, or a donation earmarked for a research activity, request a new account. Submit the request through the MyAccounts application on the [RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:340), with all the documents it asks for. The same Portal page gives access to the MyAccounts user guide.

The Research Grants Office opens the account and confirms it by email. It does not open an account on its own, so a late request can delay the release of the study's funds. In most cases, each research project and each source of funding has its own account.

The principal investigator holds approval authority on the account, unless the request names delegates in MyAccounts. Through MyAccounts, the principal investigator can see, for each account, a summary of revenues and expenditures, a detailed activity report and a salary report.

Questions about opening an account go to the [Account Opening Office](mailto:rimuhcaccountopenings@muhc.mcgill.ca).

## What the Research Grants Office manages

The Research Grants Office manages an account from opening to close-out. Once the account is open, the office:

- Checks that spending follows the terms and conditions of the funding agency or the sponsor.
- Invoices the sponsor, and submits financial reports on each funder's schedule and under its rules.
- Coordinates audits with the funding agencies.
- Closes the account formally at the end of the study or the grant.

For an ongoing study, the office is the liaison between the principal investigator, the RI-MUHC, the sponsor and the funding agencies. Each account has an Account Monitor, listed under My Profile in MyAccounts, who answers questions about the budget, spending approvals and financial reporting. Other questions about balances, expenditure reports or reporting go to the [Research Grants Office](mailto:researchgrants@muhc.mcgill.ca).

The office also manages the awards of the [Canada Foundation for Innovation (CFI)](https://www.innovation.ca) held by RI-MUHC researchers, and the financial management of the study accounts of the Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM).

## Include overhead before the account opens

Overhead is set when the budget is prepared, before the proposal is submitted or the agreement negotiated, and the account carries it from then on. The RI-MUHC's [overhead rates by funding source](/kb/funding/budgets-contracts#include-overhead-in-the-budget), and the rules on exceptions, are on Study Budgets. For a source of funding not listed there, ask the [Pre-Awards Office](mailto:pre-awards.rimuhc@muhc.mcgill.ca).
