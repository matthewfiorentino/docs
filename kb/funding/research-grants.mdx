How research accounts are opened and managed at the RI-MUHC — from first account request through financial reporting and closeout.

## When to engage Research Grants

The Research Grants Office, part of Research Services, manages the financial administration of research projects at the RI-MUHC. For industry-sponsored clinical trials, the process typically begins once the Clinical Trial Agreement is executed and the Sponsor has confirmed site approval — at that point, the <Tooltip tip="US equivalent of the Canadian Qualified Investigator (QI)." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#pi">PI</Tooltip> requests a new research account through the MyAccounts Application, and Research Grants opens the account and begins managing the fund flow.

This timing matters practically: the research team hired to help prepare a submission — the <Tooltip tip="The team member who handles day-to-day administration and coordination of the clinical trial." headline="Clinical Research Coordinator (CRC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#crc">CRC</Tooltip>, research nurse, or coordinator who builds the <Tooltip tip="The QI's collection of documentation demonstrating compliance, study integrity, and participant safety." headline="Investigator Site File (ISF)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#isf">ISF</Tooltip> and submits to <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip> — is typically funded from an existing PI grant account, not the new study account. The study account does not exist until after all approvals are in place. Budget for startup personnel accordingly.

<Warning>
Account opening is the PI's responsibility. Research Grants does not open accounts automatically upon contract execution. The PI must initiate the request through MyAccounts. Delays in doing this can delay the release of study funds.
</Warning>

## Opening a research account

A separate account is opened for each research project and source of funding. To open an account, the PI submits a new account request through the **MyAccounts Application** on the RI-MUHC Portal, with all required supporting documents attached. Once received, Research Grants opens the account and sends email confirmation. The PI holds approval authority on the account unless delegates are specified in MyAccounts.

Through MyAccounts, the PI can view a summary of revenues and expenditures, a detailed activity report, and a salary report for each account.

For industry-sponsored clinical trials, the Research Agreements Office ensures Research Grants receives a copy of the signed contractual agreement — the accounts process does not begin until that copy is on file.

<Note>
The MyAccounts Application is accessible via the [RI-MUHC Portal](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/pls/apex/f?p=106:340). The same portal page provides access to the User Guide. For account opening questions, contact the Account Opening Office at rimuhcaccountopenings@muhc.mcgill.ca.
</Note>

## What Research Grants manages

Research Grants handles the full post-award lifecycle of a study account. Once an account is open, the office monitors compliance with the terms and conditions set by the funding agency or sponsor, invoices the sponsor and submits financial reports on the required schedule, and formally closes the account at the end of the study or grant period.

For ongoing studies, Research Grants serves as the liaison between the PI, the RI-MUHC, the sponsor, and any funding agencies. PIs with questions about account balances, expenditure reports, or financial reporting requirements should contact the office directly at researchgrants@muhc.mcgill.ca.

Research Grants also manages Canada Foundation for Innovation (CFI) awards held by RI-MUHC researchers, and is responsible for the financial management of CIM research study accounts.

## Overhead rates

Overhead recovers the indirect costs of the infrastructure, administration and services that research relies on. The RI-MUHC sets overhead on all its research by funding source, as a percentage of eligible direct costs, in its Institutional Cost Recovery Policy (2026). The PI includes overhead in the budget before submission or negotiation, and cannot waive, reduce or negotiate it on their own; any exception needs the prior written approval of the RI-MUHC's Chief Operating Officer. If a budget leaves overhead out or reduces it, the RI-MUHC can decline the project, or cover the overhead by reducing the project's other direct costs where the funder's guidelines and the RI-MUHC's priorities allow. Ask the Pre-Awards Office which costs are eligible.

| Funding source | Overhead rate | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| Industry-funded clinical trials | 35% | On all eligible direct costs. |
| Tri-Agency grants (CIHR, NSERC, SSHRC) | No added overhead on the Tri-Agency portion | The federal Research Support Fund covers that portion's indirect costs. Other portions, such as matching funds, carry the highest rate their funder allows. |
| US National Institutes of Health (NIH) | 8% | On direct costs, excluding equipment. |
| US Department of Defense | The RI-MUHC's negotiated rate | Negotiated each year. Ask the Pre-Awards Office for the current rate. |
| Unrestricted donations and fundraising transfers from an MUHC foundation | 15% | |
| Industry funds transferred through an MUHC foundation with a charitable receipt | 35% | |
| Other funders and sponsors, such as charities and other foundations | The highest rate the funder allows | 35% if the funder sets no rate. |

For a source not listed here, such as a transfer from an MUHC department, ask the Pre-Awards Office.
