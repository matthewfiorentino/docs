Every research budget at the RI-MUHC includes both the study's direct costs and its institutional costs, also called overhead. The principal investigator, who leads the study at the MUHC, is accountable for preparing the budget and approves it. The principal investigator is also responsible and accountable for adhering to the budget and for meeting the study's contractual commitments.

No institutional office reviews a study budget. For a study run by the Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM), the CIM's budget specialists review it before the principal investigator approves it.

The terms the budget is built on go into the study's agreements, which the [Research Agreements Office](mailto:contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca) reviews. What an agreement needs, and how it is signed, is on [Research Agreements](/kb/funding/clinical-trial-agreements).

## Start from the Clinical Trial Budget Template

The RI-MUHC's Clinical Trial Budget Template, launched in January 2026, is the recommended starting point for building a study budget. It includes staff salary tables based on the institutional salary scales, the time and effort for each study visit, and the costs of MUHC services. From these, the template produces both a project cost summary and a budget in the format of the Canadian Institutes of Health Research (CIHR).

## Include overhead in the budget

The RI-MUHC applies overhead to eligible direct costs to recover its institutional costs. Its standard rate is 35 per cent, unless the funder, the sponsor or an institutional policy sets another rate. Every proposal and agreement must include the highest overhead rate the funder or sponsor allows.

Include overhead when you prepare the budget, before you submit the proposal or negotiate with the sponsor. Every proposal also requires the completion of the RI-MUHC's Pre-Awards Checklist, which the [Pre-Awards Office](mailto:pre-awards.rimuhc@muhc.mcgill.ca) provides.{/* TO CONFIRM (the Pre-Awards Office): where researchers get the Pre-Awards Checklist, and whether the Pre-Awards Office is the right contact for it. The policy (section 4.1) requires it for all proposals and names no place to find it. */}

Consult the RI-MUHC's Research Services on the budget and its compliance with the policy. Make sure that the discussions before an agreement reflect the policy's requirements.{/* TO CONFIRM (the Pre-Awards Office): which office of the RI-MUHC's Research Services researchers consult on the budget, and its address. | Other funders and sponsors, such as charities and other foundations | The highest rate the funder allows, or 35 per cent if it sets none. |

A temporary rate adjustment must be approved and documented in writing before the proposal is submitted. If a budget leaves overhead out or reduces it, the RI-MUHC can decline the project. ## Plan what the budget must cover

A study's financial agreements may cover:

- Fees for the sponsor-investigator, the principal investigator, the sub-investigators and other members of the research team.
- Payment per participant or per visit, and payment for participants who complete the study.
- The payment schedule.
- Reimbursement of participants' expenses, such as transportation and parking.
- Reimbursement of costs for patient referrals, or for remote trial conduct by sub-investigators at satellite sites.
- Reimbursement of the MUHC departments and other service providers that support the study, such as nursing, pharmacy, laboratories and radiology.

The site must be aware of the financial implications of every study it takes part in, including the direct and indirect costs of using its facilities. The sponsor must also address the costs of treating participants for trial-related injuries, in line with the regulatory requirements that apply.

## Estimate staff costs

The RI-MUHC calculates staff costs from its institutional salary scales:

- One full-time employee works 1,820 hours a year (35 hours a week for 52 weeks).
- Benefits add 19 per cent to the base salary.
- The cost to the study is the base salary times 1.19, times the share of a full-time position the study funds.
- For a study that runs over several years, add a cost-of-living increase of 5 per cent a year, compounded.{/* TO CONFIRM (the Research Grants Office or the Pre-Awards Office): the 35-hour week and the 19 per cent benefits rest only on the RI-MUHC's handbooks (FACTS "Budget assumptions"), which are never cited; no source found for the 5 per cent cost-of-living increase (searched the Cost Recovery Policy 2026, the provided documents for "cost-of-living" and "5%"). ## Budget the REB and authorization fees

{/* No section strip (MF, Nov. 15, 2026, ledger row 202, answer 1, "ok"): the fee rule's scope (funding wholly or partly by private enterprise, private not-for-profit funders included) matches none of STYLE-HUB F1's fixed tags, and the section's first sentence states it. */}

Quebec's public health institutions charge private enterprise for the review and authorization of research projects. The fees are set in a schedule of Quebec's health ministry, the Ministère de la Santé, de la Prévention et des Aînés, issued as an annex to directive 2023-016 of the Ministère de la Santé et des Services sociaux (MSSS), the ministry's former name.

The schedule is indexed every April 1 to the pension index of the Quebec Pension Plan. The amounts below are in force from April 1, 2026.

| Fee item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Scientific review | \$647 |
| Ethics review | \$6,475 |
| Authorization | \$1,942 per institution |
| Annual ethics monitoring | \$647 per participating institution, for up to 10 institutions, at each renewal |
| Annual authorization monitoring | \$647 per institution, at each renewal |
| Major amendment | \$647 per amendment |

The scientific review and the ethics review are each billed once per project, and only when a committee of the institution does them. A single-site study whose two reviews are done by the MUHC's committees costs \$9,064 in its first year: the scientific review, the ethics review and one authorization.

The MUHC REB bills any project funded wholly or partly by private enterprise, where the private funding exceeds the exemption amount in the schedule (\$19,420 from April 1, 2026). The REB also bills, among others:

- Clinical trials funded and sponsored by a company that makes a drug, a medical device, software or another therapeutic product.
- Investigator-initiated clinical trials with private or mixed funding for which the RI-MUHC is the sponsor.

The REB counts every legal person governed by private law as private enterprise, not-for-profit organizations included. A project can be billable before any contract is signed. Each review is billed whatever the REB decides, and the fees are not refunded.{/* TO CONFIRM (the REB office, Centre for Applied Ethics; 14-open-questions.md): are projects funded by the MUHC Foundation billable? When you submit the study, identify every source of funding in the submission form. If private enterprise funds the study wholly or partly, give the private enterprises' billing contacts and the budgets. If you do not give the billing contact information at submission, follow up with the [Research Agreements Office](mailto:contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca), the RI-MUHC's finance department or both.{/* REB-SOP 350.002, section 5.1.3, says "the contracts office and/or the finance department of the RI-MUHC"; the Contracts Office is the Research Agreements Office's former name (FACTS "The Research Agreements Office"). Include the REB and authorization fees in the study budget. If they are not in the budget, the principal investigator is held personally responsible for paying them.

When you close or abandon the project, [notify the REB](/kb/close-out/close-out#notifying-the-reb-and-health-canada). Every REB action before the REB confirms the project's closure is billable.

## Negotiate the budget with an industry sponsor

<div className="hub-scope"><span className="hub-scope-label">Applies to</span><span className="hub-scope-tag">Industry-sponsored studies</span></div>

In an industry-sponsored study, the principal investigator negotiates the budget with the sponsor while the [Research Agreements Office](mailto:contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca) negotiates the agreement's clauses. The budget respects the rates the hospital departments set. The research contract must give the total amount granted, and a detailed budget with the cost of each examination, test or other procedure the protocol requires.

[Negotiating an Industry-Sponsored Budget](/kb/funding/budget-negotiation) takes you through the sponsor's proposed budget line by line. For a study run by the CIM, the CIM's budget specialists can help prepare and negotiate it.
