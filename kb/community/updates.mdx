<Update label="March 2027">
  ## The Clinical Research Hub is live

  The RI-MUHC's Clinical Research Hub went live with the RI-MUHC's new standard operating procedures (SOPs). The Hub holds the clinical research SOPs, guidance for each stage of a study, the training requirements for each study level and guides to the applications used in clinical research, such as Nagano and TalentLMS. Each role on a study team also has an orientation page.

  Start from the [Hub's home page](/). If something is missing or unclear, write to the [Research Facilitator](mailto:research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca).
</Update>
