<Update label="August 2026">
  ## Clinical Research Hub is live

  The RI-MUHC Clinical Research Hub is now the central resource for clinical research at the MUHC. SOPs, orientation guides, training requirements, Nagano guides, Knowledge Base articles, and CIM documentation are all in one place.

  Start here: [Welcome to the Clinical Research Hub](/)

<Update label="July 2026">
  ## New guidance on pediatric research

  A dedicated article on conducting research with pediatric populations at the MUHC is now available. It covers age-specific consent and assent requirements, the role of the legally authorized representative, Quebec-specific rules for mature minors, and additional safeguards for vulnerable pediatric populations.

  Read the full article: [Pediatric Research](/kb/recruitment/pediatric-research)
</Update>

<Update label="April 2026">
  ## Clinical Research Hub in preview

  The RI-MUHC Clinical Research Hub is now in preview, replacing the previous intranet research pages. All SOPs, training requirements, Nagano guides, orientation guides, and CIM documentation are consolidated here.

  If you have feedback or notice missing content, contact the Research Facilitator.
</Update>

<Update label="March 2026">
  ## CATALIS FAST TRACK now available

  The RI-MUHC now participates in the CATALIS FAST TRACK program for industry-sponsored trials.

  Contact the Research Facilitator to confirm eligibility and initiate the process.
</Update>
