Community Connect is a series of sessions on clinical research at The Institute. The [Research Facilitator](mailto:research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca) hosts them with experts from across the institution. The experts answer common questions, explain submission processes, clear up misconceptions and give the reasons behind institutional requirements. Sessions are open to all members of the research community and include time for questions. Sessions are held about once a month, on topics drawn from researchers' questions and from institutional updates.

The Clinical Research Hub lists each upcoming session and keeps the recordings of past sessions.
