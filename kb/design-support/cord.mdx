[Capture and Optimization of Research Data (CORD)](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca) helps research teams collect and manage their data, from the data plan in a protocol or grant application to the end of the project. CORD builds and hosts REDCap databases for RI-MUHC studies. CORD is a unit of the RI-MUHC's Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE). It works in close collaboration with the Division of Digital and Information Technology and the Maelstrom research program.

## What CORD offers

[CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca)'s services cover the whole life of a project's data:

- Reviewing the data plan in a study protocol or grant application: how the data will be collected, cleaned, stored and shared, and what that will cost.
- Reviewing data collection tools, such as case report forms (CRFs), participant questionnaires and data dictionaries, and suggesting improvements such as clearer skip patterns or better response options.
- Building REDCap projects, putting the collection tools into REDCap and testing them, so that the database works and meets the study's needs.
- Hosting REDCap projects.
- Data management, such as data monitoring, quality control, cleaning, extraction, integration, version tracking and database updates.
- Training on REDCap and help with it throughout the project.

Consider involving CORD at the protocol or grant stage, because the design of a database is costly to change once data collection has started.

## Who can use CORD

[CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca) hosts REDCap projects for teams of the RI-MUHC and of its affiliated institutions, the MUHC and McGill University. CORD offers its paid support services, such as reviewing data tools, setting up databases and managing data, to all researchers, whatever their institution. Staff, students and trainees can submit requests, but each request must be linked to a principal investigator.

## Use REDCap for a study

For MUHC sponsor-investigators, the RI-MUHC supports REDCap for investigator-initiated eCRFs of low or moderate complexity (ideally no more than 1,000 variables). For a more complex study that needs more variables, programming a few variables per form is recommended.

REDCap is meant as an electronic case report form (eCRF) that collects data transcribed from source documents. The RI-MUHC's standard operating procedures (SOPs) consider REDCap [unfit for use as an electronic consent form](/sops/cr-014#5-8-1), because it allows only one signature per form and source data should be filed apart from the eCRF.

[Sponsors and sponsor-investigators must validate](/sops/cr-014#5-8-1) any electronic data capture system they use, REDCap included, to ensure its reliability, precision, expected performance and compliance with Part 11 of Title 21 of the US Code of Federal Regulations (21 CFR Part 11). For a Health Canada-regulated study, ask [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca) for the validation records that cover your use. Inspectors can ask for them.

Any REDCap self-training should be documented on a Training Log.

When you prepare for the sponsor's or sponsor-investigator's site feasibility visit, the study team should [contact information technology (IT) through IT Rescue](/sops/cr-004#5-1-3) on the RI-MUHC Portal to have any study-specific software evaluated.

## Before your REDCap project collects data

When you request a REDCap project, you accept the REDCap service terms of [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca):

- The RI-MUHC's REDCap is for non-commercial research purposes: internal research, operations and education. Non-commercial purposes can include research funded by commercial organizations, but not the use of REDCap to provide contracts or services to outside organizations.
- The principal investigator manages user access and roles in the REDCap project, or may designate a team member to do it after the project is created.
- Every trainee, researcher and administrative staff member must complete a REDCap User Account Request Form and agree to the REDCap Use Policy before receiving access. Anyone who does not is denied access.
- Test the project before it moves to production status and starts collecting data. When it reaches production, all data entered while testing are deleted.
- A research study needs approval from a research ethics board (REB) before it moves to production and starts collecting data.

For a REDCap project or other CORD services, fill in the online [CORD Service Request Form](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/cim/redcap/surveys/?s=LPCYP8KP4JHP8C79). The form is for requests that CORD bills, not for general questions. For a general question, write to [CORD](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca).

## Fees

The first hour of expert support is free for each research initiative.

| Service | RI-MUHC and MUHC principal investigators | Other principal investigators, including McGill University | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setting up the first REDCap project | \$200 | \$300 | A one-time fee for the first project of a study or initiative. The fee covers creating the project and adding its users. |
| Each additional related project | \$75 | \$115 | A one-time fee for each further REDCap project of the same study or initiative. |
| REDCap hosting | \$87.50 for each 3 months (\$350 a year) | \$131.25 for each 3 months (\$525 a year) | A maintenance fee, invoiced once a year, or every 3 months if the project lasts less than a year. One fee covers all the related projects of the same initiative. |
| Expert support and technical assistance | \$55 an hour | \$55 an hour | Consultation, technical assistance and troubleshooting. The first hour is free for each initiative. |

The rates have been in effect since April 1, 2025, and may change with prior notice. The prices are in Canadian dollars, and the final fees are set in the contract.
