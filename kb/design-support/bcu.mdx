The [Biostatistics Consulting Unit (BCU)](mailto:bcu@rimuhc.ca) gives statistical advice for basic, clinical and epidemiological research. Its help covers a whole study, from the grant application and the design to the analysis, the publications and knowledge translation. The BCU is one of the service platforms of the RI-MUHC's Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation (CORE).

The BCU serves members of the RI-MUHC and researchers from other institutions. The BCU also takes consultations from industry, government and non-government organizations.

## What the BCU offers

The [BCU](mailto:bcu@rimuhc.ca) can help at any stage of a project. Consider involving it at the study design or grant application stage, before data collection starts, because statistical decisions are harder to change once the data are collected.

Its services are:

- Preparing the relevant sections of grant proposals.
- Sample size calculations.
- Randomization plans.
- Experimental design.
- Data management and statistical analysis plans.
- Statistical analysis, including advanced analytical methods.
- Statistical interpretation, manuscripts, reports for a <Tooltip tip="An independent committee that the sponsor may establish to assess, at intervals, the progress of a clinical study, its safety data and its critical efficacy endpoints, and to recommend to the sponsor whether to continue, modify or stop the study." headline="Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#dsmb">Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB)</Tooltip> and other reports.
- Training, education and knowledge transfer.

The BCU works on these kinds of studies:

- Epidemiologic and population-based studies.
- Clinical trials in Phases II, III and IV.
- Translational studies that combine clinical and biological data.
- Analyses of large surveillance and administrative databases, such as those of the Régie de l'assurance maladie du Québec (RAMQ), Medicare and Statistics Canada.
- Big data and large cohort analyses.

The BCU works in R, SAS and Stata.

## How to request a consultation

The [BCU](mailto:bcu@rimuhc.ca) invites all its clients, new and current, to use CrossLab, its online system for service requests and billing. The system needs a one-time registration.

To register:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the BCU's service centre">
    Go to the [BCU's service centre on CrossLab](https://rimuhc.corefacilities.org/service_center/show_external/4719).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register">
    Select **Register**, in the upper right corner. Select your <Tooltip tip="The investigator who leads the study team at a site and is responsible for conducting the study there." headline="Principal Investigator (PI)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#pi">principal investigator</Tooltip> or lab, and check your contact information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for confirmation">
    The CrossLab team reviews your registration. Once it is confirmed, you can submit requests. For help, see [Agilent's registration guide](https://help.ilab.agilent.com/en_US/99540-getting-started-with-ilab/325013-registering-with-ilab).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To submit a request:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Go to the [BCU's service centre on CrossLab](https://rimuhc.corefacilities.org/service_center/show_external/4719) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the request">
    Select the **Request Services** tab, then **Initiate request**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the form and submit it">
    The BCU team reviews the request. The team either agrees to do the work or asks you for more information.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For questions about CrossLab or a request, write to the [BCU](mailto:bcu@rimuhc.ca).

## Fees

The [BCU](mailto:bcu@rimuhc.ca) charges by the hour. Its rates have been in effect since June 1, 2026, for new and existing service requests:

| Client | Rate |
|---|---|
| Principal investigators with an active RI-MUHC account | \$100 an hour |
| MUHC clinicians and RI-MUHC affiliates without an active RI-MUHC account | \$110 an hour |
| Other principal investigators, including those affiliated with McGill University or other academic or research institutions | \$120 an hour |
| Industry | Ask the [BCU](mailto:bcu@rimuhc.ca) for its rates. |

The BCU reviews its rates every year and may change them, with prior notice.

Early career researchers and researchers who hold no research funding receive the first four hours of consultation for a grant submission at no cost at the time of service. The free hours apply to at most two grant applications for each principal investigator each year, one project per grant competition. If the grant is funded, those four hours are charged to the project.
