The Accelerating Clinical Trials–Clinical Trial Unit (ACT-CTU) is the RI-MUHC's clinical trial unit. The RI-MUHC set it up as a member of the Accelerating Clinical Trials (ACT) Canada Consortium, a national initiative that the Canadian Institutes of Health Research (CIHR) funded in January 2023 to boost the impact of clinical trials in Canada. The unit helps RI-MUHC researchers plan the methods of their trials, strengthen their protocols and grant applications, and start trials that have sites in other provinces.{/* TO CONFIRM (the ACT-CTU's manager, amanda.lovato@rimuhc.ca, through MF): that the unit helps start trials that have sites in other provinces rests only on the Town Hall slide of May 20, 2026 (session material, never cited: "ACT-CTU: Helping Accelerate Your Investigator-led Trials Outside Quebec"). ## What the ACT-CTU offers

The [ACT-CTU](mailto:amanda.lovato@rimuhc.ca) runs these programs for RI-MUHC trialists:

- **Protocol Review Sessions.** Live forums where trialists receive real-time feedback on a proposed trial from experts. The sessions are also meant to strengthen applications to granting agencies.
- **Clinical Trial Rounds.** A monthly lecture series by experts from across Canada and from the RI-MUHC on clinical trial topics, such as innovative design methods, complex trials and the design of pilot studies.
- **Support with Health Canada submissions.** A contracts specialist, funded by the ACT-CTU, supports trialists' submissions to Health Canada, while the allocated funds last.
- **Pilot trial funding.** The unit connects early-stage studies with seed funding. Its first internal call for flagship feasibility trial proposals, in 2024, chose three pilot trials.
- **Help with sites in other provinces.** Two initiatives of the ACT Consortium, described in [the section below](#start-a-trial-that-has-sites-in-other-provinces).

For help with the methods of a trial, the unit's intake routes requests to the RI-MUHC's [Novel Trial Methods Hub](#get-help-with-the-methods-of-a-trial).{/* TO CONFIRM (the ACT-CTU's manager): the routing rests on the Town Hall slide of May 20, 2026 (session material) and on the coordinator's relay of MF's word; see the note under "Get help with the methods of a trial". */}

## Start a trial that has sites in other provinces

For a trial that also has sites in other provinces, the [ACT-CTU](mailto:amanda.lovato@rimuhc.ca) can help with two initiatives of the ACT Consortium that aim to shorten the trial's start-up:{/* TO CONFIRM (the ACT-CTU's manager, through MF): no source read says that the ACT-CTU helps with these two initiatives; the only evidence is the Town Hall slide of May 20, 2026 (session material). See the question in the opening. */}

- **ACT's Governing Participating Site Agreement.** The regulated version of this agreement is a pre-negotiated, legally binding contract for clinical trials that Health Canada regulates, that run at several institutions and that the federal research agencies fund. Institutions that adopt it, as the RI-MUHC has, agree to its terms in full. Each trial then adds a study letter with its own details and any permitted changes. The agreement is not for industry-sponsored research.
- **CanReview.** CanReview, a pan-Canadian collaboration that the ACT Consortium supports, gives one research ethics review to a multi-site clinical trial, for the sites and research ethics boards that take part in it. As of November 2026, no Quebec research ethics board takes part, and the RI-MUHC has declared its intent to become a participating site.

In theory, a Quebec researcher can use [CanReview](https://canreview.ca/) for the sites of a pan-Canadian study that are outside Quebec and that take part in CanReview. The Quebec site is still submitted in Nagano, so the study needs at least two parallel submissions. A change is being worked on, with no timeline yet.{/* TO CONFIRM (the Research Facilitator; already asked, 14-open-questions.md, batch 77): when the RI-MUHC or the MUHC REB joins CanReview, or the Nagano requirement changes, tell us, so this page and kb/design/cross-border-studies can be updated. */} [How to submit the sites of a pan-Canadian study](/kb/design/cross-border-studies#submit-the-quebec-sites-in-nagano) is on its own page.

## Who can use the ACT-CTU

The ACT-CTU's programs are for RI-MUHC researchers. The unit's aim is to build a clinical trial community at the RI-MUHC and to strengthen its trial capabilities, with a focus on trials that investigators lead themselves.
