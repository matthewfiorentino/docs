The MUHC organizes research with human participants, their data or their biological material under its [*Regulatory Framework for Clinical Research at Santé Québec – McGill University Health Centre: Organization and Good Practices*](https://rimuhc.ca/conduct-research/policies#framework){/* TO CONFIRM (the rimuhc chat; master doc, Decisions for MF) at the rimuhc.ca launch: the Framework's page section, rimuhc.ca/conduct-research/policies#framework (the rimuhc chat, Nov. 13, 2026; the PDF's address will change in WordPress, so the page is linked, not the PDF). */}. The Framework covers research at the RI-MUHC as well as at the MUHC.

## Who reviews and authorizes your study

Your study needs a favourable scientific review, ethics review and institutional feasibility review, which run in parallel, and then <Tooltip tip="The MUHC's final authorization for a study to begin, issued by the Personne mandatée once the scientific, ethics and institutional feasibility reviews are favourable and, where the study needs it, Health Canada has authorized it." headline="MUHC Authorization" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#muhc-auth">MUHC Authorization</Tooltip>, the final step. You submit your study in <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's electronic study submission and management platform. All studies requiring MUHC Authorization are submitted through Nagano." headline="Nagano (RI-MUHC)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip>, the MUHC's registry of all research under its auspices. [Study Submission and Review](/kb/submission/submission-overview) explains each step and the other conditions to meet before recruitment.

The MUHC's <Tooltip tip="The ethics committee that reviews and approves research involving human participants. Also called CER at French-language institutions." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">Research Ethics Board (REB)</Tooltip> conducts the ethics review and the [scientific review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#scientific-and-ethics-review-by-the-reb).

A [study at more than one Quebec site](/kb/design/multi-centric) in the public health network is reviewed by one Quebec REB for all of them, which may be another institution's REB; the MUHC still does its own institutional feasibility review and authorization.

The REB oversees your study for as long as the study runs. The MUHC's board of directors entrusts the REB with reviewing research under the MUHC's auspices; the REB reports directly to the board and is independent in the projects it reviews. Its office is part of the MUHC's [Centre for Applied Ethics](https://muhc.ca/cae).

In the institutional feasibility review, <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's Quality Assurance department, which checks the credentials and training of each investigator who submits a study, audits research and maintains the SOPs. In GCP, quality assurance is the planned, systematic actions that ensure a trial complies with GCP and the applicable regulatory requirements." headline="Quality Assurance (QA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qa">Quality Assurance</Tooltip> (QA) checks the credentials and training of each investigator who submits the study, the Research Agreements Office handles the study's agreements, and the MUHC departments whose services the study uses review that use. Every review, and what each needs from you, is listed under the [institutional feasibility review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#institutional-feasibility-review); QA's other work is on [Quality Assurance](/kb/governance/quality-assurance).

Quebec's health ministry, the Ministère de la Santé, de la Prévention et des Aînés, requires each establishment in its network that conducts research to authorize formally all research under its auspices. At the MUHC, the President and Chief Executive Officer holds that authority and has delegated it to the <Tooltip tip="The person the MUHC has mandated to authorize research: the Director of Innovation, Performance and Quality, who issues MUHC Authorization for each study." headline="Personne mandatée" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#personne-mandatee">Personne mandatée</Tooltip>, the Director of Innovation, Performance and Quality. The authorization letter that the Personne mandatée issues sets out your commitments and the MUHC's.

Do not start any research activity, including recruitment, data collection or analysis, until you have both REB approval and MUHC Authorization.

A study without MUHC Authorization isn't covered by the MUHC's institutional insurance.{/* The Research Facilitator, through MF, Nov. 13, 2026 (FACTS "The Research Facilitator, Nov. 13, answers 1, 3 to 5, 7 to 11 and 13"). */}

If you aren't sure which office handles part of your study's review, the Research Facilitator can help you plan it.

Based on: the *Regulatory Framework*, sections 1.2.2, 1.2.3, 3.1.02, 3.1.03, 3.1.05, 3.1.06 and 3.1.07A; the [*Cadre de référence ministériel pour la recherche avec des participants humains*](https://publications.msss.gouv.qc.ca/msss/document-002696/) of the Ministère de la Santé et des Services sociaux (October 2020, French only), Normes 3 and 4; [SOP-CR-007](/sops/cr-007), sections 3.0, [5.4.2](/sops/cr-007#5-4-2), [5.4.7](/sops/cr-007#5-4-7) and [5.6.1](/sops/cr-007#5-6-1); the Centre for Applied Ethics' [home page](https://muhc.ca/cae).

## Have your research agreements reviewed

The Research Agreements Office drafts, reviews and negotiates every [research agreement](/kb/funding/clinical-trial-agreements) for a study. A research contract with a company must involve the study's principal investigator, the researcher who leads it at the MUHC. Besides the company and the principal investigator, the MUHC's President and Chief Executive Officer and the RI-MUHC's Executive Director and Chief Scientific Officer, or their designated representatives, must sign it.

You should never sign a binding contract or agreement before the Research Agreements Office has reviewed it, and investigators must not sign on behalf of the RI-MUHC. A research agreement is also required when the study involves collaborators external to the RI-MUHC; McGill University researchers are considered external for this purpose.

Based on: the *Regulatory Framework*, section 4.1.01; [SOP-CR-007, section 5.4.6](/sops/cr-007#5-4-6).

## Who is accountable for research at the MUHC

The MUHC's board of directors adopts the *Regulatory Framework* and is ultimately accountable for all research under the MUHC's auspices with human participants, their data or their biological material. The MUHC's President and Chief Executive Officer holds institutional responsibility for that research. The MUHC, an establishment of [Santé Québec](/kb/governance/laws-regulations#apply-quebec-rules-at-the-muhc), is affiliated with McGill University's Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences, and RI-MUHC researchers and MUHC clinicians are affiliated with McGill.

The RI-MUHC, the MUHC's research arm, is a legal entity separate from the MUHC and is governed by its own board of directors. Its Executive Director and Chief Scientific Officer directs and manages all RI-MUHC research.

The Executive Director and Chief Scientific Officer is responsible for ensuring that the requirements of <Tooltip tip="International standard for design, conduct, monitoring, recording, and reporting of clinical studies." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#gcp">good clinical practice</Tooltip> (the International Council for Harmonisation's guideline E6, for drugs, and the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) standard ISO 14155, for medical devices), of the RI-MUHC's [standard operating procedures](/sops) and of federal and provincial regulators are available to the research community, and that appropriate measures are taken to secure and maintain compliance with them.

The Director of the Centre for Innovative Medicine and Clinical Research, who reports to the Executive Director and Chief Scientific Officer, oversees the support of clinical research at the RI-MUHC, including the Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM), and the conduct of principal investigators in clinical research.

Every study must have a principal investigator, who has the qualifications its type of research requires and is responsible for the project. To lead a study as principal investigator, the MUHC requires you to hold [research privileges or researcher status](/training/compliance-requirements#human-research-privileges-and-researcher-status), or a written institutional authorization, first; the RI-MUHC's Performance Office issues the certificates for privileges and status.

## Work with the Centre for Innovative Medicine

The CIM is a clinical research facility of the RI-MUHC. It is located at the MUHC, but its operations, management and services fall under the RI-MUHC. [CIM Services, Facilities and Platforms](/cim/services) describes what it offers, and [Planning Your Study with the CIM](/cim/planning) explains how to engage it. If your study uses CIM services, a CIM agreement must be in place.

Based on: the *Regulatory Framework*, section 2.2.07; [SOP-CR-002, section 5.5.3](/sops/cr-002#5-5-3).

## Research at The Neuro

At the Montreal Neurological Institute-Hospital (The Neuro), research is under the MUHC's auspices when it involves people who receive services from the MUHC as participants, uses MUHC resources or is conducted under the responsibility of researchers in a way that involves their affiliation with the MUHC or the RI-MUHC. That research is approved by the MUHC's REB, or by the REB of another establishment of Quebec's health network, and authorized by the MUHC's Personne mandatée.

All clinical trials under Health Canada's purview at The Neuro must be managed by its Clinical Research Unit.

Based on: the *Regulatory Framework*, Appendix A, sections 1 and 5.

## Raise a complaint or a concern

A complaint from a participant in a research project goes to the MUHC's [Quality and Complaints Commissioner](https://muhc.ca/ombudsman), the Ombudsman's Office, independent of the MUHC's directorates. Participants are told, when they sign the consent form, how to reach the commissioner, and they have the same rights as any other user of the MUHC's services.

A concern about ethics or scientific integrity in the conduct of a research project goes to the Personne mandatée, the MUHC's person responsible for the responsible conduct of research; [Research Integrity](/kb/governance/research-integrity) explains how it is handled.{/* Who the person responsible for the responsible conduct of research is, and that the Framework's Appendix B (the events and allegations team, the intake committee) is in force: the Research Facilitator, through MF, Nov. 13, 2026. */}

Based on: the *Regulatory Framework*, section 3.1.13, which cites section 671 of the Act respecting the governance of the health and social services system, and section 6; the MUHC's [Ombudsman page](https://muhc.ca/ombudsman).
