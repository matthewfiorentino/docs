The MUHC organizes research with human participants, their data or their biological material under its [*Regulatory Framework for Clinical Research at Santé Québec – McGill University Health Centre: Organization and Good Practices*](https://rimuhc.ca/conduct-research/policies#framework). The Framework covers research at the RI-MUHC as well as at the MUHC.

## Who reviews and authorizes your study

A study needs a favourable [scientific review, ethics review and institutional feasibility review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#how-a-study-is-reviewed). <Tooltip tip="The MUHC's final authorization for a study to begin, which the Personne mandatée issues once the scientific, ethics and institutional feasibility reviews are favourable and, where the study needs it, Health Canada has authorized it." headline="MUHC Authorization" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#muhc-auth">MUHC Authorization</Tooltip> is the final step. You submit your study in <Tooltip tip="The MUHC's electronic platform through which studies conducted at the MUHC are submitted for review and managed, from the initial submission to the study completion report." headline="Nagano" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#nagano">Nagano</Tooltip>, the MUHC's registry of all research under its auspices. A study must also meet [other conditions before recruitment begins](/kb/submission/submission-overview#before-recruitment-and-keeping-approval-valid).

The MUHC's <Tooltip tip="A body, not affiliated with the sponsor, whose main mandate is to approve the start of biomedical research involving human participants and to review it periodically, to protect the participants' rights, safety and well-being." headline="Research Ethics Board (REB)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#reb">Research Ethics Board (REB)</Tooltip> conducts the ethics review. The REB also conducts the [scientific review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#scientific-and-ethics-review-by-the-reb), unless a recognized peer committee has already reviewed the science and you provide documentation of its review.

A [study at more than one Quebec site](/kb/design/multi-centric) in the public health network is reviewed by one Quebec REB for all of them, which may be another institution's REB. The MUHC still does its own institutional feasibility review and authorization.

The REB oversees your study for as long as the study runs. The MUHC's board of directors entrusts the REB with reviewing research under the MUHC's auspices. The REB reports directly to the board and is independent in the projects it reviews. The [REB's office](mailto:reb@muhc.mcgill.ca) is part of the MUHC's [Centre for Applied Ethics](https://muhc.ca/cae).

In the [institutional feasibility review](/kb/submission/submission-overview#institutional-feasibility-review):
- <Tooltip tip="The RI-MUHC's Quality Assurance department, which checks the credentials and training of each investigator who submits a study, audits research and maintains the RI-MUHC's SOPs." headline="Quality Assurance (QA)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#qa">Quality Assurance</Tooltip> (QA) [checks the credentials and training](/kb/governance/quality-assurance#pass-the-qa-check-when-you-submit-a-study) of each investigator who submits the study.
- The [Research Agreements Office](mailto:contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca) handles the study's agreements.
- The MUHC departments whose services the study uses review that use.

Quebec's health ministry requires each establishment in its network that conducts research to authorize formally all research under its auspices. The ministry's official name is the Ministère de la Santé, de la Prévention et des Aînés. At the MUHC, the President and Chief Executive Officer holds that authority and has delegated it in writing to the <Tooltip tip="The person whom an establishment of Quebec's health network formally mandates to authorize the research conducted under its auspices." headline="Personne mandatée" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#personne-mandatee">Personne mandatée</Tooltip>, the Director of Innovation, Performance and Quality. The authorization letter that the [Personne mandatée](mailto:personne.mandatee@muhc.mcgill.ca) issues sets out your commitments and the MUHC's.

Do not start any research activity, including recruitment, data collection or analysis, until you have both REB approval and MUHC Authorization.

A study without MUHC Authorization [isn't covered by the MUHC's institutional insurance](/kb/funding/research-liability-insurance#know-what-covers-your-study).{/* The Research Facilitator, through MF, Nov. 13, 2026 (FACTS "The Research Facilitator, Nov. 13, answers 1, 3 to 5, 7 to 11 and 13"). */}

If you aren't sure which office handles part of your study's review, the [Research Facilitator](mailto:research.facilitator@muhc.mcgill.ca) can help you plan it.

## Have your research agreements reviewed

Every research agreement for a study with a third party must be drafted, reviewed and negotiated by the [Research Agreements Office](mailto:contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca). [Research Agreements](/kb/funding/clinical-trial-agreements) sets out which studies need one, who signs it and at what stage it can be signed.

## Who is accountable for research at the MUHC

The MUHC's board of directors adopts the *Regulatory Framework*. The board is ultimately accountable for all research under the MUHC's auspices with human participants, their data or their biological material. The MUHC's President and Chief Executive Officer holds institutional responsibility for that research.

The MUHC is an establishment of [Santé Québec](/kb/governance/laws-regulations#apply-quebec-rules-at-the-muhc) and is affiliated with McGill University's Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences. RI-MUHC researchers and MUHC clinicians are also affiliated with McGill.

The RI-MUHC, the MUHC's research arm, is a legal entity separate from the MUHC and is governed by its own board of directors. The RI-MUHC's Chief Scientific Officer directs and manages all RI-MUHC research. The *Regulatory Framework* gives this position its former title, Executive Director and Chief Scientific Officer.{/* MF, Nov. 15, 2026 (ledger row 207): "The Executive Director and Chief Scientific Officer has been retitled to just Chief Scientific Officer"; "COO (now Exec Director) handles all admin/finance stuff and CSO handles all science stuff". */}

The Chief Scientific Officer is responsible for making sure that the requirements of <Tooltip tip="A standard for the design, conduct, performance, monitoring, auditing, recording, analysis and reporting of clinical studies that provides assurance that the data and reported results are credible and accurate, and that the rights, integrity and confidentiality of participants are protected." headline="Good Clinical Practice (GCP)" cta="Full definition" href="/kb/foundations/glossary#gcp">good clinical practice</Tooltip>, the RI-MUHC's [standard operating procedures](/sops) and federal and provincial regulators are available to the research community. The good clinical practice requirements are the International Council for Harmonisation's guideline E6 for drugs and the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) standard ISO 14155 for medical devices.

The Chief Scientific Officer is also responsible for making sure that appropriate measures are taken to secure and maintain compliance with these requirements.

The Director of the Centre for Innovative Medicine and Clinical Research reports to the Chief Scientific Officer. The Director oversees the support of clinical research at the RI-MUHC, including the Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM), and the conduct of principal investigators in clinical research.

Every study must have a principal investigator, who has the qualifications its type of research requires and is responsible for the project. Before acting as principal investigator at the MUHC, a person must hold [the credential the MUHC requires for their profession and study](/training/compliance-requirements#human-research-privileges-and-researcher-status), or a written institutional authorization. The RI-MUHC's [Performance Office](mailto:riprograms@muhc.mcgill.ca) issues the certificates for privileges and status.

## Work with the Centre for Innovative Medicine

The CIM is a clinical research facility of the RI-MUHC. The CIM is located at the MUHC, but its operations, management and [services](/cim/services) fall under the RI-MUHC. If your study uses [CIM services](/cim/planning), [a CIM agreement must be in place](/sops/cr-002#5-5-3).

{/* Sources: the Regulatory Framework, section 2.2.07; [SOP-CR-002, section 5.5.3](/sops/cr-002#5-5-3) ("if using CIM services, a CIM agreement must be in place"). */}

## Research at The Neuro

At the Montreal Neurological Institute-Hospital (The Neuro), research is under the MUHC's auspices when it meets any of these conditions:
- It involves people who receive services from the MUHC as participants.
- It uses MUHC resources.
- It is conducted under the responsibility of researchers in a way that involves their affiliation with the MUHC or the RI-MUHC.

Research at The Neuro under the MUHC's auspices is approved by the MUHC's REB or by the REB of another establishment of Quebec's health network. The MUHC's Personne mandatée authorizes it.

All clinical trials under Health Canada's purview at The Neuro must be managed by its Clinical Research Unit.

## Raise a complaint or a concern

A complaint from a participant in a research project goes to the MUHC's [Quality and Complaints Commissioner](https://muhc.ca/ombudsman), at the Ombudsman's Office. The commissioner is independent of the MUHC's directorates. When participants sign the consent form, they are told how to reach the commissioner. Participants have the same rights as any other user of the MUHC's services.

A concern about ethics or scientific integrity in the conduct of a research project [goes to the Personne mandatée](/kb/governance/research-integrity#raise-a-concern-about-how-research-is-conducted), the MUHC's person responsible for the responsible conduct of research.{/* No address here on purpose: whether the Personne mandatée's inbox (personne.mandatee@muhc.mcgill.ca, the Contacts row, for MUHC Authorization) suits a confidential concern is an open question for the Personne mandatée's office, kept in a hidden note on Research Integrity's Contacts (the Framework's Appendix B, §3.03: the complainant's identity "must be kept confidential"); the linked section gives the reporting routes. */}{/* Who the person responsible for the responsible conduct of research is, and that the Framework's Appendix B (the events and allegations team, the intake committee) is in force: the Research Facilitator, through MF, Nov. 13, 2026. */}
