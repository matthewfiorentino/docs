## Contacts by service

| Write to | About |
|---|---|
| The Performance Office, [riprograms@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:riprograms@muhc.mcgill.ca) | Research Privileges and Researcher Status. |
| Quality Assurance (QA), [QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:QAClinicalResearch@muhc.mcgill.ca) | Human research standard operating procedures (SOPs), QA's feasibility review checks and regulatory questions. |
| The MUHC Research Ethics Board (REB), [reb@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:reb@muhc.mcgill.ca) | Ethics review of a study before you submit it. Outside Nagano, write to the reviewing panel's own inbox on the Centre for Applied Ethics' [contact page](https://muhc.ca/cae/page/contact-information). If you aren't sure which panel reviews the study, write to this address. When communication with the REB takes place outside Nagano, the REB asks you to include the REB number or equivalent designation assigned to the study in all your correspondence with it from then on. |
| The Personne mandatée's office, [personne.mandatee@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:personne.mandatee@muhc.mcgill.ca) | MUHC Authorization. |
| The RI-MUHC's Training Advisor, [training-formation@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:training-formation@muhc.mcgill.ca) | Questions about TalentLMS and the training records it keeps. |
| The Centre for Innovative Medicine (CIM), [clinical.research@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:clinical.research@muhc.mcgill.ca) | Whether the CIM can support your study, and its services. |
| Capture and Optimization of Research Data (CORD), [cord@rimuhc.ca](mailto:cord@rimuhc.ca) | Data management plans and REDCap. Requests for REDCap projects and billed services go through the [CORD Service Request Form](https://portal.rimuhc.ca/cim/redcap/surveys/?s=LPCYP8KP4JHP8C79). |
| The RI-MUHC's Equity, Diversity and Inclusion (EDI) team, [edi.ri@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:edi.ri@muhc.mcgill.ca) | Sex- and gender-based analysis plus (SGBA+) consultations, inclusive recruitment and retention, tools, training and programs for equity-seeking groups. |
| The Research Agreements Office, [contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:contracts@muhc.mcgill.ca) | Legal questions about research agreements. |
| The MUHC's Directorate of Innovation, Performance and Quality, [efvp@muhc.mcgill.ca](mailto:efvp@muhc.mcgill.ca) | Privacy impact assessments (EFVP). |
